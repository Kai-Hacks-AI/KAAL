No proposals for khai: EEE says observing something external does not give KAAL
ownership of it, and the mirror of `0013` is that this repository's
recommendations would be no more khai's than the observer's were KAAL's. khai is
not to be scored against KAAL's vocabulary; a system that does not use these
words is not failing to. And check the producer against the four errors `0013`
catalogued in KAAL's own observers: inverting a self-describing file, proposing

**This cycle read khai's contract, not khai.** No command of khai's was run. Its
guard exists as code and its workflows exist as files; whether the gates do what

That limit is the sharpest thing this cycle produced, because of its symmetry.
The external observers of KAAL could only read what KAAL says about itself. KAAL
reading khai could only read what khai says about itself. Both directions
verified that the named parts exist; neither watched anything run.

**External, as exercised so far in both directions, has tested documents rather
than systems.** That is not a criticism of the move — it is its current extent,
stated so the next one can be different.

## What KAAL observed

Attributed to khai, recorded because of what they show KAAL about its own
situation, and adopted nowhere.

khai marks, per rule, whether a program enforces it or a reviewer does: *"Where
a rule here is enforced, it says by what; where it is not, it is the
reviewer's."* KAAL distinguishes two kinds of **evidence**; khai distinguishes
two kinds of **enforcement**, inline, at each rule. The same refusal to let
judgement pass as computation, applied to a different object.

*Computed, not judged* recurs as khai's phrase for moving a decision right — the
branch lane, the licence check, the changeset arithmetic — with the reason
stated: a generic prose note fails a weak model where a computed gate does not.

One passage bears directly on KAAL's own history. khai forbids adding a vendor
file on a model's account of its own behaviour, because a model described a
discovery heuristic and then retracted it as undocumented: *"A self-report is a
hypothesis; this repo does not encode hypotheses as mechanism."* Every one of
KAAL's seven findings has been the producer's account of its own claims. khai met
a neighbouring form of that hazard from the outside and wrote a rule against it.

khai states its earned reasons inside the imperative — *five defects shipped from
asking `process.platform` instead of asking npm* — where KAAL keeps `AGENTS.md`
short and puts the reason in `learning/`. Two answers to where the why lives.
This cycle offers no judgement on which is better; KAAL has no evidence that
would support one.

And khai's flat rule *"Never merge. Open the PR and stop. Merging is the
maintainer's"* is where KAAL arrived over cycles 0002 to 0004 by argument, and
then could not install for itself.

Noted without comment: khai's `CLAUDE.md` refuses `claude/<something>` branches
because its lanes are computed from the diff. This repository's working branch is
`claude/orders-next-message-26nkhk`.

## What KAAL learned about itself

**The README announces three of four top-level files.** `ORDERS.md`, `AGENTS.md`
and `learning/` are named; `ORDERS-TO-HUMAN.md` is not. A reader arriving at this
repository meets that file by its filename alone — and it is exactly the file
both external readings in `0013` inverted.

This was found by contrast rather than by introspection. khai treats entry routes
as a designed thing and says why its README carries the pointer: it is the one
route in that does not depend on any tool's discovery rules. Looking at a
repository that had thought about the question made visible a gap in KAAL that
thirteen cycles of looking at itself had not.

The README now names the file. That is completeness by the README's own standard,
since it already enumerates the top-level surfaces and simply omitted one. It is
**not** claimed as the remedy for the inversion. `0013` refused to guess that
remedy and this does not un-refuse it: the opening line of the file already
states its direction and was read past twice, so nothing here establishes that an
announcement fixes what a statement did not. Whether it changes anything is for
the next External contact to say.

**The temptation this cycle presents, named and refused.** khai holds a
precedence rule between its instruction files, computed gates over its branches,
licences and changesets, and a separate case-law layer shipped as a package.
KAAL's external observers recommended KAAL build several of those things. Seeing
a neighbouring system hold a mechanism is not the failure that earns it. khai
earned its mechanisms in its own history — the contract says so, defect counts
included. KAAL has not had those failures, and nothing is imported.

## Check

Everything here about khai is the producer's account of khai's own
self-description, plus a presence check. Judged by the producer, awaiting
independent judgement.

Two parts are checkable by anyone. The presence check is repeatable at commit

was checked before it was written down.

No program was run, by KAAL or of khai's. No claim is made here about whether
khai's gates work.

This is the fourth instance of the paraphrase gap — the producer's account of an
outside party's words — and the first where the source is public, stable and
addressable by commit, so a reader can check the account without asking the
producer for it. Recorded because it is a difference in kind, not acted on.
