# 0003 — An added interpretation is not an order

2026-09-19. Cycle run by an agent (Claude) on `claude/orders-next-message-26nkhk`,
in response to kaihacksai's second review, of `1d85fa7`, which again requested
changes and left PR #1 unauthorized.

## The failure

Cycle 0002 presented the independence requirement — that a producer cannot
validate their own claim — as derived from MOVE. It is not in MOVE. Checked
against the source: `ORDERS.md` contains no "author" and no "other than", and
its single occurrence of "independent" describes External contexts, not
validation.

What actually happened, in three steps. Cycle 0001 wrote into `AGENTS.md` that
the boundary is where "someone other than the change's author" can see what
crossed — an interpretation, since MOVE requires only that the boundary be
observable and says nothing about to whom. The first review quoted that phrase
back while identifying a real gap. Cycle 0002 then treated the phrase as MOVE's
own content and built a derivation on it. Within three steps, a judgement the
repository had made *about* the orders had become, in the repository's own
account, something the orders *said*.

Worth naming, because it will recur: the repository launders its own
interpretations into the authority of the orders it was given. The briefing
warned against inheriting mechanisms discovered under another history. This is
the same disease in the register of meaning rather than machinery, and it is
harder to see, because an interpretation leaves no artifact to point at. It
simply becomes how the text reads.

Two independent reviews so far, two findings, and both are provenance errors.
0001 mislabelled the **standing** of its claims — judged by the producer,
presented as validated. 0002 mislabelled their **source** — a repository
judgement, presented as an order. Two data points is thin, but the shape is
consistent enough to act on: what this system has so far got wrong about itself
is not what it believes, but where its beliefs came from.

A second and smaller overreach travelled with the first, and came from the same
confidence: 0002's flat claim that nothing about who may judge is computable.

## Plan

Reclassify the independence rule by its actual source while keeping it in
force, narrow the computability claim to what one instance supports, and mark
added judgements as added in the lines of `AGENTS.md` that make them.

Refusal conditions set before starting: refuse CI, branch protection, schemas,
skills, scripts and approval machinery, as the review permitted record and
guidance only. Refuse to withdraw the independence rule — review found it
mislabelled, not wrong, and withdrawing it would be over-correction dressed as
humility. Refuse to invent a provenance format, field or tag; marking belongs in
the prose of the claim, and a format here would be a mechanism nothing has
earned. Refuse to edit the bodies of 0001 or 0002.

## Do

`AGENTS.md` now opens by saying which two kinds of thing are written in it and
why added judgements are marked. *Where you work* separates what MOVE requires —
a boundary, and that it be observable — from what this repository decided: that
the boundary is the pull request, and that the observer differs from the author.
*Who may validate* now opens by stating that `ORDERS.md` does not answer the
question, attributes the rule to cycle 0002 as an Agent's judgement, keeps the
separability observation on MOVE's side of the line, and states the additional
premise plainly: validation is worth having because it can catch what the
producer could not see in their own work. If that premise is wrong, the section
is wrong with it. That sentence is now in the guidance rather than implied by
it.

The computability claim, narrowed honestly, splits in two. Whether the judge's
identity differs from the producer's is plainly computable — it is a comparison
a Program could make. What resists computation is whether a given evaluation
actually judged the thing at issue, which is where the meaning lives. What this
cycle establishes is only that deciding *what validation means* required
judgement now, not that enforcement is forever a matter of meaning.

An amendment is appended to 0002 stating both overreaches. Its body is
untouched.

## Check

Judged by the producer, awaiting independent judgement. That includes the claim
that the independence rule now sits on the correct side of the line, which is a
judgement about a judgement and exactly the kind the producer is worst placed to
assess.

One observation this cycle can put slightly above bare assertion: neither
correction was generated by the producer noticing its own error. Both took an
independent reviewer, twice, on work the producer had already checked and
believed sound. That is weak evidence — one reviewer, two instances, no
counterfactual — but it is evidence in favour of the very premise *Who may
validate* now rests on, and it arrived without being sought. It is the closest
thing to a test this repository has produced so far, and it is still not a test.

Still no program ran.

## Act — what this cycle leaves for the next

An emerging tension, watched rather than acted on: every review round so far has
produced a numbered record. Three cycles, three rounds, one pull request. If
that continues, `learning/` becomes a transcript of a conversation rather than a
memory of what was learned, and a later reader will not be able to tell which
records carry anything durable. Two rounds of one PR is too little to act on.
The condition that would earn a change is a cycle whose record adds nothing a
previous record already said — and if that happens, the cheapest fix is to amend
instead of numbering, not to invent a format.

What remains uncontrolled is one level up from where the last two cycles
thought it was. Provenance — both the standing of a claim and its source — is
carried entirely by prose that a producer writes about its own work. That is the
same weakness both reviews found, and marking interpretations in `AGENTS.md`
does not remove it; nothing prevents cycle 0004 from writing an unmarked
interpretation. The failure that would earn a mechanism looks like an
interpretation cited as an order, caught only after it has already shaped a
decision. If that recurs despite the marking, the repetition is HAPTIC's signal
to move rightward, and the smallest available move is not a schema but a check a
reader or a Program can run: does every claim in `AGENTS.md` that is not in
`ORDERS.md` say so? That check is computable. Whether its verdicts mean anything
is not.

Open from 0002 and still open: whether an Agent may serve as the independent
judge. Both judgements that have actually caught something here were Human.

Embed and External remain unexercised. This is the second consecutive cycle
consumed by review of the first. Recorded as an unpaid obligation, not an
