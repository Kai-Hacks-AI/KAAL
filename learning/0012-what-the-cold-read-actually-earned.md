## First: a global reading order was not earned

`learning/README.md` was given the rule *read the newest record first*, with the
`0010` tension attached to it.

What the cold reader actually observed was narrower: reading in order and
stopping early can leave a reader relying on a claim a later record overturned.
What it did not observe, and what nothing here establishes, is that
newest-first is the right way through this corpus generally. It plainly is not
for much of it. `0003` only means anything after `0002`; the amendments only
mean anything beside the claims they correct; and several records exist
precisely to preserve a mistake in its original form, which a reader arriving
backwards meets as a correction to something they have not yet read. A
newest-first habit also invites treating the latest producer-authored synthesis
as an index of what matters, which is the producer writing the reader's map.

So the rule is replaced by what the failure earned, which is a guarantee and an
obligation rather than an order of traversal. The guarantee already existed and
was simply never stated to the reader: when a record's claim is superseded, a
note saying so is appended to *that record*. The obligation follows from it —
read to the end of the record whose claim you intend to rely on, because nothing
here is corrected in place, and the end of a record is where you learn whether
its middle still stands. Sampling a passage is what goes wrong, not reading in
any particular direction.

The `0010` fact about standing stays, but as a fact rather than an instruction:
the standing of this corpus lives in the most recent record that addresses it,
and that record is always the least validated thing here.

## Second: the cold reader's finding is not in the files

`0011` calls the navigational hazard "the reader's own finding... the only part
of these two cycles not written by the party it concerns."

That is wrong in exactly the way this repository keeps being wrong. The cold
reader's report is not in the files. What the files hold is the producer's
account of what that reader said. The account may be faithful — and the producer
is not in a position to certify that it is. Under the file-only brain, what a
later reader can inspect is a paraphrase, and calling it independently authored
evidence promotes it into a standing it does not have.

This is the second instance of one structural gap. `0006` recorded that the
reviewer's own words are not in Git either, so every account of every criticism
here is the criticised party's paraphrase. Now the same is true of the one
external instrument this repository has used. Everything that has ever entered
this file brain from outside has passed through the producer.

What would change that is not a transcript — the Human has refused transcripts
twice, and the ask is durability of substance, not of correspondence. It is the
external party writing into the files directly, as its own author. That is not
earned, is not requested, and is recorded here only so that the next cycle
tempted to call a paraphrase independent knows what the actual remedy would look
like.

## The guidance's second data point

`AGENTS.md` says that when a cycle states *why* a choice is required, that
sentence is the weakest thing in the cycle. `0011` was its first prospective
use and reported that it caught one thing.

It also missed both findings in this review, in the same record, and both are
exactly what it describes: *why* a reading rule was sufficient, and *why* a
paraphrase counted as independent evidence.

Applied once, caught one, missed two. That is the honest tally and it is better
evidence than the first instance alone, because it establishes the guidance is
not sufficient by itself — which nobody claimed, but which is now observed
rather than assumed. The count in `AGENTS.md` moves to seven, and the line keeps
saying that nothing establishes it helps.

Seven findings, and the list still has the same shape: standing, source,
premise, carrier, scope, sufficiency, attribution. Not once the claim itself.
