## A repository cannot state its own current standing

That last sentence is the cycle's actual finding, and it was predicted before the
crossing rather than discovered after it.

`AGENTS.md` says a validation attaches to the body as it stood when judged.
Writing down that a body was validated changes the body. So the statement is
always about a predecessor, and is itself unvalidated at the moment of writing.
Every validation this repository ever records will be one cycle late, written by
a producer who cannot complete it.

That is not a flaw to engineer away. It is the same fact as *a producer cannot
validate its own claim*, seen from the side of the record rather than the side of
the judge, and it is the price of the rule being honest. What it costs a reader
is one thing, and the files should say it plainly: the most recent records in
this repository are always the least established ones, and a reader who wants
validated knowledge should not read the end of `learning/` first.

## Two observations from the crossing

Neither is a judgement; both are reads of state that anyone can repeat.

The tree of accepted `main`, extracted with its repository metadata discarded —
fourteen files, no `.git`, nothing to query — still yields the standing of its
claims, the judge, what he judged against, the exclusions he stated, and the
control in force. This is the Human's file-only constraint holding on real
accepted experience rather than on a rehearsal.

And the Engine's own record of the crossing is wrong. `894f54d` is authored as

thing the control installed at cycle 0004 exists to prevent. The files say
otherwise and are correct. Nothing was designed to demonstrate that Engine
metadata can be wrong while the files stay honest; it simply happened on the
first crossing, and it is the strongest evidence this repository has for the
boundary the Human drew.
