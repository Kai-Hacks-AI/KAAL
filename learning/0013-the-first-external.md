# 0013 — The first External

2026-09-19. Cycle run by an agent (Claude) on a branch restarted from accepted
`main`. The first External contact, thirteen cycles after the orders said not to
wait for the engine to look finished.

## The instrument, and the boundary the Human set

The Human ran two observations through Perplexity on the single prompt "Analyze
KAAL", with the model chosen by the service. The first read accepted `main`
after PR #1, the second after PR #2.

The boundary he set with them: evidence about KAAL may enter KAAL; the
observer's proposed solutions do not become KAAL merely by being proposed. They
are recorded below as an observer's recommendations and are not adopted.

Stated before anything else, because two cycles were spent learning to say it:
**the observers' reports are not in these files.** What is here is the
producer's account of what they said. That is now the third instance of one
structural gap — the reviewer's words in `0006`, the cold reader's in `0012`,
and now an external service's. Everything that has entered this brain from
outside has passed through the producer, and this record is no exception.

**Human-supplied context, cycle 0013:** External runs in both directions.
KAAL exposed to something it did not construct, and something it did not
construct looking at KAAL, are both valid External moves and both valid
learning. EEE's wording describes the first; the Human supplied that the second
counts. This is his, not derived from the orders.

## What transmitted

Both readings recovered the central idea — that KAAL keeps produced, judged and
validated apart and refuses to let an agent's account become accepted truth by
default.

Both recovered it from the **filenames**. Both quote the record titles: judged
is not validated, an added interpretation is not an order, the producer cannot
install its own constraint, and in the second reading, a validation does not
reach forward. Neither quotes a record body.

That is a finding nothing here anticipated. `learning/README.md` deliberately
declines to specify a format and says nothing about naming, so the titles became
the corpus's primary transmission surface by accident rather than design. It is
recorded as an observation, not acted on: one instrument, two runs, and the
alternative — writing titles *for* external readers — would be the producer
composing the reader's index, which `0012` already refused for a different
reason.

The scope-of-validation discipline transmitted in the second reading only, and
not from the files. The observer reports what the validation does not establish
by reading it off the squash commit trailer — the Engine metadata `0008`
demoted to convenience. Neither reading reports per-body standing from `0009` or
`0010`. So the files-only path for standing was not exercised by this contact,
and a question stands that these files cannot answer: without that trailer,
would the observer have found the standing at all?

## What did not transmit

`AGENTS.md` marks three kinds of thing — what the orders say, what the Human
supplied later, and what this repository judged for itself. Neither reading
noticed the distinction exists. It cost cycles 0003 through 0008 and several
reviews to get right, and it was invisible to both readings.

Neither reading reported the standing of any body, which means the sampling
hazard from `0011` was not exercised either. Neither repeated `0008`'s
superseded claim — but neither went looking for standing, so this is not
evidence that the navigation invariant added in `0012` works.

## The misreading that survived everything

Both readings describe `ORDERS-TO-HUMAN.md` as the human-control layer,
preserving human authority and decision control. The file's third line says the
opposite: "This file holds the reverse direction: things KAAL needs done that
only the Human can do." The second reading then builds on the inversion,
praising the separation for making "the human control plane visible."

Two readings, two different instruments, three records added in between, and the
inversion is stable. The title dominated the content, and the content's own
correction sits in the opening paragraph.

This is the finding the cold read of `0011` could not have produced, because
that agent was told what to look for. It is the first thing External has
supplied that Embed could not.

**Nothing is changed in response, deliberately.** The obvious remedies are a
clearer name and an opening line that states the direction. The opening line is
already there and was read past, twice. So the defect is not a missing sentence,
and the fix is not the one that first suggests itself. One instrument's stable
misreading does not establish what would actually work, and guessing the remedy
here is the sufficiency move this repository has been wrong about seven times. A
second independent inversion, or a reader acting wrongly on it, is what would
earn a change.

## The pressure from outside

Both readings propose the machinery `ORDERS.md` names as not to be bootstrapped:
a claim-ledger schema, a precedence model, CI policy checks, a threat model, a
reference implementation, an evidence schema, an evaluation matrix. Both had read
the refusal. Both called the restraint a strength. Both proposed the machinery
anyway.

The second goes further and recommends opening issues for the unknowns the
records already name — which would move knowledge out of the files into Engine
state, two paragraphs after praising the constraint that forbids it.

None of it is adopted. It is recorded because it is evidence about the world this
repository sits in rather than about the repository. `ORDERS.md` anticipated the
danger of inheriting the previous KAAL's mechanisms; the actual pressure arrived
from outside, unprompted, twice, from a reader that had the refusal in front of
it. "Let failures earn them" will not be left alone, and the refusal has to be
re-made at every contact rather than settled once.

One thing the second observer did that is worth recording without adopting: it
reported its own connector returning a merged flag inconsistent with its merge
timestamp, and explicitly declined to conclude the changes had been rejected. An
instrument reporting its own inconsistency and refusing the inference is what
this repository's evidence vocabulary asks for, arrived at without having read
it as a rule.

## The confound

Between the two readings, the corpus gained three records **and** the instrument
changed: the first reading used web search and attached citations to unrelated
repositories and papers, the second used a GitHub connector and was accurate
about commits, file counts and dates. The accuracy gain is at least as
attributable to the instrument as to the corpus.

This was stated before the readings were seen rather than after, as this cycle's
refusal condition. The delta does not cleanly measure KAAL and no conclusion here
treats it as if it did.
