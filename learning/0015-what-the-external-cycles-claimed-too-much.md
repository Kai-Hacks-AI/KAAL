## Four corrections

**"Two different instruments" was not observed.** `0013` says the two readings
came from two different instruments. What is observable in the reports is that
the *access path* differed: the first carries web-style citations to unrelated
repositories and papers, the second carries connector evidence. The supplier was
the same, and the service chose the model, so whether the underlying model
differed between runs is unknown here. The honest statement is that the access
path changed and the model may have; "different instruments" promoted an unknown
into a fact.

**The filename claim was causal and the evidence was not.** `0013` says both
readings recovered the central idea *from* the filenames, and calls the titles
the corpus's primary transmission surface. What was observed is narrower: the
titles were quoted explicitly in both reports and no record body was. A report
can paraphrase a body without quoting it, and the second report recovered
detailed material beyond any title. That the titles were salient enough to be
repeated is the finding; where the understanding actually came from is not
established and is not recoverable from the reports.

**The consequence attributed to the issues recommendation is too strong.**
`0013` says the second observer's recommendation to open issues would move
knowledge out of the files into Engine state. An issue can duplicate knowledge,
point at it, or operationalise it while the files continue to carry it. The
recommendation may still be machinery this repository has not earned — that is
the reason to decline it, and it stands on its own without attributing a
consequence the recommendation does not necessarily have.

**"The next External should observe behaviour rather than documents" prescribes
more than the evidence supports.** What these two cycles establish is that both
directions stayed at self-description and contract surfaces. They do not
establish that the next valuable contact must be executable behaviour; a deeper
encounter with content — architecture, engines, composites — could teach
something real without running anything. The narrowed conclusion is that future
External should go beyond self-description and contract surfaces, with the form
left open.

That narrowing outlived the record: asked what to do next, the producer
recommended a behaviour test on the same reasoning, before the review arrived.
The error was not confined to the file.

## What `0014` did not read, and why that is the finding

`0014` read khai's README, `AGENTS.md` and vendor files, verified the artifacts
they name, and stopped. It did not read `khai-arch`, the engines or the
composites — the architecture and content surfaces that hold the bulk of a
repository that is four thousand markdown files deep.

The prompt was open. So what KAAL chose to inspect, given a free encounter with
a large unfamiliar repository, is itself evidence: it went to the governance and
instruction surfaces and stayed there. `0014` is right that it read the contract
and not the system, and this is the sharper version of that admission — not a
limit imposed by time or access, but a direction of attention.

The obvious reading is that a repository made entirely of governance read
another repository's governance and recognised itself. That reading is the
producer's and is worth no more than that. What is checkable is the list of what
`0014` says it read.

Nothing is expanded to compensate. The Human's reason for naming khai was
withheld from the encounter deliberately and is not recorded here; retrofitting
it would destroy exactly the evidence the open prompt produced.

## What this says about the guidance

`AGENTS.md` has carried, since cycle 0008, the line that a cycle's statement of
*why* a choice is required is its weakest sentence. Seven findings had landed
there. These four land there too — the identity of the evidence, the cause of a
transmission, the consequence of a recommendation, and the sufficiency of a
conclusion about what comes next — bringing it to eleven.

Four of the eleven arrived in a single review of one candidate, with the
guidance in force and applied. Whatever the line does, it did not reduce the
rate here. That is a third data point and the least flattering: not inert, not
sufficient, and not visibly slowing the error down. It stays as guidance and the
count is updated; no stronger control is proposed, because the failures are
about meaning and nothing here computes meaning.
