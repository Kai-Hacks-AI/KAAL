# review/C1 skill-trial-review

Introduces the independent review of a delivered Skill and its recorded trial: a review record and the reviewer's own Skill-versus-baseline trial, kept per application. Review knows no subject. A subject is named only in the data of an application under `applications/`. A first slice: no gate, no change to the subject, no publication.

## Baseline

`main` holds `kaal-review` 0.0.1 (the Skill Node, three Definitions and the Agent Skill) and no review of any delivered Skill and its trial. It holds `kaal-controls`, whose shape is followed. This Change cites nothing that is not on `main`. The first application names its subject by commit in its own data.
