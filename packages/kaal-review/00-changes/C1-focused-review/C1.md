# review/C1 focused-review

Introduces the review as a comparison chosen by a focus. A focus names a reference, what was promised or intended, and an actual, what was delivered, and the review compares them. Two focuses are defined: promotion, where what a promotion promised to change is compared with what changed, and capability, where a capability's Intent is compared with what it delivers. Any other focus is possible when it is provided with its own reference and actual. A review is judgement and a test is proof: Review judges what can only be judged, points at what could be tested, and runs no test of its own. The boundary is symmetric and stated here once: Review relies on no Skill of Testing, and Testing relies on none of Review's. Review knows no subject. A review is kept per application, one focus applied to one subject. A first slice: no gate, no change to the reviewed work, no publication.
