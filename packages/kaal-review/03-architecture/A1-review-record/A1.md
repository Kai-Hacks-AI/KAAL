# review/A1 review-record

Inside `packages/kaal-review/00-changes/C1-focused-review/applications/<name>/`, one folder per application: `subject.json` (the `focus`, the `reference` and the `actual` each as a label and what is read, whether the evidence is a `trial`, what is `inspected` and the `questions` to answer, the last two as lists of strings), `review.md`, the record, and, where the evidence is a trial, `trial/`, holding the reviewer's `key.json`, `author-statements.json` (a snapshot of the author's statements, so that none is reused), its `skill.<n>.*` and `baseline.<n>.*` runs (prompt, raw output, record), at least three of each. `review.md` has the chapters Focus, Reference, Actual, Comparison, Evidence, Not checked, Limits, For the Owner and No authority. The Cases run over every application and need at least one. The focuses promotion and capability are defined by Review; any other focus must carry its own reference and actual. The trial adds repeated runs, a reviewer-written key fixed before the runs and everything the work says it reports in the answers. No script and no change to the installed Skill.
