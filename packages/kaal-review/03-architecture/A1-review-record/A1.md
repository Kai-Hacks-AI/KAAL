# review/A1 review-record

Inside `packages/kaal-review/00-changes/C1-focused-review/applications/<name>/`, one folder per application: `subject.json` (the `focus`, the `reference` and the `actual` each as a label and what is read, what is `inspected` and the `questions` to answer, the last two as lists of strings), `review.md`, the record, no test or trial folder: a review holds no proof of its own. `review.md` has the chapters Focus, Reference, Actual, Comparison, Evidence, Not checked, Limits, For the Owner and No authority. The Cases run over every application and need at least one. The focuses promotion and capability are defined by Review; any other focus must carry its own reference and actual. Each finding in the Comparison is marked Proven (citing the test evidence and its result), Judged (saying why it can only be judged) or Testable, not tested (saying what a test would have to show). No script, and no reference to Testing and no change to the installed Skill.
