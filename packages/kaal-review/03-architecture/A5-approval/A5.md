# review/A5 approval

| Element | Owner | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the Approver's declared authority and the decision to approve | Review | `13-source/github-apps/approver.json`, `readDeclaration(text, app)` in `judge-app.mjs` (the Inspector stays the default), and the pure judgement `judgeApproval(evidence, facts)` in `13-source/scripts/judge-approval.mjs`, which holds the list of trusted triggers as a constant |
| the facts (head, base, main, pure merge), the reviews and the posting of an approval | Hosting | the adapter that supplies the facts with its attribution, mints the Approver's token with the abilities declared and a recorded `appId`, calls the approve endpoint and refuses a token without the ability; it judges nothing about approval |
| what makes a SYNC clean, and `verify Sync` and `approve Sync` as controls | Release | the check at the head and the wiring that asks the Approver |
| the signed check | the Inspector, with Controls | one check per registered control, signed at a revision |
| creating and installing the App, storing its key, drawing its logo | the Owner, by hand | nothing in the repository |

`judgeApproval(evidence, facts)` takes the evidence of the run and the pull request as data and the facts that Hosting's adapter attributes to itself (`source: 'hosting'`: the head and base being decided, the revision of main and the pure-merge fact), and answers `{ decision: 'approve' | 'abstain', findings }`. It does not fetch the pull request, does not read the check from GitHub and does not post: Hosting gives it the evidence and acts on the answer, and a later push moves the head, so an approval already given is dismissed by the ruleset and the Approver is asked again for the new head. The CTO's call is that the Approver's workflow executes only code from main, on a trusted trigger, never checks out the head and judges from facts; `judgeApproval` takes the facts of that execution as evidence and abstains where they are missing. What decides is attributed to Hosting or fixed in the code of main, never selected by the caller. The workflow itself and its proof at WIRE are not in this Change. None of it is shipped in the Skill, which stays free of KAAL's own Apps.
