# review/A5 approval

| Element | Owner | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the Approver's declared authority and the decision to approve | Review | `13-source/github-apps/approver.json`, `readDeclaration(text, app)` in `judge-app.mjs` (the Inspector stays the default), and the pure judgement `judgeApproval(evidence, facts)` in `13-source/scripts/judge-approval.mjs`, which holds the list of trusted triggers as a constant |
| the facts (head with its parents, base, main, author, the runs of the signed check), the reviews and the posting of an approval | Hosting | the adapter that registers each fact it returns, answers `factOrigin(fact)` and offers `readPullRequestFacts`, mints the Approver's token with the abilities declared and a recorded `appId`, calls the approve endpoint and refuses a token without the ability; it judges nothing about approval |
| what makes a SYNC clean, and `verify Sync` and `approve Sync` as controls | Release | the check at the head and the wiring that asks the Approver |
| the signed check | the Inspector, with Controls | one check per registered control, signed at a revision |
| creating and installing the App, storing its key, drawing its logo | the Owner, by hand | nothing in the repository |

`judgeApproval(evidence, facts)` takes the evidence of the run (the head and base it means to decide on, and its execution) and the facts Hosting produced for the Approver (`readPullRequestFacts({ grant, pullRequest })`: head with its parents, base branch tip, revision of main, author and `checks`, every run of the named check at the head with GitHub's own status and conclusion; `readPullRequestFacts({ repo, number, check }, { grant })`), and asks Hosting's `factOrigin` about the whole fact before it trusts it; a part taken out of the fact carries no origin. A pure merge is judged here from the parents of the head (the base tip and main, no other); Hosting gives no verdict on it. It is built by `makeJudgeApproval({ factOrigin })`, which a Case uses with a registry of its own, and the delivered `judgeApproval` is that factory with Hosting's export. It answers `{ decision: 'approve' | 'abstain', findings }`. It does not fetch the pull request, does not read the check from GitHub and does not post: Hosting gives it the evidence and acts on the answer, and a later push moves the head, so an approval already given is dismissed by the ruleset and the Approver is asked again for the new head. The CTO's call is that the Approver's workflow executes only code from main, on a trusted trigger, never checks out the head and judges from facts; `judgeApproval` takes the facts of that execution as evidence and abstains where they are missing. What decides is produced by Hosting and recognised by its registry, or fixed in the code of main, never selected by the caller. Facts are not passed across a process or workflow boundary; the judgement reads them again with the Approver's own grant. The workflow itself and its proof at WIRE are not in this Change. None of it is shipped in the Skill, which stays free of KAAL's own Apps.
