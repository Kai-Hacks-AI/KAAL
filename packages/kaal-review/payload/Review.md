# Review

Review is the capability for reviewing work done in KAAL: a human observing the work, and Agents reviewing each other's work. Its scope is that alone. What a test demonstrates is Testing's, and what makes a Skill valid is Engineer Skill's.

A review reports what was observed and the evidence for it. It grants no authority: who may approve, merge or decide is not set by having reviewed, and a review by Agents never stands in for a human observing the work.
