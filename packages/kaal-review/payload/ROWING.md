# ROWING

ROWING is a mnemonic for disciplined review:

```
R  Review
O  Observed
W  Work
I  Intelligent
N  Not
G  Generalized
```

Review is the activity: neither carrying out the Work nor owning its intent. Observed Work is the Work as it was actually realized, with its evidence, and not the Work the reviewer imagines should have existed. Intelligent means judgment: deterministic checks are evidence for it, never a replacement for it. Not Generalized keeps the review with the Change that was intended: its Intent, Requirements and Architecture, the realized Work and its evidence. The review does not redesign the system, widen the Change or put a preferred general solution in place of the one intended. Broader consequences it finds are observations and future Changes, not part of this one.

ROWING is the loop of Work and review while the Work is still mutable: the Work is reviewed as observed, findings are resolved, and the two go round together, like rowers keeping one stroke, until they converge. The Owner holds the target and stays out of the stroke. ROWING owns no finality: what makes the Work final, and when, belongs to its owner and not to Review. Nor does an observation become generalized Knowledge by having been made in a review. Learning from Work once it is complete is WORK, which belongs to Learning and is distinct from ROWING.
