# Review

Review is the capability of examining a result, reporting what was found, and establishing whether review has converged. A result is whatever is put to review: it may come from any viewpoint or any capability of KAAL, and Review is specific to none of them. A review is a round: one reviewer's report on one result as it stood, naming that result and its exact identity, stating whether there are findings or the review has converged on it, and giving each finding concretely enough that the one who made the result can resolve it without asking what it meant. Review has converged exactly when the latest round says so and names the result as it now stands; change the result afterwards and it has not.

Review is a capability and not a role. Any role may review a result, provided the actor reviewing holds the authority and the independence the situation needs: it is not the actor that made the result, and it does not hold the seat merely because it is able to. A round states, in plain words, under whose authority the reviewer holds the seat and in what independence of the one whose result it is. Such a statement is something an owner can inspect and is not proof; a reviewer that cannot make it does not report convergence.

Its scope is that alone. Review does not decide when a review is owed, which actor reviews, how a seat is assigned, what is done with findings, or what follows convergence; whatever asks for a review decides that. It is not the discipline by which a result and its review interact, not the lifecycle of anything that is reviewed, and not a retrospective, which is learning after the activity and is another capability. Review is optional: it is not Core, a valid embedding does not need it, and embedding KAAL does not install it.
