# review/I8 validation-is-traceable

What can be validated is more than what can be verified, and a validation obligation and its judgement can be found as readily as a Test Case and its observation. A reader can name an element, anything at all, and learn which assessments of it are defined, which of them have a result, and which results still apply to the revision in question. The three stay apart: an observed result, an assessed conclusion and an authorized decision. A judgement informs a decision and is never one.

**Done when** a reader, given a tree and an element, can find the assessments defined for it, say which have no result, which results apply to a given revision, and see the conclusion of each with its reasoning, limits and who appraised it.
