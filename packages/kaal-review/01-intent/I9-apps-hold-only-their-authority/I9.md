# review/I9 apps-hold-only-their-authority

A check or a review that a GitHub App signs is trusted because of who signed it, so what an App may do, and what can reach its key, must be as small as the judgement it signs. Each App Review owns has one authority, declared as the abilities it needs and nothing more, and the part that judges a change holds no key. No App decides on, approves or merges anything it raised, and an App never opens, approves, merges or pushes beyond its one authority. A verdict that could not be reached is UNKNOWN and is never signed as a pass.

**Done when** the authority of each App is written down once, a declaration that holds more than its authority is refused, no App is declared that both raises and approves, and a workflow that lets the judge hold the key, or lets the signer run anything of the change, is refused.
