# review/I7 independent-review

An Agent other than the author inspects a delivered Skill and its trial, challenges what the evidence establishes, runs trials of its own and names what is left for the Owner to decide, so that those who accept the Skill can see what is and is not shown. A review grants no authority. Review knows no subject: it is applied to one. Owner: Review.
