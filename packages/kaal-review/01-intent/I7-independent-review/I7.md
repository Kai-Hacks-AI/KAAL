# review/I7 independent-review

An Agent other than the author compares, under a stated focus, what was promised or intended with what was delivered, challenges what the evidence establishes, separates what a test proves from what can only be judged, judges the latter, points at what could be tested, and names what is left for the Owner to decide, so that those who accept the work can see what is and is not shown. A review grants no authority. Review knows no subject: a focus is applied to one. Owner: Review.
