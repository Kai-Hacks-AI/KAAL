# review/I10 review-has-scope-and-reference

Review is broader than testing, and testing is more precise. A test verifies one exact subject; a review may examine an element, a file, a folder or a set of them. Three things are told apart and none overlaps another. The **scope** is what is examined. The **reference** is what the scope is compared against, an Intent, a Requirement, an Architecture element, a Defect, a file, or the scope itself when no outside reference is needed. The **criteria** are the dimensions and rules by which that comparison is judged: they say how to judge, never what to judge against, and they do not restate or stand in for the reference. Every review has all three, and a review that lacks any of them is not valid. A reader can ask, for every review item, what it examines, against what and by which criteria, and can ask the other way: which items examine this file, or this folder, and which are judged against this Requirement.

**Done when** every item states its scope, its reference and its criteria, an item that lacks one of them is not valid and says which, a reference that is the scope itself is stated as such and not left out, and a reader, given a tree and a file, a folder or a reference, finds the items that examine it or are judged against it.
