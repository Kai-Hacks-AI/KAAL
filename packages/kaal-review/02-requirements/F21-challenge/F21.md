# review/F21 challenge

The review answers, with evidence and without asking the subject to change: whether a Case the subject fails tests the Skill's intended behavior or only part of how the trial asked for it to be reported; what the Skill-versus-baseline comparison establishes and what it does not, where both arms match the key on most statements; and how far the key can be called independent of the Skill, where an answer or key was changed after being seen. The application lists its own questions in `subject.json`, and the record answers each.
