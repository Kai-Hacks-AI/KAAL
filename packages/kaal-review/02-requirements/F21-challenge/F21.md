# review/F21 compare-and-challenge

The review compares the reference with the actual in both directions: what was promised and not delivered, and what was delivered and not promised. Each finding states what was checked. It also challenges the evidence: whether a Case that fails tests the intended behavior or only part of how it was asked for; what a comparison with a baseline shows and does not show; how independent a key is of the work, where an answer or key was changed after being seen. The application lists its own questions in `subject.json`, and the record answers each.
