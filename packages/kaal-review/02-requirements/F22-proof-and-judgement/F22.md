# review/F22 proof-and-judgement

A review is judgement and a test is proof. Each finding of the comparison is marked Proven or Judged. A Proven finding cites the test evidence, a Case or a recorded trial, and its result, and relies on it without repeating it. A Judged finding says why it can only be judged and gives the reasoning. Where a finding could be proven by a test and none exists, the review does not run one: it marks the finding Testable, not tested and says what a test of it would have to show, so that whoever owns testing can act on it. The review runs no test and no trial of its own, and it does not rebuild a key.
