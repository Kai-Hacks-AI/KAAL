# review/F22 proof-and-judgement

A review is judgement and a test is proof. Each finding of the comparison is marked Proven or Judged. A Proven finding cites the test evidence, a Case or a recorded trial, and its result, and relies on it without repeating it. A Judged finding says why it can only be judged and gives the reasoning. Where a finding could be proven by a test and no test exists, the review does not run one: it marks the finding Testable, not tested and names it for Testing as a Gap or a Defect. The review runs no test and no trial of its own, and it does not rebuild a key.
