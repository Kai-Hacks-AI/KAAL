# review/F40 approval-decision

The decision to approve is a pure judgement over two things kept apart by where they come from, and its answer is one of two: `approve` or `abstain`, never a third. The **facts** are produced by Hosting's adapter, read by the Approver's own grant: the head with its parents, the base branch tip, the revision of main, the author, and `checks`, every run of the named check at the head, newest first (each with its name, the revision it judged, its status and conclusion in GitHub's own words and who signed it); Hosting selects nothing. Two proofs are kept apart and neither stands for the other. **Ancestry** is the parents of the head: exactly the base tip and main, which shows the shape of a SYNC. It does not show that the merge changed nothing else, because a merge commit can carry further changes. **Content purity**, that the merge is clean and carries nothing else, is established only by the check `verify Sync`, signed by the Inspector at the exact head. Hosting gives no verdict on either: the judgement reads the ancestry from the parents, the check from the runs, and a field that offers a pure merge is never read. They are never read from a title, body or branch name. Attribution is never a field in the data, because a field can be copied: Hosting registers each fact it returns and the judgement asks `factOrigin(fact)` first, which answers the registered origin (the repository, the revisions, the read that produced it and the App that read it) or `null` for anything Hosting did not produce. A fact with no origin, a copy, a value rebuilt by hand or after a round trip through JSON is `facts-unattributed`, and a fact that another App than the Approver read is `facts-other-reader`. Facts do not cross a process or workflow boundary: where the judgement runs apart from the read, it reads them again through Hosting with its own grant, and a fact passed across is never accepted. The **evidence** is what the run claims: the head and the base it means to decide on, and its own execution. Nothing that decides is chosen by the caller: a deciding value that the evidence also claims is taken from the facts alone, and none is read from a parameter. It answers `approve` only when the facts are attributed to Hosting and were read by the Approver, the head and the base of the evidence are those of the facts, the ancestry is right (the head has exactly two parents, the base tip and main) and, separately, the content is shown pure (there is at least one run of `verify Sync`, and every run of it is at that same head, completed with the conclusion `success`, and signed by the Inspector; ancestry alone is never approval and a passing check on the wrong ancestry is never approval; an older success never hides a newer failure or a run still pending, and the order of the list decides nothing), the author is not the Approver, and the execution is trusted under review/F41. Every other case answers `abstain` with a finding: `facts-unattributed`, `facts-other-reader`, `head-moved`, `base-moved`, `not-sync-ancestry`, `check-missing`, `check-not-pass` (any run that is a failure, not completed or any other conclusion than `success`), `check-other-revision`, `check-other-signer`, `own-pull-request` and `malformed-evidence`. Missing facts or evidence, a shape that is wrong, and a head without a list of parents all abstain, and a head whose ancestry is not the base and main (one parent, three, a repeat, another commit, a base that lags) abstains as `not-sync-ancestry`; every, so the judgement fails closed. A pull request titled or branched like a SYNC whose head is not a merge of the base and main abstains, and a pure merge with an unlike title approves, so the kind comes from content. The judgement reads data and never runs, fetches or posts anything, and it never throws. The function that asks for the origin is Hosting's own export, built into the judgement; a Case reaches the judgement only through a factory that takes the lookup, so no caller of the delivered judgement can supply one.

**Done when** facts that Hosting produced for the Approver, showing the ancestry of a SYNC and a passing check signed by the Inspector at the same revision, with evidence of trusted execution, are `approve`, and each case listed above is `abstain` with its own finding, a hand-built or copied fact is never believed, no deciding value is taken from the caller, and nothing else is ever answered.
