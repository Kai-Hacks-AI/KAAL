# review/F40 approval-decision

The decision to approve is a pure judgement over evidence given as data, and its answer is one of two: `approve` or `abstain`, never a third. The evidence names the pull request (its head, its base and its author), the content (whether the change is a pure merge of the base, supplied from the bytes by Hosting's adapter, never from the title, body or branch name) and the check (its name, the revision it judged, its conclusion and who signed it). It answers `approve` only when the head is the head being decided, the base is the base being decided, the content is a pure merge, the check is `verify Sync` at that same head, its conclusion is `pass`, it was signed by the Inspector, and the author of the pull request is not the Approver. Every other case answers `abstain` with a finding: `head-moved`, `base-moved`, `not-pure-merge`, `check-missing`, `check-not-pass` (a failed, unknown or any other conclusion), `check-other-revision`, `check-other-signer`, `own-pull-request` and `malformed-evidence`. Missing evidence, evidence of the wrong shape, and a pure-merge fact that is anything but true all abstain, so the judgement fails closed. A pull request titled or branched like a SYNC that the content does not show to be a pure merge abstains, and a pure merge with an unlike title approves, so the kind comes from content. The judgement reads data and never runs, fetches or posts anything, and it never throws.

**Done when** evidence of a pure merge with a passing check signed by the Inspector at the revision being decided is `approve`, and each case listed above is `abstain` with its own finding and nothing else is ever answered.
