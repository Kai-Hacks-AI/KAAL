# review/F40 approval-decision

The decision to approve is a pure judgement over two things kept apart by where they come from, and its answer is one of two: `approve` or `abstain`, never a third. The **facts** are produced by Hosting's adapter, read by the Approver's own grant: the head and the base of the pull request, the revision of main, whether the change is a pure merge of the base, its author, and the check (its name, the revision it judged, its conclusion and who signed it). They are never read from a title, body or branch name. Attribution is never a field in the data, because a field can be copied: Hosting registers each fact it returns and the judgement asks `factOrigin(fact)` first, which answers the registered origin (the repository, the revisions, the read that produced it and the App that read it) or `null` for anything Hosting did not produce. A fact with no origin, a copy, a value rebuilt by hand or after a round trip through JSON is `facts-unattributed`, and a fact that another App than the Approver read is `facts-other-reader`. Facts do not cross a process or workflow boundary: where the judgement runs apart from the read, it reads them again through Hosting with its own grant, and a fact passed across is never accepted. The **evidence** is what the run claims: the head and the base it means to decide on, and its own execution. Nothing that decides is chosen by the caller: a deciding value that the evidence also claims is taken from the facts alone, and none is read from a parameter. It answers `approve` only when the facts are attributed to Hosting and were read by the Approver, the head and the base of the evidence are those of the facts, the facts say the change is a pure merge, the check is `verify Sync` at that same head, its conclusion is `pass`, it was signed by the Inspector, the author is not the Approver, and the execution is trusted under review/F41. Every other case answers `abstain` with a finding: `facts-unattributed`, `facts-other-reader`, `head-moved`, `base-moved`, `not-pure-merge`, `check-missing`, `check-not-pass` (a failed, unknown or any other conclusion), `check-other-revision`, `check-other-signer`, `own-pull-request` and `malformed-evidence`. Missing facts or evidence, a shape that is wrong, and a pure-merge fact that is anything but true all abstain, so the judgement fails closed. A pull request titled or branched like a SYNC that the facts do not show to be a pure merge abstains, and a pure merge with an unlike title approves, so the kind comes from content. The judgement reads data and never runs, fetches or posts anything, and it never throws. The function that asks for the origin is Hosting's own export, built into the judgement; a Case reaches the judgement only through a factory that takes the lookup, so no caller of the delivered judgement can supply one.

**Done when** facts that Hosting produced for the Approver, showing a pure merge and a passing check signed by the Inspector at the same revision, with evidence of trusted execution, are `approve`, and each case listed above is `abstain` with its own finding, a hand-built or copied fact is never believed, no deciding value is taken from the caller, and nothing else is ever answered.
