# review/F40 approval-decision

The decision to approve is a pure judgement over two things kept apart by where they come from, and its answer is one of two: `approve` or `abstain`, never a third. The **facts** come only from Hosting's adapter and carry that attribution (`source: 'hosting'`): the head and the base being decided, the revision of main, and whether the change is a pure merge of the base, all read from the repository and never from the title, body or branch name. The **evidence** is what the run and the pull request claim: the pull request (its head, its base and its author), the check (its name, the revision it judged, its conclusion and who signed it) and the run's own execution. What decides is never chosen by the caller: facts without the attribution of Hosting are `facts-unattributed`, a value of the facts that the evidence also claims is taken from the facts alone, and nothing that decides is read from a parameter of the caller. It answers `approve` only when the head and the base of the evidence are those of the facts, the facts say the change is a pure merge, the check is `verify Sync` at that same head, its conclusion is `pass`, it was signed by the Inspector, the author of the pull request is not the Approver, and the execution is trusted under review/F41. Every other case answers `abstain` with a finding: `facts-unattributed`, `head-moved`, `base-moved`, `not-pure-merge`, `check-missing`, `check-not-pass` (a failed, unknown or any other conclusion), `check-other-revision`, `check-other-signer`, `own-pull-request` and `malformed-evidence`. Missing facts or evidence, a shape that is wrong, and a pure-merge fact that is anything but true all abstain, so the judgement fails closed. A pull request titled or branched like a SYNC that the facts do not show to be a pure merge abstains, and a pure merge with an unlike title approves, so the kind comes from content. The judgement reads data and never runs, fetches or posts anything, and it never throws.

**Done when** facts attributed to Hosting that show a pure merge, with evidence of a passing check signed by the Inspector at the same revision and of trusted execution, are `approve`, and each case listed above is `abstain` with its own finding, no deciding value is taken from the caller, and nothing else is ever answered.
