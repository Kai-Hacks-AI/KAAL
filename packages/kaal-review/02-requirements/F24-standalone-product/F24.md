# review/F24 standalone-product

The delivered `kaal-review` package operates in isolation. Packed and extracted into an empty directory, it works with only its permitted shared product dependencies available, `kaal-hosting` and `kaal-foundation`. Its actual contents, its declared runtime dependencies, every KAAL package its shipped files reference, and its behaviour are checked. No other KAAL package, `kaal-testing` included, is required. Engineering-time use of other packages to develop or test Review is not restricted.
