# review/F24 standalone-product

A consumer who receives the packed `kaal-review` product outside the workspace, with only its permitted shared dependencies available, `kaal-hosting` and `kaal-foundation`, can use what Review declares: its Skill loads as an Agent Skill, its Nodes register into an Engine and are listed there, and everything the product needs at run time resolves without any other KAAL package, `kaal-testing` included. The expectations come from Review's own declaration, so the product may evolve without changing this requirement. The proof is an acceptance scenario that installs the packed product into a fresh host holding only its Engine and its permitted dependencies, and resolves what it needs there. Engineering-time use of other packages to develop or test Review is not restricted.
