# review/F25 judge-a-finding

A review with the focus `finding` judges one finding raised against a piece of work. Its reference is what the work's Change declared it would do; its actual is what the finding says is wrong, read with the work. The record adds two chapters to those of review/A1. Verdict gives the challenge the finding was put to, the evidence read, and one of valid, invalid or unknown, with the mark Proven, Judged or Testable, not tested; where the failing element lies relative to the declared scope; and what the review did not check. Handover lists which of three handovers apply, to fix in this Change, to prove, to record, with the reason for each and what the receiver needs. Handover names no capability, package, branch, thread or person as the receiver: it states what is handed over and under what condition. The review fixes nothing, runs no test, records no defect and decides nothing, and a valid finding is not thereby owned by the work it was found in.
