# review/F25 judge-a-finding

A review with the focus `finding` judges one finding raised against a piece of work. Its reference is what the work declared it would do; its actual is what the finding says is wrong, read with the work. The record adds two chapters to those of review/A1. Verdict gives the challenge the finding was put to, the evidence read, and one of valid, invalid or unknown, with the mark Proven, Judged or Testable, not tested. It also states Found at, where the finding was placed, copied as the finding gives it, and what the review did not check. Handover lists which of three handovers apply, to prove, to record, to place, with the reason for each and what the receiver needs. Handover names no capability, package, branch, thread or person as the receiver: it states what is handed over and under what condition. The review does not decide where a valid finding is to be fixed. It fixes nothing, runs no test, records no defect and decides nothing, and a finding found in a piece of work is not thereby owned by it.
