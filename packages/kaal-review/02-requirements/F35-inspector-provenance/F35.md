# review/F35 inspector-provenance

What judges and signs a change is not changed by that change, and a signed verdict belongs to the revision it judged. The execution and signing surface is a declared list of paths: the workflow of the Inspector, its source, its judgements and its App declarations, and the judge and sign scripts of Controls. A change that touches any path of that surface, reaches it through a dot segment, comes as a list that cannot be read, or meets an empty surface is never judged as a pass by the Inspector; its answer is unknown, with the finding `touches-inspector`, and the Owner decides. Evidence of a judgement is data that names the revision, the base and the run, and says it came from the judge job. Evidence is out when it is of another revision (`stale-revision`), names none (`unbound`), is of another base (`stale-base`), is of another run or did not come from the judge job (`foreign-run`), or cannot be read or holds a verdict that is none of pass, fail or unknown (`malformed-evidence`). Evidence that holds an unknown verdict never signs a pass, and missing evidence is never a pass. Both judgements are pure, read no Git or GitHub, and never run what they read.

This requirement fixes what is judged from files. That the definition of the workflow itself is taken from the trusted base, and not from the change, cannot be shown from the workflow text; it is the trust boundary of the CTO and is open in review/C6.

**Done when** a change off the surface with evidence of its own revision, base and run is in, and each breach named above is never a pass and has its own finding.
