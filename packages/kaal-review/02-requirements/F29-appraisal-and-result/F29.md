# review/F29 appraisal-and-result

An assessment that was performed is recorded as a Review result, one Markdown file named `RV<n>.md`, with front matter and two chapters. Its front matter holds `id` (`<namespace>/RV<n>`), `appraises` (the id of the item), `revision` (the revision of the subject appraised, as an identifier the consumer recognises), `appraiser` (who or what appraised), `relation` (`author`, `not-author` or `unknown`: the relation of the appraiser to the author of the subject at that revision), `execution` (`performed` or `could-not-perform`) and, when performed, `conclusion` (F30). The chapter `Execution` states the evidence seen and what was not done (for example, a suite not re-run). The chapter `Result`, present only for a performed assessment, states the reasoning, the limitations and a disposition addressed to the owner of the subject. Definition, execution and result are three things: an item is not proof that an assessment happened, and an execution that could not be performed has a reason and no result. A result may list `parts`, each with its own conclusion, so that one unassessed responsibility stays visible.

**Done when** a record is read as an execution and, only when performed, a result; a record that claims a conclusion without having been performed, or that is performed without one, is a named finding; and a conclusion other than `unresolved` is refused while any listed part is `unresolved` or `not-satisfied`.
