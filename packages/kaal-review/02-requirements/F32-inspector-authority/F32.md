# review/F32 inspector-authority

The authority of the App named "KAAL Inspector" is one declaration, a file read as data, `13-source/github-apps/inspector.json`. It holds `app` (`inspector`), `name` (`KAAL Inspector`), `owner` (`review`), `purpose` (one sentence), `abilities` and, optionally, `logo`. The abilities are neutral words and never GitHub permissions: Hosting owns the closed list of them and maps each to what GitHub grants, renders the manifest and mints the token. The Inspector's abilities are exactly `post-checks`, `read-contents` and `read-pull-requests`. A declaration whose abilities hold anything else, lack one of them, repeat one, or whose `app`, `owner` or `purpose` is absent or not as stated is a finding, and is not read as the authority of the Inspector. Review's policy for what an Inspector may hold is its own; whether an ability is real, and what it becomes on GitHub, is Hosting's.

**Done when** the declaration of the Inspector is read and its app, name, owner, purpose, abilities and logo are returned, and a declaration that holds an extra ability, lacks one, repeats one or names another App is a named finding.
