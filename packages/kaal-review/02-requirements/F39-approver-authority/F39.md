# review/F39 approver-authority

The authority of the Approver is one declaration, `13-source/github-apps/approver.json`, in the form of review/F32: `app`, `name`, `owner`, `purpose`, `abilities`, and optionally `logo`, read as data. The abilities are exactly `approve-pull-requests`, `read-checks`, `read-contents` and `read-pull-requests`, neutral words of Hosting's closed list, and the owner is `review`. It holds neither `post-checks` nor `raise-pull-requests`, because the Approver neither signs a check nor raises a pull request. The reader of review/F32 takes the App as a second argument, with the Inspector as its default, so the Inspector's reading does not change: a declaration of another App, an extra, a missing or a repeated ability, another owner, no purpose, abilities that are not a list and unreadable text are each a finding, and a reading of the Approver's declaration against the Inspector's authority, or the reverse, is a finding. Together with the Inspector's declaration the set is in under review/F33, which holds `approve-pull-requests` for the Approver alone.

**Done when** the Approver's declaration is read as data with exactly its four abilities, each breach listed above is out with its own finding, the Inspector's Cases still hold unchanged, and the two declarations together are in under F33.
