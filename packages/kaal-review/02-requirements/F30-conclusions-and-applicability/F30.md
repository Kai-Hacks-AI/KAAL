# review/F30 conclusions-and-applicability

A conclusion is one of `satisfied`, `not-satisfied` and `unresolved`. These stay apart from three states that are not conclusions: no record at all (the assessment never ran), `could-not-perform` (it was tried and failed, with a reason, for instance a tool that failed), and a missing piece of evidence, which is `unresolved` with the missing evidence named. A result applies to the revision it names and to no other. For a given revision a result is `applies` when its revision is that one, `carried` when a later record `carries` it to that revision and says why the subject and the evidence remain applicable, and otherwise `stale`. A carry-forward is a record with `carries` (the id of the result carried) and `revision`, a `Result` chapter that states the reason, and the conclusion of the result it carries. A carry-forward with no stated reason, with no such result, or with another conclusion, is a named finding. Nothing carries silently, and an unrelated edit asks for no review.

**Done when** over a tree of results the three conclusions and the three non-conclusions are told apart, results are classified `applies`, `carried` or `stale` for a stated revision, and a carry-forward without its reason is a finding.
