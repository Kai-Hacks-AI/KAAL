# review/F28 validation-pointer

The `validates` of an item is the counterpart of the `tests` of a Case, and is read the same way: as text, from wherever the items sit, with nothing assumed about the folder, the Change or the station. Three reverse questions are answered from the tree alone: which items validate this subject; which items have no result, or none that applies to the revision under consideration; and which results apply to that revision. A subject with no item is answered with an empty list and no judgement of its own. The answers are facts. A pointer is not coverage: that an item points at a subject says nothing about whether the subject is sufficiently validated, and the answer carries no `covered`, `satisfied` or similar verdict. Which assessments are required is the subject owner's policy, and is not read here.

**Done when** the three questions are answered over a tree whose items sit in several places and point at subjects of different kinds, an unknown subject gives an empty answer, and no answer states coverage.
