# review/F34 inspector-separation

The workflow of the Inspector has two jobs, and the separation between them is judged as text, in or out. The judge job holds only `contents: read`, names no secret, keeps no credentials, and checks out only the base commit. The sign job needs the judge job, checks out only the base commit, takes the verdicts as data, and alone names the secret of the Inspector's key. The workflow starts from no permissions, never uses `pull_request_target`, and its sign job reads nothing of the change. A workflow that lets the judge hold a secret, lets the signer check out or read the change, lets the key be named outside the sign job, keeps credentials in the judge job, starts from write permissions, uses `pull_request_target` or lacks either job is out, each with its own finding. The judgement reads the workflow and never runs it.

**Done when** the workflow that keeps the separation is in, and each of the listed breaches is out with its own finding.
