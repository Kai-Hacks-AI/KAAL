# review/F31 no-authority-independence-recorded

A result informs acceptance and authorizes nothing: a `satisfied` is not product approval, permission to merge or permission to activate a control, and a record that states such a decision is a finding. The relation of the appraiser to the author is recorded on every performed result (F29), and Review does not decide when an independent appraisal is required: that is the policy of whoever owns the subject, which may read the recorded relation. Review does not state that an `author` or `unknown` relation is invalid.

**Done when** a result that declares an approval or a merge is a finding, a result with no `relation` is a finding, and no result is refused for its relation alone.
