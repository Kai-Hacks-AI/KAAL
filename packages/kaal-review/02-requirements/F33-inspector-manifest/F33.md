# review/F33 no-app-decides-on-what-it-raised

Across the declarations of the Apps, the ability `approve-pull-requests` is held by the Approver and by no other App, and no App holds both `raise-pull-requests` and `approve-pull-requests`. The judgement reads a set of declarations as data and answers in or out: an App other than the Approver that holds `approve-pull-requests`, an App that holds both abilities, and a declaration with no `abilities` list are each a finding. An absent Approver is not a finding: the Approver's declaration is the work of its own PLAN. The judgement never creates, changes or installs an App, and does not know what GitHub makes of an ability.

**Done when** a set of declarations that keeps the rule is in, and each of an Inspector that approves, a Dispatcher that approves, an App that raises and approves, and a declaration without abilities is out with its own finding.
