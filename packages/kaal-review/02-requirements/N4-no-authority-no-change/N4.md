# review/N4 no-authority-no-change

The review approves, merges and decides nothing, and it changes nothing of the subject or in any capability other than Review, and it names no subject outside the data of an application. No requirement, Case or script of Review depends on the subject. It does not weaken the Skill's Cases or the key to make the work pass, and it does not ask for either to be tuned to its own trial. It reports on the work, not on the Agent that did it.
