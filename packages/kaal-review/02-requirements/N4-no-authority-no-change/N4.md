# review/N4 no-authority-no-change

The review approves, merges and decides nothing, and it changes nothing of the reviewed work or in any capability other than Review, and it names no subject outside the data of an application. No requirement, Case or script of Review depends on the subject. It runs no test, and it neither refers to nor depends on nor calls a Skill or package of any other capability: it points at what could be tested and leaves the testing to its owner. It does not weaken a Case or a key of the reviewed work to make the work pass, and it does not ask for either to be tuned to its own trial. It reports on the work, not on the Agent that did it.
