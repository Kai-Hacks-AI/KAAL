# controls/A200 accepted-failure-in-script-and-skill

`scripts/accept.mjs` is a pure function, `judgeAcceptance`, over a list of control results and a list of acceptances. It owns the join: which failing Test Cases an acceptance covers, whether the report may flag ACCEPTED, and whether delivery may be permitted. The adopter owns where the lists are kept, who may add an acceptance, and how a verdict reaches CI. It reads no file and knows no repository, path, flag or role. `references/acceptance.md` carries the teaching and `SKILL.md` one pointer line.
