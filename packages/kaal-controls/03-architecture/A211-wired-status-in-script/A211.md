# controls/A211 wired-status-in-script

`judgeWired` sits in its own script, `scripts/judge-wired.mjs`, beside the other evidence judges, and takes inventories in the shape of `findUnrecorded` (controls/F207) so that one discovery can serve both. It owns the three statuses and the refusal of evidence that contradicts itself, and nothing else: discovery of the delivery, the proof of running and the place the report appears are the adopter's.
