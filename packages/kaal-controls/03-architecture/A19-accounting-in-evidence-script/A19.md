# controls/A19 accounting-in-evidence-script

`scripts/evidence.mjs` keeps `judgeEvidence` and adds three things to it: records with no `by` go to `unrecorded` and count nowhere; a RED or UNKNOWN record without `handled` makes the reason `unhandled` and is listed in `unhandled`; a record with `handled` goes to `handledRuns`. `stateLine` shows the number of unhandled runs. `references/judging-status.md` says what `by` and `handled` hold and the rule on who records a run. The Skill's own files name no other package (controls/T777). The script knows no repository path and the converter that writes records is Engineering's.
