# controls/A10 status-in-finder-and-skill

The finder `skills/kaal-controls/scripts/find-controls.mjs` takes `status` as a sixth field of a control and lists every control, valid or not. It returns each control with its declared `status`, its `rank`, `valid` and `problems` (field, reason, recovery), checks `status` against the four values, and keeps `refused` as the view of the controls that fail validation. The Skill reference `references/declaring-controls.md` shows the `status` line and names the four values in order and shows the check command. `SKILL.md` stays under its word cap and only points at the reference.

The check `skills/kaal-controls/scripts/check-controls.mjs` imports the finder, collects the problems of the controls, returns the whole inventory with them, exports `DIRT` and exits 1 on any problem. It prints every control it found. It writes nothing. The rule lives in the package alone: Engineering deploys the check (controls/A11) and does not copy it.

The Skill reference `references/judging-status.md` gives the judgment help of controls/F24 for what the check cannot decide. `SKILL.md` points at it and stays within its cap. Scripts live in the Skill's `scripts/` folder and judgment help in its `references/` folder.
