# controls/A18 neutral-finder-and-discovery

`scripts/find-controls.mjs` keeps its single job of finding and gives it neutral inputs: the Intent in either of two forms with `intentForm` on each control, any station name checked by shape, and an optional `links` argument for the Cases of a control that replaces the reading of first lines when handed in. It names no station and resolves no Intent. `registry.mjs` takes any station name. The control's own objects (the id shape, the four types, DIRT, the evidence kinds) stay as they are. Two new references sit in the Skill: `discovering-controls.md`, the four questions, and `exchanges.md`, the seeks with their fallbacks; `SKILL.md` points at both within its 600 words, and the Skill's files name no other package (controls/T777). The offers of other capabilities (release's stations, Testing's links, the holder of the Intents) are theirs, not part of this package. The exchange is Core's kind (core/C51, approved, DO not yet merged); this Change uses only its design rule and types no Node by it.
