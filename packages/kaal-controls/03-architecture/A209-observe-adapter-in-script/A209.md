# controls/A209 observe-adapter-in-script

`scripts/observe-adapter.mjs` is one pure function, `observeAdapted`, over a value the adopter passes in. It owns what counts as running through the adapter: the import of `runControl` from the adapter module and its call. The adopter owns the delivered entry and its revision, the station and the flag, and feeds the observations to `findUnadapted` (controls/A203) through the adapter of controls/C141.
