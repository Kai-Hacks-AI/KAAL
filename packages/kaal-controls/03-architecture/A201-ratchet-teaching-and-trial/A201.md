# controls/A201 ratchet-teaching-and-trial

The teaching lives in the Skill: the ratchet text and the declaring text, placed and sized as Core's standard says. The trial lives beside the Change, in `00-changes/C140-skill-ratchet/trial/`, because it is an instrument of this Change and not Skill content, and the Skill stays free of any word about KAAL or about how it is judged. Controls owns the trial; research owns the technique of research the trial draws on; Core owns the standard the Skill follows.
