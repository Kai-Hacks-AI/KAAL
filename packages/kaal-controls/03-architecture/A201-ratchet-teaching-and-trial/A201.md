# controls/A201 ratchet-teaching-and-trial

The teaching lives in the Skill: the ratchet text and the declaring text, placed and sized as Core's standard says. The experiment content, the prompts, the rubric and the pass bar, lives beside the Change, in `00-changes/C140-skill-ratchet/trial/trial.md`, because it is an instrument of this Change and not Skill content, and the Skill stays free of any word about KAAL or about how it is judged. The record of a run is not ours: it is a Research rehearsal record in `02-learnings/rehearsals/`, in the form and with the technique the kaal-research Skill defines, and only its `scores.json` evidence is controls' rubric content. Controls owns the trial; Research owns the technique and the record contract; Core owns the standard the Skill follows.
