# controls/A210 ratchet-step-in-script

`judgeRatchetStep` sits in its own script, `scripts/judge-ratchet.mjs`, and imports the three judges from `scripts/ratchet.mjs`. It owns the parsing of the two file texts and the choice of the step, and nothing else: the judgement of a step stays in the library, so the library does not learn to read text and the choice does not move into a wiring. The adopter owns how the files are read at the base and the head, which Changes the step carries, the window and where the files live.
