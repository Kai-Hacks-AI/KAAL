# controls/A207 demand-evidence-in-script

`scripts/demand-evidence.mjs` is one pure check, `findEvidenceDue`, over values the adopter passes in. It owns the finding and its name. The adopter owns what it is given and where it runs. It runs through the adapter of controls/C141, which owns the mode, the ratchet filter, failing closed, the log and the record.
