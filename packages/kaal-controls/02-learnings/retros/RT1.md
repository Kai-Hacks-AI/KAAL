Account of: I am the controls thread. I wrote the PLAN, the DO and the clean agent trials of C21 (control modes), and then this retro of my own collector. This is my account only: the CPO and Testing corrected the Change several times, and I have not asked either what they lacked.

## Liked
- subject: the CHECK defined before it ran
  keeps: Test Cases, judgments J1 to J5 and four planned weakenings were written first and each pointed at the element it examined, so the independent appraisal had something exact to read and found two real wording gaps (a filter alone would pass a grown baseline; an unrecognised mode did not block) that no Case could see.

## Learned
- subject: my trial claims were author-reported and not retained
  source: the CRO assessment of #2319 and Testing's review of #2347: the raw answers sat in `/tmp`, so the record cannot be inspected. C22 rests no claim on it. J3 is narrowed to two statements: design placement satisfied by inspection; agent application partly evidenced, persistence of the next baseline unresolved, no benefit of the Skill shown.
- subject: an approved PLAN is not the product model
  source: the CPO approved the PLAN with `advisory` and a shipped `AGENTS.md`, then corrected both against the established taxonomy and the two-file layout; the target documents and Cases changed on purpose and are recorded in `change.md`.
- subject: a headless trial agent carries the session's team memory unless it is switched off
  source: my first trial's baseline arm already knew the ratchet idea; a direct question to an agent showed the memory, and the rerun with memory disabled gave the three-arm result on #2319.
- subject: the Skill changed what agents said about type, growth, unknown and exit, and nothing about who owns what
  source: the targeted check of the responsibility split on #2319, author-reported: agents with and without the Skill placed it correctly, so it shows the split is understood, not that the Skill teaches it.
- subject: a mutation can overstate what it tests
  source: Testing found M7 only changed the reason of the UNKNOWN answer while the outer handler stayed; M8 now removes it. A mutation is named for the line it changes and checked against the assertion that turns RED.
