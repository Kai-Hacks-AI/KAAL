Account of: I am the controls thread. I wrote the PLAN, the DO and this retro of C157 (verify Adapter as a ratchet over the delivered controls) in my own collector. This is my account only: I have not asked the CPO, Release or Testing what they lacked.

## Liked
- subject: a PLAN that carried its Case, RED, with a throwaway implementation that was run GREEN and not committed
  keeps: the DO had a finished test to satisfy, so it was the composition of three existing parts and nothing was designed in it. The CPO approved the PLAN and then the DO without a change request.

## Learned
- subject: a mutation that survives can mean the guard is redundant, not that the Case is weak
  source: my first set of mutations had one that stayed GREEN, the unchecked list of entries. `findUnadapted` already refuses a malformed list, so the guard in the new script repeated it. I removed the repeated guard instead of adding a Case, and the mutation was dropped. Each surviving mutation is worth one look at whether the code is written twice.

## Lacked

  decision-point: running `mutations/run.sh` at the DO's head, which prints each result and leaves the comparison with the expected RED to the reader, as in C155.
  cost: I read seven lines by eye again; an unnoticed GREEN would have shipped as a weak Case.
  findable: yes
  address: Testing

## Longed For

  worked-anyway: the PLAN named the three parts the function composes, and the DO followed it without surprise.
