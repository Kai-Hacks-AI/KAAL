Account of: I am the controls thread. I wrote the PLAN and the DO of C145 (demand Record) and this retro of my own collector. This is my account only: I have not asked the CPO or Testing what they lacked.

## Liked
- subject: timing kept apart from correctness
  keeps: `findRecordDue` makes no status comparison; it takes `assessRecord`'s names and answers only when a correction is due, per control and per fact, so a lag that is not yet due is never confused with a lag that is false.

## Learned
- subject: ownership must be established before an unrelated control is excluded
  source: the CPO found that my first `unknownOwned` ignored unknown evidence whenever `owners[control]` was missing, so an unowned control's unknown passed silently. Each unknown name is now validated and its control's ownership is required first; T1520 carries the negative assertion and the CPO accepted the extension.
- subject: a Case may be extended with an assertion without changing the Requirement
  source: F209 item 5 already said a control with no owner makes the check throw; the extension only made the Case verify what the Requirement states.
