# controls/C27 a finding gets one recorded response, Contain, Accept, Ratchet or Eliminate

C26 ends where a decision starts: a qualified scan lists findings and says `needs: review`. C27 states the decision model that follows, **CARE**, owned by Controls, and gives it a check that a decision record is complete. The model is generic: it says nothing of a repository, a station or a person's role beyond who proposes and who selects.

**The flow.** Detection lists the findings (C26). Review records a **disposition** for every finding: **valid**, **invalid** (with the evidence that it is not a finding), or **unknown** (with what must be investigated, and judged again). Only a valid finding takes a **response**. For a valid finding Review proposes a response and the authorized decision-maker selects it. A disposition is not a response: an invalid or unknown finding is never assigned Contain, Accept, Ratchet or Eliminate, and is never implicitly accepted. An unknown finding stays open until it is judged again.

**The dispositions, and what each must carry.**

| Disposition | Meaning | The record must say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| valid | the finding is real | who reviewed it; it then needs exactly one response |
| invalid | the finding is not a finding (the check or the observation is wrong) | who reviewed it, the evidence |
| unknown | not yet judged | who reviewed it, what must be investigated |

**The four responses, and what each must carry** (valid findings only).

| Response | Meaning | The record must say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contain | the exposure is limited while the finding stays | an owner, what limits the exposure |
| Accept | the finding stays, knowingly | an owner, why it is acceptable |
| Ratchet | a preventive strategy that supports progressive corrective work (C23, C24, C25); it is not acceptance | an owner, the corrective work it supports |
| Eliminate | the finding is corrected | the corrective work |

Every response also names who proposed, who selected, and the finding's id. A finding without a disposition is unreviewed, a valid finding without a response is undecided, and neither is ever accepted. A response chosen by the scan (a baseline written from the scan) is not a decision.

**The check.** `scripts/care.mjs`, pure: `judgeDecision({findings, dispositions, decisions})` says PASS when every finding of the qualified scan has exactly one well-formed disposition, every valid finding has exactly one well-formed decision, and no decision names a finding that is not valid; FAIL with reasons `undisposed`, `duplicate-disposition`, `bad-disposition`, `uncovered` (a valid finding without a decision), `duplicate-decision`, `bad-response`, `decision-not-valid` (a decision for an invalid or unknown finding), `unknown-finding` or `missing-field` (naming the finding and the field); UNKNOWN when an input is missing or not the right shape. The answer also lists `unresolved`, the findings still unknown, so a PASS cannot be read as settled. PASS says the record is complete. It does not say a judgement or a response is right, never selects one, and never activates a preventive control.

**The Skill.** `references/care.md` teaches the dispositions and the model in setup-agnostic words (no repository, path, capability or role name), and `SKILL.md` gains one pointer line within its 600 words.

**The mnemonic.** controls/I16 carries the Owner's wording, CONTROL DIRT WITH CARE, beside RATCHETS in controls/I15. It is a memory aid and is not tested. The Skill carries CARE only.

## Not here

Where the decisions are stored, who is authorized to select, planning's dates for the corrective work, and what a PASS lets an adopter do are the adopter's (release's) and the Owner's. See the choices left open in `change.md`.
