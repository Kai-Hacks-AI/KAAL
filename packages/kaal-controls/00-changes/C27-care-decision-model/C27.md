# controls/C27 a finding gets one recorded response, Contain, Accept, Ratchet or Eliminate

C26 ends where a decision starts: a qualified scan lists findings and says `needs: review`. C27 states the decision model that follows, **CARE**, owned by Controls, and gives it a check that a decision record is complete. The model is generic: it says nothing of a repository, a station or a person's role beyond who proposes and who selects.

**The flow.** Detection lists the findings (C26). Review judges each one valid, invalid or unknown. For a valid finding Review proposes a response. The authorized decision-maker selects the response. The record of that is the CARE decision of the finding.

**The four responses, and what each must carry.**

| Response | Meaning | The record must say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contain | the exposure is limited while the finding stays | an owner, what limits the exposure |
| Accept | the finding stays, knowingly | an owner, why it is acceptable |
| Ratchet | a preventive strategy that supports progressive corrective work (C23, C24, C25); it is not acceptance | an owner, the corrective work it supports |
| Eliminate | the finding is corrected | the corrective work |

Every decision also names who proposed, who selected, and the finding's id. A finding without a decision is undecided, never accepted. A response chosen by the scan (a baseline written from the scan) is not a decision.

**The check.** `scripts/care.mjs`, pure: `judgeDecision({findings, decisions})` says PASS when every finding of the qualified scan has exactly one well-formed decision and no decision names a finding that is not in the scan; FAIL with reasons `uncovered`, `unknown-finding`, `duplicate-decision`, `bad-response` or `missing-field` (naming the finding and the field); UNKNOWN when an input is missing or not the right shape. PASS says the record is complete. It does not say the response is right, never selects one, and never activates a preventive control.

**The Skill.** `references/care.md` teaches the model in setup-agnostic words (no repository, path, capability or role name), and `SKILL.md` gains one pointer line within its 600 words.

**The mnemonic.** controls/I16 carries the Owner's wording, CONTROL DIRT WITH CARE, beside RATCHETS in controls/I15. It is a memory aid and is not tested. The Skill carries CARE only.

## Not here

Where the decisions are stored, who is authorized to select, planning's dates for the corrective work, and what a PASS lets an adopter do are the adopter's (release's) and the Owner's. See the choices left open in `change.md`.
