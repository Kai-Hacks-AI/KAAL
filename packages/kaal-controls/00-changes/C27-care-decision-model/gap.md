# C27 gap

| Gap | Where | Is | Should be |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| controls/G104 | what happens to a finding after qualification (controls/C26) | the qualified scan lists findings that need Review, and nothing says that each finding gets a recorded Review disposition, what the responses are, what each must carry, or that a valid finding is decided once and by someone named | the CARE model: a Review disposition for every finding (valid, invalid with evidence, unknown to investigate), and for each valid finding exactly one of four responses with the fields that make it a decision and not a silence, a proposal and a selection both recorded; invalid and unknown findings never take a response |
