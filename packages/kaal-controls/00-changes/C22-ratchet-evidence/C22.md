# controls/C22 ratchet evidence

A follow-up to C21 on the same collector, from the CRO assessment of the merged DO. C21 gave the ratchet and the way it is verified. C22 makes the evidence behind that claim inspectable and bounded, and closes one contract mismatch, so that C21 can be the reusable model for other ratchets.

1. **Durable evidence.** The prompts, the fixtures, the rubrics, the anonymised answers with their keys and the scoring records of the clean trial and the targeted J3 check are kept in this Change (`trial-clean/`, `trial-j3/`). What could not be kept (the raw transcripts, the scorer's per-cell quotes, whether a run opened the Skill) is named there, and the results are author-reported observations that can be checked against the retained answers, not independently verified experiments.
2. **J3 in two statements.** Design placement of the responsibility split is satisfied by inspection of the documents. Agent application of it is partly evidenced: agents detected the candidate-controlled baseline trap and placed comparison, reference acquisition, enforcement and finding names correctly with and without the Skill; mode-switch ownership was named in two of three runs per arm; persisting the next baseline was not demonstrated; no benefit of the Skill was seen. The DO adds one small end-to-end exercise (accept an improvement, store the advanced baseline, judge the next candidate) and records it. Testing restates J3 in these two statements at the DO's head.
3. **Exact mutations, run on the final version.** The four weakenings are kept as patches (`mutations/M1` to `M4`) with a runner (`mutations/run.sh`) that loads each alone into a scratch copy and runs the Cases. Run at `5ce2331b`, the head of C21: the baseline is GREEN and each mutation is RED on a named Case (`mutations/run-at-5ce2331b.txt`). The DO runs it again on its own head. It is a script and four files, not a standing framework.
4. **CLI uncertainty.** A missing file or malformed JSON given to the command gives a structured UNKNOWN result and exit 2, as the contract says, not a stack trace and exit 1. New Requirement F35 and Case T1115, RED on the collector, GREEN after the DO.

The PLAN adds no script, Skill word or workflow: documents, the evidence bundle, F35 and T1115. Rollout of any ratchet and the authorization to switch mode remain consumer work.
