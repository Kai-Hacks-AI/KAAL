# controls/C7 minimum Controls

Introduces the smallest version of `kaal-controls` on `main`: the contract of a control, an Agent Skill that guides an agent through the design of one, and a worked example. It reuses the semantics of the unpromoted collector `kaal/controls` and carries none of its layout.
