# C10 act

A 4L retrospective of the Change, from the viewpoint of the agent that carried it. It closes C10 in the collector. What `main` teaches can only be recorded after the promotion, and is not claimed here.

## Liked

The concept held when it met a real check. `verify Stages` fitted the form without bending it: an id, a title, one sentence, a type and one Intent. The control test pointed back and the finder listed it with its test, found from the control. Declaring the control in the PLAN as a draft that the Case reads kept both Cases RED and still showed that they could pass.

## Learned

An existing check has no Intent of its own. The strategy and the earlier Changes are prose and Changes, so the Intent had to be written first, and that was one allocation to ask for, from the owner of the capability. A control that sits in one capability's folder and is found by a finder in another needs only the frontmatter, so the folder was a free choice, and it is still only a proposal. A control blocks nothing by itself: saying so in the item kept the type honest, since the enforcing is another control.

## Lacked

A way to say that a control has a Requirement or Architecture behind its Intent, and to see that from the control. The Intent is answered by one Case, which is thin. A decided numbered folder for control items. A word for whether a control is wired to a ruleset, which is another control and not part of this one.

## Longed For

The second control, `analyze Vulnerabilities`, as the check that the form also fits a control that is not a stage check. The rename of the package to its scope, as its own small Change.

## Closed

The Gap of this Change, G6, is closed by Cases T540 and T541 and the control `release/CTL1`, green in the collector.
