# controls/C143 verify Delivery finds that something is delivered and no control declares it

One small control with one property, built on the library of controls/C141 (the adapter and `deriveStatus`). Asked for by Kai: small, meaningful controls, each following the change protocol until it blocks.

**The control.** `controls/CTL2`, `verify Delivery`, type detective, status designed. For every entry of `delivered` that is neither the id nor the path of a control in `controls`, the finding is `unrecorded:<entry>`. What is delivered is the adopter's declared set; the check reads no file.

**The check.** `scripts/verify-delivery.mjs` exports `findUnrecorded({controls, delivered})`, pure. It returns a sorted list of unique names, empty when nothing is found, and throws on an input that is not a list, so the adapter fails closed with `UNKNOWN`. It reads no file and knows no repository.

**The rollout, by the change protocol.**
1. **Detective.** The check reports through the adapter and blocks nothing.
2. **Advisory.** The adopter runs it as an advisory check behind its own flag, and evidence is collected: Cases on fixtures, real runs on pull requests, and a provoked failure.
3. **Preventive.** Only on the Owner's explicit go, and only with the provoked-fail evidence the protocol asks for. Since controls that exist today would fail, it runs as a ratchet (C24, C25): their names form the baseline, shrink with a history as release migrates them, and the ratchet ends at zero.

## Not here

Wiring the control as a station or a required check, the flag, the ruleset, and migrating the controls it finds are release's. Nothing is implemented before the Owner's go on this PLAN.

## Revised after the CPO clarification

What is delivered is established from the actual delivery at the applicable revision (`{revision, entries}`), independently of any declaration; a list the adopter merely supplies is not proof, and evidence that is missing or of another revision makes the check throw, so the verdict is UNKNOWN and never clean. This follows the four independent facts of controls/C141 (defined, implemented, running, tested): each has its own proof for the specific control and revision, none implies another, and missing evidence is UNKNOWN (the check throws, so the adapter fails closed), never a clean verdict and never proof of absence.
