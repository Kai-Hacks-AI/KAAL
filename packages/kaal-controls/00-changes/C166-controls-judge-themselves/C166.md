# controls/C166 the package judges its own controls

Product priority: one way of doing controls, independent of the adopting solution, and a package that practises it. The five controls of the package (CTL1 to CTL5) are recorded as `designed` although their checks exist and are tested, and nothing in the package applies its own check of records (CTL3) to them. The Change adds one pure library function, `judgeSelf`, that applies CTL3 to the package's own controls from its own files, and raises each record to what its proof shows. It wires, enforces and installs nothing.
