# controls/C18 criterion judgement

The package checks as far as a script can whether a control's Test Cases show what its type promises, and has the agent judge the rest. A provoked failure must not have withheld a Case or a fixture and, as far as a record says, must show what the type needs: a refusal at a declared preventive station, a report, a restoration. Every control has a verify part (a script's result: does it do what it says) and a validate part (an agent's judgement: is that the right thing for its Intent). The agent reads the control's criterion beside its Cases and says whether they cover it, reads it beside the Intent and says whether it serves it, and records both as judgements beside the results. The judgement is shown, marked stale when the control or its Cases changed, and never changes a verdict. It builds on controls/C15 and the state line of controls/C16, and adds no enforcement.
