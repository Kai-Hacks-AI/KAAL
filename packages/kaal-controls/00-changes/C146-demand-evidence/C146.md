# controls/C146 demand Evidence finds that a proposed activation or enforcement transition of a control carries the evidence it needs

One small control with one property, built on the library of controls/C141 (the adapter and `deriveStatus`). Asked for by Kai: small, meaningful controls, each following the change protocol until it blocks.

**The control.** `controls/CTL5`, `demand Evidence`, type detective, status designed. For every transition discovered completely at the applicable revision (`activation` or `enforcement`) the finding is `evidence-due:<kind>:<fact>:<id>` for each needed fact that is false, and `evidence-due:<kind>:authorized:<id>` without the Owner's separate authorization bound to the exact control, kind and revision. An activation needs defined, implemented and pre-installation testing; an enforcement needs defined, implemented, independently proven installation, running and operational testing. Missing, stale or incomplete evidence, or a discovery that may be partial, never permits: the check throws, so the adapter answers `UNKNOWN`, never filtered by an allowance. KAAL Control types (Directive, Preventive, Detective, Corrective) have no hierarchy and no mode ladder or advisory type is introduced; whether a finding is advisory or prevents delivery is GitHub configuration. The precise evidence is the existing protocol's; evidence acquisition is the adopter's.

**The check.** `scripts/demand-evidence.mjs` exports `findEvidenceDue({base, head, records})`, pure. It returns a sorted list of unique names, empty when nothing is found, and throws on an input that is not a list, so the adapter fails closed with `UNKNOWN`. It reads no file and knows no repository.

**The rollout, by the change protocol.**
1. **Detective.** The check reports through the adapter and blocks nothing.
2. **Non-blocking.** GitHub configuration, not a KAAL type, makes the finding non-blocking: the adopter runs it behind its own flag, and evidence is collected: Cases on fixtures, real runs on pull requests, and a provoked failure.
3. **Preventive.** Only on the Owner's explicit go, and only with the provoked-fail evidence the protocol asks for.

## Not here

Wiring the control as a station or a required check, the flag, the ruleset, and migrating the controls it finds are release's. Nothing is implemented before the Owner's go on this PLAN.

## Revised after the CPO clarifications

No KAAL mode ladder and no advisory type. Enforcement requires independently proven installation, pre-installation and operational testing are distinct, and the Owner's authorization is bound to the exact transition and revision. Transition discovery must be complete; missing or stale evidence never permits. Nothing here is ratchet-eligible: authorization and readiness are mandatory for every new transition, so no baseline stands in for them, and the check refuses the ratchet mode, which the adapter answers as UNKNOWN. Only the facts a transition's kind needs are required, so an activation never asks for a post-installation fact. The judge stays pure and acquisition is the adopter's. This follows the four independent facts of controls/C141.
