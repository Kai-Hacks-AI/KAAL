# controls/C179 the verdict carries its cause

This morning a required check ended UNKNOWN with "rules cannot be read" while the real cause, a workflow-token rate limit, was swallowed on the way. `runControl` does the same by construction: a check that throws is caught, its error is dropped, and the verdict says only `could-not-judge`. The Change makes every UNKNOWN that comes from a thrown check carry the underlying cause (what was asked, the kind of failure, the place) and one guiding line chosen by kind. Hosting supplies the typed platform failure; Controls carries it into the verdict unchanged and interprets no platform text. It wires, enforces and installs nothing.
