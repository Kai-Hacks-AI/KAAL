# controls/C144 verify Record finds that the record of a control agrees with its evidence

One small control with one property, built on the library of controls/C141 (the adapter and `compareClaims`). Asked for by Kai: small, meaningful controls, each following the change protocol until it blocks.

**The control.** `controls/CTL3`, `verify Record`, type detective, status designed. For every control (`{id, claimed}`) it compares each declared claim with its own fact, one by one, both directions, with no exemption for `implemented`. A confirmed finding is `<fact>:lags:<id>` or `<fact>:overclaims:<id>`, so the ratchet identity is stable per control and fact. A fact that is unknown, unverified or stale is not a finding and not clean: it is reported distinctly, and the check throws `UnknownEvidence`, carrying the confirmed discrepancies and the unknown list, so that the adapter answers `UNKNOWN`, which never passes and is never filtered by an allowance. Only confirmed findings can be baselined. It judges correctness only, is the backstop on a promotion into main, and stays detective and non-blocking; when a correction is due is controls/C145's, and activation is a separate Owner decision.

**The check.** `scripts/verify-record.mjs` exports `assessRecord` and `findDisagreeing({controls, facts, revision})`, pure. It reads no file and knows no repository.

## Revised after the CPO clarifications

Each claim is compared with its own fact. Unknown is kept distinct through the C141 adapter and never clean or baselined, and confirmed discrepancies are kept beside it. This follows the four independent facts of controls/C141.
