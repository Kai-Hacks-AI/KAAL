# C21 judgments

Definitions only. A definition says what must be assessed, by which criteria and from which evidence, and which elements it examines. It is not its execution (the appraisal, in CHECK) and it is not its result (the judgment record: subject, revision, evidence seen, limitations, and a disposition of satisfied, not satisfied or unresolved). A required judgment without a disposition blocks the CHECK; a general approval of a pull request is not one. The appraiser is not the author of the DO. Names are the CTO's: Test Case for a scripted assertion, judgment for an assessment by reading, trial for an agent run.

The separation judged here: Intent says why (`I15`), Requirements say what must hold (`F34`), Architecture says who does what (`A20`, `interface.md`), Defects are named separately and not hidden in a Case, Code is the script and the Skill's files.

What an adopter inherits when J1 to J5 are satisfied: the comparison and the transition rule, and the confidence the Cases and the sensitivity record give them. What it supplies: its own check, stable finding names, evidence that its reference is the accepted one, that its verdict stops the action, that nothing else writes the baseline, and authorization to change mode.
