# controls/C21 control modes

kaal-controls learns the general concept of how a check is put to use. One check gives one answer, in or out of its specification, and flags both what is missing and what is too much. The same answer is used in one of three modes: detective (it reports), preventive (it blocks) and ratchet (a preventive that blocks only a finding whose name is not in a recorded baseline, which only shrinks). A ratchet whose baseline reaches nothing is cleaned up into plain preventive and its baseline is removed, and a ratchet with no such exit is not accepted. The type of a control is design and does not change with its mode; a claim is as strong as the mode and no stronger.

Testing and judgment are defined before anything runs. Test Cases say what is verified and the result expected, and judgment definitions say what must be assessed, by which criteria and from which evidence; each points at the elements it examines. They are executed in CHECK, where the executions produce observations and documented judgments, and a definition is neither its execution nor its result (`verification.md`, `judgments.md`). CHECK also shows that the Cases are sensitive: selected weakenings of the script are loaded one at a time and the Case that must notice each is named, with the assertion that fails.

It is worked in the order of BASS, one step at a time, and each element states what verifies it. Bare is the concept as agreed in conversation. Agent is not part of this Change (no instructions file is shipped). Skill is one reference on modes and a pointer. Script is a pure function that applies a mode to an answer, and the finder that accepts the three modes.

One approach to ratchets is established here, once, with the testing and judgment expectations every adopting ratchet benefits from. The contract of a ratchet is complete: a baseline only shrinks across accepted runs, an allowance for a finding that is resolved is removed with the improvement and cannot excuse its later return, and a run that cannot show its reference is unknown and blocks. C21 owns the comparison and the verification of the transition from one accepted baseline to the next. Getting the reference, writing the advanced baseline and making the verdict stop an action are each the adopter's, and the Change says so. What an adopter inherits, and what it must supply itself, is stated in `interface.md`.

Mnemonic for the ratchet, the Owner's choice: **RATCHETS**, Raises Agents Toward Controlled Higher Engineering Targets Systematically. Of the readings offered (Ratchets, Routes, Regulates, Reinforces) the Owner prefers Raises, because it captures improvement and not only enforcement. The mnemonic is a memory aid for the concept and is not tested.

Not in this Change: any particular check or layout (engineering's C30 applies the concept and is only an example here), any place where a mode is switched on, and who may switch it.
