# controls/C19 portable controls

kaal-controls finds, types and reports controls in a repository that has none of KAAL's own objects. It carries a reference that helps an agent find the controls a repository already has by asking what directs, what prevents, what detects and what corrects, wherever they are. A control names its Intent in one of two forms, a linked object or its own words. The finder accepts any station name and checks only its shape. What belongs to other capabilities (the Intent, the stations, the links from Test Cases) is sought through an Exchange with a fallback that works alone; the controls' own objects stay fixed.

Slice 1 of the portability list. Later slices: C20 says what realizes a control in any shape and lets evidence stand for every shape (a ruleset rule, an agent's judgement, a manual step, a person or another system outside the repository); then the thin-control rule, chains of a detection required at a station, the note on enablers, and a Case on a repository with none of KAAL's vocabulary.
