# controls/F237 a check reports measures

1. A check of `runControl` may answer a list of findings (as today) or `{ findings, measures }`. A measure is `{ name, value, unit, better? }`: `name` and `unit` are non-empty text, `value` a finite number, `better` `'lower'` or `'higher'`.
2. `checkMeasures(list)` in `scripts/lib/measures.mjs`, pure, returns `{ measures, problems }`: the valid measures in the order given, and a problem named `<name or index>:<reason>` for each dropped one (not a finite number, empty name or unit, duplicate name, bad `better`, not an object).
3. `runControl` carries the valid measures unchanged into `measures` of the result, of the log line (the names and values) and of the record; the dropped ones are listed in `measureProblems` of the result and of the record, so a reader of history tells an invalid observation (a problem named, no measure) from an absent one (neither key) and from a zero (a measure with value 0). A check that answered a list, or threw, has neither.
4. A measure never changes the verdict, `blocked`, the findings, the mode or the flag: the result without its `measures` and `measureProblems` is exactly the result of the same check without measures.
5. A measure that was not reported is unknown: a check that reports none, throws, or drops an invalid one leaves no measure of that name, never a zero, and the record has no `measures` entry for it; nothing fills a gap.
6. Controls defines no measure name, reads no platform and counts nothing; the name, the unit and the number are the check's.
