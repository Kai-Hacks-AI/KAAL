# controls/F17 shape-kept

Adding `type` keeps what controls/F13, F14 and F15 decide, except that an item they refused whole now stays listed and fails validation (controls/F20). A control still needs `id`, `title`, `description` and a `has` with at least one Intent, ids stay unique, a Change id is never read as a control, and an item with `state`, `tests` or any other field is refused whole.
