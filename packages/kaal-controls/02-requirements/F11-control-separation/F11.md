# controls/F11 control-separation

A control is kept apart from what carries it out and from what makes its result binding.

- **Mechanism.** What executes the control, such as a script, a workflow, a review or a third-party action, is not the control. The same mechanism can serve different classes.
- **Enforcement.** What makes the result binding, such as a ruleset or a gate, is not the control. A control is preventive at a place only where the system of that place enforces its result. Where nothing enforces it, a control that judges is detective.
- **Place.** A control states, for each of the four places a Change passes through, whether it applies and how: Development, Integration, Promotion and Publishing. A place it does not apply to is stated as such.
