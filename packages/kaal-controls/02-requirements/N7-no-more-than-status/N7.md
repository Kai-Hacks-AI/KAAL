# controls/N7 no-more-than-status

The only new field is `status`. This Change adds no check that a status is true, no rule for moving from one status to the next, no history of statuses, no enforcement and no further relationship from a control. It adds no workflow, no required check and no wiring: running the check on GitHub, or requiring it, is another Change. The four words are a vocabulary for what a declaration claims, not a lifecycle the finder runs.
