# controls/F204 record-derived-and-registration-judged

1. `scripts/adapter.mjs` exports `deriveStatus({declared, records, evidence})`, pure. `declared` is the status the control claims, `records` the adapter records of that control, `evidence` the verdict `PASS` or not that `evidence.mjs` gives its Cases. It answers `{claimed, derived, finding}`.
2. `derived` is `tested` when `evidence` is `PASS`; else `running` when some record has `x: 'real'` and a verdict other than `UNKNOWN`; else `designed`. `implemented` is never derived.
3. `finding` is `record-lags` when the derived rank is higher than the claimed one, `overclaims` when the claim is `running` or `tested` and the derived rank is lower, and `null` otherwise. A claim of `implemented` is never a finding by itself, because only an observation shows it.
4. A record of a control other than the one asked about, a record with `verdict` `UNKNOWN` and a record with no `by` count for nothing.
5. `judgeRegistration({controls, delivered, adapted, records})` is pure. `controls` is what the finder lists (id, status, stations, path, and the evidence verdict if known), `delivered` the ids and paths the adopter says are delivered, `adapted` the ids the adopter says run through the adapter, `records` the adapter records.
6. It answers `{verdict, findings}`, `findings` a sorted list of unique names: `unadapted:<id>` for a control with a station or delivered that is not in `adapted`, `unrecorded:<path>` for a delivered path no control declares, and `record-lags:<id>` and `overclaims:<id>` from item 1. `verdict` is `PASS` with no finding, `REPORT` with findings, and `UNKNOWN` when an input is not a list.
7. The findings are names, so the check runs in detective, ratchet or preventive mode through the adapter itself, and the controls that exist today can be allowed by a baseline (C24) with a history (C25). The judge writes nothing and never edits a claim.
