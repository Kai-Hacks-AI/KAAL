# controls/F20 shape-kept

Adding `status` keeps what controls/F13, F14, F15 and F16 decide. A control still needs `id`, `title`, `description`, a `type` and a `has` with at least one Intent, ids stay unique, a Change id is never read as a control, and an item with `state`, `tests` or any other field is refused whole.
