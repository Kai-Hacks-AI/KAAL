# controls/F10 control-classes

Controls states what a Control is and owns four things about it, so that no other document defines them.

- **Class.** A control is directive, preventive, detective or corrective. Class is a matter of design. It does not change with what has been observed.
- **Verb.** The control's name is a verb and a subject, and the verb matches the class: guard or freeze is preventive, verify is detective, clear or drain is corrective, direct is directive.
- **Verdict.** A control that judges returns PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN. UNKNOWN never passes.
- **Responsibility.** A control states what it is responsible for deciding or changing, and nothing else.
