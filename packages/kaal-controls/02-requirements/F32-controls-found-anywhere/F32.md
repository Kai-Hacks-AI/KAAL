# controls/F32 controls-found-anywhere

1. A control names its Intent in exactly one of two forms: `has`, the Intents as standing objects, or `intent`, one sentence in its own words. A control with neither, with an empty `intent`, or with both is listed with the problem, never dropped. The finder gives each control `intentForm`, `linked` or `stated`, and resolves nothing: a report never reads a stated Intent as a linked one.
2. A station is any name of letters, digits and hyphens, with a mode of `detective` or `preventive`, each station once. The finder holds no list of stations: a name that fits the shape is a station, and the registry lists a control at any station it names. A shape that is wrong (no mode, a mode other than the two, a repeated station) stays refused.
3. The Cases of a control can be handed in as links, `{ case, tests }`. When links are handed in they are the only source and no file's first line is read, so a Case that points at a control by its first line but is not in the links does not count. When none are handed in the first line is read as before.
4. The Skill says how to find the controls a consumer already has without assuming a place: it puts the four types as four questions, names each control by what it controls, asks what each is for, asks for the pairings, writes a draft declaration for the owner to accept, and records what it did not find as unknown with what was asked and of whom. The draft claims no state above `designed` and no operation. This is judged by trial, not by a Case.
5. What the Skill needs from other capabilities (what a control is for and where the Intents are kept, the points where work passes, the checks that point at a control) is stated as seeks in `references/exchanges.md`, each in ordinary words that name no other capability and each with a fallback that works alone. The objects of controls (id, type, status, the evidence kinds and the rule of a provoked failure) are not offered and not overridden.
6. A control's line in the state report says what it controls: its id, its title and its type come first, then the claim, the computed state and the progress, so a reader sees which control it is and what kind without opening the file.
