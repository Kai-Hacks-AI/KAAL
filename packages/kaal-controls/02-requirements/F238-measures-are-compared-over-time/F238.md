# controls/F238 measures are compared over time

1. `compareMeasures(before, after)` in `scripts/lib/measures.mjs`, pure, takes two lists of valid measures (of two runs, or of the records of two revisions) and returns one entry per name in either list: `{ name, before, after, delta, unit, change }`.
2. `change` is `new` (only in after), `gone` (only in before), `incomparable` (the units differ; no delta), `same` (equal values), and otherwise `better` or `worse` by the `better` direction the measure declares in `after`. A measure that declares no direction is `changed`, never `better` or `worse`.
3. Entries are sorted by name. The function reads nothing and decides nothing: a caller that wants a gate builds it on the entries, under its own control or ratchet.
4. Invalid input (a list that is not valid measures) throws, so a comparison never passes by silence.
