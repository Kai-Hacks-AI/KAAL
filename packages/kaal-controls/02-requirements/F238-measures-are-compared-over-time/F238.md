# controls/F238 measures are compared over time

1. `compareMeasures(before, after)` in `scripts/lib/measures.mjs`, pure, takes two lists of valid measures (of two runs, or of the records of two revisions) and returns one entry per name in either list: `{ name, before, after, delta, unit, change }` (a field a side lacks is absent).
2. `change` is `unknown` when the name is missing on either side (with `missing: 'before'` or `'after'` and no `delta`; a missing measurement is never read as zero, never `better`, `worse` or `same`), `incomparable` (the units differ; no delta), `same` (equal values), and otherwise `better` or `worse` by the `better` direction the measure declares in `after`. A measure that declares no direction is `changed`, never `better` or `worse`.
3. Entries are sorted by name. The function reads nothing and decides nothing: a caller that wants a gate builds it on the entries, under its own control or ratchet.
4. Invalid input (a list that is not valid measures) throws, so a comparison never passes by silence.
