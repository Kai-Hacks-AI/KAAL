# controls/F13 control-evidence

Three kinds of evidence are kept apart, and a claim is no stronger than the kind it rests on.

- **Structural.** The documents, declarations and settings that say what the control is. Configured is not operating.
- **Behavioral.** What the control's judge returns for given inputs, observed by running it.
- **Operational.** What the control did for a real subject in a real place, observed from a record for that subject.

A claim of enforcement or effectiveness is made only from operational evidence. A control starts RED: at least one Case fails on an assertion against `main` while the behavior is missing, and the RED is observed before the DO. Whatever has not been observed is stated as not observed.
