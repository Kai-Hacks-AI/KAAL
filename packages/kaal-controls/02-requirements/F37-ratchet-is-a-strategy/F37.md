# controls/F37 ratchet-is-a-strategy

1. A station mode is `detective` or `preventive`. `<Station>:ratchet` is refused, and the problem names the strategy declaration as the recovery.
2. A control declares a ratchet with `strategy: ratchet` together with `baseline-source`, `owner` and `exit`, each non-empty. The control is a preventive control.
3. `strategy` with another value, `strategy` on a control that is not preventive, or `strategy: ratchet` with any of the three missing, is listed with the problem naming the missing or wrong field.
4. `baseline-source`, `owner` or `exit` without `strategy: ratchet` is listed with a problem.
5. A deadline is no field of a control: it is planning's, and an unknown field stays refused.
