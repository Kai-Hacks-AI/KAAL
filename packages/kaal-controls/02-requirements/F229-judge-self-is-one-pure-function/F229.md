# controls/F229 judge-self-is-one-pure-function

1. `scripts/lib/judge-self.mjs` exports `judgeSelf({ files, revision })`. `files` are the `{ path, text }` entries of the package (as hosting supplies them) and `revision` is the identity of what was read. It returns `{ findings, unknown }`, the answer of `assessRecord` for the facts of F228, and nothing else.
2. It is pure: it reads no file and runs no command, imports nothing outside the package's own library, and names no station, platform or KAAL term (controls/N8).
3. A claim that lags or overclaims is a finding named `<fact>:<lags|overclaims>:<id>`. Missing files, no controls found, or a revision that is not a non-empty string throw, so the adapter answers UNKNOWN and nothing passes by silence.
4. It sits beside the other library files and is not a shim; it is published (listed under `files` by being under `skills`).
5. This Change adds no workflow, flag, station or required check. Running `judgeSelf` as a control of the package at a station is a WIRE and the Owner's decision.
