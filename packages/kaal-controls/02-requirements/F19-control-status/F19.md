# controls/F19 control-status

A control declares `status` in its frontmatter. It is mandatory and holds exactly one of `designed`, `implemented`, `running` or `tested`, spelled in lower case, in that order of progress (DIRT). The progress is cumulative: a `tested` control is also running, implemented and designed.

- `designed`: the control is declared and its Intent, Requirements and Architecture say what it does.
- `implemented`: the mechanism exists, but is not yet running on the boundary it is meant for.
- `running`: the mechanism runs on its boundary and reports.
- `tested`: evidence shows that the control works as intended. A Case that tests the control, run against the real mechanism, is such evidence.

A control with no `status`, an empty `status`, or a `status` outside the four stays in the inventory and fails validation, and its problem names `status` with a recovery (controls/F20). The finder reports the status as declared, and for a valid status a `rank` of 1 to 4 in the order above, so a `tested` control ranks as running too. It reports no rank and invents no status for an invalid or missing one. Whether the status is true is judged by evidence, not by this requirement: the finder checks the value, not the claim.
