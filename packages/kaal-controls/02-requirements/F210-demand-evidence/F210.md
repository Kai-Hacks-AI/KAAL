# controls/F210 demand-evidence

1. `scripts/demand-evidence.mjs` exports `findEvidenceDue({discovery, transitions, facts, authorizations, revision})`, pure. KAAL Control types are Directive, Preventive, Detective and Corrective, with no hierarchy; this check invents no mode ladder and no advisory type. Whether a finding is advisory or prevents delivery is GitHub configuration. Evidence acquisition is the adopter's, and the check only judges what it is given.
2. `discovery` is `{revision, complete}`: the adopter's proof that the list of `transitions` is the complete discovery of the transitions proposed at the applicable `revision`. A transition is `{control, kind, revision}`, `kind` being `activation` (the control is to be installed by a WIRE) or `enforcement` (an installed control is to be made to prevent delivery). When `discovery.complete` is not `true`, `discovery.revision` is not the applicable `revision`, a transition names another revision, or a `kind` is neither, the check throws, so that the adapter fails closed with `UNKNOWN`: a transition list that may be partial permits nothing.
3. `facts` maps a control id to `{revision, defined, implemented, installed, running, testedPre, testedOperational}`, each `true` or `false`, established independently by its own proof for the specific control and revision. `installed` is the independently proven installation. `testedPre` is Test Case evidence applicable before installation and `testedOperational` is operational testing after it; neither stands in for the other and none implies another. A fact that is `null`, missing, or stale (the entry's `revision` is not the applicable one) makes the check throw: missing or stale evidence never permits.
4. An `activation` needs `defined`, `implemented` and `testedPre`. An `enforcement` needs `defined`, `implemented`, `installed`, `running` and `testedOperational`. The precise evidence is the existing protocol's, which the control's declared `evidence` states. The finding is `evidence-due:<kind>:<fact>:<id>` for each needed fact that is `false`.
5. `authorizations` is the list of `{control, kind, revision}` that the adopter has independently established as the Owner's separate authorization, bound to the exact control, kind and revision. A transition with no matching entry gives `evidence-due:<kind>:authorized:<id>`; an entry for another kind, control or revision counts for nothing. A malformed list throws.
6. The answer is a sorted list of unique names, empty only when every discovered transition carries its evidence and its authorization. Only these confirmed findings can be baselined; `UNKNOWN` never passes and is never filtered by an allowance. It reads no file and writes nothing, and knows no repository, path, flag or service.
