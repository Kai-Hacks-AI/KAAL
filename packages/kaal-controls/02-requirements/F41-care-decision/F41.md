# controls/F41 care-decision

1. `scripts/care.mjs` exports `judgeDecision({findings, decisions})`, pure. `findings` is a list of finding ids (strings) and `decisions` a list of records `{finding, response, proposedBy, selectedBy, reason, ...}`.
2. A response is one of `contain`, `accept`, `ratchet`, `eliminate`. Every decision carries a non-empty `finding`, `proposedBy`, `selectedBy` and `reason`. `contain` and `accept` also carry a non-empty `owner`; `contain` carries `limits` (what limits the exposure); `ratchet` and `eliminate` carry `corrects` (the corrective work). A `ratchet` is refused without `corrects`, so it cannot stand for acceptance.
3. It answers `PASS` when every finding has exactly one decision and every decision is well formed and names a finding in the list. Otherwise `FAIL` with `reasons`, each `{kind, finding, field?}`: `uncovered` (a finding with no decision), `unknown-finding` (a decision for a finding not listed), `duplicate-decision`, `bad-response`, `missing-field` (naming the field). `UNKNOWN` when `findings` or `decisions` is missing or not a list, or a finding is not a string.
4. `PASS` means the record is complete. The answer holds only `verdict` and `reasons`: it selects no response, writes no baseline and activates nothing.
5. `references/care.md` teaches the four responses, the flow (detection, Review proposes, the authorized decision-maker selects) and that no finding is accepted by being old, in words that name no repository, path, capability, id or role. `SKILL.md` points at it and stays at or under 600 words.
