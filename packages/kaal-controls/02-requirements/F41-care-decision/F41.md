# controls/F41 care-decision

1. `scripts/care.mjs` exports `judgeDecision({findings, dispositions, decisions})`, pure. `findings` is a list of finding ids (strings); `dispositions` a list of records `{finding, disposition, reviewedBy, ...}`; `decisions` a list of records `{finding, response, proposedBy, selectedBy, reason, ...}`.
2. A disposition is `valid`, `invalid` or `unknown`. Every disposition carries a non-empty `finding` and `reviewedBy`; `invalid` also carries `evidence`, `unknown` also carries `investigate` (what must be investigated). A response is one of `contain`, `accept`, `ratchet`, `eliminate`. Every decision carries a non-empty `finding`, `proposedBy`, `selectedBy` and `reason`. `contain` and `accept` also carry a non-empty `owner`; `contain` carries `limits`; `ratchet` and `eliminate` carry `corrects`. A `ratchet` is refused without `corrects`, so it cannot stand for acceptance.
3. It answers `PASS` when every finding has exactly one well-formed disposition, every `valid` finding has exactly one well-formed decision, and no decision names a finding that is not `valid`. Otherwise `FAIL` with `reasons`, each `{kind, finding, field?}`: `undisposed` (a finding without a disposition), `duplicate-disposition`, `bad-disposition`, `uncovered` (a valid finding with no decision), `duplicate-decision`, `bad-response`, `decision-not-valid` (a decision for an invalid or unknown finding), `unknown-finding` (a disposition or decision for a finding not listed), `missing-field` (naming the field). `UNKNOWN` when `findings`, `dispositions` or `decisions` is missing or not a list, or a finding is not a string.
4. `PASS` means the record is complete. The answer holds only `verdict`, `reasons` and `unresolved` (the findings whose disposition is `unknown`, sorted, each once): it selects no response, assigns none to an invalid or unknown finding, writes no baseline and activates nothing.
5. `references/care.md` teaches the three dispositions, that only a valid finding takes a response, the four responses, the flow (detection, Review judges and proposes, the authorized decision-maker selects) and that no finding is accepted by being old or left unjudged, in words that name no repository, path, capability, id or role. `SKILL.md` points at it and stays at or under 600 words.
