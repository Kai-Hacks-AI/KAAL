# controls/F204 record-derived-from-evidence

1. `scripts/adapter.mjs` exports `deriveStatus({id, declared, records, evidence})`, pure. `declared` is the status the control claims, `records` the adapter records, `evidence` the verdict `PASS` or not that `evidence.mjs` gives the Cases of the control. It answers `{claimed, derived, finding}`.
2. `derived` is `tested` when `evidence` is `PASS`; else `running` when some record of this control has `x: 'real'`, a verdict other than `UNKNOWN` and a recorded `by`; else `designed`. `implemented` is never derived.
3. `finding` is `record-lags` when the derived rank is higher than the claimed one, `overclaims` when the claim is `running` or `tested` and the derived rank is lower, and `null` otherwise. A claim of `implemented` is never a finding by itself, because only an observation shows it.
4. A record of another control, a record with verdict `UNKNOWN`, a record with no `by` and a record of a kind other than `real` count for nothing toward `running`.
5. It writes nothing and never edits a claim.
