# controls/F40 whole-tree-before-preventive

1. `scripts/scope.mjs` exports `holdsProperty({findingsOf, judge, base, head})`, pure. It holds when the findings `judge(base, head)` reports equal `findingsOf(head)` minus `findingsOf(base)` as sets. It says `holds: false` with `onlyInJudge` and `onlyInDiff` when they differ, and `holds: false` with `reason: 'unstable-findings'` when a finding is not a string or a tree names one twice.
2. `qualify({property, provoked, scan})` is pure and answers one of: `UNKNOWN` when an input is missing; `NOT_QUALIFIED` with the reason `property-fails` (a genuine mismatch), `not-provoked` (the seeded violation was not found, or did not clear when removed) or `unstable-findings` (malformed finding ids, whether `holdsProperty` reported them or the scan holds them, never collapsed into `property-fails`); `QUALIFIED` with `findings` (the scanned findings, sorted, each once, possibly none) and `needs: 'review'` when it has findings or `needs: 'owner-go'` when it has none.
3. `qualify` never proposes a ratchet, never accepts a finding into a baseline and never switches a control. A non-empty scan is evidence for the CARE decision (Contain, Accept, Ratchet, Eliminate), not a ratchet recommendation or activation: the answer holds only `verdict`, `findings` and `needs`, with no response, baseline or history field. Review judges the findings and proposes CARE responses; the authorized decision-maker selects one.
