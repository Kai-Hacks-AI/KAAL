# controls/F201 skill-teaches-ratchet

1. The `description` of `skills/kaal-controls/SKILL.md` names a ratchet, a baseline and non-regression, so that a search for any of the three leads to the Skill. The router in `SKILL.md` points a question about a ratchet to the ratchet text.
2. The ratchet text sets a report, a plain gate and a ratchet side by side: what each does when someone adds a violation and when someone fixes one, and which fits a system that starts with violations.
3. The ratchet text holds one worked example in setup-agnostic words: a baseline of a few named findings, a new finding fails, an old one passes, a fix shrinks the baseline, a baseline that grew fails, a missing or unreadable baseline is unknown and not a pass, and the ratchet ends at zero.
4. The fields that declare a ratchet (`strategy`, `baseline-source`, `owner`, `exit`) are taught in `references/declaring-controls.md` only. The ratchet text does not repeat them and points there.
5. The ratchet text teaches in generic words, with no file path, flag, field name or tool of any setup: the accepted baseline is read from the base and never from the change under review, the history only grows and each shrinking change adds one line, and retirement removes the baseline and the strategy and keeps the history (C24, C25).
6. The ratchet text says what a ratchet does not prove: stopping a regression is not compliance, a count can hide one finding swapped for another, and a name hides a worse finding of the same name.
7. Size, layout of the references and how they are produced are Core's standard (core/C52) and the owners'. This Requirement adds no word count and no structure of its own, and when the two differ, the standard wins.
