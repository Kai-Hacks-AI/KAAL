# controls/F12 calibrated-claims

A claim about a control is no stronger than what has been observed. A rule written down is not enforced, a setting is not operation, and one operation is not effectiveness. A preventive claim names the action, the actor or interface, and the enforcement boundary, and never says the action is impossible. A result that reports nothing shows only that nothing was found, and what was not observed stays unknown. What verification, validation and a finding are is Testing's, and the Skill cites it.
