# controls/F205 stale-record-blocks-where-relevant

1. `scripts/adapter.mjs` exports `judgeDue({stage, base, head, installed, records})`, pure. `stage` is `collector`, `raise` or `promotion`. `base` and `head` are lists of `{id, status, mode, evidence}` for the controls before and after the pull request (`mode` is `off`, `detective` or `preventive`, resolved by the adopter; `evidence` is the verdict `PASS` or not that `evidence.mjs` gives the control's Cases). `installed` lists the ids the adopter says are installed. `records` are the adapter records.
2. The answer is `{verdict, findings}`, `findings` a sorted list of unique names, `verdict` `PASS` with none, `REPORT` with some, and `UNKNOWN` when an input is not a list or `stage` is none of the three.
3. `collector`: for a control in `installed` whose claim in `head` is `designed`, the finding is `record-due:<id>`. A control whose claim is `implemented` or higher is not due.
4. `raise`: for a control whose `mode` in `head` is higher than in `base` (`off` below `detective` below `preventive`; a control absent from `base` was `off`), the finding is `evidence-due:<id>` unless the evidence is carried: for a raise to `detective`, a record of that control with a verdict other than `UNKNOWN` and a recorded `by`; for a raise to `preventive`, a derived status of `tested` (`deriveStatus`). A control whose mode did not rise is never asked.
5. `promotion`: the findings are `record-lags:<id>` and `overclaims:<id>` of `deriveStatus` over every control in `head`, as `judgeRegistration` lists them.
6. The judge never writes. A record is written only by a normal Change of the capability that owns the control, never by a WIRE and never by CI. Where each stage runs, and whether it blocks, is the adopter's: a station for each, detective first, preventive only on the Owner's go.
