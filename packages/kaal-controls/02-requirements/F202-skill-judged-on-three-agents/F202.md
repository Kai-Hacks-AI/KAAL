# controls/F202 skill-judged-on-three-agents

1. `00-changes/C140-skill-ratchet/trial/trial.md` holds what is controls' in the trial: three prompts, one rubric and the pass bar, in plain text, with no tool, agent or setup named in a prompt and no word of KAAL. Anyone can run it on any agent that loads Skills.
2. How a run is recorded is Research's. Each agent (Claude, Codex, Antigravity) gets one rehearsal record in the form of the kaal-research Skill, under `packages/kaal-controls/02-learnings/rehearsals/`, naming its harness and its technique, holding both arms (`skill` and `blank`, at least three runs of the prompt set per arm), and judged for form by the Research Skill's own judgement. This Change defines no record form of its own. The records of the second and third agent challenge the first, as Research's form provides.
3. Each record carries, as its evidence of role `scores`, a `scores.json` of the rubric scores: per run its arm, its prompt, whether `SKILL.md` was opened (R1) and a score of 0 to 3 for each criterion that applies to the prompt (R2 to R6). The scores are the judge's.
4. The trial passes for an agent when its `skill` runs all opened `SKILL.md`, its mean on R2, R3, R4 and R5 is at least 2, its mean on R6 is 3, and on no criterion is its `skill` mean below its own `blank` mean. The Skill passes when it passes for all three agents.
5. A Case finds the records, asks the Research Skill's judgement whether each is well-formed, and applies item 4 to the scores. It never decides whether the teaching works.
6. The trial shows that the Skill works on these three agents. It makes no claim of an effect in general, and each record's claims and limits say so.
7. When Research's contract changes (research/C2, #2467), the records follow the contract as ratified, and where the two differ Research's wins.
