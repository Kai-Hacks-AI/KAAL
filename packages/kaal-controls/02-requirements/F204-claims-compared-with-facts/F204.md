# controls/F204 claims-compared-with-facts

1. Four facts about a control are independent: **defined** (a valid declaration), **implemented** (a corresponding implementation), **running** (an actual execution) and **tested** (applicable Test Case evidence). None is a stage of another and none implies another. Each has its own point of proof, for the specific control and the applicable revision.
2. `scripts/adapter.mjs` exports `compareClaims({id, claimed, facts})`, pure. `claimed` is `{defined, implemented, running, tested}`, booleans the control declares. `facts` is the same four keys, each `true`, `false` or `null`, established by their own proof. It answers `{id, findings, unknown}`.
3. `findings` is a sorted list of `<fact>:overclaims` where the claim is `true` and the fact `false`, and `<fact>:lags` where the claim is `false` and the fact `true`. Each fact is compared alone: a true `tested` never makes `running` true, and a lagging `running` is not forgiven by a passing `tested`.
4. `unknown` is the sorted list of facts that are `null` or missing. A missing proof is UNKNOWN, never proof of absence, and never a finding. A missing or malformed `claimed` or `facts`, or a missing `id`, throws.
5. `scripts/adapter.mjs` also exports `executed({id, records, revision, established})`, pure: the adapter's one fact, **running**. A committed record is a claim, never proof of its own authenticity. `established` is the list of `{control, run, sha}` that the adopter has independently verified exist (the service check is the adopter's, outside the pure library). It is `true` only when a record is valid and complete (`control` this `id`, `x: 'real'`, a non-empty `run`, `sha` the applicable `revision`, `verdict` one of `PASS`, `FAIL` or `REPORT`, `by: 'adapter'`) and the same `control`, `run` and `sha` are in `established`; otherwise `null`, never `false`. A missing, empty or non-string `id` or `revision` makes it throw: invalid identity is never matched against a record that carries the same invalid value, and a run is established only for the exact control, run and revision. A record not established, a record with no `run`, a malformed or `UNKNOWN` verdict (the run could not be judged, so it is no execution evidence), a record of another control or revision, one not by the adapter and one of a kind other than `real` count for nothing. Running describes that the control executed and judged, not what it found: a pass, a fail and a report of findings (a detective run, or a control switched off by its flag, controls/F216) all count, and the separate Tested fact is untouched.
6. The adapter supplies execution evidence only. It is no authority for the other three facts, which their own proofs establish: the declaration check, the implementation or delivery found as it is, and the Test Case evidence.
7. Nothing here writes or edits a claim.
