# controls/I14 runs shown honestly

Tested is a claim about runs, and a run history is no better than what it leaves out. A control that passed enough runs and also failed or could not be decided on others has not shown it works until those are explained. A run belongs to whoever ran it, and a control is not shown by those who built it. Wherever kaal-controls is used, a tested claim means every run is accounted for and each says who recorded it, and what could not be shown stays unknown.
