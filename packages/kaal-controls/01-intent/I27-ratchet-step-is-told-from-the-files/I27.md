# controls/I27 the step of a ratchet is told from its files by the library

A ratchet moves in three kinds of step: it is adopted, it shrinks, it is retired. Which one a change is can be read from the baseline and the history of the base and of the head, and nothing else. The library that judges the steps tells them apart and judges each, so a wiring only hands over the files and the Changes the step carries, and never decides a step or parses a baseline itself. A file it cannot read is unknown, never a pass.
