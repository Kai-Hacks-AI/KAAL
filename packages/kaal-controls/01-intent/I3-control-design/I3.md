# controls/I3 control design

An agent designs and reasons about a control the same way every time: it says what undesirable outcome the control addresses and why, classifies the control, separates the control from what executes and enforces it, places it at a point of the lifecycle, traces it through Intent, Requirements, Architecture and Cases, and claims only what its evidence supports. It does this from the shared contracts of the other capabilities, without inventing control semantics and without a second copy of any contract.
