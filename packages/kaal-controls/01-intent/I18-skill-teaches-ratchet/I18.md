# controls/I18 the Skill teaches a ratchet to an agent that has never heard of KAAL

An agent asked about a ratchet, a baseline or non-regression, in a repository that has nothing to do with KAAL, finds the Skill, reads the part that answers, and gives an answer it can act on: what a ratchet is next to a report and a plain gate, how it behaves in a small worked example, how its baseline, its history and its end work, and what it does not prove. It does this in words of its own field, without file paths, flags or field names of any setup. The Skill is judged on this by running it, on the agents its users run, and not by counting words.
