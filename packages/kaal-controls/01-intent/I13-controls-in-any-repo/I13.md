# controls/I13 controls in any repo

kaal-controls works in a repository that has none of KAAL's objects, as it does in KAAL. A control could be anywhere: in the repository, on the platform, in another system, in an organisation's process or in people's habits, and the Skill assumes no place. It helps an agent find the controls a consumer already has by the four types as four questions, and says that what was not found is unknown and not absent. Every control names what it is for, in the form the consumer has: a standing Intent where there are Intent records, its own words where there are none. What other capabilities own (the Intents, the stations, the links from Test Cases) the Skill seeks through an Exchange and keeps a fallback that works alone. Its own objects, the control, its types, its states, its evidence kinds, stay fixed and are not for a consumer to override.
