# controls/I36 a PLAN is proven red, never skipped

A PLAN records Cases that must fail until the DO, because a Case that was never seen failing proves nothing. The package suite now runs in a required lane, where any red Case fails the check. The intent is that the lane keeps both properties at once: a PLAN's Cases are observed failing by their assertion, and nothing else is allowed to fail, so the lane is not weakened. A Case that passes early, errors before it asserts, or stays red after its Change is closed is found and fails the lane.
