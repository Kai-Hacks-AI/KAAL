# controls/I22 verify Record

What a control records about each of its four facts, defined, implemented, running and tested, must agree with the proof of that fact. A record that says less than its proof shows, and one that says more, are both found by name, fact by fact, whichever way it is wrong. A fact with no established proof is unknown, which is neither agreement nor a finding.
