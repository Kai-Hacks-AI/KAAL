# controls/I15 one check, three modes

A check gives one answer: in or out of its specification, naming what is missing and what is too much. What that answer does is not the check's own, it is the mode the check is put to use in. Detective reports it, preventive blocks, and a ratchet is a preventive that blocks only what is worse than a recorded baseline, which only shrinks. When a ratchet's baseline is empty it becomes plain preventive and the baseline is removed, and a ratchet with no such exit is not accepted. The type of a control stays its design; the claim about it follows the mode, so that only a blocking mode may be said to prevent and a reporting one only reveals. An agent that finds or writes a check in any repository, with no other capability, can say which mode it is in and claim no more than that.

Mnemonic for the ratchet: **RATCHETS**, Raises Agents Toward Controlled Higher Engineering Targets Systematically. A ratchet raises the standard and never lets it fall back.
