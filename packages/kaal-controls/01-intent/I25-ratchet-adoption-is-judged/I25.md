# controls/I25 the adoption of a ratchet is judged by the library like every later step

A ratchet begins with one line in its history, the adoption: the baseline it starts from and the Change that adopted it. Every later step is judged by the shared library, and so the first step is too, by the same library, with the same rule that a line names its Change and its time, and that nothing earlier is rewritten. An adopter does not need a check of its own to accept the first line.
