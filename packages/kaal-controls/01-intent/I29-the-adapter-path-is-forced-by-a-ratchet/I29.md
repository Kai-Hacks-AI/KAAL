# controls/I29 the adapter path is forced by a ratchet over the delivered controls

Every control runs through the one shared library (controls/I19). That is made true by the library's own ratchet strategy, not by a rule at declaration: verify Adapter (controls/I20) judges the complete map of delivered controls, the controls that are confirmed to bypass the adapter today are its baseline, a control that bypasses it and is not in the baseline is refused, and the baseline only shrinks, with a history, until it is empty and the control is plain preventive. A control that is built on another path then cannot enter the delivery, so a rejection when it is declared adds nothing. Nothing is baselined, and nothing is enforced, from evidence that does not confirm the bypass.
