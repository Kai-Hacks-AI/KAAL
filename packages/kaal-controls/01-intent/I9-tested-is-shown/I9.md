# controls/I9 tested is shown

A control that claims `tested` has shown it. Each control says what its own minimum evidence is, in the one language the repository already has: its Test Cases, run against kinds of X such as a fixture, a real merge, a provoked case or a before and after pair of trees, at least so many times each, with at least one real failure. A claim of Tested is judged against that minimum and recorded runs, and evidence that is missing, short or not green leaves the claim unresolved, never Tested. A control that does the same work in another mode, such as a check that becomes a gate, is another control and needs its own evidence.
