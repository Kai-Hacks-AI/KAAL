# controls/I37 a verdict that cannot judge says why

A control that ends UNKNOWN has not failed and has not passed, and the person who reads it needs the reason: what was asked, what kind of failure came back and where. A reason that is swallowed on the way costs hours of misdiagnosis, as a rate limit that read as "rules cannot be read" did. The intent is that every non-PASS verdict guides to its real cause from the fact source, without the controls library interpreting the platform and without any cause turning into a pass or a fail.
