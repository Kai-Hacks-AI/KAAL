# controls/CTL5 demand Evidence

A detective control with one property: a proposed activation or enforcement transition of a control carries the independently correlated evidence it needs and the Owner's separate authorization. It is meant to become preventive by the change protocol, in its own Change (controls/C146), after the Owner's go.
