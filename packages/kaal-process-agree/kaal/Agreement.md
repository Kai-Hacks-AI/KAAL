# Agreement

Composes: Intent 7317b19e2a09ee18bbac37c3ed5ccb4e20e108bf2aa569faae5d7348b0ed22cf and Review 7c3d4d8e6f56f9d3a6bf6e49d1ed912ec1808ac44e61541196160ddf9ab46d5f. Boundary: one loop, in which one Worker and one Reviewer work toward agreement on one result under one grant.

Agreement is the Process by which a Worker comes to agreement with an independent Reviewer on a result, and by which the Work is handed to a human when they cannot. It composes Intent, which gives the result its Way of Working and its exact identity, and Review, which gives the round and the convergence of review on an exact identity. It redefines neither, neither names it, and a Skill that is composed this way is not made to depend on another.

An Agreement runs within a grant of the Owner: the Owner names who works, who reviews, and how many rounds may be run toward agreement. Within that grant the Worker proceeds without a human. What it may do next is not its own to decide. It follows from the record of the loop, which holds each version of the result put forward, each report of the Reviewer and each direction of a human, and from nothing the Worker says. Agreement is recognized in that record and never declared: the Reviewer the grant names reported that review has converged on exactly the result as it now stands, and nothing has changed since.

A report counts for its outcome, for what it is about, and for who made it. What is written in it for people to read is not read as authority. When the record shows that agreement can no longer be reached by the Worker alone, the Process requires HOW, Human Observes Work: no authority was granted, a report is not the Reviewer's, the record is not a loop, the Reviewer contradicts itself, review is asked again with nothing changed, an earlier version has returned, or the rounds granted are spent. The unresolved result and the evidence are then put to a human, whose direction either returns the Work to the loop with as much authority as the human gives, or ends it. Agents may assist the human; that a human directs is what makes it HOW.

Its scope is that alone. Agreement is a Process, a Skill for a kind of work, and not a capability that stands for itself. It does not grant authority, assign or choose a Reviewer, establish the result, or decide what follows agreement, and it prescribes no order for any other Skill. It is not a general workflow. It cannot show that a report is truly the Reviewer's or that a direction is truly a human's; those are for an owner to inspect. Agreement is optional: it is not Core, a valid embedding does not need it, and embedding KAAL does not install it.
