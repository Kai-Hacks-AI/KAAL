# research/C1 rehearsal method

Research gets its first object, the rehearsal record `RH<n>`: our own idea tried on agents and recorded so a reader can inspect it and can tell what it shows from what it only suggests. A finder lists records, and a form-only judgement says whether a record states what a credible trial must state. The method is the points of the CRO review of `controls/C21` (#2319), made generic: durable evidence, what fresh meant, verified exposure, and claims kept apart from what was demonstrated, with the untreated arm and blind scoring required of the claims that need them and not of every record. It stands alone, with no KAAL dependency. This Change is the PLAN only.
