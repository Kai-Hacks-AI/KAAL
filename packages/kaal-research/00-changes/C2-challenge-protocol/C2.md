# research/C2 challenge protocol

A rehearsal can now be rerun by someone with a different setup. Each record states its harness and a short protocol, may challenge another record, and names the technique and version it used. Techniques are a growing, versioned set kept in the Research Skill; this Change ships the form and the first technique (`judged-trial`, the method of RH1), and leaves later techniques, such as automated parallel runs across several agent harnesses, for later Changes. It stays consumable outside KAAL. This Change is the PLAN only.
