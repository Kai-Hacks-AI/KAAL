# research/I1 a claim about our own idea rests on a trial anyone can inspect

When we try our own idea on agents, such as whether a Skill teaches a method, a reader who was not there can see what was run, on what, against what, and with what result, and can tell what the trial shows from what it only suggests. A claim is never stronger than the runs and the evidence behind it, a favourable, an unfavourable and an inconclusive result are reported alike, and the record does not depend on the author's temporary files or memory. The method is the same wherever the idea comes from, so a consumer that has only Research can use it.
