# research/I2 a trial can be challenged by someone with a different setup

A result from one agent, model or harness is a result about that setup. A reader with another setup, such as another agent, another model or an automated harness, can rerun the trial from the record alone, and say whether the result holds for them. The rerun is a record of its own that points at the one it challenges, and the methods for running and judging trials grow over time without making earlier records unreadable. The method stays usable by a consumer that has only Research.
