# research/F4 exposure and claims

Each run says whether the treatment reached the agent: `opened` (an excerpt in the evidence shows the agent opening it), `delivered`, `unchecked`, or `none`. Each claim names the runs it rests on, says whether it is `demonstrated` as `yes`, `partly` or `no`, and, unless `yes`, says what is `not-shown`. A claim about the treatment is `yes` only if every treated run it rests on is `opened`. The verdict is `favourable` only if scoring was blind and every claim is `yes`; `unfavourable` and `inconclusive` have no such bar. `Limits` states what the run count can and cannot show, with no invented threshold. The judgement is about form: it does not say the claims are true.

**Done when** a record meeting this is `well-formed`, a claim marked `yes` on an unchecked run, a `partly` or `no` claim with nothing not shown, a claim resting on no or an unknown run, an excerpt that is not in the evidence, no claim at all, no statement of what the count shows, and a `favourable` verdict that is unblinded or has an undemonstrated claim are each `malformed`, and an honest unfavourable or inconclusive record with undemonstrated claims is `well-formed`.
