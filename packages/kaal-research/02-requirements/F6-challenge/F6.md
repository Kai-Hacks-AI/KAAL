# research/F6 challenge

A rehearsal may challenge another: `challenges: <id of a rehearsal>` in its front matter. A challenge is an ordinary record, with its own design, evidence, runs and claims, plus an `outcome` (`replicates`, `differs` or `inconclusive`) and `differs-in`, a line that names what differs from the record it challenges (agent, model, harness, judge, materials), or `none` for a plain rerun. The `outcome` is the challenger's own statement about the rerun, and it is not a verdict on the original. A challenge record is judged for form like any other, and the finder lists the id it challenges beside each record (empty when it challenges none), so a reader can list the challenges of a record. The judgement does not look up the challenged record.

**Done when** a challenge with an outcome and `differs-in` is `well-formed` and listed with the id it challenges, a plain rerun with `differs-in: none` is `well-formed`, and each of these is `malformed` with its own rule: a `challenges` that is not a rehearsal id, a record that challenges itself, a challenge with no or an unknown `outcome`, a challenge with no `differs-in`, and an `outcome` or `differs-in` on a record that challenges none.
