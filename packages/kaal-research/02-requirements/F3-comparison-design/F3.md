# research/F3 comparison design

The `Design` section states: the arms, one of which is the untreated arm, which receives no treatment at all (an older version of the treatment is a treated arm, and compares versions, not treatment against none); the run count of every arm, equal unless a note says why not, and equal to the runs listed; what `fresh` meant in practice and whether that was checked; the model and settings; whether scoring was blind and, if so, how; and when the key was frozen and when the treatment was fixed, the key first. A run's `exposure` is `none` in the untreated arm and not `none` in a treated arm.

**Done when** a record that states all of this is `well-formed` and each omission or contradiction is `malformed` with its own rule: no untreated arm, fewer than two arms, a missing or unequal or mismatched run count, no statement of fresh, model, settings, isolation check or blinding, blinding claimed without how, a key frozen after the treatment, an exposed untreated run, an unexposed treated run.
