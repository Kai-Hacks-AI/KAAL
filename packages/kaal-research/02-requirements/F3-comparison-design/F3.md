# research/F3 comparison design

The `Design` section states what was run, and is `well-formed` whatever the design is. It states: the `arms`; `untreated`, the one arm that receives no treatment at all, or `none` when there is no such arm (an older version of the treatment is a treated arm, so a version comparison says `untreated: none`); the run count `n` of every arm, equal to the runs listed; what `fresh` meant in practice; the model and settings; and whether scoring was blind (`blind: yes` with how, or `no`). Optionally, when the key was frozen and when the treatment was fixed: if one is given both are, the key first. A run's `exposure` is `none` in the untreated arm and not `none` in a treated arm.

A design with no untreated arm, no blind scoring, one arm, or unequal arms is not malformed. It limits what its claims may say (F4), and the record states it.

**Done when** a record that states all of this is `well-formed`, a version comparison with no untreated arm, an unblinded record, and one with unequal run counts are `well-formed`, and each omission or contradiction is `malformed` with its own rule: no arms, an `untreated` that names no arm, a missing or mismatched run count, no statement of fresh, model, settings or blinding, blinding claimed without how, only one of the two key times, a key frozen after the treatment, an exposed untreated run, an unexposed treated run.
