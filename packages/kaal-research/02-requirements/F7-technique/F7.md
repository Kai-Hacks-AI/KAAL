# research/F7 technique

A rehearsal names the technique it used and its version: `technique: <name>@<n>`, a lower-case name of words joined by hyphens, then `@`, then a whole number from 1. A technique is a way of running and judging a trial, kept in the Research Skill, and a version changes whenever its steps change; a record is read against the version it names, and a later version never makes an earlier record unreadable. Two records may name different techniques or different versions, and both are `well-formed`. The judgement checks the form of the name and does not look up the technique, because the Skill a reader holds may be older or newer than the one the author held. The finder lists the technique beside each record.

**Done when** records naming `judged-trial@1` and `judged-trial@2` and one naming another technique are `well-formed` and listed with their technique, and each of these is `malformed` with its own rule: no `technique`, a name with no version, a version that is not a whole number from 1, and a name that is not a lower-case word with hyphens.
