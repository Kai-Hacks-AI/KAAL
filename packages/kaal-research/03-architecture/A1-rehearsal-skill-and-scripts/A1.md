# research/A1 rehearsal-skill-and-scripts

The package `@kaihacksai/kaal-research` will hold a Skill `skills/kaal-research/` for rehearsals: `SKILL.md` for the agent that plans and runs a trial, and two scripts in `scripts/`, `find-rehearsals.mjs` (`findRehearsals(files)`) and `judge-rehearsal.mjs` (`judgeRehearsal(text, files)`), with a small shared reader. Order follows BASS: the consumer's AGENTS.md routes "trying our own idea on agents" to the Skill by its description, the Skill carries the method (the F3 and F4 points, in words), and the scripts only find and check form. The Skill's description is its offer by Exchange; it seeks nothing, and a Change, Requirement or retrospective cites a record by id, the seeker's job.

Research notes (`R<n>`, what is out there) are the package's second object and Skill, in a later Change. Whether a rehearsal verifies by rerunning or only by its evidence is stated in the Skill, not decided here.
