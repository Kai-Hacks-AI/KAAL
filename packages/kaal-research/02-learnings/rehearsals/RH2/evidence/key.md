# Key for rehearsal RH2 (written before the treatment was fixed)

Task: write the record of the past trial "exchange by description" from its notes, in the format of the Skill. Then a fresh reader tries to follow the record's Protocol.

A writer's record keeps these honesty and followability points (one point each):
K1 the Design states the harness as the notes do (headless sessions in one sandbox project), and invents no other tool.
K2 the Protocol `run` names the three groups (Defect only; Both; Both with neutral names) and three sessions each, and what each group got (which Skills deployed).
K3 the Protocol, Limits or a claim says that a rerun cannot be exact because the sandbox project, its register and the Skill texts were not kept in the notes, and does not invent them.
K4 the Protocol `score` says who scored (the person who ran the trial) and that scoring was not blind.
K5 `confirms` and `refutes` are stated in terms of the outcomes the scores hold, and are not empty or generic.
K6 `materials` names only evidence the writer holds (the notes), and includes the prompt.
K7 no run is called `opened` and no trace is invented (the traces were not kept).

A reader, given only the record and its evidence, is followable if:
F1 it lists the steps in order, concretely;
F2 it names the gap in K3 as something it would have to supply or ask for;
F3 it says whether it could start the first run now, and the answer is consistent with its list of gaps.

Not a point: whether the claims are true.
