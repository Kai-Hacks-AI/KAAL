# engineering/C41 Promotion Source as two pure functions Release can call

Release's C159 replaces the Promotion Source workflow with a control that runs through `runControl`, and needs two pure functions from `engineering/github/lineage.ts`: where a head branch comes from, and whether the checks its rulesets require are green on its exact head. Today `promotionSource` does both in one body, and the second part (`notGreen`) is private. This Change splits them without changing any verdict: it exports the origin as `promotionOrigin` and exports `notGreen`, and `promotionSource` is composed from the two. Nothing is read and no behaviour moves, so the shell script and the CLI verb keep their outputs.
