# engineering/C38 the KAAL job that observes the Cases for hold Act

Release's hold Act judge reads Testing's observation of the Cases (`judgeHoldWithEvidence`, release C125; `run-controls-act.mjs hold --evidence <file>`), and nothing here produces that file, so hold Act reports UNKNOWN on every pull request (the observation "is not available"). Testing plans one generic command, `kaal-testing observe-at` (testing C82), that runs a given list of Case files at a revision and writes an observation record. It has no workflow, no path and no lister of Cases. Kai gave the go (26 Nov) for Engineering to plan its part, the KAAL adaptation: where and when the observation runs in this repository, and the path hold Act reads. This Change plans one workflow source and the contract at its edges. It changes no workflow that is installed, no ruleset, no flag; its delivery is a WIRE on Kai's go.
