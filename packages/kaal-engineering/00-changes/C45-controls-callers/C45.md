# engineering/C45 the callers of the controls scripts read through hosting

Controls C160 (#2815) makes the controls Skill library-only: its scripts stop reading files, walking directories, normalising line endings and running Git, and judge what they are given as `{path, text}` entries. Whoever reads must therefore be someone else, and under the ownership cut the consumer adaptation is Engineering's. This Change plans the callers: entry points that read through Hosting (`read`, `list`, `readFile`, `readRevision`) and hand text to the controls functions, and the workflow sources that call them. Controls removes the reading from the Skill only after these callers are on main, so no live check breaks.
