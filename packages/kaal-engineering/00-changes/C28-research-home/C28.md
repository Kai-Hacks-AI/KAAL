# engineering/C28 research and trial homes

Gives research and trials a place in the layout: `research` (what is out there) and `trials` (our own ideas, tested) become siblings of `retros` in `02-learnings`. Only the layout template and its Cases change. The notes and their rules belong to kaal-research and a trial capability, neither of which exists yet. No package is migrated, no existing note moves, and nothing is wired.
