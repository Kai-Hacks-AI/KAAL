# engineering/C28 learnings homes

Gives research, rehearsals and reviews a place in the layout: `research` (what is out there), `rehearsals` (our own ideas, tested) and `reviews` (targeted reviews worth keeping) become siblings of `retros` in `02-learnings`. Only the layout template and its Cases change. The notes and their rules belong to kaal-research and a rehearsal capability, none of which exist yet except kaal-review. No package is migrated, no existing note moves, and nothing is wired.
