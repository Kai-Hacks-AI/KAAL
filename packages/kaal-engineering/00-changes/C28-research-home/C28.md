# engineering/C28 learnings homes

Gives research, rehearsals and reviews a place in the layout: `research` (what is out there), `rehearsals` (our own ideas, tested) and `reviews` (targeted reviews worth keeping) become siblings of `retros` in `02-learnings`. Only the layout template and its Cases change. The notes and their rules belong to kaal-research (two Skills: research and rehearsal) and kaal-review; kaal-research does not exist yet. No package is migrated, no existing note moves, and nothing is wired.
