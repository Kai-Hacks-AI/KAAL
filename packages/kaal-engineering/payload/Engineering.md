# Engineering

Engineering is the capability that explains KAAL's own engineering setup: how one source passes through `packages/<cap>`, `engineering/<cap>`, npmjs and `.kaal/<cap>`, which folders are installed copies of an approved stage, what is waste, and the two legs that move at different pace. It owns the enduring ratchet concept: waste goes down and clean goes up. Its scope is that alone.

It is private to KAAL and not expected to live outside it, so it may use KAAL terminology. It explains only the setup and names a concept another capability owns once, pointing to the owner: WIRE to Release, the Case to Testing, DIRT to Controls and registration to Core. It adds no control, no script and no code.
