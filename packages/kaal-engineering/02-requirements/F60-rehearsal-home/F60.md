# engineering/F60 rehearsal home

A rehearsal is where we test our own idea: we run something and observe what happens. Research looks at what is out there and a rehearsal tries what is ours, so they are two objects with two names, and they are siblings: a rehearsal belongs to the capability whose idea it tries, in that capability's `02-learnings/rehearsals/`, beside `research` and `retros`, with the id `RH<n>`. What was learned by trying steers what is planned next, as a retrospective does. `14-operations` does not hold rehearsals. The rehearsal is the object of a capability that does not exist yet, and so is the rule for what verifies one and what evidence sits beside it; the layout only gives it a place. A package with no `rehearsals` is not judged for it, as for any absent folder.

**Done when** the template names `rehearsals` among the children of `02-learnings`, and the check says PASS for a folder with `02-learnings/rehearsals`, with or without `research`, `retros` and `reviews`, FAIL naming it for any other folder inside `02-learnings`, and FAIL naming it for a `rehearsals` folder inside `14-operations`.
