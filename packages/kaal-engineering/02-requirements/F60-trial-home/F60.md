# engineering/F60 trial home

A trial is where we test our own idea: we run something and observe what happens. Research looks at what is out there and a trial tests what is ours, so they are two objects with two names, and they are siblings: a trial belongs to the capability whose idea it tests, in that capability's `02-learnings/trials/`, beside `research` and `retros`. What was learned by trying steers what is planned next, as a retrospective does. `14-operations` does not hold trials. The trial is the object of a capability that does not exist yet, and so is the rule for what verifies one and what evidence sits beside it; the layout only gives it a place. A package with no `trials` is not judged for it, as for any absent folder.

**Done when** the template names `retros`, `research` and `trials` as the only children of `02-learnings`, and the check says PASS for a folder with `02-learnings/trials`, with or without `research` and `retros`, FAIL naming it for any other folder inside `02-learnings`, and FAIL naming it for a `trials` folder inside `14-operations`.
