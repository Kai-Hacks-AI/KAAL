# engineering/F100 Promotion Source judged in two pure parts

`engineering/github/lineage.ts` exports `promotionOrigin`, the origin of a head branch as a verdict with its delivery stock, and `notGreen`, the reasons a head is not green on its exact commit, and `promotionSource` is composed from them with the same verdicts as before. Both are pure over facts.

**Done when** the two exports exist as described, `promotionSource` gives the same verdicts, and T1580 to T1582 are GREEN.
