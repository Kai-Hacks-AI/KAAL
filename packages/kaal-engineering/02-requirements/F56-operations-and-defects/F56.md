# engineering/F56 operations and defects

The layout template has `14-operations` after `13-source` and `15-defects` last of the numbered concerns, and no `02-controls` and no `14-defects`. `14-operations` holds the controls over the package itself (development, delivery and solution) in `controls`, and may hold `runs` for judged and stamped fallback records where evidence cannot be re-run or re-derived. It holds nothing else, and what verifies it is its `controls`. `runs` is optional and is created only when a record exists.

**Done when** the template names `14-operations` with exactly the children `controls` and `runs`, `runs` optional, scopes the controls to the package for development, delivery and solution, says its `controls` verify it, and names `15-defects`, and no folder named `02-controls` or `14-defects` is left.
