# engineering/F49 controls

`14-operations/controls` holds only controls over the package itself, with respect to its development, its delivery and its solution. Controls are contextual, not shippable: a consumer establishes and owns its controls, their evidence and their operational state. Each control links to the Intent it serves, and through that Intent and its Requirements and Architecture the consumer finds the machinery it uses. The capability ships solution elements and machinery, never controls. A control about how a consumer runs its own repository is consumer space and is not part of the layout.

**Done when** the template scopes the controls of `14-operations` to the package and names the three covered aspects, says the consumer owns controls and links each to an Intent, says the capability ships no control, and lists consumer controls as consumer space and not as a folder.
