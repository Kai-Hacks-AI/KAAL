# engineering/F49 controls

`02-controls` holds only controls over the package itself, with respect to its development, its delivery and its solution. A control about how a consumer runs its own repository is consumer space and is not part of the layout.

**Done when** the template scopes `02-controls` to the package and names the three covered aspects, and lists consumer controls as consumer space and not as a folder.
