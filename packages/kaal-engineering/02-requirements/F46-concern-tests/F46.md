# engineering/F46 concern-tests

The reference says each concern carries its own tests in its own folder: `test-cases/` in `10-intent`, `11-requirements`, `12-architecture` and `14-defects`, and `unit-tests/` in `13-source`. A defect's test-cases are the ones that prove it.
