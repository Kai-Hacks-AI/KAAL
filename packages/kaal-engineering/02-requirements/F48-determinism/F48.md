# engineering/F48 machinery and content

Every entry of the layout is either machinery or content, never both. Machinery is `AGENTS.md`, `13-source` and what is built from it into `dist`, plus the package's root files. Content is `00-backlog` to `14-defects`. Only scripts, unit tests and test-cases are deterministic, the rest is validated by review, and `dist` is generated from `13-source`, never edited and not committed.

**Done when** every entry of the template has exactly one kind, the machinery entries are those named, the deterministic set is the three named, and `dist` is generated, from the source, and not committed.
