# engineering/F59 research home

Research means external references: what is out there, a paper, a tool's documentation, market research. A research note belongs to the capability that asked its question, in that capability's `02-learnings/research/`, beside the retrospectives in `02-learnings/retros/`. What was learned by reading steers what is planned next, as a retrospective does, so it sits in the steering group. `14-operations` does not hold research. The note is kaal-research's object, and so is the rule for what verifies one and for what sits beside it; the layout only gives it a place and leaves what is inside `research` to that capability. A package with no `research` is not judged for it, as for any absent folder. Our own trials of our own ideas are not research; they sit beside it, in `02-learnings/trials` (F60).

**Done when** the template names `retros`, `research` and `trials` as the only children of `02-learnings`, and the check says PASS for a folder with `02-learnings/research`, with or without `retros` and with files or folders inside `research`, FAIL naming it for any other folder inside `02-learnings`, and FAIL naming it for a `research` folder inside `14-operations`.
