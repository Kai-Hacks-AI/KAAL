# engineering/F59 research home

A research note belongs to the capability that asked its question, in that capability's `02-learnings/research/`, beside the retrospectives in `02-learnings/retros/`. What was learned by trying something or by reading a source steers what is planned next, as a retrospective does, so research sits in the steering group and not in Operations. `14-operations` does not hold research. Research has two directions, inbound (what comes from outside) and own (our trials and experiments); both are notes in the same folder, told apart by the note's `kind`, not by a folder. The note is kaal-research's object, and so is the rule for what verifies one and for what evidence sits beside it; the layout only gives it a place, and leaves what is inside `research` to that capability. A package with no `research` is not judged for it, as for any absent folder.

**Done when** the template names `research` and `retros` as the only children of `02-learnings`, and the check says PASS for a folder with `02-learnings/research`, with or without `retros` and with files or folders inside `research`, including a folder per direction, FAIL naming it for any other folder inside `02-learnings`, and FAIL naming it for a `research` folder inside `14-operations`.
