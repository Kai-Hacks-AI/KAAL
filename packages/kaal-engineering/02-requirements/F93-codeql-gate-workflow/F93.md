# engineering/F93 CodeQL gate workflow

The source of the CodeQL workflow declares the configurations it expects and has a job `verify CodeQL` that runs after every `analyze` job, including when one failed, and says in or out of the specification for the merge-result sha. The job reads with `security-events: read` and `contents: read` only. The matrix of `analyze` and the declared expected configurations name the same configurations. The triggers are unchanged. The judge that decides in or out is a control of release, called from the job.

**Done when** `13-source/workflows/codeql.yml` holds the job with `needs: analyze` and a condition that does not skip it when `analyze` fails, with only those two read permissions; and the declared configurations equal the matrix, so a language in one and not the other turns a Case RED.
