# engineering/F93 CodeQL gate workflow

The source of the CodeQL workflow declares the configurations it expects and the two thresholds, and has two jobs, `verify CodeQL analysis` and `verify CodeQL findings` (named after the release controls CTL34 and CTL35), that run after every `analyze` job, including when one failed, and cannot be skipped. Each installs and builds Hosting only (`npm ci --ignore-scripts -w @kaihacksai/kaal-hosting`, then its build) on the trusted checkout, runs its judge on the result of `analyze` and then `judge-run` in preventive mode on the findings file the judge wrote, so a job fails on FAIL and on UNKNOWN. The jobs read with `security-events: read` and `contents: read` only. The matrix of `analyze` and the declared expected configurations name the same configurations. The triggers are unchanged, no `paths` filter narrows them, and no job has an `if` but the not-cancelled form or a `continue-on-error`. The matrix is written as the one-line list `language: [a, b]` and the declaration as one quoted, space-separated string. The thresholds are the legacy rulesets' values, unchanged: security alerts `high_or_higher` and analysis alerts `errors`. The judges are release's controls, called from the jobs.

**Done when** `13-source/workflows/codeql.yml` holds both jobs with `needs: analyze`, a condition that does not skip them, only those two read permissions, the judge step first and `judge-run --mode preventive` second; the declared configurations equal the matrix, so a language in one and not the other turns a Case RED; and the two thresholds are declared as above.

The PLAN (#2346) said one job `verify CodeQL`; release split the judge in two commands and two controls on the CPO's direction (release/C129), so this requirement says two jobs and its Cases (T1393, T1394) were amended with the DO and T1412 added.
