# architecture/N1 abstract-and-small

The Skill is independent of any organization, domain or technology, and its examples come from several domains, software among them. It prescribes no change or release governance. It is one compact Skill, a key and Cases, with no metamodel, no process and no gate. It is not deployed to where agents discover skills and is not published.
