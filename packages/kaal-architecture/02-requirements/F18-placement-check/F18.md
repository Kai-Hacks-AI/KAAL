# architecture/F18 placement-trace

Architecture can trace a placement in two directions, and the check reads that trail inside the observation boundary of architecture/F15.

- **Upwards**, when the implementation follows a faulty specification: `origin: specification` and `tracesTo` lists the chain Source, Architecture, Requirement, Intent as ids (an A-record, a Requirement F or N, an Intent I).
- **Sideways**, when a failure observed in one component is inherited from another: `origin: dependency` and `tracesTo` lists the components as `hard:<capability>` or `soft:<capability>`.

For `source`, `tracesTo` may be empty. An entry that is not well formed, an origin other than the four named, or a missing `fixOwner` with an established origin is INVALID. An id whose namespace is observed and holds no such record is INVALID. An id whose namespace is not observed is UNKNOWN and is never reported as invalid. A Defect with two placements is INVALID. The check is detective, reports and does not block, resolves nothing across packages, and does not judge whether the origin is the right conclusion.
