# architecture/N4 restructure-not-trim

Restructuring moves the content of the Skill and removes none of it: every definition, rule and instruction the Skill gave before is in the router or in a reference after it, with the same meaning. The Test Cases that read the Skill (F1 to F4, N1) read it as an agent does, the router and its references together, and their assertions do not change. The Skill still names no organization, domain or technology and says nothing of transition or delivery of Changes.
