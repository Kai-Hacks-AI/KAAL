# architecture/N4 restructure-not-trim

Restructuring moves the content of the Skill and keeps its meaning: every concern, rule and instruction the Skill gave before is stated in the router or in a reference after it, though not necessarily in the same words. Whether the meaning is kept is a judgement, made by review against a mapping of old to new places (in the DO), and the Test Case guards only that no rule family has gone missing. The Test Cases that read the Skill (F1 to F4, N1) read it as an agent does, the router and its references together, and their assertions about what the Skill says do not change. The Skill still names no organization, domain or technology and says nothing of transition or delivery of Changes.
