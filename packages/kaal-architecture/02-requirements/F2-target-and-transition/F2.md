# architecture/F2 target-and-transition

Separately from its concern, a statement is **target-state**, describing the end state, or a **transition**, describing a movement from one state to another: a before, an until, a stage, a step, a move or a retirement.

Architecture describes the target. A transition statement is flagged as belonging outside target-state Architecture, and the Skill goes no further. It does not say who governs a transition or how, and it does not treat the transition as a third concern of Architecture.
