# architecture/F17 defect-placement

A Defect is held by the capability where it was found, the namespace of its id, until Architecture places it. Its fix owner is that capability unless a placement names another. A placement is an A-record whose frontmatter has `places`, a nonempty list of Defect ids, and `fixOwner`, one capability name, and which addresses a Requirement as every A-record does (architecture/F14). A Defect has at most one placement. Placing a Defect renumbers nothing and rewrites no Defect record: the placement is the explicit, traceable move, and the Defect keeps its id and stays where it was registered.
