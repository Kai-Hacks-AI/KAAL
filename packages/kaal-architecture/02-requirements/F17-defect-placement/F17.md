# architecture/F17 defect-placement

Three concepts are kept apart.

- **Found at** is the observed discovery location: the Defect record, close to the affected source and its connected Test Cases. It is owned by Defect and Testing, and Architecture never moves, renumbers or rewrites it.
- **Origin** is an architectural conclusion established by analysis, not an observation: the failing source itself, a faulty specification, or a dependency the failure is inherited from.
- **Placement** identifies where the correction belongs and follows from the origin.

A placement is an A-record that addresses a Requirement as every A-record does (architecture/F14) and adds `places`, the one Defect it places, `origin` (`source`, `specification`, `dependency` or `unknown`), `tracesTo`, the trail that supports the origin (architecture/F18), and `fixOwner`, one capability name. The placement carries the reasoning; the Defect carries none of it and is not duplicated. From a Defect, its placement is found by a derived reverse lookup over the placement records observed, never by a pointer written into the Defect. A Defect with no placement is held at where it was found, and its fix owner is that capability; that is a default, not a conclusion about its origin. A Defect has at most one placement.
