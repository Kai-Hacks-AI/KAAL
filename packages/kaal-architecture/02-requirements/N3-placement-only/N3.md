# architecture/N3 placement-only

Architecture decides only the origin and the placement of a Defect. It does not register, number, rate, fix, close or change the status of a Defect, does not read or restate Review's finding, and does not restate what Defect or Testing own. It does no fixing, execution, CI or workflow orchestration, adds no Control, no gate and no dependency, and reuses the record mechanics of architecture/F14 to F16.
