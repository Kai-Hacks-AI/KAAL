# architecture/N3 placement-only

Architecture places a correction and nothing before or after it. It does not raise or judge a finding, prove a Defect (Testing does) or take the CARE decision (Contain, Accept, Ratchet, Eliminate). It records no Born and no Fixed. It does not register, rate, fix, close or change the status of a Defect, and does no fixing, execution, CI or workflow orchestration. It assumes no one-to-one mapping between a Defect and a correction. It reuses the existing `addresses` model: no special placement field, no new library, no Control, no gate and no dependency.
