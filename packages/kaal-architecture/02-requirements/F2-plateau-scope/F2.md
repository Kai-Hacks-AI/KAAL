# architecture/F2 plateau-scope

Architecture describes the target state of a plateau, whether final or intermediate. How to travel between plateaus, including whether several independently manageable Changes are needed, and how completed Changes are assembled and delivered, belong to other capabilities. The Skill neither depends on their Skills or internals nor classifies or governs them.
