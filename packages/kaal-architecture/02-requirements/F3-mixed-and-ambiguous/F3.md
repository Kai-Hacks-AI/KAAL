# architecture/F3 mixed-and-ambiguous

A statement that holds both Solution and Delivery is split into its parts, and is not classified by its dominant part. A term that can mean either concern is named as ambiguous and put to its author, never guessed. A statement written as one concern that in fact fixes the other, such as a means that limits what the solution offers, is reclassified by what it fixes. Each is raised before design proceeds.
