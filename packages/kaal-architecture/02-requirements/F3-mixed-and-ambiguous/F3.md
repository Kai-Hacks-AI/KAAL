# architecture/F3 mixed-and-ambiguous

A statement that holds both Solution and Delivery is split into its parts, and is not classified by its dominant part. A constraint belongs to the concern it actually constrains, even when it influences the other: a Delivery constraint that limits what the solution can offer stays Delivery, and a Solution constraint that restricts the choice of means stays Solution. If a limitation is independently accepted as a Solution requirement, that distinct constraint is stated explicitly. A term that can mean either concern, or a statement whose context or authority is insufficient to tell, is named and put to its author, never forced. Each is raised before design proceeds.
