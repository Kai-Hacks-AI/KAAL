# architecture/F18 correction-placement

The Architecture record that addresses a Defect is the functional placement of its correction. It is not a diagnosis and not an automatic assignment to where the Defect was found or where it began: discovery and origin do not dictate where the correction goes. The record chooses over both architectures. Delivery architecture decides which layer should change. Solution architecture decides which capability or component, or which existing or new third-party dependency, provides the correction. From a Defect, the records that address it are found by a derived reverse lookup over the records observed; the Defect carries no pointer and is not rewritten. A Defect that no observed record addresses is unplaced: no place is assumed for it. Whether the choice is the right one is for review, not for this check.
