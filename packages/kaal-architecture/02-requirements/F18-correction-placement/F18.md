# architecture/F18 correction-placement

Two dimensions, each held by its own objects, with no required order between them. **The problem** is the Defect. **Found** is its manifestation, where it showed up, for example in operations: external input raised by Review, not a KAAL artifact. **Born** is its origin, the level on the V where it started, from intent, requirements, architecture, code, test or operations: a validated finding yields a Defect and the Test Case that proves it, and that record and Case, owned by Defect and Testing, establish it, independently of CARE. **The correction** is the solution. **Placed** is the architecture component or building block, a KAAL component or an external one, where a correction belongs: the Architecture record that addresses the Defect. **Fixed** is the delivery, a hotfix or a normal Change, that makes the correction. Fixed is not inevitable and placement is not authorization to fix: CARE decides, and Architecture records neither the decision nor the delivery.

The placing record is informed by Born and is not an assignment to where the problem was found. If the Defect was born in code, the architecture stands and the code is corrected to it. If it was born in architecture, the placing record is itself re-judged and changed, and the code is then judged against it again. If it was born in requirements or intent, the correction starts there and the placement follows. Architecture records no Born and no Fixed.

A Defect and its corrections are many to many. One Defect may be addressed by several records, because it may need several corrections, and one record may address several Defects. From a Defect, the records that address it are found by a derived reverse lookup over the records observed, in the order handed in; the Defect carries no pointer and is not rewritten. A Defect that no observed record addresses is unplaced: no place is assumed for it. Whether the placement is the right one is for review, not for this check.
