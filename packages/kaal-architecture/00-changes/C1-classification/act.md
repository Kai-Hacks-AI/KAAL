# C1 act

A 4L retrospective of the Change, from the viewpoint of the agent that carried it. It does not close C1: the T3 decision below is open. Promotion and publication are not claimed here.

## Liked

The CPO reviews that cut scope. The first handover reached for Change and Release management as well; the reviews narrowed the Skill to two concerns, Solution and Delivery, and to the target state of a plateau, and the Skill became small enough to read in one sitting. Writing the key before the Skill, and keeping runs 1 and 2 on the record next to run 3, meant the evidence could be read rather than trusted.

## Learned

A categorical rule can sound sharp and be wrong. "A means that limits what the solution offers is a Solution statement" misplaced a constraint; the correct rule keeps a constraint with the concern it constrains and asks where authority is missing. A key is an author's judgment, not an oracle: S10 was corrected after an answer disagreed, and S4 was revised after review, and both are disclosed. A match score says little about usefulness; the baseline differed from the Skill only on S4, and run 3 shows the Skill-only agent also did not ask.

## Lacked

An independent seat. The author wrote the Skill, the key and the prompt; the observer was a fresh subagent session, not a separate team. More than one run per arm, and different agents, so that a disagreement can be told from noise. A way to state, in a Case, that a judgment capability is useful without a key match.

## Longed For

An independent Review of this trial that challenges the S4 key and the baseline comparison and states what it could not verify. Several runs per arm. A place on main where a defensible disagreement is recorded as such, not as a failed Case.

## Open

Not closed. Gaps G1 and G2 are covered by Cases T7 and T8, but T3 is RED on S4: the Skill-only agent found none where the key says ambiguous. That is the observed result and the recorded limitation (`do.md`, run 3). How to resolve it, and whether the Change may close with it, is a pending CPO decision. This ACT does not claim acceptance or closure. An independent review may inform that decision and cannot substitute for it.
