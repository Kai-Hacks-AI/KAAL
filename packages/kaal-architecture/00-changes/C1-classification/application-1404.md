## 2. Terms

- "Boundary" and "place" name the same four stations in F5, F6 and `--place`.
- "Placement" in F5 and G17 means where the control applies; in Architecture it is the relationship from a requirement to an element.
- "Change Isolation" names the control, the judge, the check and the job.

## 3. Ownership, as a question

F4, F6, A18 and the attestation rule look like Solution. The preventive rule, rulesets and queue are `guard Integration`'s already. Claim wording is Controls'. The adapters, workflows and hook are Delivery. Whether the work is cut accordingly, and how, is for the owners of the Change Isolation Change.
