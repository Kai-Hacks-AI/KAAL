# architecture/C18 skill-router

Restructures the Skill of Architecture into a router and references, so that it meets the Skill standard of Core (core/C52) without losing a rule. The Owner gave the go to prepare this PLAN on 25 Nov; it is a separate Change from the placement of Defects.

## Baseline

`skills/kaal-architecture/SKILL.md` on `main` is one file, 545 words after the frontmatter (598 with it) and 52 lines. Core's draft counts the body only, excludes the frontmatter, and warns softly above 200 words, medium above 400 and hard, blocking, above 600. The Skill is therefore at the medium level, 55 words under the block, with no `references/` folder. Five Test Cases read the Skill: T14 (F2), T15 (N1), T16 (A2), T17 (A1), T18 to T20 (F1, F3, F4); each reads `SKILL.md` alone.

## The shape of the DO

- `SKILL.md` becomes a router that targets 200 body words: what Architecture describes (the target state of a plateau), the test that tells Solution from Delivery in one line, the report in one line, and one line for each reference naming it by its relative path.
- Four references hold the detail, each moved from the present Skill and keeping its meaning: `references/concerns.md` (the two concerns and the test), `references/unclean.md` (mixed, constraint across the line, ambiguous), `references/report.md` (what to report), `references/scope.md` (scope and rules).
- The Test Cases T14, T18, T19 and T20 read the Skill as an agent does, the router and its references together; what they assert about the Skill does not change, though a phrase they match may sit in a reference. T15 keeps its word ban, applied to the whole Skill, and drops its 120-line limit; A2 says router and references instead of "at most 120 lines". Line counts and the layout are Core's standard, not Architecture's.
- Nothing is removed. The DO carries a mapping of each section of the present Skill to its new place, for the reviewer to judge that the meaning is kept. T32 only guards that no family of rule is missing.

## PLAN

Adds I4, F19, F20 and N4, and the Test Cases T30 to T32. T30 (router size) and T31 (references) are red now: the body has 545 words and there is no references folder. They are `.red.mjs` in this Change and move beside their Requirement in the DO. T32 is green now and stays green: it checks that eleven families of rule are still stated somewhere in the Skill, by their concepts and not by their sentences.

## Not decided here

- **Measure.** Core's check (`kaal skill check`, core/C52 DO) is not merged. T30 counts words itself, body only, by whitespace, as Core's draft does, because the package takes no dependency (architecture/N2). The 200 words are this Change's target, not a standard of Architecture's own. When Core's check exists it decides, and the DO or a later Change runs it.
- **The Where chapter.** Controls asked whether Architecture can be the last chapter of a generated Skill reference. That needs a `parent` on A-records and a reader paragraph convention, and is a later Change. This one only leaves the router room for it: 200 words, with the references named one per line.

## What this Change does not do

It changes no rule, definition or example of the Skill, adds no record mechanics, no Control and no gate, deploys and publishes nothing (the Skill stays private and out of `.agents/skills`), and changes no other capability's Skill.
