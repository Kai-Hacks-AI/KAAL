# C2 act

A 4L retrospective of the Change, from the viewpoint of the agent that carried it. It does not close C2 in the sense of validating the Skill: the Review and the Owner decide that. Promotion and publication are not claimed here.

## Liked

Freezing the requests by hash before the run, and showing them to the CPO first. The CPO could change them, and I could not tune them afterwards. Having no key at all made the Cases simple: they check that the evidence is whole and say nothing about whether an answer is good.

## Learned

Kai's two notes changed the trial before it ran. The architecture description is not the solution or delivery it describes, and a boundary reads better said positively, as where the work starts and ends. I only noticed the first because the earlier scenarios had no statement about a description. Reading the output, I could see the Skill arm raised more targeted questions about whether a limit is an accepted Solution requirement, and the baseline also reasoned well. I cannot say from one run per arm that either is better.

## Lacked

More than one run per arm, and an observer who did not write the Skill. A way to hand an agent its prompt that is both byte-exact and free of any tool: I read it from a file, with one Read call, which the agent could in principle have exceeded.

## Longed For

The independent Review's assessment of judgment and of the line with Change, before anyone relies on the Skill. A positive statement of the boundary in the Skill itself, instead of the partly negative Scope section, if the Review and the Owner want it. Several runs per arm, with different agents.

## Open

Not validated. The original T3 stays RED against unchanged run 3. T10 and T11 are green as evidence-integrity Cases only. Whether the Skill adds value over the baseline, whether its classifications are useful, and whether it stays out of planning or executing a move between states are for the independent Review and the Owner. This ACT claims no acceptance and no closure.
