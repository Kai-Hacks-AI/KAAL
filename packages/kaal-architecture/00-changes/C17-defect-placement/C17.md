# architecture/C17 defect-placement

Gives Architecture the placement of a Defect: where a Defect is to be fixed, which sets the fix owner. Review has the finding, Defect and Testing have the mechanics, Architecture has the functional place. Where a Defect is found carries it until Architecture moves it.

## Baseline

`kaal/architecture/classify` holds the record mechanics (F14 to F16, `lib/records.mjs`). A Defect today is a record of the capability that found it, for example `hosting/D14`, raised from a finding, with an Owner. Nothing states that the place to fix it may be elsewhere, and nothing carries a move.

## PLAN

Adds I3, F17, F18 and N3, and the Cases T27 to T30. The Cases are red. They wait for the DO: `lib/placement.mjs` with a reader of a placement, the fix owner and the check, reusing `lib/records.mjs`.

## Open questions for the CPO

1. A placement is an A-record with `places` and `fixOwner` that lives, like every A-record, in the `03-architecture` folder of the capability it places the fix in. The alternative is to keep placements in Architecture's own package.
2. Does the found-in capability learn of a placement through the record only, or does its Defect record gain a pointer? This PLAN adds no pointer and changes no Defect record.
3. Who acts on a placement, and when a fix owner declines it, are not decided here.

## What this Change does not do

It changes no Defect record in any package, registers no Defect, introduces no Control and no gate, and resolves nothing across packages.
