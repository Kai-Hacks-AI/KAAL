# architecture/C17 defect-placement

Gives Architecture the placement of a Defect: where its correction belongs, from an origin it can trace. Review has the finding, Defect and Testing have the mechanics, Architecture has the functional place. A Defect is held where it was found until Architecture places it, and placing it never rewrites the discovery.

## Baseline

`kaal/architecture/classify` holds the record mechanics (F14 to F16, `lib/records.mjs`). A Defect today is a record of the capability that found it, for example `hosting/D14`, with its Test Cases. Nothing separates where a Defect is found from where it originates, nothing traces that origin through the specification or the dependencies, and nothing carries the conclusion.

## PLAN

Adds I3, F17 to F19 and N3, and the Cases T27 to T30. The Cases are red. They wait for the DO: `lib/placement.mjs` with a reader of a placement, a derived lookup from a Defect, and the check, reusing `lib/records.mjs`.

## Three concepts

| Concept | What it is | Owned by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Found at | the observed discovery location: the Defect record, close to the source and its Test Cases | Defect and Testing, unchanged |
| Origin | an architectural conclusion from analysis: `source`, `specification`, `dependency` or `unknown` | Architecture |
| Placement | where the correction belongs, following from the origin, with its trail | Architecture |

## How the record captures the reasoning and is found from the Defect

The placement is an A-record. Its `origin` and `tracesTo` are the reasoning: upwards through Source, Architecture, Requirement and Intent for a faulty specification, sideways through `hard:` and `soft:` dependencies for an inherited failure. It names the Defect in `places`. The Defect record is untouched, so nothing is duplicated and the original discovery, id and test evidence are preserved. From the Defect, the placement is found by a derived reverse lookup over the placement records observed. An origin that is not established is UNKNOWN and names no destination.

## Open questions for the CPO

1. A placement lives, like every A-record, in the `03-architecture` folder of the capability its correction is placed in, so it can be found only inside the observation boundary. The alternative is to keep placements in Architecture's own package.
2. `tracesTo` entries are ids and `hard:`/`soft:` capability names. Whether Architecture should read the dependency strength from the capabilities' own declarations is left for later.
3. Who acts on a placement, and what happens when a fix owner declines it, are not decided here.

## What this Change does not do

It changes no Defect record in any package, registers no Defect, does no fixing, execution, CI or workflow orchestration, adds no Control and no gate, and resolves nothing across packages.
