# architecture/C17 defect-placement

Lets Architecture do for a Defect what it already does for a Requirement: address it, so that the record is the functional placement of a correction.

## Baseline

`main` holds the record mechanics (F14 to F16, `lib/records.mjs`): an A-record's `addresses` may name only a Requirement, an F or an N. A Defect today is a record of the capability that found it, for example `hosting/D14`, with its Test Cases.

## Two dimensions, not one lifecycle

| Dimension | Stage | Object | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Problem** | **Found**: its manifestation, where it showed up | the finding, external input from a human, an agent or a control | raised by Review; not a KAAL artifact |
| **Problem** | **Born**: its origin, the level where it started (intent, requirements, architecture, code, test, operations) | the Defect, verified by a Test Case with reproducible evidence; a validated finding stays a Finding until then | Defect and Testing |
| **Correction** | **Placed**: the architecture component, KAAL or external, where a correction belongs | an A-record that `addresses` the Defect | Architecture |
| **Correction** | **Fixed**: the delivery, a hotfix or a normal Change | the Change, with whatever else is needed | Change |

The two dimensions are not a mandatory four-stage sequence. CARE treats a verified Defect, not a finding, and is the decision boundary between them: Contain, Accept, Ratchet or Eliminate (by hotfix or a normal Change). Fixed is not inevitable, and placement is not authorization to fix. A Defect and its corrections are many to many: one Defect may need several corrections, and one correction may address several Defects. A Defect that is not accepted is not a different kind of Defect: acceptance is a CARE response to it, not a state of it, and the term "accepted Defect" is not used.

Born informs Placed. Born in code: the architecture stands and the code is corrected to it. Born in architecture: the placing record is re-judged and changed, then the code is judged against it again. Born in requirements or intent: the correction starts there and the placement follows. The model is the same in a hotfix, which can carry PLAN and DO in one pull request, and in a collector, which can split them across pull requests.

## PLAN

Adds I3, F17, F18 and N3, and the Cases T27 to T29. F17 widens what `addresses` may name to a Defect. F18 states the two dimensions, that the addressing record is the placement, that CARE decides what is done, and that Defects and corrections are many to many, and that a Defect addressed by none is unplaced. The special `places`, `origin` and `fixOwner` fields and `placement.mjs` of the earlier head are dropped. The Cases are red: T27 fails because `checkAddresses` rejects a Defect id, T28 and T29 because `addressing` is not yet exported. The DO changes `lib/records.mjs` only: accept a Defect id in `addresses`, and add `addressing(id, records)`, the derived reverse lookup, which returns every record that addresses the Defect. No new mechanism is added by this revision.

## Open questions for the CPO

None open. Where Born is recorded is settled: it is the Defect and its Test Case (defect/C1, #2113). This Change reads no Born and no CARE decision and checks no consistency between Born, Placed and the Change that fixes it.

## What this Change does not do

It changes no Defect record in any package, raises, judges or proves no finding, takes no CARE decision, does no fixing, execution, CI or workflow orchestration, adds no Control and no gate, and resolves nothing across packages.
