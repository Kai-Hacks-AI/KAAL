# Scope and rules

## Scope

Architecture describes the target state of a plateau, whether final or intermediate. It prescribes no notation, tool or procedure, and it stays within the target state of one plateau. How to travel from one plateau to the next, and how finished work is brought together and delivered, belong to other capabilities. Do not classify, plan or govern them here, and do not depend on how they work. If a statement is only about such travel, say that it is outside the target state and leave it there.

## Rules

- Classify by the concern each part constrains, not by who wrote it or how firmly.
- Never guess an ambiguous term.
- Do not invent a third concern. A statement is Solution, Delivery or both, and a statement that fits neither is flagged, not forced. Where context or authority is insufficient, ask rather than force a classification.
- Keep examples and wording of your own report free of any one organization, domain or technology.
