# architecture/I3 defect-placement

A problem and its correction are two dimensions, not one lifecycle. The problem is a Defect: it is found where it shows up and born where it originated. The correction is a solution: it is placed at an architecture component and fixed by delivery. Architecture places a correction the way it places the solution of a Requirement: by addressing the Defect, not by assuming the correction belongs where the problem was found. Placing a correction is not a decision to make it: whether it is made is CARE's (Contain, Accept, Ratchet, Eliminate), and Fixed is not inevitable. A Defect and its corrections are many to many. Owner: Architecture.
