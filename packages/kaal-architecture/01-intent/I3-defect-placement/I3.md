# architecture/I3 defect-placement

The correction of an accepted Defect has a functional place, and Architecture chooses it the way it places the solution of a Requirement: by addressing the Defect, not by assuming it belongs where it was found or where it began. A finding is external input; Testing provides proof and Review judges whether the problem is valid and where it lies. Only an accepted Defect becomes a subject of KAAL. Owner: Architecture.
