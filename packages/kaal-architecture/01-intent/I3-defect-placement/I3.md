# architecture/I3 defect-placement

A Defect is found in one place and may belong to be fixed in another. Architecture states the functional place a Defect is to be fixed, which names its fix owner, so that the finding, the registration and the fix stay separate and a Defect is not fixed by whoever happened to find it. Review raises the finding; Defect and Testing hold the mechanics of the Defect and its Test Cases. Owner: Architecture.
