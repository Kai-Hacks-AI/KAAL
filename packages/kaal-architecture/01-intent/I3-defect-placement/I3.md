# architecture/I3 defect-placement

A problem is found first, born second, placed third and fixed where it is placed. Architecture places the correction of an accepted Defect the way it places the solution of a Requirement: by addressing the Defect, not by assuming it belongs where it was found. A finding is external input. The Defect, with the Test Case that proves it, establishes where the problem was born, and only an accepted Defect becomes a subject of KAAL. Owner: Architecture.
