# architecture/I3 defect-placement

A Defect is found in one place and may belong to be corrected in another. Architecture establishes from analysis where a Defect originates and so where its correction belongs, as a relationship that can be traced, so that the discovery, the origin and the placement stay three things, the discovery record and its evidence are preserved, and no destination is guessed. Review raises the finding; Defect and Testing hold the mechanics of the Defect record and its Test Cases. Owner: Architecture.
