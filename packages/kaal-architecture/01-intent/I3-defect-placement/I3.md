# architecture/I3 defect-placement

A Defect is found first, born second, placed third and fixed where it is placed. Architecture places the correction of an accepted Defect the way it places the solution of a Requirement: by addressing the Defect, informed by where Review judged it was born, not by assuming it belongs where it was found. A finding is external input; Testing provides proof and Review judges whether the problem is valid and where it was born. Only an accepted Defect becomes a subject of KAAL. Owner: Architecture.
