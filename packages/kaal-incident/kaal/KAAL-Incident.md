# KAAL Incident

KAAL Incident is the capability by which a KAAL client deliberately addresses to KAAL an account of something that involved the client's embedded KAAL and should not have happened, or in which behaviour the client expected of KAAL failed. The account is a carrier, kept locally with the embedded KAAL so that it remains available for later collection by KAAL. Its scope is the creation and local carriage of that carrier alone.

A carrier is addressed to KAAL by the fact that it was made with this capability. It is not the client's own incident, and the capability neither replaces nor governs whatever the client keeps for its own incidents, defects, backlog, ideas or requests; the client remains responsible for telling what it keeps for itself from what it addresses to KAAL. A carrier has two parts, each a different kind of statement: what Happened, as the client observed it, and what the client Expected of KAAL instead.

KAAL Incident is optional. It is not Core, a valid embedding does not need it, and embedding KAAL does not install it. It does not decide whether, how or when a carrier leaves the client, is collected, judged or answered: transport, submission and collection are other problems.
