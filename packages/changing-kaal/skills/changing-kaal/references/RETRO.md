# Reference: RETRO.md

`RETRO.md` is the one artifact that closes a change. Its form is exactly this, and nothing else:

```
# Retro

## Learned

...

## Liked

...

## Lacked

...

## Longed

...
```

No metadata, scores, action items, owners or summary.

## Whose retro it is

It is written from the participating agent's own seat, and reports the agent's own experience of performing the change. It does not infer what the user thought, speak for reviewers or a team, invent anyone's sentiment, or say what the agent expects to be welcome. Where the agent has nothing honest to say for a dimension, it says so plainly rather than filling the space.

## The four dimensions

They are distinct in meaning.

- **Learned**: knowledge or understanding gained through doing the change that was not known or clear at the beginning.
- **Liked**: something that worked well, from the agent's perspective, and is worth preserving.
- **Lacked**: something absent during the change whose absence made the work harder, weaker, slower or less certain.
- **Longed**: a desired future possibility or improvement, from the agent's perspective. It is not a restatement of something Lacked.

Do not take one observation and write it four ways. If an observation could fit several dimensions, put it in the one that is its primary meaning and find different observations for the others. Each dimension holds observations the others do not.
