# Service

Service is the responsibility for what a Service is in KAAL: a Service's identity as an offering, the one Capability accountable for providing it, and what consumes it. Its responsibility ends there. Relating and navigating Nodes is Graph's, what the four CASE kinds are is Core's, and carrying out a Service belongs to whoever delivers it.
