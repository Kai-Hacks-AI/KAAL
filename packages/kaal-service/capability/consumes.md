# consumes

`consumes` relates a Node of any CASE kind, a Capability, an Agent, a Skill or an Extension, to a Service it depends on. The consumer's Node carries it, as an Edge typed by this Definition.

Consuming a Service needs nothing from its provider, and makes the consumer accountable for nothing in it.
