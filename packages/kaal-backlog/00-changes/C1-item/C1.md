# backlog/C1 item

Introduces the backlog item: a captured wish that nobody has committed to yet and that may stay undelivered as long as its owner wants. This first slice states what a valid item is and gives a pure `check` that accepts or refuses a set of items. It follows the shape of `kaal-controls`: the package is private, outside the workspaces, and cites nothing that is not on `main`.

## Baseline

`main` has no backlog capability. Backlog-like items are scattered (`engineering/follow-ups.md`, open lines in project memory). `packages/kaal-change` holds Intent, Requirements and Architecture for Change; it has no place for a wish that is not yet an Intent.
