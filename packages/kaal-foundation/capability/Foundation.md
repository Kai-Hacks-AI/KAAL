# Foundation

Foundation is the responsibility for the shared, pure and deterministic primitives any KAAL Capability can build on without a dependency cycle. It is a sibling of Hosting and depends on nothing.

Foundation is also a deliberate collector for shared concerns whose eventual specialized home is not yet known. That ownership is explicit and provisional: each concern is reviewed in Foundation's README and moves by a reviewed Change once its specialized home is known.
