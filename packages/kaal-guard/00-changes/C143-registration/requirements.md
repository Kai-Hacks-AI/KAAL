# C143 requirements added (proposed; identifiers to be confirmed by the CPO)

- **guard/F43 Registration.** A capability or a Change registers a check by a declaration in its own place, `.kaal/guard/registered/<capability>/<name>.md`, a `Guard/Check` with the member `capability`. The unchanged hook invocation and the unchanged CI job run it beside the hard-wired checks. Registering touches neither `.githooks` nor CI. (T209)
- **guard/F44 A candidate adds only for itself.** A pull request may add registrations. A registration the base holds that the candidate deletes, renames, or whose command, facts, outcomes or `capability` it changes is cannot judge, naming it, and the candidate's version never runs. Removal or weakening arrives only from `main`. (T210)
- **guard/F45 In flight.** A registration may declare `depends`. The checks that run are those of the capabilities whose paths changed and of the capabilities that depend on them, transitively. `--full` runs every registration and is what the gate (merge queue into `main`, or a periodic run) uses. Hard-wired checks are never selected away. (T211)
- **guard/F46 No opt-out.** A registration cannot replace, shadow or switch off a check declared in `.kaal/config/guard/`; one named like it is cannot judge. (T212)
- **guard/A21 Registration and selection.** Discovery of registrations, the base comparison of F44 and the selection of F45 sit in the runner's declaration reading (guard/A1, guard/A3); the adapters stay unchanged. The shape of a capability (ownership, definition, contract) follows Core's (T213).
