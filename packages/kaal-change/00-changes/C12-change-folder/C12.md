# change/C12 change-folder

Makes a Change folder change-oriented and its closure derivable: the Change's Cases are hot only during the Change. Its registration with Guard is one passive file in its own folder, created at PLAN and never deleted; the check derives the state (open, closing, closed) and ratchets the count of RED Cases (PLAN same or up, DO same or down, ACT only at all GREEN, UNKNOWN never a pass), and a closed Change is cold. Closure is `act.md`. A derived folder control judges the orientation of artifacts (the change kinds are C and G; a Defect is enduring), and a derived closing control judges the continuity of effective verification of the enduring objects the Change touched, from the execution evidence a run already produced, not by particular Case files. Kai's decision of 18 Nov 2026: a Case in a Change folder that protects something enduring is a test-design defect, and kaal-change owns the change-oriented setup. A first slice: the controls are detective, nothing is wired into a hook, workflow or ruleset, nothing is published.

## Baseline

`main` holds `packages/kaal-change` with ids to `change/C10`, `I24`, `F52`, `A25`, `N14`, `T67`. The Change Record Definition (`capability/Change-Record.md`) says nothing about when a record is closed or what a Change folder may hold. Guard (collector `kaal/guard/registration`, C143/C144) discovers a `kaal-guard.json` per package and per open Change folder and says a closed Change's registration ends; it does not say how closure is known. On `main`, 56 of 56 Change folders hold `act.md`, and 15 Cases in 5 release-management folders (C26, C27, C56, C66, and C67 with Defect D1's four Cases and its document) test an enduring object from inside the folder; a Defect is an enduring object (investigation: `guard/lifecycle-managed-registration/`).
