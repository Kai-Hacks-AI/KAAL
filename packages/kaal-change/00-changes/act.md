# kaal-change collector act

The one concluding 4L retrospective of the `kaal/change` collector as a whole, from the viewpoint of the agent that carried it. The collector holds one Change, change/C10, whose own retrospective is `C10-transition/act.md`; this record looks at the collected work and its delivery, not only at that Change.

## Liked

The collector stayed one Change, one Skill and one package. After the owner said there is one `kaal-change` Skill, every later decision had a place to go: the guide is a reference of that Skill, the two reasoning concerns (Transition and Isolation) each own their findings and output, and the isolation worksheet is conditional on Isolation. The review loop also worked as a control: each CPO round, on the PLAN, the amendment, the DO and the orchestration, narrowed the work instead of widening it, and every approval named an exact head.

## Learned

Delivery and qualification are different things from integration. Integration checks (CI, Workspaces, Acceptance, inventory digests) passed several times while the open questions were about meaning: where the guide belongs, whether the worksheet is generic, whether the trial shows benefit. Adding a guide to an existing Skill touches its shipped inventory, digests and Cases, so placement errors were found by checks, not by reading. A trial that binds its prompt to the Skill as shipped must be re-run for every wording edit, and prior runs must be kept as history. Eight new runs on two other prompts scored the same in both arms, which shows no regression and cannot show a benefit. The collector's own planned RED Cases from earlier Changes (FAR, release, scope) remain red on main too; they are not this collector's and were not touched.

## Lacked

A statement, before the first PLAN, of the shape of the whole collector: one Skill, two concerns, the worksheet belongs to Isolation. It was reached in steps and each step cost a PR. A way to run the package's own acceptance tests in the agent's container without a built Hosting package. A reviewer who is the Owner for the trial: the key, the observer and the author share a model family, so the labels cannot be called settled.

## Longed For

Disposition of S7 by the Owner. The key says the statement is transition; three blind observers read it as closer to mixed. Nothing in the collector depends on that label, the key is unchanged, and the disagreement stays recorded in `C10-transition/trial-report.md`. A harder example, several runs per arm and a consuming agent that selects the Skill the way a host does, would turn "partly useful" into a result. Until then the collector claims only that the guide improves the handover reasoning on the example and does not worsen isolation or protection in the runs made.
