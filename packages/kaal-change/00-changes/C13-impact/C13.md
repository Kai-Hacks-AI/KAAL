# change/C13 impact

Makes two ideas explicit in the `kaal-change` Skill: the impact of a Change, and how its result reaches the people and work it affects. Both are questions the agent asks and answers; neither is a lifecycle, a template field or a check. The wording is generic: it must work for a consumer that knows nothing about KAAL and may have no repository.

## Baseline

`references/transition.md` asks five questions about going from A to B. It covers what is replaced and what stays protected. It does not ask what becomes different for others, who already depends on what changes, or how the result reaches them. The isolation reference derives `affectedOutside`, a structural reading of potential impact from traces. A behavioural impact, such as something once permitted that is now refused, and the way a result is adopted, appear only as prose in individual Changes and as lessons learned afterwards.

## PLAN

Adds I30, F57, F58, N16, A37 and the Cases T1129 to T1134, all RED. Adds the draft wording `impact.md`: one new section, **Impact and adoption**, for `references/transition.md`, and one sentence in its Output chapter. It leaves the five questions and the existing Cases unchanged.

- **F57.** The guide asks what becomes different (including what was permitted and is now refused), who is affected and how many, what could go wrong, and whether the Change's own delivery passes under the rule it changes. Each claim is marked stated, observed or inferred. For a small Change one line is enough.
- **F58.** The guide asks how the result reaches those affected, naming availability, integration and activation, which may coincide or be kept apart. It prescribes no number or order of steps.
- **N16.** The section is KAAL-agnostic: no term of KAAL's own delivery, no assumption of a repository.
- **A37.** The section sits in the Transition concern of the Skill and says how it relates to the isolation concern's `affectedOutside`.
