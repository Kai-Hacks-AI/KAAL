# change/C14 declare pr types

Kai ruled on 26 November 2026 that kaal-change owns the Change folder and its PDCA, and with it the chapters and content of the four request types that carry a Change: PLAN, DO, ACT and HOTFIX. Hosting (hosting/C113) owns the format of a declaration and renders the templates and bodies from it. Release's C138 DO waits on this declaration. This PLAN makes kaal-change declare the four types in Hosting's form. It starts no implementation, installs nothing and touches no delivery file.

## Baseline

The four templates exist only as hand-written text under `.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE/` (and as Release's sources under `engineering/release-management/05-src/wiring/templates/`; `plan` and `do` have none). No capability declares them as data. Their text holds host words: a "pull request" noun and "comments". HOTFIX also opens with a visible line, `**Change envelope.** The evidence is in the repository files of the Change(s) named below, at the revision under review.`, which sits outside the comment. Release owns both the text and the installation today.

## PLAN

Adds I31, F59, F60, N17 and the Cases T1553 to T1556, parked RED (`.red.mjs`) and turned into delivered `.test.mjs` Cases by the DO with their expectations unchanged. Adds the draft `declaration.json` in this folder: the four types in the form of hosting/C113 (`type`, `intro`, `chapters` of `{ name, guidance }`, `raise`). The DO delivers it unchanged at `packages/kaal-change/13-source/pull-request-types/kaal-change.json`, the path hosting/C113 names.

- **F59.** The declaration names exactly PLAN, DO, ACT and HOTFIX and, for each, the chapters of today's template in the same order (PLAN: Change, From, To, Because. DO: Change, Implement, Refactor. ACT: Change, Records. HOTFIX: Change, Defect, Urgency, Approach), each with its guidance.
- **F60.** The `raise` rules are the owner's: no Change id in a title, a declared draft flag, and squash auto-merge for PLAN, DO and ACT. No head or base: routes stay in Engineering's C37 as route eligibility, and the declaration holds chapters and fixed text only.
- **N17.** The declaration is KAAL data only: no host word, no file name, no comment syntax, no recognition rule. Where the templates say "pull request" or "comments" the declaration says "this body" or "outside this body".

The chapters, their order and the guidance are today's templates, with the host words taken out. This Change does not change what a type asks for. The one exception is the HOTFIX envelope line, which is kept and waits on Hosting's format (question 2).

## Questions

1. **Path in a legacy package.** Settled by Engineering on this PLAN: `13-source/pull-request-types/kaal-change.json` is the right path, the first target-layout folder of the package and not a migration.
2. **HOTFIX envelope line, kept.** The line is visible to author and reviewer today. The form's `intro` is rendered as a comment, so declaring it there would hide it. The CTO ruled that the envelope stays visible and that Hosting's declaration format gains a way to declare visible text; Hosting plans that. This draft leaves the line out until the format has it, and does not claim it is preserved. The DO delivers PLAN, DO and ACT, and is gated for HOTFIX on that format: it adds the line, unchanged, as visible text once Hosting's format can hold it. Removing the line would need an explicit Owner decision, which is not asked here.
3. **Routes.** Settled by Engineering on this PLAN: head and base patterns stay in C37 as route eligibility, so no type here declares a head or a base.
4. **Format not merged.** hosting/C113 is a merged PLAN; its DO (#2613) is open. The Cases here read the declaration as plain JSON and do not call Hosting's validator. When the validator merges, the DO adds one Case that passes the file through it.
