## Delivery evidence

The DO records the exact reused source/dependency closure, architecture findings
and exclusions, named mutation results, public-consumer evidence and current-head
repository CI. Where the product is host-independent, it still receives the
existing three-host acceptance required by the repository. Existing assertions
are preserved; skipped, blocked and unexecuted Cases are never passed.

Independent validation reviews the ownership transfer from Engineering, the
minimum Change foundation, the semantics of scope versus impact, the completeness
boundary and the double-versus-real-provider example. Review and CI are independent
integration obligations. Promotion retains full cross-capability assurance.
