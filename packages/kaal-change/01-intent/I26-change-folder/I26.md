# change/I26 change-folder

A Change gets from the current state to the target state, and its folder holds only what is about that move: its Gap, its baseline, its change-oriented Cases and its record. Everything outside the folder is about the target state, including every enduring domain object such as a Defect, and the verification of those objects lives there. When the Change closes, its registration with Guard ends and verification of the enduring objects it touched continues without a gap, whatever happens to particular Test Cases: Cases remain free to be moved, split, consolidated, rewritten or replaced. The record of the Change is preserved. Owner: Change.
