# change/I26 change-folder

A Change gets from the current state to the target state, and its folder holds only what is about that move: its Gap, its baseline, its change-oriented Cases and its record. Everything outside the folder is about the target state, including every enduring domain object such as a Defect, and the verification of those objects lives there. The Change's Cases are hot only during the Change: they count RED while the Change is open, the count of RED moves one way per kind of transport (PLAN up or same, DO down or same) and ACT is allowed only when all are GREEN. Then `act.md` closes the Change, its Cases go cold, and nothing is unwired. Verification of the enduring objects it touched continues without a gap, whatever happens to particular Test Cases: Cases remain free to be moved, split, consolidated, rewritten or replaced. The record of the Change is preserved. Owner: Change.
