# Change

A Change is one bounded alteration proposed to what KAAL holds, and the coordination of the work that brings it about. It is not the technology that carries it: a pull request is one realization of a Change.

A Change is self-contained: it can be understood and judged on its own, without the conversation, transcript or handover it came from. Its record holds supporting rationale, coordination, references and evidence. The work stays where its owner keeps it; the record is not a second copy.

Integrating a Change does not prove that the Intent it realizes achieved its Outcome.
