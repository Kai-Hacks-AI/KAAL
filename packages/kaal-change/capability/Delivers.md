# Delivers

**Change —delivers→ Intent** is a Change's relationship to the Intent it delivers.

The Change states it, as an Edge of this Definition going out from its Change Node, and states it alone. What an Intent is, and whether a target is a well-formed one, is the Intent capability's to judge. Change checks only that the target is present and that the pin still holds.
