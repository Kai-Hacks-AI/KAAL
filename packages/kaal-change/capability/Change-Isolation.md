# Change Isolation

Change isolation is the explained boundary of a proposed alteration: its selected scope, potential impact, incoming and outgoing interface contracts, and the evidence needed to develop and assess the real subject independently of outside collaborators. It follows owner-defined traces across Intent, functional and nonfunctional Requirements, relevant Defects, Architecture, Test Cases and realizations without owning those meanings.

Incoming interfaces are exercised by drivers and outgoing collaborators can be controlled by appropriate doubles. Passing isolated tests does not establish boundary compatibility or real integration. Model-scoped analysis, source authority and actual execution evidence are separate concerns; missing evidence remains unknown.
