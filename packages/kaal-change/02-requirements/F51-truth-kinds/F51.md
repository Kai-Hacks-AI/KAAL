# change/F51 truth-kinds

The agent labels every expectation with its kind of truth: historical (true of A only, superseded), enduring (true of A and B, a protection that remains), target (true of B only) or transition (evidence that A became B). It splits an expectation of two kinds, never cites a historical Case as evidence of B, never keeps a superseded Case as a protection of B, and never retires an enduring Case because its subject is replaced.
