# defect/F3 state

The state of a Defect is derived from the records of the Cases that point at it, at one revision, and never stored on the Defect. A record is `{case, run, revision, outcome, complete}`, and outcome is `GREEN`, `RED` or `UNKNOWN` as a Case runner reports it. `complete` is the runner's own statement that the run finished and observed everything that should count (for Testing, `kaal-testing/observe`: no execution problem and a complete Case catalog); the Defect capability establishes none of it. A `GREEN` counts only with `complete: true`; a `GREEN` without that statement, or with `false`, is `UNKNOWN`, so complete evidence is established by the runner and never inferred from the records received. A `RED` confirms whether or not `complete` holds. The runs that count are the records the caller hands over for the revision judged; a Case with no such record is `UNKNOWN`. A record of another revision is stale and does not count. A missing record, or any outcome other than `GREEN` or `RED`, is `UNKNOWN`. A Case is `RED` if any applicable run is `RED`, `GREEN` if every applicable run is `GREEN`, otherwise `UNKNOWN`. A Defect is `confirmed` when some applicable Case is `RED`, even if every other Case is `UNKNOWN`: one RED Case is enough to show that the Defect exists. It is `answered` only when it has Cases, every applicable Case is `GREEN` with complete evidence. Without a RED and without complete GREEN evidence it is `unknown`, including when no Case points at it. `UNKNOWN` is never read as `RED` or `GREEN`.

A Defect carries no mandatory path-based reach and its records carry no coverage metadata: it expresses its scope semantically in its description, and the state comes from the applicable linked Case evidence at the judged revision. Review validates the Defect's meaningful scope and the Case's adequacy, robustness and reproducibility (Review —validates→ Defect, Review —validates→ Test Case); Testing owns the verification evidence (Test Case —verifies→ Defect); Architecture owns the placement of the correction. Missing, stale or incomplete evidence stays `unknown`.

Several Cases that point at one Defect are different manifestations, contexts or conditions of the same undesirable condition. They do not make several Defects and do not make several fix locations. Each Case's own outcome is kept alongside the derived state and is never merged into it. The state is evidence: whether it allows a merge is the consumer's policy. It does not classify a Finding, does not select a CARE treatment (Contain, Accept, Ratchet, Eliminate) and does not authorize a release.
