# defect/F2 edge

A Test Case points at a Defect with its first line `// Case <id> tests: <defect id>`, as a Case points at a Control. A Defect lists no Test Case, and several Cases may point at one Defect. What a Defect is about is free text; the mechanic needs no typed edge to a Requirement.
