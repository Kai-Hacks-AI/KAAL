# defect/F2 observation

Given the registers, a revision and observations `{case, outcome, revision}` from Testing, the mechanic derives for each registered Defect whether its condition is `reproduced`, `not reproduced` or `unknown`. The result is derived each time and never stored on the Defect, and `fixed` is not a stored fact.

- An observation is applicable only when it names the revision judged. Any other revision is stale, and a missing observation or an outcome other than `pass` or `fail` (unknown, skipped, errored, timed out, incomplete) is `unknown`.
- Several Cases connect to one Defect. If any applicable Case fails, the condition is `reproduced`. It is `not reproduced` only when every connected Case applicably passes. Otherwise it is `unknown`.
- A failing, applicable Case that no register lists is reported `reproduced unregistered`. An entry with no Defect is `invalid`.

The mechanic reports evidence. Whether a reproduced or registered Defect may be tolerated, and whether a not-reproduced one must leave its register, is the policy of the consumer or integration gate.
