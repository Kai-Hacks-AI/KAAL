# defect/F5 reach

A Defect claims its reach: where its condition can manifest, as a list of files and folders (paths from the repository root) in its record (`reach:`), with no special values: the repository root `.` stands for everything, for example `.` for repo-wide, `packages/` for packages only, or `.kaal/, engineering/` for a failed package deployment. Each Case covers a corner, and a record names the parts of the reach its run covered (`covers`). A claimed part that no GREEN applicable record covers counts toward `unknown`, never toward `answered`, and the mechanic names it. A covered part covers every part under it, and `.` covers all. A Defect that claims no reach cannot be `answered`. What a Case covers is what its record declares in `covers`, not what the Case reads: a Case whose condition is fully decided by what it reads (for example a top-level listing that decides whether `packages/` holds a non-package entry) declares the whole part, `packages/`, and covers it. A RED Case confirms the Defect whatever the reach.
