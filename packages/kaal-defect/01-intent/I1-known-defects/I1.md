# defect/I1 defect inventory

A Defect is a passive description of an undesirable condition, usually in free text, and its state is derived from the Test Cases that point at it, as a Control's is. A Defect is recorded in the context where it was born, and its record is authoritative: the inventory is discovered from those records, not kept in a second list. A Defect claims where its condition can manifest, and what no Case covers there stays unknown. The inventory of unresolved Defects is an input to planning, not an order to fix and not a release veto; what a Defect allows is for the gate that consumes the state to decide. A validated Finding stays a Finding: validation says the observation is legitimate, not that a Defect is verified. It has three legitimate next directions: an Intent (what we want to achieve or change), the investigation of a suspected Defect (what undesirable condition exists), or a backlog item (possible work recorded and prioritized, with no Intent committed and no Defect asserted). None is automatic or exclusive; the choice depends on the perspective and the decision being made. A backlog item is not proof of a Defect and does not authorize implementation. A Test Case, manual or automated, with reproducible evidence is what verifies a suspected Defect. CARE (Contain, Accept, Ratchet, Eliminate; Eliminate by a hotfix or by a fix through a normal Change) is a later treatment of a verified Defect, chosen elsewhere. Defect derives and reports evidence; it does not classify a Finding, select a treatment or authorize a release.
