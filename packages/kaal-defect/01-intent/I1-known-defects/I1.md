# defect/I1 known defects

A Defect is a passive description of an undesirable condition. It is not forgotten while it is unfixed: its context lists it in a register, and the evidence of connected Test Cases, at a revision, says whether the condition is reproduced, not reproduced or unknown. What a registered Defect allows is for the consumer of that evidence to decide.
