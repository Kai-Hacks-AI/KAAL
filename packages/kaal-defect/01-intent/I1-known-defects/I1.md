# defect/I1 known defects

A Defect is a passive description of an undesirable condition. It is not forgotten while it is unfixed: its context lists it in a register, together with the Test Cases linked to it. Whether the condition is reproduced is Testing's observation, and what a registered Defect allows is the policy of whoever consumes both.
