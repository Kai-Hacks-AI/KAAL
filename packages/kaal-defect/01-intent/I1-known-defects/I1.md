# defect/I1 known defects

A Defect is a passive description of an undesirable condition, usually in free text, and its state is derived from the Test Cases that point at it, as a Control's is. A Defect claims where its condition can manifest, and what no Case covers there stays unknown. A context lists the Defects it knows, so a known Defect is not forgotten while it is unfixed, and what a known Defect allows is for the gate that consumes the register and the state to decide.
