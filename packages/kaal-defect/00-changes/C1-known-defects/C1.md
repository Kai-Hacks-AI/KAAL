# defect/C1 known defects

A Defect is a passive description of an undesirable condition, usually in free text, and holds no state. Its state is derived from the Test Cases that point at it, the way a Control's status is judged from the Cases that test it: a Case points at a Defect with its first line, and a judge reads the records a Case runner made. Each context also keeps a register of its known Defects, beside its Defect records. kaal-defect ships no register, only the mechanic that reads the Defect records, registers and Case headers of a tree, derives each Defect's state, guards the registers and lists what each owner has to fix. Running the Cases is Testing's. What a Defect's state allows is the policy of the consumer or integration gate. No Defect is moved and nothing is wired to CI.
