# defect/C1 known defects

A Defect that a Test Case proves is RED would stop every merge if nothing said it was known. Each context keeps a register of such Defects, beside its Defect records. kaal-defect ships no register, only the mechanic that reads every register in a tree and reports, per Test Case, what the evidence shows: a proven known failure, a registered Case that is now GREEN, a registered Case whose result is unknown, a failure nobody registered, or an invalid entry. A verdict is evidence about the Defect and not permission to merge: whether a known Defect may be tolerated is the policy of the gate that consumes it. The mechanic takes Testing's results as a plain list that keeps `pass`, `fail` and `unknown`, and imports no Testing. No Defect is moved and nothing is wired to CI.
