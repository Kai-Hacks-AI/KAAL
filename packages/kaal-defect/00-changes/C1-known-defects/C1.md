# defect/C1 known defects

A Defect is a passive description of an undesirable condition and holds no execution state. Each context keeps a register of its known Defects, beside its Defect records: the description plus links to the Test Cases that show it. `known` is only membership of that register. kaal-defect ships no register, only the mechanic that reads every register in a tree, guards that each entry points at a Defect in its own folder, gives each Defect's linked Test Cases for Testing to observe, and lists what each owner has to fix. Whether a condition is reproduced, not reproduced or unknown is Testing's observation, derived from its own results at a revision. The mechanic reads no result, runs nothing, imports no Testing, and what a registered Defect allows is the policy of the consumer or integration gate. No Defect is moved and nothing is wired to CI.
