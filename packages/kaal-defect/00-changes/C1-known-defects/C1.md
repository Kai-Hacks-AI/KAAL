# defect/C1 known defects

A Defect is a passive description of an undesirable condition and holds no execution state. Each context keeps a register of its known Defects, beside its Defect records, and `known` is only membership of it. kaal-defect ships no register, only the mechanic that reads every register in a tree. Given Testing's observations of connected Test Cases at a revision, it derives per Defect whether the condition is reproduced, not reproduced or unknown. That is derived each time, never stored, and stale, incomplete or missing evidence is unknown. It runs nothing, imports no Testing, and reports evidence only: what it allows is the policy of the consumer or integration gate. No Defect is moved and nothing is wired to CI.
