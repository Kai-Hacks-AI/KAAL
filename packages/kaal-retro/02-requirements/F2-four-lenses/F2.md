# retro/F2 four-lenses

A retro of format `4L` has four sections in this order, `Liked`, `Learned`, `Lacked`, `Longed For`. Each section is a list of entries or the line `None raised.`; `Learned` may not say it, because it is the step that looks outside and an empty search is itself an entry. Every entry has a `subject:` line naming the one thing it is about, and no subject appears in two entries, in any section. Each lens needs its own fields and no others:

- **Liked** is a concept worth keeping and protecting that exists: `subject`, `keeps` (what would be lost without it).
- **Learned** is a finding from outside the consumer: `subject`, and `source` (where it came from) or `searched` (what was looked for and that nothing relevant came back).
- **Lacked** is an observed blocker, something that should have been there for the agent to succeed better or faster: `subject`, `decision-point`, `cost`, `findable` (`yes` when it existed and was missed, `no` when it was absent), and `address` (`AGENTS.md`, `Skill` or `script`). It is the only lens that carries an address, so a fix is proposed once.
- **Longed For** is an opportunity noticed that never blocked anything: `subject`, `worked-anyway` (a line saying nothing was blocked or slowed). No address, no fix, and `None raised.` is welcome.

A retro decides nothing and is addressed to the owner.
