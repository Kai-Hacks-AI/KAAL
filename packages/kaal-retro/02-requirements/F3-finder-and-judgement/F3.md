# retro/F3 finder-and-judgement

Retro offers two scripts. The finder is handed `{ path, text }` files and returns `{ retros, refused }`, each retro with `id`, `format`, `reflects` and `path`, and exits 0, or 2 when anything is refused; it searches no folder. The judgement is handed one retro's text and returns a result: `well-formed`, or `malformed` with a list of problems, each naming the section, the entry and the rule it breaks, for the form of F2 only: sections and their order, the fields each lens needs and no others, `address` only on Lacked, `findable` yes or no, a subject in one entry only, and `Learned` never empty. A format it does not know is `unjudged`, never `well-formed`. It says nothing about whether what a retro states is true or whether the fix is right: a well-formed retro may be wrong, and the owner decides.
