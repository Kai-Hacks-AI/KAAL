# retro/F5 lens-homes

The Skill's text gives each of the four lenses its own question and its own home, and the questions differ in what they ask about, not in tense:

- **Liked** asks what I relied on that held up and should be kept. It is about something that exists.
- **Learned** asks what I found when I went looking outside the consumer. It is about a source.
- **Lacked** asks what was missing when I had to decide, and what that cost me. It is about an observed blocker.
- **Longed For** asks what I would like to exist that I never needed. It is about something that did not block me.

The text also says what to avoid, in a short paragraph. An **echo** is the same point said again: a Lacked that reappears as a Longed For, a Learned that repeats the fix a Lacked already names, or one entry written twice in different words. A **mirror** is the same thing seen from both sides: a Liked X and a Lacked absence of X, such as "the finder exists" and "nothing points to the finder". Both are avoided by one rule: a point has one home. Where it cost me something it is a Lacked, and otherwise it sits where it exists or where I found it. The paragraph says that a form check sees a repeated subject but not an echo or a mirror under different words, so the author decides while filling the lenses, and there is no checklist of questions. The text asks for no new field, script or section.
