# Retro

Retro is the capability for conducting a retrospective on any subject by a chosen method, with 4L as its first method. It produces observations, interpretations and potential improvements, labeled so they can be told apart, and it decides, approves and creates nothing. The Skill that teaches it is `kaal-retro`, which works installed alone and depends on no other package.
