# 4L from my seat

Four lenses on the same work. Each asks me a different question, so a point fits in one lens only. Every entry starts with `- subject:` naming the one thing it is about, and a subject appears once in the whole retro.

## Liked: what did I rely on that held up and should be kept?

It is about something that exists and carried me correctly, worth keeping and protecting. Fields: `subject`, `keeps` (what would be lost without it). I point at something that exists, never at something I wish for. I do not invent one: `None raised.` is allowed. A point that helped and cost me nothing stays here.

## Learned: what did I find when I went looking outside?

It is about a source: knowledge from outside my own repository or consumer, found by looking at practice elsewhere, literature or a tool's own documentation. It is not empty, because looking is the step. Fields: `subject`, and `source` (where it came from; I say when it is secondhand) or `searched` (what I looked for and that nothing relevant came back). I state what the source says so that it applies beyond this repository, and never what to do here: what to do belongs to the Lacked it bears on.

## Lacked: what was missing when I had to decide, and what did it cost me?

It is about an observed blocker. Fields:

- `subject`: what was missing.
- `decision-point`: what I was deciding.
- `cost`: what it cost me, a wrong step, a detour, a correction or a question I should not have needed.
- `findable`: `yes` when it existed and I missed it (the fix is routing: AGENTS.md or the Skill's description), `no` when it was absent (the fix is to write it down in its home).
- `address`: where the fix belongs: `AGENTS.md`, `Skill` or `script`.

Lacked is the only lens that carries an address, so a fix is proposed once, and two Lacked entries may share an address. A real blocker is a Lacked even though I coped in the end. A cost that only could have happened is not.

## Longed For: what would I like to exist that I never needed?

It is about something that did not block me. Fields: `subject` (a thing that does not exist yet), `worked-anyway` (a line saying nothing was blocked or slowed). No address, no fix. `None raised.` is welcome. A good prompt: what could have gone wrong here and did not?

## The line between Lacked and Longed For

The test is what I did differently because of the absence. If its absence made me take a wrong step, a detour, a correction or a needless question, it is a Lacked, however well I coped. If I changed nothing, it is a Longed For and its `worked-anyway` line is true. I do not file a real blocker as a wish, and I do not inflate a wish into a blocker.

## Echo and mirror

I avoid two things across the lenses. An **echo** is the same point I say again: a Lacked that reappears as a Longed For, a Learned that repeats the fix a Lacked already names, or one entry I write twice in other words. A **mirror** is the same thing seen from both sides: a Liked X and a Lacked absence of X, such as "the finder exists" and "nothing points to the finder". One rule avoids both: a point has one home. Where it cost me something it is a Lacked, and otherwise it sits where it exists (Liked) or where I found it (Learned). The form check sees a repeated subject but not an echo or a mirror in other words, so I decide while filling the lenses.

## Result shape

```
---
id: <capability>/RT<n>
format: 4L
reflects: [<capability>/C<n>]
---
Account of: <who I am and what I did with respect to what this retro looks back on, in my own words>

## Liked
- subject: ...
  keeps: ...

## Learned
- subject: ...
  source: ...

## Lacked
- subject: ...
  decision-point: ...
  cost: ...
  findable: yes | no
  address: AGENTS.md | Skill | script

## Longed For
None raised.

Decisions: none made, the owner decides.
```

The `Account of:` line comes first and is not empty; its wording is mine, because how I identify myself is my choice. A retro states whose account it is, because one agent's view is not everyone's. It records no approval, assignment or commitment.
