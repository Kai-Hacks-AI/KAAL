# 4L

A method for a retrospective in four categories. It is one method among possible others. Go through all four, in this order or any order that suits the conversation, and ask each question about the subject.

## Liked

What worked well? What would we want to keep? Note the observation and why it worked. It may point to an improvement: a good practice worth spreading or protecting.

## Learned

What did we discover or understand that we did not before? Separate what was observed from what we conclude from it. It may point to an improvement: acting on something now understood.

## Lacked

What was missing or insufficient? Name the gap as it showed up, not who is to blame. It may point to an improvement: closing the gap.

## Longed For

What would we like to have or to do better? Wishes are observations about desire, so record them as such. It may point to an improvement: something to try or provide.

## Result

A 4L result is a short record with these parts. Each exists for a reason the method gives.

- Name the method and subject at the top (`Method: 4L`, `Subject: ...`), because a reader must know what was looked back on and how, without asking.
- Ask all four questions and give each its own heading. A category with nothing to report says `None raised.`, because silence cannot be told from a question never asked.
- Label every entry `- Observation:`, `- Interpretation:` or `- Potential improvement:`, because 4L moves from what happened to what it means to what could change, and the reader must see which is which.
- State whose views are included and whose are missing (`Participants: ...`), because a retrospective of one person's account must not read as everyone's.
- Close with `Decisions:`, saying none were made and that the owner decides, because a retrospective offers improvements and decides nothing. Record no approval, assignment or commitment.

Example shape:

```
Method: 4L
Subject: ...
Participants: ...

## Liked
- Observation: ...
- Potential improvement: ...

## Learned
- Interpretation: ...

## Lacked
None raised.

## Longed For
- Observation: ...

Decisions: None made. The owner decides.
```

---

Each category can yield potential improvements, and none is ranked above another. Record improvements as proposals for the owner to decide on, not as decisions.
