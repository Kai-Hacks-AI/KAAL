# 4L from the agent's seat

Four lenses on the same work. Each asks a different question, so an entry fits in one lens only. Every entry starts with `- subject:` naming the one thing it is about, and a subject appears once in the whole retro.

## Liked: what held up

A concept that exists and carried you correctly, worth keeping and protecting. Fields: `subject`, `keeps` (what would be lost without it). Point at something that exists, never at something you wish for. Do not invent one: `None raised.` is allowed. If the thing you like is the same one you also lacked a pointer to, it is one subject: keep it where it cost you something, in Lacked.

## Learned: what I found outside

Knowledge from outside your own repository or consumer, found by looking: practice elsewhere, literature, a tool's own documentation. It is not empty, because looking is the step. Fields: `subject`, and `source` (where it came from; say when it is secondhand) or `searched` (what you looked for and that nothing relevant came back). State it so it applies beyond this repository. A Learned carries no fix.

## Lacked: what blocked me

Something that should have been there for you to succeed better or faster, and that you observed cost you a wrong step, a detour or a correction. Fields:

- `subject`: what was missing.
- `decision-point`: what you were deciding.
- `cost`: what it cost.
- `findable`: `yes` when it existed and you missed it (the fix is routing: AGENTS.md or the Skill's description), `no` when it was absent (the fix is to write it down in its home).
- `address`: where the fix belongs: `AGENTS.md`, `Skill` or `script`.

Lacked is the only lens that carries an address, so a fix is proposed once. Two Lacked entries may share an address. No cost observed means it is not a Lacked: it is a Longed For.

## Longed For: what would be nice

An opportunity you noticed that never blocked or slowed you. Fields: `subject` (a thing that does not exist yet), `worked-anyway` (a line saying nothing was blocked). No address, no fix. If you can name the cost it caused, move it to Lacked. `None raised.` is welcome. A good prompt: what could have gone wrong here and did not?

```
None raised.

```

A retro states whose account it is, because one agent's view is not everyone's. It records no approval, assignment or commitment.
