# Retro

Retro is the responsibility for conducting a retrospective on any subject: finished or ongoing work, a decision, an event, an experience, a process or a product. It selects and applies a retrospective method, keeps what was observed apart from what it is taken to mean and from what might be improved, and leaves every decision and authorization to the owner of the subject. Its Skill, also named Retro, teaches the work and is installable and usable on its own.

Retro does not own what a retrospective's findings lead to, and it does not own what any other capability learns from them. It overlaps with no other capability's responsibility.
