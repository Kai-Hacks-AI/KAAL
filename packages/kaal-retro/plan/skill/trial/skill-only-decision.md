Method: 4L
Subject: My decision, a year ago, to leave a stable job for a freelance career
Participants: Only the person who made the decision (their account). Views of colleagues, clients, family or anyone else affected are missing and not written here.

Assumptions: Purpose is to look back to understand what happened and to inform the still-open question of whether to continue. Scope is the year since the decision, including the first four months. 4L fits because the subject is a personal experience with mixed outcomes and a decision still ahead.

## Liked
- Observation: Income is about the same as in the stable job.
- Observation: Fewer hours worked, on things chosen.
- Interpretation: Equal income for fewer hours suggests better pay per hour, and choice of work may be what makes the hours feel worthwhile.
- Potential improvement: Note which kinds of chosen work drive both pay and satisfaction, and protect them.

## Learned
- Observation: Income varies more than before.
- Interpretation: Equal average income with higher variance means more risk for the same return; whether that is acceptable depends on personal tolerance, which this account does not state.
- Potential improvement: Track monthly income for the year to see the size and pattern of the variance, instead of relying on the impression of "about the same".

## Lacked
- Observation: No savings buffer during the first four months.
- Observation: Missing colleagues.
- Interpretation: The missing buffer likely made the early period more stressful and narrowed the options for turning down work; the account does not say how it played out.
- Potential improvement: Build a buffer of several months of expenses now, if not yet done, so the variance is easier to absorb.
- Potential improvement: Create regular contact in place of colleagues, such as a peer group, a shared workspace or recurring meetings with other freelancers.

## Longed For
- Observation: A longing for colleagues' company (see Lacked).
- Observation: The account does not state other wishes.
- Potential improvement: Before deciding whether to continue, write down what would have to be true in a year for continuing to feel right (income floor, buffer, social contact, hours), and compare it with now.

Decisions: None made. This retrospective decides nothing, including whether to continue freelancing. That decision rests with the person who made the original decision.
