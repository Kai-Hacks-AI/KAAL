# Neighbourhood street party: 4L retrospective (organiser's viewpoint)

## Liked

The kids' games were the hit; they kept the afternoon going and drew families in. About 80 people came, a good turnout for a street party. When the band cancelled the day before, a friend's speaker filled in, so the music never stopped. When it rained for an hour, everyone crowded into the garage and the party carried on rather than ending.

## Learned

A single point of failure on a headline item (the band) can be absorbed if a friend with a simple fallback is one phone call away. Weather is a certainty to plan for, not a risk: one hour of rain was survivable only because the garage happened to exist. Food planned for fewer people than came ran out by 3pm, so headcount was underestimated. The games mattered more than the band.

## Lacked

A confirmed backup for the music before the day. A planned rain shelter, since the garage was improvised. A food quantity based on a real headcount (an RSVP or a rough count) and a top-up plan. Help sharing the load: I organised it, and I seem to have carried most of it.

## Longed for

Next year: ask for RSVPs or a rough count early and buy about 20 percent over it, with a late top-up run planned for around 2pm. Book the band with a written fallback, or lean on a speaker and playlist from the start. Name the garage, or a gazebo, as the rain plan in advance. Keep and expand the kids' games. Give two or three neighbours clear jobs (food, music, games) so it is easier for the organiser.
