Method: 4L
Subject: Last Saturday's neighbourhood street party (about 80 attendees)
Participants: The organiser only. Views of attendees, helpers, the band and the friend with the speaker are missing.
Scope and purpose: The day itself, plus the lead-up where it affected the day. Purpose: make next year easier. Assumption: 4L fits because the aim is a broad look back with improvement ideas.

## Liked
- Observation: The kids' games were the hit.
- Interpretation: Activities for children probably drew families and kept the party lively.
- Potential improvement: Keep and possibly expand the kids' games next year.
- Observation: A friend's speaker filled in after the band cancelled the day before.
- Observation: When it rained for an hour, everyone crowded into the garage and the party carried on.
- Interpretation: Improvised solutions worked, which suggests the group was flexible.

## Learned
- Observation: The band cancelled one day before; the food ran out by 3pm; rain lasted an hour.
- Interpretation: Roughly 80 guests ate more than was planned for, so the headcount or per-person quantity was underestimated.
- Interpretation: A single entertainment booking and no weather plan left the day dependent on luck and improvisation.
- Potential improvement: Note the 80-person attendance as a baseline for next year's food planning.

## Lacked
- Observation: Food ran out by 3pm.
- Potential improvement: Plan food for more than 80, stagger serving, or ask neighbours to bring dishes.
- Observation: No planned shelter for rain; the garage was used ad hoc and was crowded.
- Potential improvement: Arrange a covered area (gazebo, marquee, larger indoor space) or agree a rain plan beforehand.
- Observation: No backup for the band.
- Potential improvement: Have a backup (a playlist and speaker, or a second act) and confirm bookings a few days ahead.

## Longed For
None raised. (The organiser has said they want next year to be easier; no further wishes were given.)
