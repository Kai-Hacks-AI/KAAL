Method: 4L (fits a mid-flight look-back by a project lead: simple, covers working, missing and wished-for in one pass)
Subject: Open-source documentation rewrite, about halfway done, looked back on mid-flight
Purpose: Understand what is happening so far and find what could improve for the second half (assumed, as the lead said "look back now, mid-flight")
Scope: Start of the rewrite to now. Not covered: content quality of individual pages, anything after today.
Participants: Only the project lead's account (as relayed). Views of the six contributors, the three inactive ones included, and of documentation readers are missing. Nothing here is written on their behalf.

## Liked
- Observation: The style guide was agreed in week 2, before most writing.
- Interpretation: Agreeing it early probably gave contributors a shared standard to write against. Unverified; no contributor view available.
- Potential improvement: Keep agreeing standards early, and record how the week 2 agreement was reached.

## Learned
- Observation: The style guide changed in week 5.
- Interpretation: The week 2 guide may have been settled before real writing exposed its gaps. The reason for the change was not given.
- Potential improvement: Ask whoever proposed the week 5 change what prompted it, and decide whether a planned review point (e.g. after the first batch of pages) would surface such issues sooner.
- Observation: The search index broke twice.
- Interpretation: Possibly a fragile build or indexing setup, or a side effect of structural edits. Cause unknown.
- Potential improvement: Find the cause of both breaks; consider a check that flags a broken index before release.

## Lacked
- Observation: Three of six contributors have been active.
- Interpretation: Capacity is perhaps half of what was planned, and reasons are unknown (availability, unclear tasks, onboarding, interest).
- Potential improvement: Ask the inactive contributors, directly and without blame, what would help them take part.
- Observation: The week 5 style change happened after pages were presumably written under the old guide.
- Interpretation: Earlier pages may now be inconsistent. Not confirmed.
- Potential improvement: Check how many pages follow the old guide and whether a migration plan or a changelog for the guide would help.

## Longed For
None raised. (The lead has not stated wishes. Contributors were not asked.)

Decisions: None made. This retrospective decides, assigns and approves nothing. The project lead, as owner, decides whether and how to act on any improvement above.
