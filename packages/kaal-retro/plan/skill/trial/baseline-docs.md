# Documentation rewrite: 4L retrospective (project lead's viewpoint, mid-flight)

## Liked

Three of six contributors have been active, and the work they did is moving the rewrite forward; we are about halfway. Agreeing a style guide in week 2 gave early contributors something to write against.

## Learned

The style guide changed in week 5, after three weeks of pages written to the week-2 version. Every change to the guide now has a cost in rework, and we did not count it when we made the change. The search index broke twice; two breakages mean the cause was not fixed the first time, or nothing was watching for it. Half of the contributors have not been active, so the plan assumed capacity we did not have.

## Lacked

A rule for changing the style guide after it is agreed: who decides, how it is announced, and what happens to pages already written. A check that tells us the search index is broken before a reader does. Knowledge of why three contributors are inactive (availability, onboarding, or unclear tasks); this retrospective does not claim to know.

## Longed for

A versioned style guide with a short change log, so a change in week 5 does not silently invalidate earlier work. An automated search index check that runs on every change. A small, explicit first task for each new contributor to re-engage the inactive three.

Note: this is written mid-flight, from the facts given by the project lead only. No project records were consulted, so causes above are stated as questions or gaps, not findings.
