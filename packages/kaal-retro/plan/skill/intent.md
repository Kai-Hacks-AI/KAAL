# Intent (retro/C1)

An agent can run a retrospective on any subject, with nothing but this Skill installed.

A subject may be finished or ongoing work, a decision, an event, an experience, a process or a product. The Skill prescribes no lifecycle, and KAAL is one consumer of it, not its prerequisite.

Value: people and agents get a short, honest retrospective whose observations, interpretations and proposals stay distinguishable, and whose decisions stay with their owners.
