# Intent (retro/C1)

An agent can run a retrospective on any subject, with nothing but this Skill installed.

A subject may be finished or ongoing work, a decision, an event, an experience, a process or a product. The Skill prescribes no lifecycle, and KAAL is one consumer of it, not its prerequisite.

The Skill gives direct guidance. An agent need not look up earlier 4Ls, PR templates or KAAL conventions and copy their shape, so the lookups and the context they cost are saved.

Value: people and agents get a short, honest retrospective whose observations, interpretations and potential improvements stay distinguishable, and whose decisions stay with their owners, at lower lookup and context cost than copying examples.
