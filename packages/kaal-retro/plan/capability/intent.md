# Intent (retro/C2)

Retro is its own capability. Retrospectives are broader than learning, and Learning learns from many sources beyond retrospectives, so the Skill is not a Learning-owned Skill.

The ownership is real and admitted through Core: a Retro Capability Node, an ownership claim that names it, and a collector that carries its name. The installable Skill stays independent of Core and of every other package.

Value: the product owner's decision is carried by identity Core can check, so the Change can be promoted without a provisional claim or a collector that names a capability that does not exist.
