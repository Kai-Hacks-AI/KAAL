# Learning in KAAL

What Learning is, and what this capability means, is stated by the Node `Learning` in a KAAL Engine, and how knowledge is kept by the Node `BRAIN`: read them there, starting from the Engine's `AGENTS.md`. This file only tells you how to do the work.

## What belongs here

Use this capability for what KAAL has come to understand from its work. Take the rest to its owner:

- a Node's identity, its relationships and their traversal: Graph;
- evidence and findings: Testing;
- observations and reviews of work: the capability for reviewing work;
- the course of a Change: the capability for Changes;
- a decision about what to do: the capability that makes it.

## Duplicates and contradictions

When statements you meet may duplicate or contradict each other:

- Identify each statement, the context that owns it, and the nature of the concern. Refer to a Node by name and id where there is one; not everything relevant is a Node.
- Remember that an apparent contradiction can come from different contexts, assumptions or times.
- Surface it to the responsible capabilities. Do not merge the statements, replace one with the other, or choose between them.

## Rules

- Knowledge grants no authority and is not approval. Never approve, merge or decide because of something learned.
- Learning keeps no central store, index, registry or records of knowledge: knowledge is retained in the context responsible for it. Invent no relationship type for it.
- Keep the Owner's mnemonics as they are named. Each comes into this capability as a Node of its own.
