# CICERO

Content In Context Enables Retrievable Overview.

Content belongs to the context of the subject or consumer it serves, and keeps its authority there. The capability that owns content decides what it is and where it is placed. An overview makes content discoverable from elsewhere by referring to it, never by relocating or duplicating it.

An overview is derived: it may be incomplete, stale or wrong, and absence from one says nothing about a subject. Retrievable is not reliable. What is found through an overview is trusted only as far as its own context establishes it, and where the two disagree, the context holds.

CICERO applies to all content in KAAL. Learning hosts it, and every capability applies it to its own content without handing that content to Learning.
