# BRAIN

Be Right And Improve Network.

A brain: KAAL's memory, a network of connected knowledge.

BRAIN retains knowledge that is identifiable and connected, in the context that owns it. Being right is holding what is currently understood there. Improving the network preserves what came before: an improvement is stated beside what it improves and refers to it, and earlier meaning is never silently rewritten.

Capabilities own meaning within their own bounded contexts, and similar or even equal statements in different contexts can each be legitimate. Needless duplication and contradiction are concerns to identify, investigate and reconcile with the capabilities responsible. Nothing resolves them automatically, whether by centralizing knowledge or by replacing one statement with another.

Identity, relationships and their traversal are Graph's, and BRAIN gives them no meaning of its own. What a capability decides from what it knows remains that capability's decision.
