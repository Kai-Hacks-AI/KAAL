# PLAN

PLAN: Prioritize, Launch, Adaption, Now.

PLAN is how Product turns possible work into an intentional roadmap: Backlog → PLAN → Roadmap.

- **Prioritize**: order the possibilities in the backlog.
- **Launch**: select an increment and authorize it for realization. Launching does not deliver the increment, and Product does not take over its delivery.
- **Adaption**: keep product direction, backlog and roadmap responsive to change. Work that starts outside the roadmap, even a single unplanned Change, is reflected by adapting the plan; it is not an exception that bypasses product direction.
- **Now**: connect the intended direction to action. A launched increment is realized by a Change, or by related Changes that Project coordinates where they require it.

PLAN is not a pipeline of four steps, a ceremony or a Project. Product prepares and maintains the backlog and roadmap. The Product Owner retains authority over priorities, authorization of increments and substantive changes to product direction. Each Change keeps its own lifecycle, which is Change's. PLAN is not the Plan phase of Plan–Do–Check–Act, which plans how a piece of work is done.
