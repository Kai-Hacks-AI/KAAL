# Product

Product is the responsibility for Product Management: supporting the Product Owner's product direction by keeping the wanted increments, the backlog, the roadmap and their priorities legible and current, and by preparing the decisions the Product Owner makes. Its scope is that alone.

Its responsibility starts where the Product Owner gives direction or an Intent asks for an increment, and ends when an increment has been selected and ordered. The Product Owner decides; Product prepares each decision and records the choice as it was made, and decides nothing itself.

It provides the Product Direction Definition. For admission it requires Core's KAAL Capability and KAAL Definition, and nothing else.

What an Intent means and what its Outcome is are Intent's. Delivering a selected increment is the work of Changes, each with its own lifecycle, which is Change's; coordinating several related Changes is Project's. Product keeps no store of its own: its artifacts stay in the context where the Product Owner reviews them.
