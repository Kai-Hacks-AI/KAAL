# Data

Data is the responsibility for Data Management: managing data so that it warrants the trust placed in it. It covers how data is organized, by classification and by schemas, formats and data models where these are genuinely generic; how it is stewarded; its quality; its provenance; and its lifecycle, from creation through retention to supersession or disposal. Its scope is that alone.

Each consuming capability keeps the meaning of its own data and the decisions made with it. Data says how data is managed, never what a capability's data means, and containing data does not make anything Data's. Data keeps no store, registry or catalog of its own.

For admission it requires Core's KAAL Capability, and nothing else.

Its neighbours keep their specialized mechanisms, and Data does not repeat them. Nodes, Edges, identity and traversal are Graph's; Graph relies on Data Management, and that dependency transfers no ownership in either direction. Forming knowledge, and making content retrievable in its context, are Learning's. Cryptographic integrity is Sealing's. Evidence is Testing's, and assurance about controls is Controls'. Storage and its mechanics are Hosting's, and where data is placed is decided by the context that holds it.
