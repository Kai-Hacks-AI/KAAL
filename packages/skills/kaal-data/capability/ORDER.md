# ORDER

Organised Ratified Data Enhances Reliability.

Whatever KAAL handles, whether information, decisions, artifacts or outcomes, merits trust to the degree that it is organised, structured and traceable, and ratified, supported by evidence or standing appropriate to its context. Reliability is warranted trust within that context, not universal truth or infallibility: ratifying something states what supports it and does not make it right, and what is ratified stays open to a better-supported successor. Being retrievable does not make anything reliable.

ORDER is Data's guiding principle. It applies wherever KAAL holds data without making Data the owner of that data, and it adds no store, record, procedure or gate of its own. How much trust a thing warrants, and on what evidence, is decided by the capability that owns it.
