# HOW

Human Observed Work.

A person observed work being done. A HOW record is the evidence of that observation: who observed, what, when, and under which conditions. It supports one proposition, that this was observed under those conditions, and nothing broader.

Observation is not verification: it cannot be reproduced mechanically. It is not validation either: seeing that something happened is not judging that it is right. Such a judgment, where one is made, is made and recorded separately. A HOW record is never presented as verification or as validation, and what it did not observe stays open.

A finding drawn from an observation is one of Testing's four kinds, and names the observation it rests on.
