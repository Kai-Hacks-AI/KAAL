# Project

Project is the responsibility for Project, Program and Portfolio Management: coordinating related Changes toward a shared Outcome where their sequencing, dependencies, grouping or integration risks require it. Its scope is that alone.

Its responsibility starts where a selected increment needs several related Changes, and ends at the Project's exit: its Outcome evidenced and judged, or the Project withdrawn. Neither a capability increment nor several independent Changes needs a Project. Project is realized first; Program, the coordination of related Projects, and Portfolio, their capacity, risks and commitments taken together, are within its scope and not yet realized.

It provides the Project Plan Definition. For admission it requires Core's KAAL Capability and KAAL Definition, and nothing else.

Coordination transfers no functional ownership. Each Change keeps its own lifecycle, which is Change's, and each capability keeps its own work. The Outcome and what it means are Intent's, verification evidence is Testing's and review judgments are Review's. What is wanted, and in which order, is for Product to prepare and the Product Owner to decide. How Changes land is Engineering's Branching.
