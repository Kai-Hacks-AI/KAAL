# Project

Project is the responsibility for Project, Program and Portfolio Management: adding governance over one or more Normal Changes toward a shared Outcome where that governance is warranted. Its scope is that alone.

Its responsibility starts where governance over one or more Normal Changes is warranted, and ends at the Project's exit: its integrated Outcome evidenced and judged, or the Project withdrawn. One Normal Change is the minimum, and several are equally valid. The number of Changes alone is not the trigger: governance may be warranted by complexity, risk, dependencies, coordination or delivery commitments. No Normal Change needs a Project merely by being one. What a Normal Change is, and how Changes are classified and governed as Changes, is Change's. Project is realized first; Program, the coordination of related Projects, and Portfolio, their capacity, risks and commitments taken together, are within its scope and not yet realized.

It provides the Project Plan Definition. For admission it requires Core's KAAL Capability and KAAL Definition, and nothing else.

Governance transfers no functional ownership. Project neither decomposes a Change by default nor takes over a Change's authorization, lifecycle, work or evidence. Each Change keeps its own authorization and lifecycle, which are Change's, and each capability keeps its own work. The Outcome and what it means are Intent's, verification evidence is Testing's and review judgments are Review's. What is wanted, and in which order, is for Product to prepare and the Product Owner to decide. How Changes land is Engineering's Branching.
