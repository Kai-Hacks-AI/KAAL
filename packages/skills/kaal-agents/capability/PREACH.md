# PREACH

Program Reports Errors, Agent Consults Human.

PREACH is the direction an error or ambiguity travels when deterministic machinery finds one it cannot legitimately resolve: from Program, to Agent, to Human.

- **Program**: the program reports what it found, with the facts and the candidate definitions, owners and relationships it can see. It invents no semantics: it does not equate, merge, choose or suppress candidates, and what it cannot resolve it reports as unresolved, never as passing.
- **Agent**: an Agent investigates with the definitions and authority already available. It resolves only what is already authorized.
- **Human**: when resolving needs a negotiation of meaning or a new decision of authority, the Agent consults the person accountable for it, and that person decides.

Not every error escalates to a person. What a program or an Agent can resolve under authority already given ends there.

PREACH runs the other way to HAPTIC, which Controls owns, and does not redefine it. Once a person has decided, the outcome can move forward along HAPTIC into an explicit, testable contract.

PREACH and HAPTIC are correlated with HACKS, Human Agent Collaboration Knowledge System, the part of KHAI, Kai Hacks AI, that concerns how people and Agents work together. They describe complementary aspects of that collaboration. The association is context only: HACKS is not a capability, and it owns neither.

Agents owns PREACH as the operational principle of how an Agent escalates, and nothing beyond it. It owns no human decision, no domain's semantics and not HAPTIC. PREACH authorizes nothing. The authority an Agent acts on, and who is accountable for a decision, are given elsewhere.
