# I WON

Intent Worked, Outcome Noticed.

I WON is the Owner's conclusion that an Intent worked: the Outcome it wanted has come about, and the Owner has noticed it. In other words, "I won: I got what I intended to get."

It is the Owner's conclusion, not a verification. A test passing or a Change landing does not make it, and it can be reached only for an Intent whose Outcome the Owner has noticed.

Its first form was IO (Intent / Outcome), the same play on words. I WON is the name that stuck.
