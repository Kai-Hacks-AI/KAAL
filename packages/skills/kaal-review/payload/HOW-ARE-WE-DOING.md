# HOW ARE WE DOING?

- **HOW**: Human Observes Work.
- **ARE**: Agents Review Each-other.
- **WE**: With Experience.
- **DOING**: Durable Observations Inform Next Goals.

HOW ARE WE DOING? names one feedback loop, not a review procedure. Work stays observable to a human, who steers where human judgment or authority is valuable without performing or verifying every operation. Agents other than the one that produced the work challenge it, independently tasked. What those reviews find that matters beyond the work at hand becomes durable in the place that owns its meaning, so later work begins with experience that earlier work had to discover.

The loop crosses capabilities, and Review hosts its name without owning all of it. Review owns the observations and judgments of HOW and ARE. Forming understanding from them is Learning's. Using what is learned to inform future goals belongs to Product, Intent or whichever context consumes it. Experience is not conversation history, and not every observation deserves to survive: one becomes durable by moving to the owner of its meaning, not by being kept here. WE and DOING are named and not yet realized, and the loop is no mandatory sequence of steps.

Read whole, the four parts ask the question the loop exists to keep asking.
