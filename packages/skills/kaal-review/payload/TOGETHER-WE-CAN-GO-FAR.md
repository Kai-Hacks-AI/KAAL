# TOGETHER WE CAN GO FAR

- **TOGETHER**: The Owner Gets Enhanced Through Holistic Experience Reviews.
- **WE**: Worked Evidence.
- **CAN**: Changes Adaption Now, earlier written Change Adapts Now.
- **GO**: Getting Optimized.
- **FAR**: Features Accepted Regression, also written Feature Acceptance Regression.

Don't work alone. KAAL is meant to have as many useful feedback loops as it can, so that collaboration, independent review, evidence, learning and improvement reinforce one another. TOGETHER WE CAN GO FAR joins two directions of that: a forward loop of planning, implementation and testing, from a feature to its acceptance and on to guarding it against regression, and a backward loop in which worked evidence, observations and learning refactor the work and improve its direction.

Review hosts this principle without taking over the responsibilities it connects. Evidence is Testing's. Forming understanding is Learning's. Changing and adapting is Change's. Direction and goals are Intent's and Product's. The composite commits KAAL to no pipeline, no order of steps and no realization of its parts, and none of its parts is a capability of its own. It complements ROWING, the review loop while Work is mutable, and HOW ARE WE DOING?, the loop that keeps observations from being lost. Its WE (Worked Evidence) is not HOW ARE WE DOING?'s WE (With Experience), and the two never share a meaning.
