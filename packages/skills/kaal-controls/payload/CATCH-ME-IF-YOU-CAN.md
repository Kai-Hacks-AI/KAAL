# CATCH ME IF YOU CAN

Control Agents Through Continuous Hunting Misbehaving Elements, Implementing Fierce Yet Objective Unbiased Checks Against Noncompliance.

CATCH ME IF YOU CAN is the principle of actively hunting misbehaving elements instead of accepting a result because it looks finished. In the terms of `Controls`, a hunt is a detective control: it reveals, and it blocks nothing.

- **Hunt every element.** An element is any part of the work and its contracts: requirements, architecture, tests, evidence, configurations and behaviour, not only Agents. A GREEN result, a passing review, or artifacts that agree with each other are where a hunt starts, not where it ends.
- **Stay objective.** A suspicion is stated as a suspicion until evidence decides it, and a hunt claims no more than its evidence supports. Discriminating evidence comes first: a check whose result would differ depending on whether the suspicion holds. A claim that no check can decide stays undecided until a judgment decides it, and is never presented as decided. A suspicion the evidence refutes is reported as refuted. A hunt that finds nothing shows only that it found nothing within what it examined, never that nothing is there.
- **Challenge both sides.** Where two elements are checked against each other, such as a test and its implementation, a requirement and its test, or a check and the configuration it reads, each side is also challenged against the contract it answers to, not only against the other side. Otherwise the two can be tuned to each other, which leaves brittle tests and mutual overoptimization.
- **Self-checking is not independent assurance.** Anyone may hunt, including whoever produced the work: hunting one's own work is self-checking, and it is encouraged. Only a claim of independent assurance requires that whoever hunted did not produce the work, as `Controls` requires for independent controls. A self-check is reported as one, and no hunt is required to be independent.

CATCH ME IF YOU CAN is a principle and no more. It defines no kinds of findings, nothing about defects or how they trace, no review judgment, and nothing about how Agents operate, how checks are executed or what they gate. Where they are present, other capabilities' contracts complement a hunt, such as Testing's findings or Review's validation, but the principle neither assumes nor restates them. Which elements a capability hunts, and when, is that capability's decision. Controls keeps no registry of hunts and executes none.
