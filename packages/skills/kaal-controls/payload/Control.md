# Control

A Control is a Node that documents one specific control. Its name names the control, and its text states what the control is meant to do, in the terms of `Controls`. The capability that owns the governed behaviour documents the control, and its objective stays that capability's.

A Control is documentation. It holds no setting, enforces nothing and says nothing of whether the control is in place, operates or is effective. That needs evidence, and claims from it are calibrated as `Controls` states.

A Control points to where it is realized through Edges typed by `Realized by`. It need not carry any: a Control without one is documented with no realization recorded.
