# CATCH ME IF YOU CAN: Intent

This is `controls/C4`, a PLAN Change. It ships no Node, code or test. `controls/C5` realizes it. (`controls/C3` is reserved for the VARISC reduction that `controls/C1` planned.)

The Owner has coined a working principle for Controls:

> CATCH ME IF YOU CAN: Control Agents Through Continuous Hunting Misbehaving Elements, Implementing Fierce Yet Objective Unbiased Checks Against Noncompliance.

A GREEN result or a self-consistent set of artifacts is not yet assurance. Work that Agents produce, and the contracts around it, can agree with each other and still be wrong: a test can be shaped to its implementation, a requirement to its test, or evidence to the claim it is meant to support. Controls already says that a claim is no stronger than its evidence. This principle adds the posture that goes looking for where that evidence is weakest.

- **controls/I2**: Controls states, as a Definition, the principle of actively hunting misbehaving elements, and keeps the hunt objective. Suspicion is pursued until evidence decides it, and is never presented as a proven defect. Controls owns the principle. The work of finding, classifying, reviewing, executing and gating stays with the capabilities that own it.
