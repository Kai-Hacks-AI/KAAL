# Roles

Roles is a provisional home for two Role definitions, the CTO Advisor and the CPO Advisor, until their owners take them. Its scope is that alone.

The principle is that each Capability defines and owns the Roles it needs to perform its responsibilities. Process defines the Role concept and keeps no central registry of every capability's Roles. A Role is discoverable through the Capability that owns it, by KAAL's existing Capability and Skill mechanisms, and Roles creates no parallel registry. A Role's availability grants no authority.

Roles is an incubation arrangement, not a precedent that future Roles are centrally owned. The eventual semantic owner of the CTO Advisor is Engineering, and of the CPO Advisor is Product. Each owner takes its definition into its own Capability by its own Change, and Roles is then removed. Until then each owner is consulted on its own definition.

It provides the CTO Advisor Definition and the CPO Advisor Definition. For admission it requires Core's KAAL Capability and KAAL Definition, and nothing else. It declares no dependency on Process.
