# Show WIP v2: Requirements (target)

Scoped `coordination/`, continuing [../coordination/requirements.md](../coordination/requirements.md) (F1 to F10, N1). F11 to F15 are new. Words as in v1: a piece, its from and into, a station, a disposition.

- **coordination/F11. Review sequence.** `showWip` also returns `review`: the pieces whose disposition is REVIEW, each `{ rank, ref, type, from, into, head, clean, reasons }`, `rank` starting at 1. The order is, in turn: the position of the piece's station in the config's `review.stations` (a station not listed follows the listed ones, in config order); the position of its type in `review.types` (a type not listed follows, `unknown` last); its reference number. Without a `review` key in the config the order is the config's station order, then reference. The order does not depend on the order of the input.
- **coordination/F12. Clean for review.** `clean` is `true`, `false` or `unknown` and is told from observed facts at this moment. A REVIEW piece is clean when its checks passed (`success`), and there is no pending sync ahead of it (F13), and its head was observed. It is not clean (`false`) with a reason for each of: its checks reported nothing (`none`); a pending sync ahead of it; its head was not observed counts as `unknown`, never `true`. `reasons` lists each cause in plain words. A piece that is not REVIEW has no entry.
- **coordination/F13. Pending sync ahead.** The config's `review.sync` names the type of a piece that brings a collector up to date (here `SYNC`). An open piece of that type whose `into` equals the `from` or the `into` of a REVIEW piece is a pending sync ahead of it, unless it is that piece. The reason names the sync by reference and the place it goes into.
- **coordination/F14. Exact head.** Facts carry `head`, the identity of the version the facts were read at. The reader of the Delivery maps it from the host. The review line shows its first seven characters.
- **coordination/F15. Render.** `render(report)` prints, after the board and the not-observed list of v1, a section `Review sequence` with one line per review entry: `rank. #ref TYPE from → into @head CLEAN` or `NOT CLEAN reasons` or `UNKNOWN reasons`; an unobserved head shows `@unknown`. With no REVIEW piece the section says so.
- **coordination/N2. Same honesty.** The additions are read-only and host-neutral like F10: built-ins only, no network, process or write API, no KAAL identity, no host term. The sequence recommends a reading order and authorizes, verifies and gates nothing.
