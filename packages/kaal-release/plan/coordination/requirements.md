# analyze Flow: Requirements (target)

Scoped `coordination/`. F are functional, N nonfunctional. The control is `analyze Flow`, a detective control: it makes the state of the flow transparent and authorizes nothing. It borrows its language from Lean and the Theory of Constraints: WIP limit, constraint, pull, expedite, andon (as `escalate`) and unblock. The layers, from the outcome backwards, are Publishing, Promotion, Integration, Development.

**Words.** The contract holds no host term. A **piece** is one PLAN, DO, ACT, SYNC, PROMOTION or HOTFIX of a Change, with DEFAULT as the fallback; HOTFIX is the expedite class, an exceptional route and never a step of the normal lifecycle. A CHECK is a verification result. The actions are OPEN (a missing piece), REVIEW, REVISE (redo the piece's own phase), LAND (let it land on approval) and WAIT.

- **coordination/F1. One script in the Skill, `analyze Flow`.** `skills/kaal-release/scripts/analyze-flow.mjs` exports `analyzeFlow(facts, declaration)` and prints the same from a command line (`node analyze-flow.mjs <facts.json> <declaration.json>`). `SKILL.md` names it, and the package carries it. It is read-only: it reads two files and prints; it opens no network connection, runs no host command and writes nothing.
- **coordination/F2. Facts input.** A JSON file with: the target's version; each collector (`name`, `at` its current version, `behindTarget`); each piece (`ref` the host's opaque reference, shown as given; `type`; `change` the Change it names; `into` and `from`, where it goes and where it comes from; `at` its current version; `state` open or landed or dropped; `fit` fits or conflicts or stale; `checks` as name and conclusion success, failure or pending; `openComments`; `review` with its state, approved or changes, and the version it was given at; `landsOnApproval`). `type` is the template type the gatherer read from the declared template, and `change` the Change id the piece names. Neither is parsed from a title or from prose here. A `review` of `null` means no review exists. An absent key means the fact is unknown. An unknown fact is reported, never defaulted.
- **coordination/F3. Declaration input.** A JSON file the consumer owns: the promotion `target`; the template `types` (promotion, sync, change types, hotfix, fallback); `policy.wipLimit` (the WIP limit of the promotion layer, one); `freshnessChecks` (the checks that fail when a collector is behind the target); `promotions`, the **ordered** pair declarations, each a collector and the Change it promotes; the `holds`; the role names for `author`, `reviewer`, `owner`. The Script names no KAAL identity.
- **coordination/F4. Output.** An ordered list of findings. Each finding is a next action: `rank`, `rule`, `action`, `target`, `layer` (promotion, integration or development), `kind` (promotion, sync or change), `pair` (the collector of its pair, if any), `purpose` (promotion or development, for a sync), `change`, `class` (expedite, for a hotfix), `reason`, `dependency`, `authority`, `status` (ready, waiting, blocked, escalate) and `evidence`. A separate `notJudged` list holds everything it could not decide. The same inputs give byte-identical output, whatever the order of the pieces.
- **coordination/F5. Actions.** OPEN, REVIEW, REVISE, LAND, WAIT. No other. Nothing is executed. A piece yields at most one finding, by precedence: REVISE (changes requested, open comments, a piece that conflicts or is stale against what it goes into, or a failed CHECK that is not waiting on a sync), then WAIT on a sync (a freshness CHECK fails while its collector is behind the target), then REVIEW (no approval at its current version), then LAND (approved at its current version, not set to land on approval), then WAIT, on a running CHECK or else on landing.
- **coordination/F6. Promotion pairs are declared, never inferred from titles.** A pair is a declared collector and Change. Its SYNC member is a SYNC piece into the collector whose `change` is the pair's Change. Its PROMOTION member is a PROMOTION piece into the target from the collector. A member that does not exist is OPEN: the SYNC when the collector is behind the target, then the PROMOTION, waiting on the SYNC. An existing PROMOTION whose SYNC is missing is waiting on it.
- **coordination/F7. A development SYNC serves a named Change.** A piece going into a collector is blocked on collector synchronization only when a declared freshness CHECK fails and its collector is behind the target. A conflict, a stale piece, any other failed CHECK and a missing review are not a sync need; they are REVISE or REVIEW. The development SYNC names the blocked Change: an existing SYNC piece into that collector with that `change`, or OPEN when none exists. The blocked piece waits on it. A SYNC that matches neither a declared pair nor a blocked Change has no purpose and is reported.
- **coordination/F8. One active promotion pair (the WIP limit).** The active pair is the first declared pair not complete. A pair is complete when a landed PROMOTION carries the collector's current version. Every other pair waits on the active one, however many of its pieces are already open; an open piece never makes a pair active. A development SYNC is not a pair member and does not occupy the lane. Nothing but a landed PROMOTION releases it.
- **coordination/F9. Order and layers.** The Promotion layer is the constraint, and every other layer is subordinated to it. The list is pulled backwards from the outcome and printed forwards: a finding follows the finding it depends on; among findings with no unmet dependency, the layer nearest the outcome comes first (promotion, integration, development); ties by reference. The publishing layer is reported as not modelled.
- **coordination/F10. Types.** A HOTFIX is the expedite class: it gets its next action, is marked `class: expedite`, is outside the WIP limit, never occupies the lane, and its unmodelled route is reported. A piece of the fallback type, or of an undeclared type, is reported and not classified.
- **coordination/F11. Authority is never converted.** Every finding names who may do it (`author`, `reviewer`, `owner`). A hold in the declaration makes the finding `escalate` for the owner. The Script cannot authorize.
- **coordination/F12. Runs where Node runs.** The Script runs unchanged on a person's machine or as a step of a workflow, with the same output for the same two files. It reads its two files and nothing else: no environment, no network, no host credential. Whatever gathers the facts is separate, and is the only part that needs host access. It exits 0 whenever it produced its findings, and non-zero only when an input cannot be read.
- **coordination/N1. Standalone.** No runtime dependency; the Script imports nothing but built-ins.
- **coordination/N2. Generic.** The Script, its fixtures and its README contain no KAAL-specific identity and no host term (release/F5, T5).
- **coordination/N3. Honest.** The README states that the Script recommends and verifies nothing.
