# Pull Promotion v1: the smallest delivery that can land and be exercised

Status: **proposal for CPO alignment** (CPO direction of 12 Nov 2026: land the smallest useful working version first). It replaces the earlier decomposition and scope documents, which are withdrawn. Nothing is implemented; no DO, merge, promotion or ruleset change is authorized.

## 1. What v1 does

1. **Walk the collectors** of the declaration, in the declared order, from `main` upstream.
2. **Identify which have fulfilled their PDCA.** A collector qualifies when every Change in it, not yet delivered, has PLAN, DO and ACT landed in that collector and the declared Check checks are green on the collector's head. Landing is the evidence of PLAN, DO and ACT; reviews are not re-judged.
3. **If several qualify, say which moves next**: the first in the declared order. If none qualifies, say why per collector, naming the missing phase, check or fact. Missing or ambiguous evidence is reported as such and never read as a pass.

It is advisory. It does not authorize a promotion (the Owner), and it does not judge CI or branch readiness (behind `main`, admission, merge queue: the preventive controls). Its output says "qualifies", never "may promote".

Show WIP and Pull Promotion stay independent: neither reads the other's output, supplier or contract.

## 5. What it costs and what it gives

It gives a command that, run by hand, answers the Owner's question on the real repository with traceable missing evidence. It costs two small Changes. Everything the CPO named as overexpansion is listed above and removed from the critical path.
