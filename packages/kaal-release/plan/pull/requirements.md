# Pull Promotion: Requirements (target)

Scoped `pull/`. F are functional, N nonfunctional. The control is `pull Promotion`, a detective control: it states whether one collector is eligible for promotion and authorizes nothing. It uses Analyze Flow's words (piece, into, from, OPEN, REVIEW, REVISE, LAND, WAIT) and adds only the verdicts READY, BLOCKED and NOT JUDGED and the judgment statuses PASS, FAIL and UNKNOWN.

- **pull/F1. One script in the Skill, `pull Promotion`.** `skills/kaal-release/scripts/pull-promotion.mjs` exports `pullPromotion(facts, declaration, flow, collector)` and prints the same from a command line (`node pull-promotion.mjs <facts.json> <declaration.json> <flow.json> <collector>`). `SKILL.md` names it and the package carries it. It is read-only: it reads three files and prints. It is a separate file from `analyze-flow.mjs`, which it never imports: the two meet in data only (decision in analysis.md, section 4).
- **pull/F2. Inputs.** `facts` and `flow` are the data of Analyze Flow (`coordination/F2`, `coordination/F4`) as they are, for the whole repository. `declaration` is `coordination/F3` plus one section, `readiness`: `owners` (the consumer's word for the owner of each judgment), `closing` (the piece type that closes every increment), `increments` (per collector: the Change, the piece types its increment carries, and optionally `after`, the collectors it must follow), `changes` (per Change: the piece types that must be on the target for it to be complete), `checkOwners` (which judgment each named check belongs to). `collector` names the one collector to judge; the facts must hold it.
- **pull/F3. No competing state model.** The Script reads only the fields `coordination/F2` and `coordination/F4` define, plus three optional additions it requests from Analyze Flow's supplier: `piece.landedAt` and `delivery.landedAt` (when it landed), `collector.checks` (the checks on the collector's head, for a collector with no promotion piece yet), and one output field of Analyze Flow, `lane.active` (the collector of the active pair, `coordination/F8` made explicit). An absent addition is an unknown fact, never defaulted.
- **pull/F4. The verdict.** Five judgments plus attribution, in this order: `increment`, `verification`, `protection`, `sequencing`, `validation`, `attribution`. Each has an `owner` (declared), a `status` (PASS, FAIL, UNKNOWN), a `because` for every status but PASS, the `evidence` that caused it and the `missing` evidence. The verdict is BLOCKED when any judgment FAILs, else NOT JUDGED when any is UNKNOWN, else READY. UNKNOWN never passes and a FAIL is never softened by another judgment.
- **pull/F5. increment (owner: the Change).** The increment of a collector is the pieces of the declared Change that landed into it since its last delivery (`coordination/F13`; all of them when it has none). Every increment is closed by the piece the declaration names as `closing` (for KAAL, ACT): the work is green and the learning is logged before the collector can be promoted (Owner, 12 Nov 2026). PASS when every carried type and the closing piece have landed and no piece of the Change into the collector is still open; FAIL naming the missing types or open pieces; UNKNOWN when no increment or no `closing` piece is declared, or a landing time needed to place a piece against the last delivery is absent. A piece that cannot be placed makes the missing types UNKNOWN, not FAIL.
- **pull/F6. Three statements.** The result states separately: the **Change** (`complete` when every type its declaration names landed on the target; ACT is a fact of the Change and, separately, what closes each of its increments, `incomplete` naming the missing types, `not judged` when undeclared); the **increment** (F5); the **collector verdict** (F4). An incomplete Change does not by itself block an increment, and an undeclared Change does not decide the collector.
- **pull/F7. Checks are judged by their owner.** Each check named in `checkOwners` belongs to one judgment (for KAAL: Testing's `verification`, Guard's `protection`, Release's `sequencing`; others as declared). Evidence is the checks on the collector's open promotion piece, else the collector's own checks, else none. A declared check that is absent, running or pending is UNKNOWN and named; a failure is FAIL naming the check and traced to the declared owner; a check reported twice with different conclusions is UNKNOWN; a check that no owner is declared for is not judged: it is carried into `notJudged` and makes `attribution` UNKNOWN. The Script reads conclusions only, never logs or prose.
- **pull/F8. sequencing (owner: Release).** FAIL when the collector is behind the target, UNKNOWN when the distance is not in the facts, plus the checks of F7 declared to it. A wiring collector is ready only after its capability is done (Owner, 12 Nov 2026): an increment may declare `after`, the collectors whose current version must already be delivered (`coordination/F13`); one that is not delivered is FAIL naming it, and absent delivery facts make it UNKNOWN. This prevents cheating: a wiring that lands first lets the contract be weakened until it goes green. Whose turn it is comes from `lane.active`: `active`, `waiting` (naming the active collector) or `not judged`. The turn never changes the verdict: eligibility and turn are separate statements.
- **pull/F9. validation (owner: Review).** Each carried piece that landed must have been approved at the version it landed at. UNKNOWN when its review fact is absent or null, FAIL when it was approved at another version or changes were requested. The review of the promotion piece itself stays Analyze Flow's REVIEW finding.
- **pull/F10. Recommendation.** One `next` action in Analyze Flow's five words, executed by no one: a declared hold on the collector or its promotion piece gives WAIT with status `escalate` for the owner; READY with a promotion piece gives Analyze Flow's own finding for that piece (`source: flow`), never a second OPEN; READY without a piece, on the lane's turn, gives OPEN a PROMOTION for the author; READY off its turn gives WAIT naming the active collector; BLOCKED gives REVISE for the author naming each failing owner and cause; NOT JUDGED gives no action, only the `missing` prerequisites by owner. The text holds no command, no approval and no bypass.
- **pull/F11. Deterministic.** The same inputs give byte-identical output, whatever the order of the pieces, the checks and the lists. The output holds no time.
- **pull/F12. Readiness is not authorization.** The output has no approval, landing or authorization field. Authorities are only the declared roles; with none declared none is invented and the gap is reported. At most one collector's `next` is ready at once, because only the lane's collector can be OPEN; every other READY collector WAITs. The Owner's go on a promotion, the merge queue and the rulesets are untouched.
- **pull/F13. Runs where Node runs.** The command reads its files and nothing else (no environment, no network, no host credential), exits 0 whenever it printed a verdict (BLOCKED and NOT JUDGED are answers) and non-zero only when an input cannot be read or the collector is not in the facts, naming which.
- **pull/N1. Standalone.** No runtime dependency; the Script imports nothing but built-ins and not `analyze-flow.mjs`.
- **pull/N2. Generic.** The Script and its fixtures contain no KAAL-specific identity and no host term (`release/F5`); the KAAL declaration lives in the Delivery.
- **pull/N3. Honest.** The README states that the Script judges eligibility on the evidence it is given, recommends only, and verifies and authorizes nothing.
