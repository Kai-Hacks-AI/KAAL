# Pull Promotion: the history of a synchronized collector (target)

Scoped `pull/` (ids are global). Corrects pull/F31 (the history is a line by ancestry); nothing of pull/F25 to F34 is relaxed.

- **pull/F36. The history is the first-parent line from the head to the first commit already in `main`.** Given the commits the host reports as the collector's own (those reachable from the head and not from `main`), the head and the merge base:
  - the history is the first-parent ancestry of the head, followed while a commit is one of those commits, oldest first. Its last entry is the head. The commit before the first entry is a commit of `main`'s history and is not required to be the merge base;
  - a merge commit on that line is a SYNC only when every parent besides the first is a commit of `main` (the host confirms that `main` contains it) or a side commit of such a merge; parent count alone is not enough. A merge of anything else is the collector's own work: a development branch with work in it fails closed, and a merge of the collector's own earlier commit has its files read like any commit. A purported SYNC whose second parent `main` does not contain is refused;
  - a SYNC keeps its place in the order, but it is not read for evidence (its paths are empty), because what it brought in belongs to `main`. A SYNC pull request leaves a merge commit on the line whose second parent is the branch's own merge of `main`, which the host also lists as a collector commit (found by the first live run; amended in the DO). Such side commits are accepted only as merges of `main` that grow from the line (first parent on the line or on another such side commit, every other parent outside the collector's commits); they are not on the history and bring no evidence;
  - the order comes from ancestry, never from the order the host listed the commits, so a host list in any order gives the same history;
  - it fails closed, with "could not order the history" naming the commit, when the head is not among the commits, when a collector commit is neither on the line nor such a side commit (a merged branch commit that is not a merge of `main`, a merged branch that does not grow from the line, or a commit that is not an ancestor of the head), or when a commit's parents are not given. A commit the host left out of the list is guarded by the host's own count (a list shorter than its total is not read, as pull/F31 says); a collector with no commits of its own has an empty history. The collector is then reported as not judged, as pull/F31 says, and never passed.
  An unsynchronized collector gives the same history as before.
