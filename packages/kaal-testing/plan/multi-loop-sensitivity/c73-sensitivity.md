## 6. Reasoned Disagreement with Exploratory Baseline (`evidence.md`)

`evidence.md` listed 12 exploratory survivors as uniform "bounded test-gap candidates". Re-evaluating them under the `testing/C73` protocol shows two points of reasoned disagreement:

1. **Disagreement on inference (`M11`):** `evidence.md` candidate #11 flagged `permuted review evidence hash order falsely marked stale`. Under `README.md` (`#105–114`) and `lib/contracts.mjs` comments (`#1–25`), whether `review.evidence` hash sets are order-insensitive is **uncontracted** (`unknown` / `proposed clarification`), not an established contract gap. Pinning `.sort()` in `contracts.test.mjs` solely because the source line calls `.sort()` would invent an undocumented contract obligation.
2. **Disagreement on evidence method (`M04` & `M16`):** The exploratory baseline checked kills via `/ERR_ASSERTION/` over TAP text. While `/ERR_ASSERTION/` happens not to fire on `SyntaxError`, plain text scanning neither attributes kills to specific tests nor protects against accidental `TypeError` crashes vs. assertion kills (demonstrated by designing `M04` with `{ forEach() {} }` ahead of `null`, and explicitly classifying `M16` as `invalid` rather than `killed`).

## 8. BASS Judgment (`Bare < Agent < Skill < Script`)

Following KAAL's `BASS` definition (`packages/kaal-engineer-skill/payload/BASS.md` — choosing the level of determinism `Bare < Agent < Skill < Script` needed for each behaviour, noting that determinism does not confer authority, ownership, or correctness, and treating the sensitivity results above as evidence for this judgment rather than a substitute for it):

- **What belongs at Script (promoted and kept):** The 11 new deterministic contract checks (`M01`–`M10`, `M12`) added to `packages/kaal-testing/test/contracts.test.mjs` verify exact `problems()` and `classify()` invariants and must repeat identically on every `npm test` run. They belong at the **Script** level in the already-executed suite.
- **What stays at Skill (guidance, not Script):** Identifying which claims are authoritative (`M11` held as `unknown` pending Testing's clarification), designing witnesses that fail on the intended assertion rather than a secondary crash (`M03`, `M04`), keeping execution/load errors apart (`M16`), checking package consumers before changing behaviour, and deciding what evidence boundaries still require separate runs (`acceptance`, `boundary`, `measure`) all require engineering judgment and remain **Skill** guidance (`SKILL.md`).
- **Why a mutation runner Script is NOT earned:** Automating a reusable mutation/sensitivity runner (`skills/kaal-testing/scripts/` or a new export) is **not earned** from this single bounded exercise: generating meaningful mutations, non-crashing witnesses, and contract classifications depended heavily on judgment over one file rather than a stable, repeatable mechanical contract. No Script runner is added here; any future extraction must be earned by repeated real capability work and scoped as its own Change.
