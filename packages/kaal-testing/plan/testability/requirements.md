# Testability Assessment: Requirements

Each Requirement names, in brackets, the Intent it serves. `boundary.md` holds the research behind the boundary.

## Functional

- **testing/F129** [testing/I38]: **Verification, in three outcomes.** The Testing Skill has one section, placed before "The three loops". It says Testing serves verification: it establishes objectively whether a defined criterion is satisfied. It tells an agent to trace a claim to its owner, and then decide, in this order: **Verify** (an observable and an oracle two readers would accept, so a Case is written, automated or not), **Challenge** (too underspecified to decide, and its owner could reasonably make it decidable: put the question to that owner), **Point at judgement** (about whether criteria, decisions or outcomes are sound and fit for purpose: say it can only be judged, and name the evidence and who should judge). It names the four owners Intent, Requirements, Architecture and Defect. It names no other Skill and relies on none.
- **testing/F130** [testing/I38]: **No invention, no rewriting.** The section forbids inventing an oracle, a threshold or an answer key to obtain GREEN. Challenging is a recommendation to the owner: Testing does not rewrite a claim. A judged outcome is recorded as a judgement, never as a passing test, and an unclear claim as unknown, using the existing finding kinds. Testing owns the rule about keys and oracles before a Case exists. It does not judge delivered evidence, including how independent a key is of the work, which belongs to whoever judges the delivery.
- **testing/F133** [testing/I38]: **Reject a brittle Case.** The section tells an agent to reject a Case that checks incidental wording or an author's preferred answer instead of the concept, and to say what it should verify or who should judge.
- **testing/F131** [testing/I38]: **A trial of the installed section.** Whether the Skill helps is judged by a trial, not by Cases. Four claims are given to agents with and without the installed section, at least two runs per arm: a decidable one (E1), a judged one (E2), an underspecified one (E3) and a Case whose oracle is an author's key (E4, the Architecture T3/S4 situation). Each run records the approach, why, any unresolved question, the owner and question for a challenge, and for a Case under assessment whether it is rejected as brittle (pilot 2 showed the answer format has no place for that). Each record is a fresh agent's raw output, kept unedited, with its prompt hash. The Skill arm's prompt contains the installed section verbatim, and the baseline's does not.
- **testing/F132** [testing/I38]: **Disagreement is kept, not resolved.** The trial keeps the author's key, with its stated doubts, as one position. Every difference between the key and an answer, between arms and between runs is listed with each position. A difference is never settled by editing the key, the Skill or an answer, and Architecture's key for T3 is never tuned. A change to the trial's key after a run is recorded with its reason and who made it. The trial states what it cannot show.

## Nonfunctional

- **testing/N27** [testing/I38]: **Boundary.** The Change adds no export to kaal-testing, no Skill, no workflow or ruleset, no Node in `capability/` or `payload/`, and no new finding kind. It makes no reference to, dependency on or call of any other Skill. Pointing at what could be tested is the other side of the boundary and another Change. It changes `skills/kaal-testing/SKILL.md` by the one section, and nothing else of the delivered package except the evidence under `plan/`.
- **testing/N28** [testing/I38]: **Cases that do not verify wording.** The Cases that read the Skill check its structure and its boundary, never a sentence. The Skill's effect is judged by the trial.
