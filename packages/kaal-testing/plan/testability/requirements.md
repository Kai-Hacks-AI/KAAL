# Testability Assessment: Requirements

Each Requirement names, in brackets, the Intent it serves.

## Functional

- **testing/F129** [testing/I38]: **Three outcomes, in order.** The Testing Skill has one section, placed before "The three loops", that tells an agent to trace a claim to its owner and then decide, in this order: **deterministic** (an observable and an oracle that two readers would accept), **judgment** (evidence, comparison or independent review, named with who judges), **pushback** (the owning concern named, with the question). It draws the boundary in these words: a test is proof, a review is judgement, and some claims can only be judged. Testing points at a claim a test cannot prove as one that can only be judged, and names who should judge. It names no other Skill and relies on none. It names the four owners Intent, Requirements, Architecture and Defect.
- **testing/F130** [testing/I38]: **No invented oracle, no rewriting.** What cannot be proven is pointed at as judgement, not given an invented oracle. The section forbids inventing an oracle, a threshold or an answer key to obtain GREEN. It makes pushback a recommendation to the owner: Testing does not rewrite a claim. It records a judgment outcome as a judgement, never as a passing test, and an unclear claim as unknown, using the existing finding kinds. The new section does not use the word "validation": the existing Skill's pair verification and validation is a KAAL convention (proven versus judged), not the IEEE 1012 meaning, and this Change does not rest on it. Testing owns the rule about keys and oracles before a Case exists. It does not judge delivered evidence, including how independent a key is of the work, which belongs to whoever reviews the delivery.
- **testing/F131** [testing/I38]: **A trial of the installed section.** Judgment is shown by a trial, not by Cases alone. The three contrasting claims (a deterministic one, a judgment one, a pushback one) are given to agents with and without the installed section, at least two runs per arm. Each run records the approach, why, any unresolved question, and the owner and question for a pushback. Each record is a fresh agent's raw output, kept unedited, with its prompt hash. The Skill arm's prompt contains the installed section verbatim, and the baseline's does not.
- **testing/F132** [testing/I38]: **Disagreement is kept, not resolved.** The trial keeps the author's key, with its stated doubts, as one position. Every difference between the key and an answer, between arms and between runs is listed with each position. A difference is never settled by editing the key, the Skill or an answer. A change to the key after a run is recorded with its reason and who made it. The trial states what it cannot show.

## Nonfunctional

- **testing/N27** [testing/I38]: **Boundary.** The Change adds no export to kaal-testing, no Skill, no workflow or ruleset, no Node in `capability/` or `payload/`, and adds no new finding kind. It makes no reference to, dependency on or call of any other Skill. Pointing at what could be tested is the other side of the boundary and another Change. It changes `skills/kaal-testing/SKILL.md` by the one section, and nothing else of the delivered package except the evidence under `plan/`.
