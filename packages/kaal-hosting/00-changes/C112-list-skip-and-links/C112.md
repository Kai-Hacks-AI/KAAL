# hosting/C112 Hosting lists a directory tree without the names and directory links a caller says to leave out

Testing's Case catalog lists every `T<n>` Case file of a checkout. Direction of the CPO: the catalog stays behind Hosting and reads no `node:fs`. Today `list(dir)` cannot do that on a normal checkout: it refuses a link to a directory with `INVALID_PATH`, which every `node_modules` holds (workspace links), and it cannot leave out `node_modules` or `.git`, so it throws or walks the world. This Change gives `list` two options, backward compatible, and nothing else: no read option, no synchronous variant.

Requested by Testing (its C74 DO waits on it) through the Hosting thread, 21 November 2026.

Provisional ids: C112, hosting/I20, F90 to F92, A40 and T297 to T302 were checked against `main` and every remote branch on 21 November 2026 (highest C111, I19, F89, N13, A39, T296); they are confirmed again at the DO. No new Nonfunctional Requirement: N10 (no dependency, one meaning of a portable name) already binds `list`.
