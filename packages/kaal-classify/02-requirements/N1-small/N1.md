# classify/N1 small

The first delivery is one compact Agent Skill, a minimal example and a key of Cases, and nothing around them. It introduces no metamodel, no workflow engine, no governance process and no gate in Continuous Integration. It reuses Intent, Requirement, Architecture, Gap, Change and Case as they are. Testing owns Case design and the relationship vocabulary is Graph's, and the Skill cites both and defines neither. The Skill is not deployed to any place where agents discover skills and is not published.
