# classify/N1 small

One compact Agent Skill, a key and Cases, and nothing around them: no metamodel, no workflow engine, no governance process, no gate in Continuous Integration. It reuses Intent, Requirement, Architecture, Gap, Change and Case as they are. Testing owns Case design and Graph the relationship vocabulary, and the Skill cites both. It is not deployed to where agents discover skills and is not published.
