# classify/F2 mixed-concerns

A statement whose parts answer the two questions differently is split into one statement per concern and semantics, and each is placed in its own element. It is not classified by its dominant part.

A term that can mean two concerns, such as "mandatory", "enforced", "pipeline" or "deployed", is named as ambiguous and put to its author before it is classified. It is never guessed. Incorrect or ambiguous terminology, and a requirement that mixes Solution, Delivery and Change, are challenged before they become an implementation plan.
