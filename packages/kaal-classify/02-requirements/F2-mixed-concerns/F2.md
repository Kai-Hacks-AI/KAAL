# classify/F2 mixed-concerns

A statement whose parts answer the two questions differently is split into one statement per concern and semantics, each placed in its own element, and never classified by its dominant part. A term that can mean two concerns, such as "mandatory", "enforced" or "deployed", is named as ambiguous and put to its author, never guessed. Both are challenged before they become an implementation plan.
