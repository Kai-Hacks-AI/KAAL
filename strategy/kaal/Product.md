# KAAL

KHAIs Artificial Agent League: a way for agents and humans to work on a product with governed, verifiable change, and a set of capabilities that other repositories can take or embed.

Strategy is organized outside to inside by three Directions, the mnemonic **TAKE CORE** (with the deliberate hint to use `kaal-core` where appropriate). The directions are KAAL-specific and are not generic structures.

## Outcomes

- [TAKE: External KAAL](outcomes/take.md)
- [EMBED: Embedded capabilities](outcomes/embed.md)
- [CORE: Engineer KAAL](outcomes/core.md)

## Boundary

Knowledge and capabilities flow outward. External applications and embedded consumers may provide observations, findings and lessons that inform KAAL, but their content never becomes authoritative KAAL content directly. Inward adoption requires an explicit KAAL-owned [Decision](decisions/) and its governed Change.
