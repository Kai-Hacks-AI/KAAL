# Direction (KAAL-defined)

A KAAL-specific grouping of Outcomes by where they act relative to KAAL: TAKE (outside), EMBED (into others and into KAAL), CORE (inside). Defined and owned by KAAL, in KAAL's strategy folder. It is a consumer extension, not a generic Roadmap or Product type; no generic capability may depend on it.
