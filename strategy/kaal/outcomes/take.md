# TAKE: External KAAL (Transfer Agent Knowledge)

An agent learns from KAAL, for example by examining its repository, and uses that learning to accomplish something in an external environment X. This is knowledge transfer, not necessarily direct application or installation of KAAL; other mechanisms remain possible.

Observations from X are inputs to KAAL and stay with their owner. They become KAAL content only through a KAAL-owned Decision.

- Draws on: [CORE](core.md) for learnable KAAL knowledge (the repository as it stands). This is not a dependency on completed engineering; hard prerequisites are left open until a first trial subject is declared.
