# Retro

## Learned

I learned that bounded context is a contract across the whole navigation surface: output size, stable addresses, continuation and refusal have to hold together. A bounded card function did not bound a command whose evidence note could grow with the corpus. The small-budget case then showed that reserving space is insufficient unless an impossible budget is refused. Those were consequences of one contract, and reviewing them as isolated symptoms cost further rounds.

I learned to distinguish the authority of each check. Core admission did not validate body citations; a seal's presence did not establish a valid marker; a BRAIN's internal validity did not establish preservation against a baseline. Naming those boundaries made the architecture and the refusal probes more precise.

## Liked

I liked being able to extract the committed prototype blocks verbatim and exercise them in an installed-artifact host. Exact commands, identities and byte counts made findings reproducible and gave me evidence for resolving them. The duplicated-script failure also showed why the published evidence itself had to be executable.

I liked that the Owner's decisions on readable names, movable locations and evidence beyond Changes changed the requirements explicitly. The result preserved Core and Sealing boundaries while remaining an architecture Change, with implementation and the first Learnings kept for later Work.

## Lacked

I lacked one replayable evidence bundle at the start of review. I reconstructed host fixtures and coverage across rounds, and the full output invariant became explicit only after several narrower failures. I should have exercised the public boundary and the complete refusal matrix together earlier. Six rounds found real defects, but reaching those cases one at a time made my review more expensive.

The Worker's account of rebuilding regression confidence by hand and the Owner's account of the review cost give me two distinct perspectives on that same practical limitation. Neither replaces the independent checks from my seat.

## Longed

I long for an unfamiliar Reviewer to reach the relevant capability Nodes, prior Changes and reusable Learnings through the first bounded BRAIN read side, without relying on this session's remembered IDs. I want the accumulated retrospective knowledge to be discoverable where later Work needs it, while preserving the practice that not every finding becomes a Learning.

I would like the next realization to explore CEIL's cue, evidence, insight and learning distinctions in actual use, with deterministic mechanics and Markdown understanding. That is a future possibility to examine before the first Learning Nodes are born; it gives me no reason to revise this sealed Work.
