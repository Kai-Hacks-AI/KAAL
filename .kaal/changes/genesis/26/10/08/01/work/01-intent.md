# Intent

KAAL Review Skill

Establish "kaal-review" as an independent, composable KAAL Skill.

Review is a capability, not a role. Any KAAL role may review a result, provided the reviewing actor holds the appropriate authority and independence.

The Skill shall support reviewing results from any KAAL viewpoint or capability, not only Work within Changing KAAL.

Preserve the distinction:

- Review provides the reusable capability to examine a result, report findings and establish convergence.
- ROWING governs the interaction between Work and Review within a Change.
- Changing KAAL governs the Change lifecycle and consumes Review where appropriate.
- Retro remains a separate capability for learning after the activity.

Do not introduce the six role Skills, redesign ROWING, or duplicate review machinery unnecessarily.

Let the Work determine the smallest reusable Review capability and how Changing KAAL consumes it.
