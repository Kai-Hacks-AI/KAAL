# Retro

## Learned

Intent is primarily a discipline of establishing what is wanted and why, not a document-generation mechanism. Its identity can be exact while the adequacy of its meaning remains a matter of judgment. Review can examine Intent as another identified result without either capability owning the other.

## Liked

The Skill primarily serves the Owner while remaining optional and reusable beyond Changing. It avoids a mandatory template, a new Role meaning and a duplicate identity model. Changing retains its fixed-target discipline, and the independent Reviewer found and resolved a real mismatch between the described and accepted format.

## Lacked

A real Owner-side exercise of the Skill on a new Intent outside the Change machinery. The relationship between an Intent established elsewhere and the copy carried into a Change is still expressed as a discipline rather than mechanically verified. That is an observation, not a blocker or an invitation to enlarge this Change.

## Longed

For the six-D capabilities to grow around their own reviewable results, with Intent as the starting point, without becoming six mandatory gates. I also want actual Owner use to show whether the Skill improves clarity and handoff before adding storage, sealing or stronger enforcement.
