# Intent — KAAL Intent Skill

Establish `kaal-intent` as an independent, optional KAAL Skill for describing and establishing Intent.

The Skill primarily serves the Owner, helping articulate **what is wanted and why**, including desired outcomes and boundaries, without prematurely defining Requirements, Architecture or implementation.

Intent shall be a reviewable result, usable beyond Changing KAAL. Where Changing consumes Intent, its fixed-target discipline must remain intact.

Do not introduce Roles, prescribe a universal document template, or redesign Changing or ROWING.

Let the Work determine the smallest reusable Intent capability and how Changing KAAL can consume it.
