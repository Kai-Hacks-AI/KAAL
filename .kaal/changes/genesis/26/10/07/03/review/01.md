## Findings

The Skill largely carries the Intent's test: no step still ends in "stop and ask", roles stay distinct from actors, Review placement is open, the handoff is not bound to a filename, the Owner's acts are explicit, and the diff touches only the Skill, its projection and the Change's work/. `check-kaal-install`, `check-kaal-seals` and the 32 kaal-changing tests pass. The findings below are narrower.

1. **The condition that releases continuation is not one an actor can test (Intent test; R5, R6, R7).** Step 7 says continuation starts "once the Owner's judgment and `retro-owner.md` exist". The judgment is by design no artifact, and the Skill says a handoff, a green check or silence is not that judgment. It never says what an actor may take as evidence that the Owner's act happened. The only available evidence is the Owner's own text, supplied to the actor or already in `retro-owner.md`. Without that sentence an actor cannot tell "the Owner has acted" from "I must keep waiting", which is the router friction the Intent names. Step 6 does not say that after `retro-work.md` the Owner's acts are the real stop, and that the actor presents the sealed Work and the retro to the Owner and waits only for the Owner's own text. Please state, in step 6 or in the Handoff and continuation section, that (a) the Owner's act is shown to the actor only by the Owner's own text, and (b) before it arrives this is the one stop. The Architecture's "Open to Review" asks exactly this; the Skill should answer it.

2. **Convergence is not carried forward between actors, and the Requirements and Architecture claim more than the steps say (R1, R6; Architecture "Where it lands"; Evidence "R1, R2, R3").** R6 says that once a round converges, sealing and `retro-work.md` follow without another go. Step 4 (seal) is unchanged and names no role. Step 3 now says that on findings, two actors "each hand the Change to the other", but a `converged` round has no such sentence. With a separate Reviewer, nothing says who seals or that the Reviewer hands to, or itself continues as, the Worker. The only coverage is the generic bullet "a converged round or a sealed Work is not a reason to ask again". The Architecture says the edit touched "steps 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8", but step 8 is unchanged in the diff, and the Evidence lists 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and omits the Handoff and continuation bullets that actually carry R6 for sealing. Please add one sentence to step 4 or step 3 on who proceeds after a converged round, and make the Architecture's list of steps match the diff.

3. **"Intent as described" in the Worker's progression blurs the Intent's ownership (R1, R5; constraint "Keep authority explicit").** Step 2 lists the Worker's progression as "the Intent as described, Requirements defined, ...", a verbatim carry-over from R1. Read beside "the Worker does not own or revise it", a reader can take the Worker to be a participant in describing the Intent. Please reword the step so the Intent is the given starting point of the Worker's progression and not one of its stages (for example, begin from the described Intent handed over, then Requirements and onward).

4. **Small inconsistencies with retro.md and rowing.md (Intent test; R7).** `retro.md` still says `retro-owner.md` is "written ... by the Owner", and the Skill's record tree says "written second, by the Owner". Step 6 and rowing.md now allow another actor to carry the Owner's text verbatim. No contradiction is intended, but neither file says "or carried verbatim by another actor". Add a short clause in `retro.md`, or point to step 6, so the two do not read as conflicting. The same holds for `rowing.md`, which lists the Owner's acts but not the carry rule.
