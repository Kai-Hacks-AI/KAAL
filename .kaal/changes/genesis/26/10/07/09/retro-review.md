# Retro

## Learned

A passing capability suite did not establish the integrity claim I was reviewing. The original checker treated a dangling carriers symlink as absence and accepted a corrupt empty or unreached record. Challenging that specific boundary found a defect that the existing tests and Worker-side probes had missed.

Review also required keeping three judgments separate: whether the implementation answered the Intent, whether the Work described the implementation accurately, and whether the candidate could be admitted. The installer contradiction and the unconditional passing-suite claim were findings about the Work's fidelity. The missing concurrent Change was an admission dependency, not a reason to redesign collection.

The authority to review came from the Owner's assignment, not from the account or tools available to me. The withdrawn Worker-appointed rounds could remain useful testing experience without acquiring the standing of independent review.

## Liked

The Worker resolved the findings in the existing Change, changed the mutable Requirements and Architecture where they were inaccurate, and left the fixed Intent and earlier round untouched. That made the second review an assessment of concrete resolutions rather than a negotiation over the target.

I could exercise the shipped script directly, including carriers created by the admitted Incident and Request scripts, binary bytes, and simultaneous attempts at one client. These probes strengthened the evidence without needing a transport implementation or access to a real client.

The Work identity and append-only rounds gave each review a precise object. Reading the Worker and Owner retros afterwards added perspectives without changing what either round had observed.

## Lacked

I had no observed external-client collection on which to judge the complete relationship between authorized access, exposure and stable client naming. The producer integration proved more than hand-built fixtures, but it still ran within a controlled local environment.

The final green full-suite result after reconciliation was available as a Worker report on the PR, outside the Change's frozen Work. I could retain the earlier validation faithfully and see why the dependency cleared, but the record did not give that later validation a durable place of its own.

The Work seal identifies the Change-local support, not the entire shipped implementation. My rounds therefore also named the realization heads I inspected; the state evaluator alone cannot establish that later code still realizes the reviewed Work.

## Longed

For a future review to follow one willing client's carriers through two authorized means of access and verify that client identity remains stable while reachability and adapter evidence change. That would test the distinction this Change established under actual use.

For later validation evidence to accumulate without rewriting the Work on which review converged, while remaining tied to the realization that was tested. I would want that possibility explored through ordinary Changes rather than repaired retrospectively here.

For the operational separation of seats to become routine: an assigned Reviewer receives the Work, challenges its claims, and returns resolvable findings without being chosen or steered by the Worker.
