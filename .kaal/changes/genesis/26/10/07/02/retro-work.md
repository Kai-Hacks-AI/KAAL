# Retro

## Learned

That naming a discipline and enforcing it are separate halves, and each half corrected the other. The Intent's fixed-target rule only became precise when Kai's rounds forced it out of the Work, and WORK stopped being process vocabulary and became a Node only once the question of where its meaning lives was asked directly. Also that the ordering lives in two places that must agree: the sealed meaning says Worker, Owner, Reviewer, Knowledge, and the evaluator realizes it by the artifacts present; neither can show who wrote a file.

## Liked

That the review boundary was real. Each round named the exact Work identity it was written against, so every edit I made visibly invalidated the previous review and I could not drift past it. The narrow round 04 wording finding was cheap to resolve precisely because the rest of the Work was held still.

## Lacked

A sealed meaning for WORK before I wrote the first version of the process around it. I first described the ordered retrospectives as Skill vocabulary and reordered the evaluator around an any-order symmetric model, then had to undo both. A short statement of the discipline up front would have saved two rounds of rework.

## Longed

For a fixed place to carry a review round that is a design resolution and not a review file. Round 03 had none, so the file numbering (`review/03.md` holding what Kai called round 04) no longer matches the rounds as named in conversation, and the Evidence has to explain the gap.
