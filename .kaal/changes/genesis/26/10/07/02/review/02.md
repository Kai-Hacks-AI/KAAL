### Finding — after Work is fixed, retrospective learning is ordered WORK

The current Work still treats the three retrospectives as equal, any-order artifacts and places the Owner's decision only after Change seal. That misses the intended knowledge flow and the useful second mnemonic.

ROWING governs the mutable inner loop:

`Work ⇄ Review → convergence → seal Work`

After Work is sealed, **WORK** governs retrospective learning:

`Worker → Owner → Reviewer → Knowledge`

Use the mnemonic explicitly:

- **W — Worker**
- **O — Owner**
- **R — Reviewer**
- **K — Knowledge**

These are not three interchangeable retrospectives. Their order is meaningful because knowledge accumulates.

1. **Worker retro first — `retro-work.md`.** The Worker reflects on performing the now-sealed Work: the inside experience.
2. **Owner judgment against Intent.** The Owner receives/observes the sealed Work and the Worker's perspective and judges whether the Work answers the fixed Intent. This judgment is not a retrospective artifact and must not be encoded as approval metadata in KAAL.
3. **Owner retro second — `retro-owner.md`.** It is written *after* that judgment. It therefore carries no approval/verdict. It is deliberately opinionated by the Owner's position: what the approved/accepted Work taught the Owner about the Product / Intent. That learning may inform a future Intent, but cannot revise this Change's fixed Intent.
4. **Reviewer retro third — `retro-review.md`.** The Reviewer reflects last, after the Worker and Owner perspectives are available, on what was learned through reviewing this Work. This completes the retrospective synthesis.
5. The accumulated result is **Knowledge**. Then the Change can be sealed.

So replace the Round-01-derived symmetric `RETROS INCOMPLETE` / any-order model with process states that encode the meaningful sequence. The exact labels are implementation detail, but the evaluator must require:

`WORK SEALED → retro-work → retro-owner → retro-review → retros/knowledge complete → seal Change`

A later retro may read the earlier perspectives; unlike the previous design, avoiding that reading is not the goal. Accumulation is the point.
