### Finding 1 — the Owner loop is outside the Change; Intent is not renegotiated inside Review

The current Work says the Owner owns Intent and stays outside the Worker ⇄ Reviewer inner loop, but Q12 / `rowing.md` also says that if Review finds the Intent itself wrong, the Owner may revise the Intent in `work/` and the same Change continues.

That collapses the outer Owner loop into the Change.

The intended shape is:

`Intent → [ Work ⇄ Review → sealed/closed Work Result ] → Accept Work Result`

The Owner gives the Intent; Worker and Reviewer row against that target; the closed Change is returned to the Owner; only then does the Owner accept/reject the Work Result. Review judges realization **against Intent**. It does not challenge, redesign or renegotiate Intent as part of the Change.

So make this explicit throughout Intent / Requirements / Architecture / Skill/reference text:

- Intent is the fixed target of the Change.
- Worker does not change Intent.
- Reviewer does not change Intent.
- Owner does not enter the inner Work ⇄ Review loop to revise Intent.
- A review finding may establish that the Intent **cannot be delivered as stated**. In that exceptional case the Change cannot converge as a successful realization; do not “resolve” it by editing Intent inside the same Change.
- What the Owner does with an undeliverable/unsuccessful result is outside the Change — including establishing a different Intent in another Change.

This is the outer loop: **Intent → Accept Work Result**. The Change realizes the target; it does not negotiate the target.
