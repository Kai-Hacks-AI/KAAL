# Retro

## Learned

That Review needed two boundaries, not one: ROWING gives the mutable Work/Review loop its discipline, while WORK gives the learning after the Work is fixed its discipline. Keeping the Owner outside ROWING but inside WORK makes the model substantially clearer: Intent remains fixed during realization, yet the Owner still gets a distinct opportunity to learn from what that Intent actually produced.

## Liked

That the process became simpler as the roles became sharper. The strongest result is not another approval mechanism; it is the opposite. Review convergence, Owner judgment, retrospection, Change closure and host admission are now different acts with different purposes. In particular, keeping my judgment out of the artifact model prevents KAAL from pretending it can prove a human or agent decision that its evidence cannot actually establish.

## Lacked

The distinction between a process role and an artifact producer was not explicit enough at the start. That ambiguity is what made the early symmetric-retro model look plausible and made Owner acceptance drift toward admission mechanics. The final Work resolves this, but reaching it required several rounds that a clearer role model could have avoided.

## Longed

For the next Changes to exercise ROWING and WORK without being about ROWING and WORK. This Change necessarily bootstraps the disciplines while defining them, so it proves that the machinery can govern itself. The more valuable evidence will be an ordinary future Change where these boundaries disappear into the normal way KAAL works.
