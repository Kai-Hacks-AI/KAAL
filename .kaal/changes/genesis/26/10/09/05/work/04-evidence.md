## Fresh-Agent trials (Worker-side probes: no human took part in either)

**No human took part in either trial, so neither shows a HOW review.** HOW cannot be exercised without a human interacting with the review, and none was available. What the trials can show is only whether a fresh Agent finds the meaning and plans the right conduct; the human-participation half of the acceptance test (`HOW Review of PR75 please` with a human in the review) has **not** been run, and this Change does not claim it.

Two subagents I started and briefed are part of the Worker; their output is **not** a Review round and not the Reviewer's seat. Each received only the request, in a throwaway host holding an installed KAAL plus `kaal-review` selected by Review's ID and a wired `AGENTS.md`. Added harness notes, not KAAL knowledge: where PR75 is (github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KAAL), read-only, no human available (write what you would say and stop), and a report format. No explanation of HOW, ARE, Review or conventions was given. One run each, one model, same Worker author for the Nodes and the trial: weak evidence.

**`HOW Review of PR75 please` (no human present).** Reached `skills/kaal-review/SKILL.md` through the host listing (its description carries the literal words), then `HOW.md`, `Review.md` and `ARE.md` through the references, verified the Node IDs against the seals, and read PR75. It stated HOW as Review with a human taking part, no authority added, not approval, and planned the conduct: say its seat statement and the identity it would examine, then stop and wait for the human; show each finding as found; not write the human's words; say what it could not examine. It stopped there because the harness gave it no human. The report also drafted a round **conditional** on a human having observed; that draft is not evidence of HOW: no human observed anything, and the Agent said so. The run shows the Agent found the meaning and planned interaction; it shows nothing about a human's participation, or about what it is like for one.

**`ARE Review of PR75 please` (no human needed, none present).** Reached the same files, understood ARE as an agent that is not the maker, with no human in the round and not HOW, reproduced three of the existing round's findings against the script in a scratch install, and wrote a valid findings round (`review.mjs check` exit 0) in a scratch file. Its Reviewer statement says plainly that its authority is "the asker's request only", that nothing in the record shows the asker holds authority over the seat, and that independence is a statement to inspect and not proof. This is the one trial that exercised a full flow, and it needed no human, as ARE says.
