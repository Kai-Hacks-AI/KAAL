# Retro

## Learned

The essential escalation is from Agent Work to independent autonomous Agent review and, when the delegated loop cannot legitimately settle the matter, to HOW — Human Observes Work. Reviewer findings are evidence to judge, not orders to implement. A finding can be valid and still outside the current authorized scope; preserving its source, evidence and disposition allows future governed Work without destabilizing the present Change. Deterministic Process states can govern iteration and escalation, but must distinguish Worker-reported evidence from independently verified authority.

## Liked

The five-round independent review materially strengthened the implementation. Concrete refusal and recovery probes exposed issues that passing acceptance tests initially missed. The resulting Process demonstrates challenge, deferral, reconsideration, dispute escalation and preserved finding histories without enlarging Core or introducing a generic findings registry. I particularly value that the Worker and Reviewer kept their responsibilities separate and that the live Codex trial's limits were stated rather than disguised as full agreement.

## Lacked

I lacked a complete end-to-end demonstration in which an independently sourced Owner grant and host records establish AGREED without relying on a Worker-supplied verification boundary. The live Codex trial proved a useful interaction but not the entire trust chain. I also lacked a practical, already-established way for a fresh Agent to discover the Process and choose the appropriate environment-specific review mechanism without reconstructing instructions from our conversation. These are honest 0.0.1 limitations, not a reason to reopen converged Work.

## Longed

I long for KAAL to make this escalation pattern ordinary: an Agent works, independent Agents challenge it, and HOW is invoked only when the Process requires human judgment or authority. I want valid but out-of-scope findings preserved for later Work, and I want a fresh Agent to discover and use this governance through KAAL's knowledge and capabilities, with vendor-specific interactions supplied by suitable Extensions or adapters. The next integrations should demonstrate that behavior across the six Ds without turning review into endless grinding or uncontrolled scope expansion.
