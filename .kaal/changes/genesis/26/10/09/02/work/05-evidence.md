# Evidence — Inner Loop and HOW Escalation

Everything here was run on this branch. Counts are from `npm test` in `engineering/kaal-process-agree` (48 acceptance tests), the package (3 of 3), and the root suite. Where something was not shown, it says so.

## 1. The three outcomes of the brief (scripted Reviewer, `acceptance/scripts.test.ts`)

1. **Autonomous convergence, no human step.** `begin` → `submit` → `request` → `relay converged` → `REPORTED` (exit 0) → with the host's records `AGREED`.
2. **Continued autonomous iteration after findings.** `relay findings` → `REVISE` (exit 0, 1 of 3 rounds used) → `submit` a revision → `REVIEW` → `relay converged`. A second test runs six findings rounds, each on a new subject, under a budget of seven, and still continues: the count is not the criterion.
3. **Escalation to HOW on an established criterion.** Budget 2: two findings rounds → `HOW`, reason `ceiling`, exit 3, and the brief:

```
HOW
next: a human directs: the Worker does not continue, conclude or escalate on its own
reason: ceiling: the rounds the Owner granted are spent without agreement
detail: 2 of the 2 rounds granted for this window have found something
subject: loop/log/03-subject.md 7cd123f0…
01 subject d4d04e8a
02 round findings by chatgpt-codex-connector[bot] d4d04e8a (loop/log/02-round.md)
03 subject 7cd123f0
04 round findings by chatgpt-codex-connector[bot] 7cd123f0 (loop/log/04-round.md)
direct with: agree.mjs direct loop --reason ceiling --direction continue --rounds <n> --words <text|@file>  (or --direction stop)
```

The Worker's `submit` and `relay` are then refused and write nothing; a direction naming another reason is refused; the right one with `continue --rounds 2` returns the loop to `REVISE` with a new window (0 of 2 used); `stop` ends it (`STOPPED`).

## 2. Each condition, and the refusals (same file)

`unrevised`, `oscillation`, `ceiling` (a budget of 1), `seat` (the Worker, another agent and a subagent as Actor), `contradiction` (reachable only via a human `continue` on `unrevised`; settled by the human who answers it, and the retry can conclude with all three rounds still in the log), `evidence` (a gap, a changed subject, two subjects in a row, a non-Intent, a forged round with no Actor), `grant` (none; one actor on both seats), `provenance` (below). Also: a report whose reviewed bytes are not the current subject is refused and writes nothing; findings that say "approved, converged, agreed" do not agree; a hand-written grant with no log is an empty loop; `grant` and structural `evidence` are not continued by a direction but a human can end them (`stop.md`, log untouched) or the record is restored; a Worker event added after HOW is ignored and a later direction is still found (both `stop` and `continue`); 50 findings rounds produce event `100-round.md` and still derive.

## 3. Reported is not verified (responds to the Owner's review of the first design)

- A Worker-written convergence is `REPORTED`, never `AGREED`.
- With host records (`state --host <records.json> --owner <login>`): refused as `provenance` when there is no such comment at the host, it was made by someone else, it is of another commit, it implies the other result (a review where the round says converged; a comment that is not an all-clear); when the grant or a direction is not shown as authored by `--owner`; when the Worker authored its own grant with a budget of 99; when the Worker wrote its own continuation (`03-how.md`). Codex's own all-clear comment counts; the "create an environment" comment does not.
- **Trust boundary:** the host's records, supplied by a party the Worker does not control. **Limit:** a Worker that also supplies the records can forge them. Not shown: a CI control that fetches the records (a separate `.github` Change).

## 4. The live Codex round (R27)

Loop at `work/trial/loop`. Grant: Worker `claude-worker (…)`, Reviewer `chatgpt-codex-connector[bot]`, 3 rounds; its words say it covers the trial only and is not a Reviewer assignment for the Change.

| Event | What happened |
|---|---|
| `01-subject` v1 | A deliberately imperfect Intent that contains a CLI recipe. |
| request 1 | Generated by `agree.mjs request`, posted on this PR (comment 6081715605). |
| `02-round` findings | Codex review 5470597438 on 846a90d: one inline P2, "Move the execution recipe out of the Intent". Recorded with `relay` (source `review 5470597438`), findings verbatim. State `REVISE`, exit 0. A real finding, correctly anchored to the Intent standard. |
| `03-subject` v2 | The Worker removed the recipe. |
| request 2 | Posted (comment 6081967028). |
| `04-round` converged | Codex comment 6081998683 on bfa83fb: "Codex Review: Didn't find any major issues." Recorded with `relay`. State `REPORTED`. |

**What this shows:** a fresh Worker can generate a targeted request, get a real independent finding, respond, and continue by itself to a recorded all-clear, without the Owner reconstructing anything.

**What it does not, stated plainly:**
- The all-clear was **not** in the requested words (`No findings`); Codex says "Didn't find any major issues". I extended the mapping to include that exact phrasing (tested), after seeing it. That is a rule fitted to an observation of one run.
- The all-clear is a review of the PR head `bfa83fb`, not demonstrably of the one file I asked for. The pinned bytes make the recorded subject exact, but "examined only this file" is Codex's to honour and I cannot show it did.
- Codex also reviewed other heads automatically or on other requests (3f5169b, whose findings on the code were real and fixed; 6f4228a on the design). Those are information, not rounds in this loop.
- **The verified run refuses it.** Fetching the host's record of who authored `grant.md` (commit 846a90d, author login `claude`) and running `state --host … --owner ChBrain` gives `HOW`, reason `provenance`: "grant.md is not shown to be authored by the Owner 'ChBrain'". That is correct: the Worker wrote that grant. The same records with the grant attributed to the Owner's login give `AGREED`, which shows the mechanics only. Whether the Worker's pushes (made with the account Kai uses) can be told from Kai's is the host's limit, not the Process's.
- Escalation to HOW was shown with the scripted Reviewer only; Codex's answer cannot be made to escalate.
- No literal replay of the 07/07 records was run. `seat` and `contradiction` are shown on constructed records of that shape.
