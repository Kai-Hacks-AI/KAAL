# Retro

## Learned

Persistent Collection requires separating what was observed from what can be asserted about identity. Identical bytes do not establish identical communications, and repeated use of a client name does not prove that the client is the same. Keeping the Record location independent of the Engine is essential for External KAAL.

## Liked

The Work converged through six independent Review rounds without enlarging Core or inventing a client registry. The implementation reuses existing Collection mechanics, preserves source evidence and provides explicit refusal behavior. The final path-handling findings demonstrate why independent review matters even when the normal acceptance suite passes.

## Lacked

I lacked an established, independently verified client-identity mechanism and a richer way to navigate persistent records across multiple collection histories. Those capabilities are deliberately outside this 0.0.1 Change. The repeated filesystem-path finding also showed that the initial tests did not cover enough equivalent path spellings.

## Longed

I want a fresh Agent using External KAAL to collect evidence from a client, preserve Requests and Incidents, and return later to find the same records without requiring the Owner to reconstruct their history. I also want this capability to integrate naturally with BRAIN, Work selection and Learning while keeping collection evidence distinct from judgments about what it means.
