# Retro

## Learned

I learned that identity claims must be tested against concrete creation acts. The existing Request writer produced two paths with identical bytes, which exposed an unjustified communication-identity claim in a plausible storage model. I also learned that an explicit Record boundary must survive equivalent path spellings: the first link guard inspected different paths from those written for a trailing slash or a relative argument. Reproducing both cases with the shipped script gave me evidence to keep the finding open until the component-based fix held.

## Liked

I liked the final use of existing Collection mechanics and strict forms rather than another capability. The Work kept source paths, raw observations and byte hashes inspectable while making continuity an explicit collector assertion. The Worker reproduced the findings, recorded the corrections and retained the distinction between evidence storage and judgment. Reading the Owner's retrospective confirmed that these limits were intentional for 0.0.1 rather than omissions to repair by expanding this Change.

## Lacked

I lacked an effective handoff to the Worker after recording findings on the branch. The Worker's retrospective explains that the expected red admission check obscured the newly recorded rounds. I added rounds 04 and 05 against unchanged Work; they confirmed the existing defect but added no new resolution evidence. I should have distinguished a fresh rereview from checking whether revised Work had arrived sooner. I also tested the plain absolute destination before testing equivalent spellings, which delayed discovery of the incomplete first fix.

## Longed

I would like future reviews to exercise the complete input boundary early: equivalent path spellings, missing mappings, repeated source paths and explicit continuity assertions. I want the next real External collection to demonstrate persistence through a different adapter and let a fresh agent recover the relevant history from the Record. The Worker and Owner both point toward easier navigation of that evidence; I would like that explored as later Work without turning Collection into interpretation or implying stronger identity proof.
