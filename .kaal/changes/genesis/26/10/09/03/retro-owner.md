# Retro

## Learned

- **Validity is not authorization.** A correct finding can belong to another phase or later Change.
- **Preservation is not repair.** Keep the finding, affected identity, violated contract, reproduction conditions, red test, observed result and disposition, even when deferred.
- **Review convergence must name its lens and obligations.** Convergence on architecture conformity cannot be presented as satisfaction of Requirements that still fail.
- **Do IT enables Detect Defects.** Executing the result produces the evidence that may challenge earlier agreed outcomes. Sealing can preserve the authoritative boundary without requiring a new seal at every D.
- **Findings are not orders.** The autonomous Worker–Reviewer loop must permit justified challenge and deferral, and escalate to HOW when scope or authority cannot be settled.

## Liked

- The allocation respects existing KAAL responsibilities: Review establishes whether a finding stands; Process/Change/Owner authority governs action; Sealing preserves agreed artifacts and Change evidence; future Work selection and Learning remain separate.
- The independent Review caught a substantive problem in the demonstration: the original convergence claim was too broad while R1 remained violated. The revised trial explicitly distinguishes architecture conformity from fulfillment of the governing requirement. A valid defect does not become invalid because implementation faithfully followed a flawed architecture.
- The preserved red test now runs with its required code retained, reproduces the specific R1 assertion rather than merely failing for a missing module, and can be rediscovered from retained Work. This is meaningful evidence for later Work.
- The Worker answered the findings without silently modifying the agreed architecture or building a generic Defect registry.

## Lacked

I lacked a simple, already-established way for a fresh Agent to preserve and later rediscover a confirmed finding without the Owner explaining where to look. The initial demonstration also showed why a red test must remain independently reproducible, with its dependencies and violated contract retained: a nonzero exit alone is not evidence of the intended defect. We still lack an exercised integration between this finding-disposition model, Work selection and the autonomous review Process in #75. Those are identified gaps for subsequent governed Work, not reasons to enlarge this converged architectural Change.

## Longed

I long for an Agent to recognize two separate questions whenever Review or Do IT exposes a finding: does the finding stand, and are we authorized to address it now? A valid finding discovered during implementation against agreed architecture should survive with its red test, even when correcting the architecture belongs to a later Change. I want this to become a discoverable, ordinary KAAL practice across the six Ds, so autonomous review can converge without obedience-driven scope creep, and HOW is invoked when genuine human judgment is needed rather than because an Agent has exhausted its context. Keep the solution small and let the responsible Skills and Processes own their respective parts.
