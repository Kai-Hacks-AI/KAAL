# Retro

## Learned

A reproducible red assertion can establish an upstream contract violation even when the implementation conforms exactly to its architecture. The decisive review question was not whether the bug disappeared, but which obligation the current Work had been handed and whether convergence named that limited lens honestly. Finding validity survived legitimate deferral.

## Liked

The Worker recorded a position on each finding and answered all three without erasing the original review or expanding the Change. Retaining the affected implementation beside the red test made the preserved Defect executable, and pinning the moving PR #75 reference made the architectural comparison inspectable. The Owner kept the remaining recommendations as future governed Work.

## Lacked

The first evidence record lacked a complete dependency recipe, so a direct replay failed for a missing module rather than the claimed contract violation. The first discovery trial also selected and copied the answer before searching for it. These gaps made preservation look stronger than the retained bytes initially supported; independent recovery was needed to distinguish assertion evidence from incidental failure.

## Longed

I would like future governed Work to exercise the same validity-and-authority distinction on a real upstream Defect, with a later Agent reaching its preserved evidence through a candidate or Learning. That would test whether the architectural separation remains usable across Changes without turning Review into Work selection or requiring a generic findings registry.
