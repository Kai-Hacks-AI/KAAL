# Retro

## Learned

That the allocation I first drew was right in its shape and wrong in two details that only an outside replay could expose. I believed the lens of the trial's convergence round was sound because I had written the sentence that justified it; the Reviewer's reproduction showed that a round examining Code against Architecture and Requirements cannot converge while R1 is false, and that the honest question is whether the Work was handed the obligation. I also learned that a preserved red test is not preserved until it runs alone: mine imported code I had deleted from the record. And I learned how quickly a draft PR becomes the wrong subject of my own reading: #75 changed under me between my first look and my second, and the gap I reported had been answered.

## Liked

Letting the existing sentences do the work. Almost every responsibility the briefing asked about was already stated in sealed or unsealed text, with a page and a line to point at, so the allocation could be an argument from what is there and no new capability had to be invented. Running the whole scenario as one replayable script with identities printed at each step also paid off: the Reviewer could replay it independently and say what was true of it. Writing my own position per finding in work/ before the next round felt right and matched the rule that the next round, not the Worker, decides standing.

## Lacked

A way to say, in the Work, which obligation a Work was handed. I had to invent a scope.md for the toy to make the continuation test visible, and for real Work the equivalent lives in a grant, an Intent and the Owner's words in different places. I also lacked an early sight of how fast #75 was moving; I pinned a revision only after being told to. And I lacked a quiet way to run a trial that emits no diagnostics: the broken-pipe noise in the lookup was mine and was left because changing the Work after convergence would have voided it.

## Longed

For the realization Change to be able to try the same scenario on a real D3 defect found in a real Change, not a toy, and to see whether the defect.md recipe survives a reader who has never heard of this convention. I would like a deferred Defect to reach the next Agent without anyone remembering to look, which is what a candidate or a Learning is for.
