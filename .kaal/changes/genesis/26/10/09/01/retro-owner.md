# Retro

## Learned

Separating the Change artifact from the Process governing it is feasible without extending KAAL Core. The distinction becomes clearer when examined against the existing code rather than inferred from capability names.

Process is a specialized Skill. Changing remains independently useful, and Processes can compose it with other capabilities.

## Liked

The independent review identified concrete architectural defects, and the Worker corrected the design using reproducible evidence.

The resulting architecture preserves existing Change identities and avoids unnecessary Core growth.

## Lacked

Process discovery remains limited, particularly when an Agent does not already know which Process is relevant.

The six-D Ways of Working are not yet sufficiently established to demonstrate the wider review-lens model.

## Longed

I want KAAL to discover the appropriate Process, obtain its participating Skills and apply relevant review lenses without requiring the Owner to assemble the instructions manually.

The GLUE heritage of progression and feedback should inform that evolution incrementally, without turning Processes into rigid pipelines.
