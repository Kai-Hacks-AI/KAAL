# Retro

## Learned

I learned that the extraction boundary must follow the call graph rather than the apparent vocabulary. Moving Work identification into the Process would leave artifact integrity dependent on it. Retaining that identification as an explicit compatibility rule makes independent artifact operation possible without changing existing verdicts.

I also learned that a targeted review of Work cannot be distinguished structurally from ROWING by the existing compass. The subject-mismatch probe was useful, but it did not establish a broader authority guarantee.

## Liked

The Worker responded to each finding with a concrete revision and preserved the original round. That made the second review a comparison against explicit obligations rather than a new architectural debate.

Small probes against unchanged scripts provided decisive evidence without turning this design-only Change into implementation work. Reading the Worker and Owner retrospectives also clarified which uncertainties were useful future learning rather than reasons to expand this Change.

## Lacked

The initial discovery route omitted how an Agent selects composing Skills from the installed set. Without that step, I could not assess its independence from the proposed BRAIN surface.

The sealed Changing KAAL wording still leaves an interpretation to settle before extraction. The design records that boundary, but I did not have an established semantic transition to review.

## Longed

I want a future extraction Change to carry comparison cases for historical Changes, orphan Work seals, missing Work seals and bare-sealed Changes, so compatibility can be reviewed through observed verdicts.

I want the proposed discovery trial to examine what an Agent reaches without supplied capability names, including whether it finds and applies the relevant standard. That would turn the current complete but limited reading route into evidence about actual use.
