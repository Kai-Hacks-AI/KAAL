# WORK

WORK is a mnemonic for the disciplined formation of Knowledge from Work once it is fixed:

```
W  Worker
O  Owner
R  Reviewer
K  Knowledge
```

Sealed Work becomes Knowledge through perspectives taken in a fixed order: first the Worker's, who performed it; then the Owner's, who holds its Intent and has judged the Work against it; then the Reviewer's, who judged it as it was realized. The order is the discipline, because knowledge accumulates: each later perspective may draw on the earlier ones, and none revises the Work or the Intent.

Knowledge is what those perspectives, taken in order, leave behind. It is not approval. The Owner's perspective follows the Owner's judgment of the Work against the Intent and carries no verdict; what it teaches may inform a future Intent, and never revises the Intent the Work was done for.
