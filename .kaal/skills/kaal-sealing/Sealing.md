# Sealing

Sealing is the capability for establishing and verifying the immutable identity of a KAAL artifact, according to the identity defined for that artifact. Its scope is that alone. It does not decide what constitutes an artifact's identity: each kind of artifact defines its own, and Sealing consumes it. It is not Core, and it is not the decision of when something must be sealed.
