# MERIT

Migrate, Enhance, Retain, Improve, Terminate.

The five decisions available for each control when it is brought into the new architecture:

- **Migrate**: move the control into the new architecture.
- **Enhance**: extend its capabilities or coverage.
- **Retain**: keep its existing behavior and realization.
- **Improve**: make the existing behavior more reliable or efficient.
- **Terminate**: remove obsolete or redundant controls or realizations.

Decisions can be combined. Each decision must distinguish the control from its technical realization: the control is what is judged, the realization is how it is carried out, and a decision may apply to one and not the other.

MERIT is a mnemonic. It owns no document, no capability and no control.
