# testing/C84 Evidence names the content it examined

A record of evidence corresponds to the content that was actually examined, and says which content that is with an identity the consumer can compare. Testing owns the contract and a pure check, `kaal-testing/identity`, and the observation record applies it: a record whose identity is not the identity the consumer examined makes every Case UNKNOWN with a named finding. The identity is an opaque string. How content is identified (a commit, a tree, a digest, a version) and where it comes from are for Hosting and the source; Testing assumes none. Recurrence of release/D20, Kai's go. Hosting's provider of content and provenance, and any ratchet over how a record boundary is taken, are not in this Change.
