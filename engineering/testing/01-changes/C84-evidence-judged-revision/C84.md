# testing/C84 Evidence names the revision it judged

A record of evidence names the revision that was judged (the head of the pull request), never the revision that triggered the run (`GITHUB_SHA`, the test merge commit). Testing owns the contract and a pure check of it, `kaal-testing/revision`, and the observation record applies it: a record whose revision is not the judged one, or is the trigger, makes every Case UNKNOWN with a named finding. Recurrence of release/D20, Kai's go. Resolving the judged revision is Hosting's, and a ratchet against raw `GITHUB_SHA` is Controls'; neither is in this Change.
