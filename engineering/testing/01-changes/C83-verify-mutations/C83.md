# testing/C83 Testing runs mutants and records which survive

`kaal-testing mutate` applies each mutant of a spec to a scratch copy, runs the Cases that should detect it, and writes a record that classifies every mutant as killed, survived, invalid or unresolved, names the survivors and says whether it is complete. It replaces the by-hand `run.sh` and the reading of its output. Kai's go. Mutants are given, not generated; nothing is wired and no check is required.
