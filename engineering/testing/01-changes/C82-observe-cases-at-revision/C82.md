# testing/C82 Testing observes the Cases of a revision and writes one record

`kaal-testing observe-at` runs the Case files it is given at a revision and writes a record `kaal-testing/observation@1`: `observed` and `evidence` as `observe` gives them, plus the facts of the run and its integrity, so that hold Act (`release/C125`) can read it with no change and the KAAL workflow (engineering) has one step to call. Generic: no workflow, no path, no Case selection. Kai's go. Nothing is wired and no check is required.
