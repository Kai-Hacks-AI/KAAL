# testing/C80 Cases are checked for form and explicit attribution

The first of four small Test Controls (`verify Case`, `demand Cases`, `limit Test-Drift`, `observe Cases`): `verify Case` is the repository's **contextual Control** (`testing/CTL1`) that consumes Testing's reusable Case-form checker (`kaal-testing/form`, `testing/C69`/`C70`) and maps its structural report and uncertainty into a Control verdict (`PASS` / `FAIL` / `UNKNOWN` with `applies`) under the repository's subject-resolution policy.

## Architecture and Ownership Separation (C69 / C74 / C80 Reconciliation)

Rather than introducing another Case parser, `C80` adopts a four-layer producer/consumer chain:

```text
Shared source inspection (@kaihacksai/kaal-hosting)
  -> Testing Case adapter (kaal-testing/cases: readCase, C74)
  -> Testing Case-form checker (kaal-testing/form: checkCaseForm -> case-form@1, C69/C70)
  -> Contextual verify Case Control (engineering/testing: judgeVerifyCase, C80)
```

- **Shared source inspection (`@kaihacksai/kaal-hosting`):** Owns syntax/AST-accurate extraction of top-level JS/ESM exports and comments without confusing template strings, block comments, or non-callable assignments with executable declarations. (Bounded provider prerequisite; not implemented inside `C80`.)
- **Testing Case adapter (`kaal-testing/cases`, `C74`):** Interprets extracted declarations as `id`, `tests`, and callable `run`, keeping `absent-attribution` distinct from `malformed-attribution`.
- **Testing Case-form checker (`kaal-testing/form`, `C69`/`C70`):** Owns the reusable Case-form rules and emits a verdict-free `kaal-testing/case-form@1` report (`testing/N34`).
- **Contextual `verify Case` Control (`testing/C80`, `testing/CTL1`):** Preserves Testing's checker findings and uncertainty verbatim, applies the repository's subject-resolution policy (never turning an incomplete subject collector into a false `unknown-subject` `FAIL`), and adds contextual applicability (`applies`) and Control rollout (`detective` → advisory → `preventive`).

`C80` neither parses JavaScript source nor redefines what a well-formed Case is; it checks form and attribution contextually and leaves assertion strength and sufficiency to Testing review and the Owner.
