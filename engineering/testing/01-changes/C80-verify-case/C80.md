# testing/C80 Cases are checked for form and explicit attribution

The first of the four small Test Controls modeled on the ACT controls (`verify Case`, `demand Cases`, `limit Test-Drift`, `observe Cases`): `verify Case` reveals any Case file that is malformed, unattributed, duplicated, missing `run()`, carrying a stale line-1 header, or pointing to an unknown subject id.

This Change is the PLAN for `testing/C80`: it introduces the Intent `testing/I48`, Requirement `testing/F173`, Architecture `testing/A59`, Control `testing/CTL1` (`verify Case`, detective first), the pure judge and command line `13-source/scripts/judge-verify-case.mjs` (drafted in `interface.md`), and five planned RED Cases (`testing/T397` to `testing/T401`) under `engineering/testing/01-changes/C80-verify-case/`, following the numbered layout of `packages/kaal-engineering/skills/kaal-engineering/references/package-layout.json`.

It checks form and attribution only: it does not judge whether a Case's assertions are strong or sufficient (which stays with Testing review and the Owner), does not wire a workflow or githook yet, and modifies no existing capability or package.
