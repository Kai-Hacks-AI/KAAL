# SINGER

Set Implementation Needs: Goals, Evidence, Requirements.

Before implementation, the human sets what it needs: the goals, the evidence that will show they are met, and the Requirements that follow. The human is the singer.

SINGER is a mnemonic. It owns no Goal, no Evidence and no Requirement.
