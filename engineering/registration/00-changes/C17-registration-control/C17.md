# engineering/C17 a control that every capability, Engineering area and Change registers an executable check

Guard guards only what declares itself with a `kaal-guard.json`. Nothing says that a capability, an Engineering area or a Change must declare itself, so forgetting to register looks the same as having nothing to check. This Change adds the control `engineering/CTL1 verify Registration`, a detective control. It states what the CPO asked for at point 1 of the review of guard PLAN #1949: each capability, Engineering area and Change registers at least one executable check through its own `kaal-guard.json`, with no empty registration.

The control does not belong to Guard, because Guard is opt-in and does not enforce the existence of a declaration (guard PLAN #1949, "Not in this Change"). It does not decide where it runs, because registration declares what is executed and never the stage (CPO point 2). Putting it into a blocking gate is a second, preventive control and needs the Owner's decision.
