# controls/C17 kaal controls declare their minimum evidence

KAAL's registered controls each declare their minimum evidence for Tested, in the language of controls/C15, and the repository gets a place for the records and a check that reads them. Nothing is enforced: no flag flips and no ruleset, workflow or hook changes. It uses controls/C15 on `main`; the computed state beside the claim (controls/C16) is a separate Change and needs nothing from this one.
