# controls/C13 check Controls control

Closes a small gap. Engineering runs the check of the controls Skill in its Engineering check, but nothing declares that as a control, so the check has no status of its own and is not in the inventory it protects. This Change declares `controls/CTL1`, `check Controls`, a detective control owned by the controls capability under `engineering/controls`, with `status: running`. It adds no hook, no workflow, no ruleset and no enforcement.
