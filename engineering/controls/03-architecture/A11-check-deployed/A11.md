# controls/A11 check-deployed

The product defines and the Engineering deploys. `packages/kaal-controls` holds the rule, the diagnostics and the recovery (controls/A10). `engineering/controls` holds one Case that runs `packages/kaal-controls/skills/kaal-controls/scripts/check-controls.mjs` on `engineering/` (controls/T778), and `A11-check-deployed/migration.md`, which tells an owner how to bring a control to a valid `status` and points at the product reference. It holds no list of the four values and no validation logic (controls/T779).

This Change adds no workflow, no required check and no enforcement. Wiring the check into GitHub, or requiring it, is a separate Change.
