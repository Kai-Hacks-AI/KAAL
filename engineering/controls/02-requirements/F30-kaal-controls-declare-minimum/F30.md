# controls/F30 kaal-controls-declare-minimum

Every valid control registered under `engineering/` declares an `evidence` line (controls/F28). A minimum names a `fixture` kind, a `real` kind (a run on a real pull request, merge or ruleset of this repository) and at least one `:fail` kind (a provoked failure the control must catch, shown by a RED base and a GREEN head). A preventive or corrective control asks for at least two `real` runs. The numbers are a starting point that each control's owner confirms in review; a control's own Change may change its line.
