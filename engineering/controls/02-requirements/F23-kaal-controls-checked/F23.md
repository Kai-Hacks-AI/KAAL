# controls/F23 kaal-controls-checked

Engineering runs the product check of controls/F21 on the controls that exist in `engineering/` when its Cases run, and a control without a valid `status` fails that Case visibly with the path, the id and the recovery the product gives. Engineering runs the check as the product delivers it and holds no copy of the rule.
