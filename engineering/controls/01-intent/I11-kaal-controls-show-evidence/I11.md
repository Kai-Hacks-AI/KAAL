# controls/I11 kaal controls show evidence

Each control registered in KAAL states its own minimum evidence for Tested, and the runs that count toward it are kept where the repository's checks can read them. A control that claims `tested` is admitted only when those records show it; a control that claims less shows how far its evidence has come beside the claim. A control that does the same work as another in a different mode, such as a check that becomes a gate, is another control with its own minimum. Nothing here enforces, flips a flag or changes a ruleset.
