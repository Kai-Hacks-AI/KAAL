# RATS!

Requirements, Architecture & Testing Steers.

While a Change is planned, before DO, three concerns are aligned with one another:

- **Requirements**, functional and non-functional (`kaal-requirements`).
- **Architecture**, functional placement (`kaal-architecture`).
- **Testing**, the corresponding test cases, including multi-loop TDD design (`kaal-testing`).

Their alignment steers PLAN. The planned outer test cases stand RED, failing for the intended missing behaviour, before coding begins.

RATS! is not a capability, not a sequence and not a checklist of artifacts. It owns none of the three concerns; each stays with its capability. "RATS!" is the developer's reminder to get this alignment right before coding.

RAT, Requirements, Architecture, Testing, is the earlier name. RATS! is the current one.
