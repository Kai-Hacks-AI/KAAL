# SWIPE

Show Work In Progress Expectations.

We swipe through the open pull requests to see the expected next move of each: WORK, REVIEW or WAIT.

SWIPE is a mnemonic for the capability `Show WIP`, which keeps its name. It owns no instruction, no capability and no control.
