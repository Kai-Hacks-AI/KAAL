# DISCO

Deliver Intended Solution Change Outcome.

Three things are delivered together:

- **Intended Solution**: the target, B. What the product and its delivery system are meant to be, described independently of how we get there.
- **Change**: the transition from the current state A to B. It holds what is known about A, the gaps, the sequence, the interim states, the temporary requirements and the evidence that B was reached.
- **Outcome**: the evidence that B was reached.

We need both target documentation and change documentation to succeed. Keeping each clean, the target free of transition and the change free of a restated target, helps agents deliver.

DISCO is a mnemonic. It owns no document, no capability and no sequence.
