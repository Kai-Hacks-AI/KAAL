# BACCHUS

Born As Current; Compatibility Has Understood Scope.

A component is born on the current appropriate stable or LTS target. Its supported compatibility is explicit and specific to that component. An older, additional or alternative target needs a concrete reason and matching verification. A change to an established compatibility contract is an explicit Change.
