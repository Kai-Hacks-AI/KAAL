# engineering/C16 change: Delivery Admission by stock (OUT from the stock, IN at the destination)

Revised at the CPO review of `0fd0838` (15 Nov 2026), which replaced the first design (class-aware extension of Promotion Source), and again at the CPO review of `c4568fc`, which fixed the governing model (stations, stocks, OUT versus IN) and asked for a mapping of existing controls to their destination stations. Owner ruling: no one-off route; do it right. Source: CTO assessment `/mnt/project-files/pull-promotion/true-pull-architecture-assessment.md`, change 2. Ids C16 and T450-T459 were checked on `main` only, not against every collector and open PR.

## From, to, because

- **From:** a pull request into `main` is admitted by one rule, `promotionSource` (`lineage.ts`): the head starts with `kaal/`. `hotfix/<name>` and `wire/<name>` cannot enter, so the first WIRE (#1705) and every hotfix are blocked. The rule judges a branch name, not the quality of what the stock delivers.
- **To:** the stock model. A **registered stock** owns its delivery semantics and its authoritative **OUT** qualification. Engineering's common **IN** mechanism, Delivery Admission, (1) routes the head to the one stock whose accepted registration claims it, (2) consumes that stock's OUT evidence bound to the exact head, and (3) applies the destination's IN requirements. Nothing in the mechanism knows what a hotfix, a WIRE or a collector is.
- **Because:** KAAL, HOTFIX and WIRE establish OUT differently. Branch names are routing and host facts, not definitions of stock quality. Existing Branching controls are headed for control-by-control retirement, so the target must not bake in their assumptions.

## Governing model (CPO review of `c4568fc`) and control map

- **Stations.** Development, Integration, Promotion, Production (`main`), Publishing. Publishing to npmjs is a distribution step after Production, not the definition of it. A ruleset belongs to a station and governs **incoming** work. The source stock decides readiness for **OUT**; the receiving station's ruleset decides **IN**. IN consumes OUT evidence; it never redefines or recomputes the source's quality criteria.
- **Stock.** The uniquely named **branch** at its **exact head sha** is one stock item (not an issue, a PR or a KAAL object). Qualification judges the whole branch at that head with the stock's own evidence (Change, Intent, Requirements, Architecture, Cases); a new head needs requalification. Stock inventory is therefore the branch list. Special stocks: `kaal/<capability>/<name>` (KAAL collector), `hotfix/<name>` (delivers a fix), `wire/<name>` (installs an approved artifact). Any other non-`main` branch is ordinary Development and not deliverable. `kaal/hotfix/*` develops the Hotfix capability and is KAAL stock; `hotfix/*` is HOTFIX stock.
- **Transaction.** A qualified item is ready for OUT and available to be pulled by an established route. Delivery Admission is one of the destination's IN conditions. A successful merge into `main` completes the transaction (the consumed branch is then deleted, which "delete branches on merge" already does). Readiness, an open PR or a passing admission verdict is not completion.
- **What C16 is not.** No universal OUT judge, no second stock inventory (admission lists nothing and counts nothing), no readiness rebuilt from issue or PR metadata (the PR is only the transport; T455), and no replacement or redefinition of another station's ruleset.

### Existing controls mapped to their destination station

| Station (IN) | Existing control | Judges | C16 effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Development | pre-push Guard checks, `Branching audit` (working branches) | a working branch is well formed | none; independent |
| Integration (`kaal/<capability>/<name>` -> collector) | `Branching` (lineage), `Change Isolation`, `ci` jobs, Guard, `Guard Acceptance Boundary` | the Change and the collector it joins | none; independent |
| Promotion (collector, `hotfix/*`, `wire/*` -> `main`) | **`Promotion Source` F1 (source rule `promotionSource`: head is `kaal/*`)** | which heads may enter | **replaced by Delivery Admission, at activation only** |
| Promotion | `Promotion Source` F2 (`promotionFreshness`: head holds `main`'s tip, merge tree equals head) | freshness | **stays, independent**; the `Promotion Source` context is not retired until F2 has a required home (a later Engineering Change); C16 does not move it |
| Promotion | `Change Isolation` and `Change Isolation (review)`, `Branching` on a `main` base, `ci`, CodeQL, Dependency Review, the merge queue (squash) | isolation, method, tests, scanning | none; independent, required as today |
| Production (`main`) | the merge itself, delete-on-merge | the transaction | none |
| Publishing | `release.yml`, the `npm` environment | distribution | none; out of scope |

**Correlation without duplication.** Every control above judges the same head sha and the merge queue requires all of them. They correlate only by that sha: Delivery Admission consumes the stock's OUT record and no other control's verdict, and no other control consumes admission's. A stock's OUT record is the stock's own qualification (for KAAL the collector's PDCA, not `ci`). During migration the legacy F1 rule and Delivery Admission both run on every head and must agree; a disagreement is reported, and the stricter one blocks. Nothing is retired before its replacement is verified, and the ruleset change is Owner-only.

## Separation: stock OUT versus destination IN

| Part | Owner | Does | Does not |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Stock OUT** | the registered stock (the capability for its collectors; Engineering for WIRE; the Hotfix capability for hotfix delivery) | establishes that this head is a qualified delivery and publishes the record, bound to the exact head, from its registered qualifier | decide admission, merge or approve |
| **Registration** | accepted on `main`, read from `main`'s tip only | says which route belongs to which stock and which qualifier is its OUT | carry quality, or the auto-pull choice |
| **Destination IN** (Delivery Admission, this Change) | Engineering | route resolves to exactly one registered stock; head is from this repository; the evidence is from that stock's registered qualifier, bound to this head, passing | recompute, reinterpret or replace any stock's OUT; read a title, label or body to classify |

`kaal/hotfix/*` (developing the Hotfix capability through ordinary KAAL PDCA, a KAAL stock) and `hotfix/*` (delivering an actual hotfix, the hotfix stock) are distinct legitimate purposes with distinct routes and stocks; neither replaces the other. A route name only identifies the candidate.

## Design

1. **Pure verdict** `admit(facts)` in `engineering/delivery-admission/admission.mjs`: `{ kind: pass | fail | unknown | not-applicable, stock, message, findings }`. Exit: pass 0, fail 1, not-applicable 3, unknown 2 (cannot judge). Built from three small functions: `route(routing, registry)`, `inRequirements(destination, routing)` and `consume(out, stock, head)`.
2. **Facts**, gathered read-only by trusted code from the default branch's tip (the posture of Promotion Source and Change Isolation: nothing of the head is checked out or run):
   - routing and host facts: `repo`, `defaultBranch`, `baseRef`, `headRepo`, `headRef`, `head` (sha);
   - `registry`: the accepted registry as pull/C14's `discoverRegistrations` returns it for `main`'s tip (`accepted`, `source: "main"`, `classRoutes`, `collectors`, `problems`) plus Engineering's own sibling file `engineering/release-management/05-src/pull-promotion/kaal-admission.json` (the `admission` section), read from `main`'s tip by the same acceptance rule (fail closed). Absent, unaccepted, not from `main`, or malformed: unknown. A registry present only in the head does not count;
   - `out`: the check run (or record) the stock's registered qualifier published **at this exact head sha**, with its verdict and record; absent, on another sha or from another qualifier is not evidence.
3. **Routing.** Routes come from the registry, never from the head's name alone. HOTFIX and WIRE routes are the `classRoutes` of Engineering's `kaal-pull.json`, the single source shared with pull. The KAAL stock's route is `kaal/**`, from `kaal-admission.json`, because every collector is admissible whether or not its capability opted into auto-pull. `admission` is `{ <STOCK>: { qualifier, routes?, migration? } }`; `routes` is an exact ref or a prefix pattern (`*` one segment, `**` one or more). `kaal/hotfix/*` routes to KAAL and `hotfix/*` to HOTFIX. Zero claiming stocks: fail. More than one: fail closed (ambiguous). A claimed route with no admission entry (no registered qualifier): fail.
4. **Consuming OUT.** No record: unknown. Record for a different sha is unknown; a record from another stock or qualifier than the routed stock's registered one fails (T450, T452). Verdict fail: fail. Verdict pass, bound to the head, from the registered qualifier: OUT is satisfied. Admission checks the record's binding, not its content.
5. **Stock-specific content stays with the stock.** HOTFIX: the hotfix stock's qualifier establishes that a Defect is identified, a reproducing Case is **RED against the defective baseline**, the same Case is **GREEN after correction**, each revision-bound, with traceability. Neither the Defect nor the Case has to exist on `main`; a `.red.mjs` to `.test.mjs` rename is a lifecycle step, not proof. WIRE: the existing WIRE judge (release/C27) and its contract produce the record, in the normal KAAL objects and traceability with a minimal envelope (the Change, what is installed, authoritative source and target, evidence of correct install). Admission consumes the judgment; it parses no PR body and defines no WIRE declaration format. KAAL: the capability's collector qualification (PDCA).
6. **Destination IN requirements** (the only policy Delivery Admission owns; it is one condition of the Promotion station's IN, beside the controls mapped above): base is the default branch (else not applicable); head repository is this repository (no forks); one stock; OUT satisfied for this head. Freshness, isolation, review, the merge queue and code scanning stay with their own controls and the ruleset.
7. **Migration, explicit.** Legacy controls stay effective until replacements are verified. An accepted entry may carry `migration: "legacy-promotion-source"`: for that route only, the legacy rule (`promotionSource`) stands in for OUT and the verdict reports the migration. There is no implicit fallback: a route with no entry fails. Migration entries are listed and are expected to retire. Only the F1 source rule is ever standing in; the freshness rule F2 is never covered by a migration entry and stays with its own control.
8. **One registry with pull (#1707, #1708, pull/C14), without conflation.** Admission and selection use the same discovery and acceptance mechanism (`discoverRegistrations`, accepted only from `main`'s tip, invalid files named in `problems`) and the same `classRoutes`. They differ in what they read. Pull reads `classRoutes` and each capability's `collectors` list (the auto-pull opt-in). Admission reads `classRoutes` (from `kaal-pull.json`) and `kaal-admission.json`, and never the `collectors` list: a collector that is not registered for auto-pull is judge-pulled and stays admissible for manual promotion, and a registered one still needs OUT evidence (T458). Capability files get no admission section in this PLAN. `kaal-pull.json` stays strict (class routes only, as the pull/C14 owner confirmed); admission data lives in its own sibling file owned by this Change, so selection never reads admission and admission never reads selection. Neither approves or merges.
9. **Check, not a new authority.** A Guard check `Delivery-Admission` (`.kaal/config/guard/Delivery-Admission.md`), `trusted: admission.mjs` from the base revision, run by a `pull_request_target` job and the merge-group job.

## Sequence (each its own Change)

1. This PLAN, CPO review at the exact head. T450-T459 RED on `main` (the module does not exist).
2. DO 1, Engineering source only (collector `kaal/engineering/delivery-admission`): the module, the facts reader, the CLI, the Guard declaration. T450-T459 green. Then ACT and promotion.
3. Stock OUT Changes, separate and owned by their stocks: WIRE record publication (Engineering, reusing the judge); the hotfix qualifier (Hotfix capability); each capability's collector qualification (assessment change 3). Until a stock's OUT exists, its route fails closed or runs under an explicit migration entry.
4. Registration entries accepted on `main`.
5. Non-blocking installation of the job (an ordinary `.github`-only DO: no `wire/*` route exists yet), then a live dry run next to Promotion Source on real PRs (#1705 is the first WIRE candidate).
6. Activation, Owner only, staged: (a) add the `Delivery Admission` context beside `Promotion Source` and run both (they must agree); (b) once freshness F2 has its own required home (separate Engineering Change), retire the `Promotion Source` context. Nothing in this PLAN touches a ruleset.

## Cases (all RED on `main`: `admission.mjs` does not exist)

T450 routing by the accepted registry: a head resolves to the one stock claiming its route; `hotfix/x` is HOTFIX and `kaal/hotfix/x` is KAAL; the name decides routing only. T451 no claiming route, ambiguous claims or a claimed route with no admission entry fail; an absent, unaccepted, non-`main` or malformed registry is unknown. T452 OUT consumption: absent or stale sha is unknown, another qualifier fails, verdict fail fails, a head-bound pass from the registered qualifier passes. T453 stock-neutral: an invented stock with a conforming record passes with no code change. T454 destination IN: forks fail, a base other than the default is not applicable, and a pass record cannot override either; admission consumes no other control's verdict (a facts object carrying freshness or isolation verdicts changes nothing). T455 a title, label, body or any issue/PR metadata cannot select a stock or supply readiness; admission lists and counts nothing. T456 migration: only an accepted `migration` entry applies the legacy source rule F1, reported as migration, and never stands in for freshness F2; without it a KAAL head needs its OUT evidence. T457 the CLI reads a facts file and exits per verdict; an unreadable file is exit 2. T458 admission never reads the auto-pull `collectors` list: a judge-pulled candidate is admissible, an auto-pulled one still needs evidence. T459 the Guard declaration trusts `admission.mjs` from the base revision.

## Not in this Change

No workflow, ruleset, hook or Guard activation; no hotfix qualifier, WIRE record publisher or capability qualifier (separate Changes of their stocks); no change to Branching, Change Isolation or Promotion Source (neither F1 nor F2 moves in this Change; F2 needs its own home before the context retires); no rename of an existing check; no GitHub App, automatic approval or merge, or lane caller.
