# engineering/C16 change: Delivery Admission by stock (STOCK qualifies the branch, OUT creates the PR, IN merges it)

Revised at the CPO review of `0fd0838` (15 Nov 2026), which replaced the first design (class-aware extension of Promotion Source), and again at the CPO review of `c4568fc`, which fixed the governing model (stations, stocks, STOCK, OUT, IN) and asked for a mapping of existing controls to their destination stations. Owner ruling: no one-off route; do it right. Source: CTO assessment `/mnt/project-files/pull-promotion/true-pull-architecture-assessment.md`, change 2. Ids C16 and T450-T459 were checked on `main` only, not against every collector and open PR.

## From, to, because

- **From:** a pull request into `main` is admitted by one rule, `promotionSource` (`lineage.ts`): the head starts with `kaal/`. `hotfix/<name>` and `wire/<name>` cannot enter, so the first WIRE (#1705) and every hotfix are blocked. The rule judges a branch name, not the quality of what the stock delivers.
- **To:** the stock model. A **registered stock** owns its delivery semantics and its authoritative **STOCK** qualification. Engineering's common **IN** mechanism, Delivery Admission, (1) routes the head to the one stock whose accepted registration claims it, (2) consumes that stock's STOCK qualification bound to the exact head, and (3) applies the destination's IN requirements. Nothing in the mechanism knows what a hotfix, a WIRE or a collector is.
- **Because:** KAAL, HOTFIX and WIRE qualify a branch differently. Branch names are routing and host facts, not definitions of stock quality. Existing Branching controls are headed for control-by-control retirement, so the target must not bake in their assumptions.

## Governing model (CPO review of `c4568fc`) and control map

- **Stations.** Development, Integration, Promotion, Production (`main`), Publishing. Publishing to npmjs is a distribution step after Production, not the definition of it. A ruleset belongs to a station and governs **incoming** work.
- **Placing controls (Owner's definition, 15 Nov).** **STOCK** = tests against branches. **OUT** = PR creation rules (also npmjs publish creation). **IN** = PR merge rules. A control is placed by what it decides: a test of a branch at its head is STOCK, a rule on whether a PR may be created or pulled is OUT, a rule on whether a PR may merge is IN. IN consumes STOCK evidence; it never redefines or recomputes the stock's quality criteria.
- **Stock.** The uniquely named **branch** at its **exact head sha** is one stock item (not an issue, a PR or a KAAL object). Qualification judges the whole branch at that head with the stock's own evidence (Change, Intent, Requirements, Architecture, Cases); a new head needs requalification. Stock inventory is therefore the branch list. Special stocks: `kaal/<capability>/<name>` (KAAL collector), `hotfix/<name>` (delivers a fix), `wire/<name>` (installs an approved artifact). Any other non-`main` branch is ordinary Development and not deliverable. `kaal/hotfix/*` develops the Hotfix capability and is KAAL stock; `hotfix/*` is HOTFIX stock.
- **Transaction.** A branch that passes its STOCK tests at the exact head is ready and available to be pulled; the OUT rules (pull/C14 selection, PR creation) decide whether and when its PR is created. IN applies at merge. Delivery Admission is one of the destination's IN conditions. A successful merge into `main` completes the transaction (the consumed branch is then deleted, which "delete branches on merge" already does). Readiness, an open PR or a passing admission verdict is not completion.
- **What C16 is not.** No universal STOCK judge, no second stock inventory (admission lists nothing and counts nothing), no readiness rebuilt from issue or PR metadata (the PR is only the transport; T455), and no replacement or redefinition of another station's ruleset.

### Existing controls placed by role (STOCK, OUT, IN) and station

| Station | Role | Control | Judges | C16 effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Development | STOCK | pre-push Guard checks, `Branching audit` | a working branch is well formed | none; independent |
| Integration (`kaal/<capability>/<name>` -> collector) | IN (merge into the collector) | `Branching`, `Change Isolation`, `ci` jobs, Guard, `Guard Acceptance Boundary` | the Change and the collector it joins | none; independent |
| Integration | STOCK | the capability's collector qualification (PDCA) | the collector is a qualified KAAL stock item at its head | none here; becomes the KAAL stock's qualification record (sequence step 3) |
| Promotion stocks | STOCK | hotfix qualifier (RED then GREEN; Hotfix capability), WIRE judge (release/C27), KAAL collector qualification | the branch at its exact head | none here; each is its stock's Change (step 3) |
| Promotion | OUT (PR creation / pull) | pull selection and lane (pull/C14, #1707/#1708), capability opt-in | whether and when a PR for a ready stock item is created | none; independent. Admission never selects and selection never admits |
| Promotion (-> `main`) | IN (merge into `main`) | **`Promotion Source` F1 (`promotionSource`: head is `kaal/*`)** | which heads may enter | **replaced by Delivery Admission (IN), at activation only** |
| Promotion | IN | `Promotion Source` F2 (`promotionFreshness`: head holds `main`'s tip, merge tree equals head) | freshness | **stays, independent, not absorbed and not weakened.** F2 protects the identity of what was qualified versus what would land in Production. Own separately verified, required home (own Engineering Change, step 4) before the `Promotion Source` context retires |
| Promotion | IN | `Change Isolation` and `(review)`, `Branching` on a `main` base, `ci`, CodeQL, Dependency Review, the merge queue (squash) | isolation, method, tests, scanning | none; independent, required as today. A job that also runs on a branch push is STOCK there and IN where it is required to merge |
| Production (`main`) | the transaction | the merge, delete-on-merge | completion | none |
| Publishing | OUT (npmjs publish creation) | `release.yml` readiness and dispatch, the `npm` environment | whether a publication may be created | none; out of scope |

**Correlation without duplication.** Every control above judges the same head sha and the merge queue requires all of them. They correlate only by that sha: Delivery Admission consumes the stock's STOCK qualification record and no other control's verdict, and no other control consumes admission's. A stock's STOCK qualification record is the stock's own qualification (for KAAL the collector's PDCA, not `ci`). During migration the legacy F1 rule and Delivery Admission both run, and are compared **route-aware** (design item 10): on equivalent KAAL routes they must agree (a difference is a regression and the stricter blocks); on HOTFIX and WIRE routes the legacy rule rejects by design, so the difference is the expected one, recorded and explained, never counted as a disagreement failure. Nothing is retired before its replacement is verified, and the ruleset change is Owner-only.

## Separation: STOCK, OUT and IN

| Part | Owner | Does | Does not |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **STOCK** (tests against the branch) | the registered stock (the capability for its collectors; Engineering for WIRE; the Hotfix capability for hotfix delivery) | establishes that this head is a qualified delivery and publishes the record, bound to the exact head, from its registered qualifier | decide admission, merge or approve |
| **Registration** | accepted on `main`, read from `main`'s tip only | says which route belongs to which stock and which qualifier is its STOCK qualification | carry quality, or the auto-pull choice |
| **Destination IN** (Delivery Admission, this Change) | Engineering | route resolves to exactly one registered stock; head is from this repository; the evidence is from that stock's registered qualifier, bound to this head, passing | recompute, reinterpret or replace any stock's OUT; read a title, label or body to classify |

`kaal/hotfix/*` (developing the Hotfix capability through ordinary KAAL PDCA, a KAAL stock) and `hotfix/*` (delivering an actual hotfix, the hotfix stock) are distinct legitimate purposes with distinct routes and stocks; neither replaces the other. A route name only identifies the candidate.

## Design

1. **Pure verdict** `admit(facts)` in `engineering/delivery-admission/admission.mjs`: `{ kind: pass | fail | unknown | not-applicable, stock, message, findings }`. Exit: pass 0, fail 1, not-applicable 3, unknown 2 (cannot judge). Built from three small functions: `route(routing, registry)`, `inRequirements(destination, routing)` and `consume(out, stock, head)`.
2. **Facts**, gathered read-only by trusted code from the default branch's tip (the posture of Promotion Source and Change Isolation: nothing of the head is checked out or run):
   - routing and host facts: `repo`, `defaultBranch`, `baseRef`, `headRepo`, `headRef`, `head` (sha);
   - `registry`: the accepted registry as pull/C14's `discoverRegistrations` returns it for `main`'s tip (`accepted`, `source: "main"`, `classRoutes`, `stocks`, `problems`) plus Engineering's own sibling file `engineering/release-management/05-src/pull-promotion/kaal-admission.json` (the `admission` section), read from `main`'s tip by the same acceptance rule (fail closed). Absent, unaccepted, not from `main`, or malformed: unknown. A registry present only in the head does not count;
   - `out`: the check run (or record) the stock's registered qualifier published **at this exact head sha**, with its verdict and record; absent, on another sha or from another qualifier is not evidence.
3. **Routing.** Routes come from the registry, never from the head's name alone. HOTFIX and WIRE routes are the `classRoutes` of Engineering's `kaal-pull.json`, the single source shared with pull. The KAAL stock's route is `kaal/**`, from `kaal-admission.json`, because every collector is admissible whether or not its capability opted into auto-pull. `admission` is `{ <STOCK>: { qualifier, routes?, migration? } }`; `routes` is an exact ref or a prefix pattern (`*` one segment, `**` one or more). `kaal/hotfix/*` routes to KAAL and `hotfix/*` to HOTFIX. Zero claiming stocks: fail. More than one: fail closed (ambiguous). A claimed route with no admission entry (no registered qualifier): fail.
4. **Consuming the STOCK qualification.** No record: unknown. Record for a different sha is unknown; a record from another stock or qualifier than the routed stock's registered one fails (T450, T452). Verdict fail: fail. Verdict pass, bound to the head, from the registered qualifier: the STOCK requirement is satisfied. Admission checks the record's binding, not its content.
5. **Stock-specific content stays with the stock.** HOTFIX: the hotfix stock's qualifier establishes that a Defect is identified, a reproducing Case is **RED against the defective baseline**, the same Case is **GREEN after correction**, each revision-bound, with traceability. Neither the Defect nor the Case has to exist on `main`; a `.red.mjs` to `.test.mjs` rename is a lifecycle step, not proof. WIRE: the existing WIRE judge (release/C27) and its contract produce the record, in the normal KAAL objects and traceability with a minimal envelope (the Change, what is installed, authoritative source and target, evidence of correct install). Admission consumes the judgment; it parses no PR body and defines no WIRE declaration format. KAAL: the capability's collector qualification (PDCA).
6. **Destination IN requirements** (the only policy Delivery Admission owns; it is one condition of the Promotion station's IN, beside the controls mapped above): base is the default branch (else not applicable); head repository is this repository (no forks); one stock; STOCK qualification satisfied for this head. Freshness, isolation, review, the merge queue and code scanning stay with their own controls and the ruleset.
7. **Migration, explicit.** Legacy controls stay effective until replacements are verified. An accepted entry may carry `migration: "legacy-promotion-source"`: for that route only, the legacy rule (`promotionSource`) stands in for the STOCK qualification and the verdict reports the migration. There is no implicit fallback: a route with no entry fails. Migration entries are listed and are expected to retire. Only the F1 source rule is ever standing in; the freshness rule F2 is never covered by a migration entry and stays with its own control.
8. **One registry with pull (#1707, #1708, pull/C14), without conflation.** Admission and selection use the same discovery and acceptance mechanism (`discoverRegistrations`, accepted only from `main`'s tip, invalid files named in `problems`) and the same `classRoutes`. They differ in what they read. Pull reads `classRoutes` and each capability's stock-level registration `{capability, stock: "kaal/<name>/*"}` (the auto-pull opt-in, pull/C14 option A). Admission reads `classRoutes` (from `kaal-pull.json`) and `kaal-admission.json`, and never the `stocks` registrations: a stock item that is not registered for auto-pull is judge-pulled and stays admissible for manual promotion, and a registered one still needs STOCK qualification (T458). Selection takes STOCK-GREEN stock items (the OUT rule) without requiring a destination IN pass; applying the IN after the pull is admission's job, so admission accepts a pulled head whose IN has not yet run. Capability files get no admission section in this PLAN. `kaal-pull.json` stays strict (class routes only, as the pull/C14 owner confirmed); admission data lives in its own sibling file owned by this Change, so selection never reads admission and admission never reads selection. Neither approves or merges.
9. **Check, not a new authority.** A Guard check `Delivery-Admission` (`.kaal/config/guard/Delivery-Admission.md`), `trusted: admission.mjs` from the base revision, run by a `pull_request_target` job and the merge-group job.

10. **Route-aware migration comparison.** `compare(facts)` in `admission.mjs` runs the legacy F1 rule beside admission and reports `agree`, `regression` or `expected-difference`. On a KAAL route (`kaal/**`) the two must agree; any difference is a `regression` and the stricter verdict blocks. On a HOTFIX or WIRE route legacy F1 rejects by design while admission admits when STOCK and IN are satisfied: that is `expected-difference`, recorded with its reason (legacy admits `kaal/*` only), and it never fails the comparison. A difference that is not explained by the route is a regression. Existing required protections stay effective until the replacement path and F2 are independently verified and the Owner authorizes the ruleset transition.

## Sequence (each its own Change)

1. This PLAN, CPO review at the exact head. T450-T459 RED on `main` (the module does not exist).
2. DO 1, Engineering source only (collector `kaal/engineering/delivery-admission`): the module (verdict, `compare`), the facts reader, the CLI, the Guard declaration. T450-T459 green. Then ACT and promotion.
3. STOCK-qualification Changes, separate and owned by their stocks: WIRE record publication (Engineering, reusing the judge); the hotfix qualifier (Hotfix capability); each capability's collector qualification (assessment change 3). Until a stock's qualification record exists, its route fails closed or runs under an explicit migration entry.
4. **Freshness home (F2), a separate Engineering Change, independent of C16.** A separately verified, required control for `promotionFreshness` (source contains the current `main` tip; the prospective merge tree equals the qualified head tree), planned with its own PLAN and Cases. It must exist and be verified before step 7(b). C16 neither absorbs nor weakens F2 at any step.
5. Registration entries accepted on `main`.
6. Non-blocking installation of the job (an ordinary `.github`-only DO: no `wire/*` route exists yet), then a live dry run next to Promotion Source on real PRs (#1705 is the first WIRE candidate), reported route-aware: KAAL routes must agree, HOTFIX and WIRE differences are the expected, recorded ones.
7. Activation, Owner only, staged, nothing before the Owner authorizes the ruleset transition: (a) add the `Delivery Admission` context beside `Promotion Source`; (b) retire the `Promotion Source` context only after F2 has its own verified required home and the replacement path is independently verified. Existing required protections stay effective throughout. Nothing in this PLAN touches a ruleset.

## Cases (all RED on `main`: `admission.mjs` does not exist)

T450 routing by the accepted registry: a head resolves to the one stock claiming its route; `hotfix/x` is HOTFIX and `kaal/hotfix/x` is KAAL; the name decides routing only. T451 no claiming route, ambiguous claims or a claimed route with no admission entry fail; an absent, unaccepted, non-`main` or malformed registry is unknown. T452 STOCK-qualification consumption: absent or stale sha is unknown, another qualifier fails, verdict fail fails, a head-bound pass from the registered qualifier passes. T453 stock-neutral: an invented stock with a conforming record passes with no code change. T454 destination IN: forks fail, a base other than the default is not applicable, and a pass record cannot override either; admission consumes no other control's verdict (a facts object carrying freshness or isolation verdicts changes nothing). T455 a title, label, body or any issue/PR metadata cannot select a stock or supply readiness; admission lists and counts nothing. T456 migration: only an accepted `migration` entry applies the legacy source rule F1, reported as migration, and never stands in for freshness F2; without it a KAAL head needs its STOCK qualification. The comparison is route-aware: `compare` is `agree` on KAAL routes, `expected-difference` (with reason) for `hotfix/*` and `wire/*`, and `regression` for an unexplained difference. T457 the CLI reads a facts file and exits per verdict; an unreadable file is exit 2. T458 admission never reads the auto-pull `stocks` registrations: a judge-pulled candidate is admissible, an auto-pulled one still needs evidence, and a pulled head whose destination IN has not yet run is admitted or refused by admission itself (selection never requires an IN pass). T459 the Guard declaration trusts `admission.mjs` from the base revision, and `admission.mjs` contains no freshness logic (no tip containment or merge-tree judgment): F2 is not absorbed.

## Not in this Change

No workflow, ruleset, hook or Guard activation; no hotfix qualifier, WIRE record publisher or capability qualifier (separate Changes of their stocks); no change to Branching, Change Isolation or Promotion Source (neither F1 nor F2 moves in this Change; F2 needs its own home before the context retires); no rename of an existing check; no GitHub App, automatic approval or merge, or lane caller.
