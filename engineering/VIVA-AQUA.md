# VIVA AQUA

Verification Improves Validation After Agents Qualify Unproven Assumptions.

Testing serves verification: it objectively establishes whether defined criteria are satisfied. Review serves validation: it judges whether criteria, decisions and outcomes are sound and fit for purpose. Validation is not merely the fallback when verification is impossible, and Review may use objective test evidence. Quality assurance needs both: verification improves validation, and agents qualify what remains unproven. Neither approves: the Owner decides.

VIVA AQUA is a mnemonic for the boundary between `kaal-testing` and `kaal-review`, which keep their names. It owns no instruction, no capability and no control.
