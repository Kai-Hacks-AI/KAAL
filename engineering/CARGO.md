# CARGO

Change Authorization Releases Guarded Outcomes.

CARGO names the collaboration between Change, Release and Guard:

- **Change** owns authorization and the PLAN/DO integrity of the transformation.
- **Release** owns the progression of authorized changes to an outcome.
- **Guard** protects declared invariants and provides trusted checks and verdicts.

The outcome is verified, not merely published. Release may consume Guard's evidence without duplicating its judgments.

CARGO is not a capability, a control or a gate. It says nothing about collectors, rulesets, workflows or any registry. Agreed by the Owner on #1334; the meaning is as Release - 0.0.1 recorded it.
