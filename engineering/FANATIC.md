# FANATIC

Functional And Nonfunctional Architecture Tests Intended Change.

A FANATIC HITMAN CLAUSE: every Artifact (A) traces Functional And Nonfunctional Architecture Tests to their Intent and the Change that carries it, and is judged locally by HITMAN and in CI by CLAUSE. I, F, N, A and T identifiers are scoped by capability (`<capability>/T1`). C is KAAL's existing Change, and a PR is only its carrier.

FANATIC names the trace and owns none of its parts. Intent, requirements and architecture belong to the capability that defines them, Tests to Testing, and Change to kaal-change.
