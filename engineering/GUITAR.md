# GUITAR

Get Us Into Test Accepted Requirements.

The Requirements a Change aims at are brought into Test: each accepted Requirement is made into Cases that stand RED until the behaviour exists.

GUITAR is a mnemonic. It owns no Requirement, no Case and no sequence. Requirements stay with `kaal-requirements` and Testing with `kaal-testing`.
