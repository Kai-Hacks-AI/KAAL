# C143 requirements added (scope narrowed by the CPO's re-review 5906885891 at abe72b4; ids provisional until the collision check at the exact head)

**Boundary and asymmetry.** Solution and Change checks must stay flexible; Engineering's delivery engine needs strong protection against bypass and tampering, and that protection is enforced outside Guard. Guard's job is stable invocation and reliable execution of the registrations that apply.

**Boundary.** Guard is permanently wired. Exactly two origins register checks with it: **Solution** (today a capability's package) and **Change** (a Change's folder, wherever it lives; its registrations are temporary). **Engineering does not register with Guard**: delivery controls and Engineering's checks are handled outside Guard, in the delivery infrastructure, and nothing here routes a repository control through it or treats one as privileged in Guard. These are registration ownership and lifecycle categories. How a Change's outcome becomes a target-state check belongs to kaal-change, not to Guard; Guard is not a Change-lifecycle governor. Discovery and selection of what is in flight belong to the Engine; Guard consumes the resolved execution plan. A registration is Guard's own declaration (a `Guard/Check` with the members `origin`, `owner`, `source`), not a Core Registration instance.

- **guard/F43 Two origins, one installed form.** A registration is sourced in the tree of its origin, at any path (a Change folder lives wherever its Change puts it; the fixtures of T209 and T210 place them in unrelated places and name no location in Guard), and carries `origin` (`solution` or `change`; any other origin, `engineering` included, is cannot judge), `owner` and its own `source` path. Its installed copy is that file verbatim at `.kaal/guard/registered/<origin>/<owner>/<name>.md`. The unchanged hook invocation and CI job run installed registrations beside the repository's declared checks, which are unchanged; registering touches neither `.githooks`, CI nor a ruleset. A copy that differs from its source, has none, or sits under a path of another origin than it declares is cannot judge, naming it: a Change's temporary registration is never taken for a persistent Solution one. (T209)
- **guard/F44 Evolution is the owner's; Guard checks execution integrity.** Solution and Change owners add, modify and remove their registrations through the applicable Change and delivery process, and whether such an edit may land is the delivery system's decision, enforced outside Guard. Guard runs the registrations as the candidate has them. It is cannot judge, naming the registration, only for execution integrity: a registration that is malformed (not a `Guard/Check`, an unknown origin or member), whose installed copy differs from its source, or whose command cannot start. Guard does not make registrations immutable and does not judge whether a change to one is legitimate. (T210)
- **guard/F45 Guard runs the plan it is given.** `run` takes an optional `--plan <file>`: the resolved execution plan the Engine produced, `{ "checks": [names] }` or `{ "unknown": true }`. Guard runs the registrations the plan names; the plan concerns registrations only and changes nothing about the repository's declared checks. A plan that is unknown, absent, or overridden by `--full` runs every registration, and the run says which of these applied; a plan that is unreadable, malformed, empty or naming no registered check is cannot judge. No plan outcome is ever a silent success. Guard computes no ownership, no dependency graph and no selection; unknown-handling is modelled at the Engine boundary, as this plan's `unknown`. `--full` is an execution capability only. **Owner decision (20:19):** the full run happens on the merge queue, which is where it fits; the separate wiring owner implements that later. This Change itself wires no gate, event or schedule. (T211)
- **guard/F46 withdrawn.** Delivery controls (branching, pull request and promotion governance, Change isolation and the like) belong to Engineering and the delivery infrastructure, outside Guard, which protects its own controls. Guard imports none of them, gives none a privileged status, and carries no requirement about shadowing them. The id is not reused.
- **guard/F47 withdrawn.** Policing which owner may register what, and refusing a cross-owner addition, is the delivery system's, outside Guard. The id is not reused.
- **guard/F48 Guard's namespace.** The `.kaal` namespace that is Guard's own is `config/guard`, `state/guard` and `guard/registered`. This **amends guard/F1 and the boundary Case guard/T30**, which allow only the first two, by a content ruling (T215) listed before and after in `change.md`; T30 and its history are not edited. (T215)
- **guard/A21 Registration.** Discovery of installed registrations, the integrity checks (F43, F44) and the plan (F45) sit in the runner's declaration reading (guard/A1, guard/A3); the adapters are unchanged. The shape of the capability (ownership, definition, installation contract) follows Core's (T213).

No DO, publication or enforcement is authorized by this PLAN. 0.0.5 stays the intended next release; this Change implies neither the other audited moves nor the Foundation dependency are done.
