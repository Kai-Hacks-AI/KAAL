# C143 requirements added (scope narrowed by the CPO's re-review 5906885891 at abe72b4; ids provisional until the collision check at the exact head)

**Boundary.** Guard is permanently wired. Three origins register checks with it: **Solution** (today a capability's package), **Change** (a Change's folder, wherever it lives; its registrations are temporary) and **Engineering** (the remaining controls). These are registration ownership and lifecycle categories. How a Change's outcome becomes a target-state check belongs to kaal-change, not to Guard; Guard is not a Change-lifecycle governor. Discovery and selection of what is in flight belong to the Engine; Guard consumes the resolved execution plan. A registration is Guard's own declaration (a `Guard/Check` with the members `origin`, `owner`, `source`), not a Core Registration instance.

- **guard/F43 Three origins, one installed form.** A registration is sourced in the tree of its origin, at any path, and carries `origin` (`solution`, `change` or `engineering`), `owner` and its own `source` path. Its installed copy is that file verbatim at `.kaal/guard/registered/<origin>/<owner>/<name>.md`. The unchanged hook invocation and CI job run installed registrations beside the hard-wired checks; registering touches neither `.githooks`, CI nor a ruleset. A copy that differs from its source, has none, or sits under a path of another origin than it declares is cannot judge, naming it: a Change's temporary registration is never taken for a persistent Solution one. (T209)
- **guard/F44 Base registrations are held.** For every origin, a pull request may add registrations. A registration the base holds that the candidate deletes, re-owns, or whose command, facts, outcomes or origin it changes is cannot judge, naming it, and the candidate's version never runs. Anti-delete, anti-weaken, anti-shadow come from the base. How a registration is retired is the owner's flow (for a Change, kaal-change's), not decided here. (T210)
- **guard/F45 Guard runs the plan it is given.** `run` takes an optional `--plan <file>`: the resolved execution plan the Engine produced, `{ "checks": [names] }` or `{ "unknown": true }`. Guard runs the registrations the plan names and every hard-wired check always. A plan that is unknown, absent, or overridden by `--full` runs every registration; a plan naming no registered check is cannot judge. Guard computes no ownership, no dependency graph and no selection; unknown-handling is modelled at the Engine boundary, as this plan's `unknown`. `--full` is an execution capability only: this Change assumes no gate, event or schedule, and the wiring that invokes it has its own owner. (T211)
- **guard/F46 No opt-out.** A registration cannot replace, shadow or switch off a check declared in `.kaal/config/guard/`; one named like it is cannot judge, and the hard-wired check still runs. Registrations stay separate from the hard-wired controls. (T212)
- **guard/F47 A pull request registers only for its own.** A registration is accepted only for an owner whose tree the pull request changes other than by that registration, judged by the ownership the base holds. A cross-owner addition, and an addition after the same pull request altered the ownership, are cannot judge. (T214)
- **guard/F48 Guard's namespace.** The `.kaal` namespace that is Guard's own is `config/guard`, `state/guard` and `guard/registered`. This **amends guard/F1 and the boundary Case guard/T30**, which allow only the first two, by a content ruling (T215) listed before and after in `change.md`; T30 and its history are not edited. (T215)
- **guard/A21 Registration.** Discovery of installed registrations, the provenance and base comparisons (F43, F44, F47) and the plan (F45) sit in the runner's declaration reading (guard/A1, guard/A3); the adapters are unchanged. The shape of the capability (ownership, definition, installation contract) follows Core's (T213).

No DO, publication or enforcement is authorized by this PLAN. 0.0.5 stays the intended next release; this Change implies neither the other audited moves nor the Foundation dependency are done.
