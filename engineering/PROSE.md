# PROSE

Product, Requirements, Order, Service, Engineering.

The Owner gives product direction in prose. PROSE reminds Engineering to realize the intended service faithfully.

Product owns direction. Engineering owns technical realization: it realizes a Product property, or returns a conflict with evidence. It never silently weakens or strengthens the property. A proposed technical representation does not by that alone become a Product requirement.

Requirements, Order and Service keep their own meanings and owners in each context. PROSE prescribes no workflow, schema or hierarchy.
