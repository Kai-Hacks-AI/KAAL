# VIVA AQUA mnemonic: Change (A to B)

This is `mnemonics/C2`, a PLAN Change. It ships no definition yet. It adds one mnemonic, VIVA AQUA, in the shape of DISCO and SWIPE. It is the memorable name of the boundary between the capabilities `kaal-testing` and `kaal-review`, which keep their names.

## A: the present

The mnemonic exists only in an approved draft. `engineering/` holds no `VIVA-AQUA.md`, so the sentence cannot be found through the KAAL Definition type.

## B: the target

`engineering/VIVA-AQUA.md` is a KAAL Definition: Verification Improves Validation After Agents Qualify Unproven Assumptions. It separates verification (proof by a defensible oracle, Testing) from validation (judgement where no oracle exists, Review), and says neither approves: the Owner decides. It states that it is a mnemonic for the boundary between `kaal-testing` and `kaal-review`, which keep their names, and that it owns no instruction, no capability and no control. See [requirements.md](requirements.md) and [testing.md](testing.md).

## Order and constraints

This Change travels alone, in its own collector `kaal/mnemonics-viva-aqua`. It changes no behavior of `kaal-testing` or `kaal-review`. The wording is the approved text, verbatim.
