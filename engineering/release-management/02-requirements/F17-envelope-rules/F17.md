# release/F17 envelope-rules

The envelope of a pull request is its title and its body, read together with the route it takes and its commits. It is judged by these rules, and each breach is one finding.

## Title

- Shape: `<TYPE>: <description>`, with TYPE an upper-case word and a description that is not empty. The shape does not depend on which types exist.
- Contamination: the title holds no Change id, link, emoji or attribution wording.

## Types

The types are a registry, not a closed set. The initially recognized types are PLAN, DO, ACT, SYNC, PROMOTION and HOTFIX, and more may be introduced later. Each recognized type is one row of the registry: its chapters, the shape of its `Change` chapter, its route rule and whether its Change id is checked against the commits. A new type adds a row and changes no shared rule. A TYPE that is not in the registry is reported as unsupported: the shared rules still apply to its title and body, its contract, route and id are not judged, and the result says so explicitly. An unsupported TYPE is never treated as a known type and is never reported as fully judged.

## Body

The rule for the body has two parts, kept apart.

- **Shared formatting rule, for every TYPE:** the body has level-2 chapters only; the first is `Change`, and it is not empty; nothing stands before the first chapter or after the last chapter's content; no chapter holds a link, emoji or attribution wording; and no HTML comment stands before the first chapter or carries attribution or metadata. This is the contamination of release/F13, stated for all types. The shared rule does not say what the `Change` chapter contains.
- **Contract of the TYPE:** the chapters a TYPE names, in order, what each may hold, and the shape of its `Change` chapter. The shape differs by TYPE: PLAN, DO and ACT name the id, the name and a description or scope; SYNC and HOTFIX name the id and the name only; PROMOTION gives one line per delivered Change with its id, name and the phases carried. Only PLAN has its contract on `main` (release/F3: Change as `<id> <name>: <short description>`, From, To, Because, each one short paragraph), and the PLAN contract is judged only for PLAN. The contracts of DO, ACT, SYNC, PROMOTION and HOTFIX are added to the judge, one row each, when their templates are on `main`.

Coverage in this version: PLAN is judged by the shared rule and its contract. DO, ACT, SYNC, PROMOTION and HOTFIX are recognized and judged by the shared rule only, and the result says that their chapters are not judged yet. Any other TYPE is unsupported as described above.

## Route

For the recognized types, PLAN, DO, ACT and SYNC take a base under `kaal/`, never `main`. PROMOTION takes `main` as base and a head under `kaal/`. HOTFIX has no route rule here: its branch lifecycle and admission path belong to the Hotfix delivery-path Change, and a route rule for HOTFIX is added only from that Change, not inferred from the HOTFIX template.

## Change identity

For PLAN, DO and ACT, the id that begins the `Change` chapter equals the `Driven-by:` trailer of every commit, and each commit has exactly one. These are authored commits that each carry one trailer by the standing rule.

SYNC, PROMOTION and HOTFIX are deliberately not judged for this agreement. Their commits are merge or promotion commits that carry the trailers of the commits they bring, and which commits count, and which id a SYNC body names, is not yet defined by their templates and delivery paths. Judging them now would invent that rule.

A clean envelope passes.
