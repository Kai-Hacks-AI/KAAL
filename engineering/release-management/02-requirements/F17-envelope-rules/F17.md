# release/F17 envelope-rules

The envelope of a pull request is its title and its body, read together with the route it takes and its commits. It is judged by these rules, and each breach is one finding.

## Title

- Shape: `<TYPE>: <description>`, with TYPE one of PLAN, DO, ACT, SYNC, PROMOTION or HOTFIX and a description that is not empty.
- Contamination: the title holds no Change id, link, emoji or attribution wording.

## Body

The rule for the body has two parts, kept apart.

- **Shared formatting rule, for every TYPE:** the body has level-2 chapters only; the first is `Change`, one line `<id> <name>: <short description>`; nothing stands before the first chapter or after the last chapter's content; no chapter holds a link, emoji or attribution wording; and no HTML comment stands before the first chapter or carries attribution or metadata. This is the contamination of release/F13, stated for all types.
- **Contract of the TYPE:** the chapters a TYPE names, in order, and what each may hold. Only PLAN has its contract on `main` (release/F3: Change, From, To, Because, each one short paragraph), and the PLAN contract is judged only for PLAN. The contracts of DO, ACT, SYNC, PROMOTION and HOTFIX are added to the judge, one row each, when their templates are on `main`.

Coverage in this version: PLAN is judged by the shared rule and its contract. DO, ACT, SYNC, PROMOTION and HOTFIX are judged by the shared rule only, and the result says that their chapters are not judged yet.

## Route

PLAN, DO, ACT and SYNC take a base under `kaal/`, never `main`. PROMOTION takes `main` as base and a head under `kaal/`. HOTFIX has no route rule here: its branch lifecycle and admission path belong to the Hotfix delivery-path Change, and a route rule for HOTFIX is added only from that Change, not inferred from the HOTFIX template.

## Change identity

For PLAN, DO and ACT, the id in the `Change` chapter equals the `Driven-by:` trailer of every commit, and each commit has exactly one. These are authored commits that each carry one trailer by the standing rule.

SYNC, PROMOTION and HOTFIX are deliberately not judged for this agreement. Their commits are merge or promotion commits that carry the trailers of the commits they bring, and which commits count, and which id a SYNC body names, is not yet defined by their templates and delivery paths. Judging them now would invent that rule.

A clean envelope passes.
