# release/F11 do-interface

The body of a DO pull request is a short interface of exactly three level-2 chapters in this order, Change, Implement and Refactor, and nothing else: no other headings, commentary, test results, CI status, execution logs, narrative of the red-to-green steps or attribution. It reuses no heading of the PLAN interface.

- Change: one line, `<id> <name>: <short description>`, with the stable id, the name and the scope of this DO.
- Implement: the intended behavior or functionality to be delivered.
- Refactor: the intended improvement while behavior stays as it is. When no refactoring is planned, the chapter says so. A change is not made merely to fill the chapter.

Each chapter is one paragraph of at most 200 characters, with no link or emoji. The body does not repeat the approved PLAN, the diff or the evidence. Those live in the files of the Change, the Cases, the diff and the checks. The interface guides the author and replaces no control.
