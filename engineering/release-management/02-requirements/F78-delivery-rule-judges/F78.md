# release/F78 delivery-rule-judges

The rule that delivery travels apart from solution (release/F73) judges in every shape its checker runs in: in the repository, and as the lone copy that the wrapper scripts run from a temporary directory. A wrapper gives the checker the delivery reader and the declaration it reads, taken verbatim from the target's tip, beside the checker. When the reader or the declaration is missing, or the declaration is malformed, the rule says `cannot judge`; that is UNKNOWN, it never counts as a pass of the rule, and the gate itself is not affected. The rule stays detective and gates nothing.
