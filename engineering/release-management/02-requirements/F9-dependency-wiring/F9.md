# release/F9 dependency-wiring

Dependency Review is bound to a place through that place's one aggregate check, never through a ruleset line of its own. The aggregate is the guard check of the step, named as `naming.md` will pick, for example `guard Integrate` and `guard Promote`. Each fans in the controls that are declared for its place, so adding a control adds a declaration and no ruleset line.

- Dependency Review is declared for each place on its own and reports its verdict for every Change the place admits: every pull request into a collector or into `main`, and every merge group the queue forms. A place where it does not report on every admitted Change is not declared.
- A verdict that is missing, late or UNKNOWN does not pass at the aggregate.
- The aggregates, and their one-time lines in the rulesets, belong to `guard Integration`. This control needs no ruleset change of its own, and no ruleset is changed by this Change.
- Until the aggregates exist the control stays detective and reports itself as not bound. It never claims to prevent.
