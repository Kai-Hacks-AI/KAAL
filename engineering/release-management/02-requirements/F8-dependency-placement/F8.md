# release/F8 dependency-placement

Dependency Review is applied where a dependency can enter on its way to `main`, and each application is declared for this control on its own.

| Boundary | Class of the behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Development OUT | detective and advisory: shows the author a finding before the Change is handed on. Declared as a placement, not realized by this Change |
| Integration IN | detective: judges a Change when it is admitted into a collector; preventive when `guard Integration` makes the verdict a condition of admission |
| Promotion IN | detective: judges a collector again when it is admitted into `main`, because advisories are published over time; preventive through `guard Integration` and the queue |
| Integration OUT | no application of its own: opening a promotion is judged at Promotion IN |

The control reports its verdict by its own check under the naming of `naming.md` of the Change. Dependabot, which watches dependencies already on a branch, is a different control and is not changed by this one.
