# release/F7 dependency-verdict

Dependency Review decides, from the dependencies a Change adds or changes and an advisory source, one of the three verdicts of the common control contract. PASS: no added or changed dependency carries a known advisory at or above the severity the control holds. FAIL: one does, and the verdict names each dependency, its version, the advisory and its severity. UNKNOWN: it cannot judge, for example the advisory source or the dependency data is unavailable. UNKNOWN never passes.

- Only what the Change adds or changes is judged. At Promotion IN that is what the promotion adds or changes relative to `main`. Dependencies already present are not judged, whatever advisories they have gained since.
- The severity policy is the one in force today and is retained: the pinned action's effective default, `low`, so any advisory counts, with no license, deny-list or scope checks and no warn-only mode. Changing it is an explicit decision of the Owner and a Change of its own.
- The verdict is a record, not only an exit status: the judge (release/A25) maps what the mechanism returns to PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN, and a mechanism that failed without a result is UNKNOWN, never FAIL by guess and never PASS (`judge-realization.md`).
- A FAIL carries corrective guidance: remove the dependency, or move to a version without the advisory. An exception for one advisory is an explicit, recorded Owner decision with a reason, never a silent allowance.
- The same Change receives the same verdict wherever it is judged, apart from advisories published in between, which is why a place judges again (release/F8).
