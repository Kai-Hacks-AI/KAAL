# release/F10 act-interface

The body of an ACT pull request is a short interface of exactly two level-2 chapters in this order, Change and Records, and nothing else: no other headings, commentary, retrospective text, test output, status reports or attribution.

- Change: one line, `<id> <name>: <short description>`, with the stable id, the name and what the Change is about.
- Records: what this ACT adds to the Change, named and not repeated. Today that is the 4L retrospective; it is a hint and not a closed list.

Each chapter is one paragraph of at most 200 characters, with no link or emoji. The template is shorter than the PLAN template. It names no follow-up action and no obligation, and it leaves the meaning of ACT open to grow. The content lives in the files of the Change, and discussion goes into comments.
