# release/F10 act-interface

The body of an ACT pull request is a short, guiding interface of two level-2 chapters in this order, Change and Records, and nothing else.

- Change: one line, `<id> <name>: <short description>`, so the Change the ACT concerns is identifiable.
- Records: what this ACT adds to the Change, named and not repeated. Today that is the 4L retrospective. It is a hint and not a closed list, so the scope of ACT stays open.

The body points to what the ACT records and does not repeat its artifacts, which live in the files of the Change. The template is shorter than the PLAN template. It names no follow-up action and no obligation, and the meaning of ACT is left to grow with experience.
