# release/F7 dependency-analysis

Dependency Review analyzes the dependencies a Change adds or changes against an advisory source, and classifies the result as one of the three outcomes of the common control contract. The analysis is deterministic. It establishes no truth of its own and applies no contextual judgment.

- **PASS** is a successful analysis that found no known vulnerable change at or above the severity the control holds. It is the only clean go.
- **FAIL** is a successful analysis that found one or more vulnerable changes. It names each dependency, its version, the advisory and its severity, and it needs a subsequent judgment.
- **UNKNOWN** is an inconclusive analysis: the advisory source or the dependency data was unavailable, or the result cannot be read. It needs a subsequent judgment, and UNKNOWN never passes.
- `passes` is true only for PASS. For FAIL and UNKNOWN, `passes: false` means no automatic clearance. It is not a final rejection and not a risk decision.
- The contextual assessment of a FAIL or an UNKNOWN belongs to a separate future agent Skill and an authorized decision process. It is not part of this requirement.

Further rules:

- Only what the Change adds or changes is analyzed. At Promotion IN that is what the promotion adds or changes relative to `main`. Dependencies already present are not analyzed, and the vulnerability state of every dependency already present in a promotion is not in scope, whatever advisories they have gained since.
- The severity policy is the one in force today and is retained: the pinned action's effective default, `low`, so any advisory counts, with no license, deny-list or scope checks and no warn-only mode. Changing it is an explicit decision of the Owner and a Change of its own.
- The outcome is a record, not only an exit status. The result interpreter (release/A25) classifies what the mechanism returned as PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN. A mechanism that failed without a result is UNKNOWN, never FAIL by guess and never PASS (`analysis-realization.md`).
- A FAIL carries corrective guidance: remove the dependency, or move to a version without the advisory. An exception for one advisory is an explicit, recorded Owner decision with a reason, never a silent allowance.
- The same Change receives the same outcome wherever it is analyzed, apart from advisories published in between, which is why a place analyzes again (release/F8).
