# release/F14 sync-interface

The body of a SYNC pull request is a short interface of exactly two level-2 chapters in this order, Change and Reason, and nothing else: no other headings, attribution, link to a conversation, generated footer or separator. A synchronization is driven by a Change and claims no Change Record of its own. Source and target are not chapters: the source is always `main`, the target is the base of the pull request, and the merge commit and the Branching check name the commit.

- Change: one line, `<id> <name>`, the stable identity of the Change that drives the synchronization. No description: the Change states itself.
- Reason: the specific purpose of this synchronization, in one short paragraph, such as making a promotion fresh or unblocking development. When the synchronization decides something itself at a conflict, the decision is stated here. Paths that take `main`'s content unchanged are not, because the Branching check names them.

The title of the pull request is `SYNC: <meaningful description>`, as the standing title convention says, and holds no Change id: the id stays in the Change chapter.

Each chapter carries concise author guidance. The body does not repeat the diff, the `main` commit, the collector, the merge method, the rule of synchronization, implementation details, test results, CI status or execution logs. Those live in Git, the pull request, the checks and `engineering/github/branching.md`. The interface guides the author and replaces no control. A worked body stands in `example.md`.
