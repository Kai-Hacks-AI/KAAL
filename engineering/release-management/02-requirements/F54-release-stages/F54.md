# release/F54 release-stages

Release management is a pipeline of **Stages**, taught by the Skill, declared and configured by the consumer, and realized by a binding.

- **Concept.** A Stage has a **Subject** (what moves through it), **gates** (named checks), **evidence** judged PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN (UNKNOWN never passes), an **authority** (who may pass it) and a **transition** (how the Subject leaves). A **route** (urgent, install) may enter the pipeline at a later Stage and passes the same gates there. The concept names no platform.
- **Declaration and configuration.** A consumer declares its Stages as plain configuration files in `.kaal/config/release/`, one per Stage, following the way Guard is configured. A Stage file states its order, name, Subject, gates by check name, authority, transition, target and the routes that may enter it. It names no platform, tool or registry. Declaring Stages does not require a new Core Node.
- **Binding.** How a platform realizes the declaration (GitHub branches, rulesets, required checks and tags; githooks hook points; a manual Owner checklist) is a separate binding, which reads the declaration, adds no Stage and changes no gate. Bindings are outside this requirement and are later Changes.
- **Separation.** Scope completeness, product verification and publication readiness remain separate gates (release/F53). A binding being absent is reported UNKNOWN, never as a pass.
