# release/F15 promotion-interface

The body of a promotion pull request is a short interface of exactly three level-2 chapters in this order, Collector, Delivers and Order, and nothing else. It reuses no heading of the PLAN, DO or ACT interfaces.

- Collector: one line, `<collector> → <destination>`, with the capability the collector carries.
- Delivers: one line per Change the collector carries, `<id> <PHASE>: <name>`, in the order the Changes landed. It names the Changes and describes none of them.
- Order: what the promotion waits for or travels with, such as a companion promotion, the synchronization that makes the collector fresh, or an older promotion ahead of it. It says `none` when nothing applies.

The body holds no description of new behavior, because a promotion delivers Changes that already landed in the collector. It holds no PLAN, test results, check results, approvals, retrospective, merge method or attribution. Those live in the files, the checks, the review history and Branching, and a statement of them in a body cannot be tied to the head it was written for.

Prior review is history: the approvals of the Changes were given to those Changes. The authorization of a promotion is the Owner's go on that promotion, recorded in its own review history, and the body neither carries nor implies it.

Each chapter is short and carries concise author guidance. The interface guides the author and replaces no control.
