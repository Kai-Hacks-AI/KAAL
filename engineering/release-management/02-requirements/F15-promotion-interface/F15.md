# release/F15 promotion-interface

The body of a promotion pull request is a short interface of exactly two level-2 chapters in this order, Change and Why now, and nothing else. They answer two distinct questions: what is delivered, and why delivering it now is worth it. It reuses no other heading of the PLAN, DO or ACT interfaces.

- Change: one line per Change the promotion delivers, `<id> <name>: <phases carried>`, with the stable id of the Change, not of a pull request, and the phases it carries (PLAN, DO, ACT). It describes none of the Changes.
- Why now: what delivering to `main` now makes possible, forward-looking. Any other Change or promotion it names carries its own reason, such as what this delivery unblocks or enables, so that no reference stands unexplained. It does not restate the PLAN, the diff, the Cases or the verification.

The body holds no source or destination (the pull request shows them), no description of new behavior (a promotion delivers Changes that already landed in a collector), no test or check results, no approvals, no retrospective, no merge method and no attribution. Those live in the files, the checks, the review history and Branching, and a statement of them in a body cannot be tied to the head it was written for.

Prior review is history: the approvals of the Changes were given to those Changes. The authorization of a promotion is the Owner's go on that promotion, recorded in its own review history, and the body neither carries nor implies it.

Each chapter is short and carries concise author guidance. The interface guides the author and replaces no control.
