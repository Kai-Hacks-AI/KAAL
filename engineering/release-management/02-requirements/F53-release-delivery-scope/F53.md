# release/F53 release-delivery-scope

`kaal-release` handles two meanings of Release and never conflates them.

- **Release as a static product entity** (`product x.y.z`) points at the **Intents** that constitute that version's product contract, retained Intents from earlier versions included. It is a statement of what the version is. It is not derived from the Changes of any one delivery.
- **Release as a delivery operation** points at the **Changes** to be delivered to realize that version. Its **delivery scope** is a Change set, each Change marked `required` or `optional`. The delivery scope is not the definition of the static Release.
- The existing **Change to Intent traceability** connects the two. The model adds no second traceability and does not infer a version's Intent set from its delivery scope.
- A report on a version keeps three questions apart. **Scope completeness**: is every required Change of the delivery scope delivered on `main`? **Product verification**: does the delivered product satisfy the contract's Intents? **Publication readiness**: may this version go to npmjs? Only the first is judged here. The other two are reported UNKNOWN until their own Changes define them, and a complete scope never implies either.
- Declaring a delivery scope or a contract publishes nothing, bumps no version and takes no promotion seat. Publishing to npmjs stays a separate step that needs the Owner's go.
