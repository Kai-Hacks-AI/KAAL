# release/F4 change-isolation-verdict

Change Isolation decides from the paths of a Change, and the rules it holds, one of the three verdicts of the common control contract. PASS: the Change conforms. FAIL: it does not, and the verdict names each offending path and the rule. UNKNOWN: it cannot judge. UNKNOWN never passes.

- The rules are Core travels alone with its listed supporting files, `.github` travels alone, and a transition that the target's ledger admits exactly is judged under that entry. The decision is path-level: an allowed supporting file passes whatever it contains.
- The rules and the verdicts exist once, in the judge. No adapter restates them.
- The same Change receives the same verdict wherever it is judged.
- How a platform derives the paths of a Change is the adapter's concern.
