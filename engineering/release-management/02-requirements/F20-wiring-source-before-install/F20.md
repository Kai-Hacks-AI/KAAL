# release/F20 wiring-source-before-install

The workflow and the ruleset that wire a control are source artifacts owned by Engineering, kept next to the artifacts of other controls, and proven by Cases on `main` before anything is installed in `.github`, `.githooks` or a live ruleset. Installing is a separate step, as the PR templates are installed. What can be tested outside the danger zone is tested there first.

- The source of a workflow reports its check for the events of its own target, under the job name that the ruleset source requires: Integration is the pull requests into the collectors `kaal/**`, Promotion is the pull requests into `main`. A target whose ruleset source has a merge queue also reports on the merge group; a target without one does not need to. Today only Promotion has a merge queue.
- The source of a ruleset is a new ruleset beside the existing ones, which are renamed with the prefix `old`. It does not require the branch to be up to date, and every context it requires is reported by a source workflow, and on the merge group wherever that ruleset has a merge queue.
- The installed copy of a workflow is the source, byte for byte.
- Applying a ruleset is never part of a Change. It is the Owner's go.
