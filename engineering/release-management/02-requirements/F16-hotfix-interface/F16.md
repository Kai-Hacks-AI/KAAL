# release/F16 hotfix-interface

The body of a Hotfix pull request is a short interface of exactly five level-2 chapters in this order, Change, Defect, Correction, Route and Recovery, and nothing else: no other headings, commentary, test results, CI status, execution logs or attribution. Apart from Change, which is the identity of the Change in every pull request, it reuses no heading of the PLAN, DO or ACT interface.

- Change: one line, `<id> <name>: <short description>`, with the stable id, the name and what this correction is.
- Defect: the symptom and its impact now, and the state that is defective. Production, `main` and the unpromoted work of a collector are three states, not one. The chapter names each state it concerns: production by package and published version, `main`, or a collector. A defect that exists only in `main` or in a collector says so and claims no production impact.
- Correction: what the correction changes and what it deliberately leaves out. A correction restores. It does not refactor, rename, reorder or bring along unrelated work that a collector holds.
- Route: how the correction reaches each state that must have it, named separately. `main`, by the promotion of the hotfix collector. Production, by saying whether a new version of a package is published and what else from `main` that version would carry, or that no publication is needed. The collectors that own the corrected behavior and must take it from `main` afterwards. Route names these and describes none of them: promotion, synchronization and publication have interfaces and controls of their own.
- Recovery: how each state named in Route returns to what it was if the correction proves wrong. `main` by undoing the one squash commit of the correction. Production by the previous published version, which is not replaced, because a published version cannot be published again.

Each chapter is short and carries concise author guidance. The body states no verification. What shows the defect and its correction is a Case that reproduces the defect, RED before the correction and GREEN after it, together with the checks and the review. The body points at none of them and repeats none. The interface guides the author and replaces no control.
