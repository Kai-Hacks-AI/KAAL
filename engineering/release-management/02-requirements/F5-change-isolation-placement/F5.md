# release/F5 change-isolation-placement

Change Isolation is applied where a Change can combine, or be combined, on its way to `main`, and each application is declared for this control on its own.

| Boundary | Class of the behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Development OUT | detective: judges a Change before it is handed on; advisory, because a developer can skip it |
| Integration IN | detective: judges a Change when it is admitted into a collector; preventive when `guard Integration` makes the verdict a condition of admission |
| Promotion IN | detective: judges a collector when it is admitted into `main`; preventive through `guard Integration` and the queue |
| Integration OUT | no application of its own: opening a promotion is judged at Promotion IN |

The rule is directive and is stated once. The offending paths and the Change they must move into are corrective guidance carried by every FAIL. A mechanism that another control shares still declares Change Isolation separately, so that its verdict is attributable to it.
