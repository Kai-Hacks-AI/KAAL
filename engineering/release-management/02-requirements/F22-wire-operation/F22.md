# release/F22 wire-operation

WIRE is a delivery operation, not a PDCA cycle. PDCA belongs to the collector; PROMOTION, HOTFIX and WIRE are the ways its result is delivered.

- Engineering defines, implements and verifies a delivery control in its collector, with isolated executable Cases that judge the control's source. The collector completes PDCA, with its ACT, before it is promoted.
- WIRE installs the approved artifact, already on `main`, into its operational location, `.githooks/` or `.github/`. The installed-equals-source Cases execute at WIRE and are never a prerequisite of the collector's ACT. A WIRE pull request names exactly three things: the Source (a path on `main`), the Target (the destination) and the Verification (the Case that compares each installed file with its source byte for byte, and the installation contract it satisfies).
- WIRE changes only target files, and each is an unchanged copy of its source, or the unchanged move of a legacy file. It develops no behaviour and redefines no requirement. If installing exposes a defect, the correction returns to the Engineering Change; WIRE never fixes it silently.
- The installed-equals-source Case and the operational acceptance belong to WIRE. They are not part of the collector's CHECK, which is what removes the circle between a collector and the Cases that are green only after the install.
- A WIRE runs on `wire/<name>`, analogous to `hotfix/<name>`, into `main` through a protected pull request with its own branch classification, required checks and ruleset. It is not a collector and takes no promotion seat; PROMOTION stays the only path from a qualified collector to `main`. It starts only once the approved source is on `main`.
- Delivery checks are Engineering's and stay outside `kaal-guard`.
