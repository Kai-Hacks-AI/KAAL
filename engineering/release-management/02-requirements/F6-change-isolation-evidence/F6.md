# release/F6 change-isolation-evidence

Every application of Change Isolation yields a record from the judge itself: the control, the boundary, the verdict and the offending paths, in a machine-readable form that does not depend on a platform's logs.

A claim that the control operated, or that it prevented an integration, cites such a record, an observed check result for a named commit, or a merge record. A declared mechanism, a configuration or a ruleset is evidence of neither.
