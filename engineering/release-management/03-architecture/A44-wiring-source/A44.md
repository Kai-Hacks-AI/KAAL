The source area `engineering/release-management/05-src/wiring/`, outside the danger zone. `workflows/<control>-<target>.yml` holds the source of a workflow, one per control and target. `rulesets/<target>.json` holds the source of the new ruleset of a target, whose required contexts are an array that many controls contribute to.
