# release/A24 analyze Vulnerabilities

The control analyze Vulnerabilities: it analyzes and classifies, it does not judge, and C5 alone prevents nothing. What it does at a target is detective until the ruleset of that target requires its check, on the Owner's go. It consumes the common relationship model, the Testing model and the common control contract, and owns none of them. Its result interpreter (release/A25) holds the classification into the three outcomes. It is applied through one deployment target, GitHub (release/A26). Guard connects the control's declared check to its places; this control restates no part of Guard.
