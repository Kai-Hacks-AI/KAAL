# release/A18 judge

The one realization of the rules and the verdicts, in `engineering/change-isolation/check.mjs`. It takes the paths of a Change and the rules and ledger that the target holds, and returns PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN with the offending paths, and a record of the verdict. It knows no platform: no pull request, branch, hook or merge group. The ledger entry that admits one exact multi-owner transition is part of its rules and is read from the target, never from the candidate.
