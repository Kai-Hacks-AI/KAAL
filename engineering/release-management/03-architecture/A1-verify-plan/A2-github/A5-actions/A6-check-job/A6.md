# A6 check-job

A component. The job named `verify Plan` runs the reader, the resolver, the evidence reader, the rules and the reporter, and reports its verdict as a status. A PASS verdict, including a PLAN whose expected RED is accepted, reports a green status. FAIL and UNKNOWN report a red status.
