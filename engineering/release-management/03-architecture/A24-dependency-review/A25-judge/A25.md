# release/A25 judge

The one place that decides a verdict from the added or changed dependencies of a Change and an advisory source, with the severity the control holds. The judge is a small function, `05-src/dependency-verdict/judge.mjs`, that takes what the mechanism returned (its outcome and its vulnerable-changes output) and returns the verdict with its findings. The mechanism of GitHub is retained and does the comparison; the judge only reads its result. No adapter restates the rule. How a platform derives the dependencies and the advisories is the adapter's concern.
