# release/A24 dependency-review

The control Dependency Review, named verify Dependencies as its verb: it judges, and C5 alone prevents nothing. What it does at a target is detective until the ruleset of that target requires its check, on the Owner's go. It consumes the common relationship model, the Testing model and the common control contract, and owns none of them. Its judge (release/A25) holds the rule and the verdicts. It is applied through one deployment target, GitHub (release/A26). Guard connects the control's declared check to its places; this control restates no part of Guard.
