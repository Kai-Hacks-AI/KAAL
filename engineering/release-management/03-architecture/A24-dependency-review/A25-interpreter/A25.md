# release/A25 interpreter

The result interpreter of analyze Dependencies. It is a small deterministic function, `05-src/dependency-analysis/interpreter.mjs`, that takes what the mechanism returned (its outcome and its vulnerable-changes output) and classifies it as PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN, with the findings. It is not an agent and not a judge: it applies no assessment, enforcement or workflow integration. The mechanism of GitHub is retained and does the comparison and the advisory lookup; the interpreter only reads its result. No adapter restates the classification. How a platform derives the dependencies and the advisories is the adapter's concern.
