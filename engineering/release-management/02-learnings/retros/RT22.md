- subject: a Case that counts text over a whole file breaks when a job is added
  source: T1344, T1358 and T1372 failed on the first run of the changed workflow source; I fixed two by changing my own step and variable names, and the third (no installation) needed the decision above.
- subject: a pattern written for a block of text can match nothing and still pass
  source: my first T1532 matched no checkout at all; a mutant that removed `persist-credentials: false` survived until I added a count of the blocks read.
