Account of: I am the release thread. I planned and built C164, the merge-group review that no longer drops a group on one refused read: one pure judgment of the group's checks, a retry of a refused read with a growing wait, and a poll interval that grows. This is my account only: the CPO and the Owner have not been asked what they lacked, and nothing here is installed; the event-driven completion is deferred and the installed workflow is unchanged.

## Liked
- subject: the Owner's direction to plan the event design first and then work it into the collector
  keeps: the PLAN named the design questions and kept the event path out of the DO, so the CPO could approve resilient polling without the open questions blocking it.
- subject: Cases written red before the DO with a stub `gh`
  keeps: T1601 and T1602 pinned retry, exit codes and the read budget, so the DO changed no expectation.

## Learned
- subject: a wait that fails closed on any failed read cannot tell a refused read from an unreadable one
  source: the merge group that dropped out on an HTTP 403 rate limit; the DO retries only refusals and ends any other failed read in exit 2.

## Lacked
- subject: the merge-group review had no Case of its own, so its loop was never tested against refusals
  decision-point: the earlier design of the two-check review, which wrote the loop inline in the workflow.
  cost: one dropped merge group and this Change.
  findable: yes
  address: Case

## Longed For

  worked-anyway: the PLAN kept the poll as the watchdog; the answer waits for a throwaway group and the Owner's go.
