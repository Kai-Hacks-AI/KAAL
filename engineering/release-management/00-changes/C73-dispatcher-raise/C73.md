# release/C73 the Dispatcher raises the PROMOTION of a collector whose ACT has merged

Second slice of the Dispatcher, after release/C72 (the decision). The KAAL Dispatcher is a GitHub App that plays the Worker role: it releases work to the next station when the work is ready. This Change gives it its identity and its first action. A workflow reads the facts of each collector through the API, decides with release/C72's `decideRaise`, and, only when the repository variable `KAAL_DISPATCH_RAISE` is exactly `on`, raises the PROMOTION as a pull request from the collector into main. Otherwise it says what it would raise.

The boundary follows release/I35. The job that reads the facts holds only the read token of the workflow and no App key. The job that raises holds the App token and reads no collector at all: it takes the decision as data, reads it strictly, and makes one write, `POST /pulls`. The Dispatcher never approves, never merges, never enables auto-merge and never pushes, and raising is idempotent per candidate state: a retried or overlapping run never makes a second PROMOTION for the same collector at the same revision, also not in the race between looking and creating. The seat (how many PROMOTIONs may be open at once) is a replaceable policy, not an invariant of the Dispatcher: today it is one at a time (Kai's loop: one PROMOTION, a SYNC only if needed to enable it, the merge, then the next), with a capacity that release/C89 and C75 may raise. Triggers are `schedule` and `workflow_dispatch` only, so the workflow always runs from the default branch.

It is a source in Engineering. Nothing here changes `.github`, a ruleset, a secret or a setting. Creating the App and storing its key are the Owner's steps, due after this PLAN merges and before the WIRE.
