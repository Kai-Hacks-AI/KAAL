# release/C124 verify Workflow names, a ratchet adopted as a preventive control

Of the 22 workflows installed in `.github/workflows`, five carry only the name `KAAL` (`branching-audit`, `change-isolation`, `ci`, `lineage-synchronization`, `promotion-source`), which tells the Actions list nothing about what they run. Nothing keeps a new workflow from doing the same. This Change adds a control, `verify Workflow names`, that says in or out of specification for the workflow names of a pull request, and adopts it as a **ratchet on a preventive control**: the five stay a known allowance that can only shrink, and any other nonconforming workflow is new and blocks. Controls owns the generic mechanics (the accepted baseline and the no-regression behavior, kaal-controls C21 and C22); release adopts the ratchet, registers the control and supplies the evidence and the exit; planning owns any deadline. It installs nothing and changes no ruleset.
