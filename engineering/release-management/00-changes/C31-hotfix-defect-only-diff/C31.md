# release/C31 hotfix defect-only diff

A HOTFIX corrects only its defect. The envelope cannot show that, and today every HOTFIX result leaves it to the reviewer. This Change gives the diff a deterministic judge on the changed paths: a HOTFIX diff carries the Case of its defect, and stays inside one capability root. What the paths cannot show, whether the correction is minimal, stays unjudged and the verdict stays UNKNOWN. It decides nothing about the Hotfix delivery path, the base, admission, wiring or any ruleset.
