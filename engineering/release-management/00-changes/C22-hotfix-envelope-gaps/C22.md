# release/C22 hotfix envelope gaps

The envelope check judges what a HOTFIX envelope can show deterministically and nothing more. A pull request from a `hotfix/` head must be a HOTFIX, an empty Defect, Urgency or Approach chapter is a finding, and the Change chapter of a HOTFIX is one line `<id> <name>`. Whether the change corrects only its defect, whether the Defect and the Urgency are true, and the base stay unjudged, and the result says so. It decides nothing about the Hotfix delivery path and adds no required defect reference, which would amend release/F16.
