# C130 baseline

The baseline is `main` after the PROMOTION of controls control-modes (#2325). `engineering/release-management/05-src/triggers/verify-triggers.mjs` judges two maps of workflow path to text (the base and the head) and answers PASS, FAIL (a workflow uses `pull_request_target` on the head and did not on the base, or the count rises) or UNKNOWN (a side could not be read). It keeps its own rule; it does not use `judge-mode`. The base is the baseline and nothing is hand-kept. The count on `main` is 5 (names above). `verify Triggers` is a detective check (release/CTL19, status designed); the requirement that makes it block is release/CTL20, a preventive control that the `promoting` ruleset names. The ruleset is loaded by the Owner; whether it is loaded today is the Owner's to say. The ratchet mechanics of kaal-controls (accepted baseline, `previous` from the base, `next` only after an accepted run, rejected runs advance nothing) are on `main`; the registration of a ratchet as a strategy of a preventive control is controls C23 (#2354, not yet on `main`).
