# release/C130 verify Triggers on the improved ratchet, registered as a preventive control

`verify Triggers` (release/CTL19) is the first ratchet of the repository: the base is its baseline, a workflow that uses `pull_request_target` may not be added and their number may not rise, and removing one passes. Five installed workflows use the trigger today: `change-isolation`, `lineage-synchronization`, `merge-group-admission`, `promotion-source` and `sync-review`. Its enforcement is a separate control, release/CTL20, which says the `promoting` ruleset requires it. This Change moves the ratchet onto the generic mechanics of kaal-controls (C21, C22, C23): `verify Triggers` reports findings, the shared judge applies the accepted baseline, and the ratchet is declared as a strategy of a preventive control, with evidence that the baseline persists and an exit when the last use is gone. It is the first real application of the improved ratchet. It installs nothing, changes no ruleset, and removes no use of the trigger.
