# release/C32 release delivery scope

`kaal-release` separates the two meanings of Release. A version as a static product entity points at its Intents. A version as a delivery operation points at the Changes to be delivered, its delivery scope. This Change declares the delivery scope as a Change set with a judge of its completeness, keeps the Intent contract as its own part of the model, and states that scope completeness is neither product verification nor publication readiness.
