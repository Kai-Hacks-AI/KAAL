# release/C134 the ratchets of verify Triggers and verify Workflow names keep their history

Controls C25 (on main, #2469) makes the history part of a ratchet: a `history.jsonl` beside the baseline file, one line per change of the baseline, written in git by the pull request that changes it and judged by `judgeProgress` and `judgeRetirement` of the shared ratchet library. Release adopts it in the two ratchets it owns, release/CTL20 (`gate verify Triggers`) and release/CTL36 (`verify Workflow names`). This is item 3 of the ratchet standard, the adoption in the existing ratchets. It is a PLAN; it adds the two files and their Cases in the DO, and no judgment, report, workflow or WIRE.
