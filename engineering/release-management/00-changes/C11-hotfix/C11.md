# release/C11 create Hotfix

Introduces the pull request template of a Hotfix: a short body of four chapters: the identity of the Change, and then the defect and the state it is in, why it cannot wait, and why the chosen path is the fastest safe one from RED to GREEN. It defines no other element of a Hotfix and changes no control, route or collector.
