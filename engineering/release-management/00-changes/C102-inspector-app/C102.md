# release/C102 the KAAL Inspector App is declared by release, with least privilege

The Owner decided on 20 Nov 2026 that release owns the Inspector App next to the Dispatcher: the two Apps are the shopfloor roles that act at the station gates. Controls keeps the concept and the rules the Inspector enforces (kaal-controls, release/C14 of controls: the judge step, the sign step, the workflow template). This Change is release's first slice and declares what an App is, so that creating it by hand is a copy of reviewed source and not a decision made in a settings page.

It declares, in Engineering source, the identity of the App (`05-src/inspector/app.json`: name, permissions, no webhook, no subscribed event, key custody by name only) and the separation of its workflow (`05-src/wiring/workflows/inspector.yml`: a judge job that holds no secret and runs the base, a sign job that is the only one to hold the key). It adds the Intent release/I55, the Requirement release/F79 and the Architecture release/A70.

Not here: creating the App (the Owner, by hand, from `app.json`), storing its key, the workflow under `.github`, a required check in a ruleset (the check can only be pinned to the App's integration id once the App exists), the two-tree run (C94) and the swap of `pull_request_target` in the legacy sync review. Each is a later slice. It depends on the judge, the sign step and the workflow template of controls (slices 2 to 4); the DO waits until those are on `main`, and the PLAN says where it reuses them.
