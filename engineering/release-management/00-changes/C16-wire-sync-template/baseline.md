# C16 baseline

The baseline is `main`. Work that is not on `main` is not delivered functionality, and this Change depends on nothing that is not on `main` once release/C9 is delivered.

- `main` holds the PLAN, ACT, DO and Promotion templates in `.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE/` and no SYNC template.
- Nothing on `main` states where the SYNC template is installed or shows that an installed copy is the capability text.
