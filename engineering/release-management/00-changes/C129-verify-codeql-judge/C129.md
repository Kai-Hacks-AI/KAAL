# release/C129 the judges of the CodeQL gate: verify CodeQL analysis and verify CodeQL findings

SECURITY.md says missing or failed CodeQL analysis, and findings above the threshold, must prevent integration. The ruleset rule "Code scanning results / CodeQL" does not guarantee it: on #2313 it completed after the first upload and stayed neutral while `actions` was still missing. Engineering's PLAN (`engineering/C32`, #2346) puts a job after every `analyze` in the workflow source and leaves the judges to release (release analysis D4). The CPO's direction on this PLAN is that the two concerns are two controls with their own evidence and verdicts, which a workflow may orchestrate but not combine. This Change plans and builds both judges: **verify CodeQL analysis** (every expected configuration has an error-free analysis of the merge-result sha) and **verify CodeQL findings** (no open alert on the merge ref at or above the threshold). It installs nothing and changes no ruleset.
