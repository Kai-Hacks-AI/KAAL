# release/C129 verify CodeQL judge, the findings of the CodeQL gate

SECURITY.md says missing or failed CodeQL analysis, and findings above the threshold, must prevent integration. The ruleset rule "Code scanning results / CodeQL" does not guarantee it: on #2313 it completed after the first upload and stayed neutral while `actions` was still missing. Engineering's PLAN (`engineering/C32`, #2346) puts one job `verify CodeQL` after every `analyze` in the workflow source and leaves the judge to release (release analysis D4). This Change plans and builds that judge: a script that reads the analyses and the open alerts of the run's sha and ref and writes the findings as a list of names. It also declares the control `verify CodeQL` (preventive). It installs nothing and changes no ruleset.
