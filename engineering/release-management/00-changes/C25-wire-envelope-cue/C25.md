# release/C25 wire envelope cue

The wiring of the Change envelope cue of release/C24 into the pull request templates installed under `.github/`: the five typed templates `plan`, `do`, `act`, `sync` and `promotion` and the default `pull_request_template.md`. Each opens its body with the one fixed cue line of release/C24, written once as visible text. It adds no hook, check, ruleset or registry, and it changes neither the chapters nor the interface of any template. It comes after release/C24 is on `main`.
