# release/C25 wire envelope cue

The Change envelope cue of release/C24 reaches the six pull request templates installed in GitHub in two steps. First the cue enters their Engineering sources on `main` through a normal KAAL promotion with no `.github` lines. Then a WIRE installs the six unchanged copies. The cue text, the chapters and the hints of every template stay as they are. It adds no hook, check, ruleset or registry, and it does not execute a WIRE: WIRE needs release/C27 and the Owner's authorization.
