# release/C58 verify, approve and review a SYNC

Declares three controls over a SYNC and builds their source. `verify Sync` (release/CTL7, detective) proves that a SYNC is a pure merge of `main`: a merge commit of the base's tip and a state already on `main`, whose tree is exactly git's clean merge of the two and which carries nothing else. `approve Sync` (release/CTL8, preventive) gives such a SYNC the approval it needs, and nothing else. `review Sync` (release/CTL9, corrective) puts every other SYNC, and every SYNC it cannot judge, in front of a person. All three answer the new Intent release/I32.

A pure SYNC holds nothing a reviewer can judge: its tree is fixed by its two parents. Today a person approves each one, and a catch-up merge dismisses that approval again.

It is a source in Engineering. Installing the workflow under `.github`, the repository setting that lets Actions approve, and switching the approval on are later steps with the Owner's go. Nothing here changes `.github`, a ruleset or a setting.
