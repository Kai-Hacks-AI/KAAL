# release/C99 verify main history

Declares the Intent release/I54 (`main` only moves forward and is never deleted) and a detective control, release/CTL22 `verify History`, with its own declared minimum evidence and its own Cases. It is the first legacy protection of the old promotion ruleset (`non_fast_forward` and `deletion` on `main`) defined the correct way: an Intent, a classification, a judge, Cases that create the failure, and the evidence it needs before it can claim Tested.

It is Engineering source: nothing is installed, wired or required here. Registering the check, replacing the old ruleset rules by a preventive control with the same judge, and deleting the old rules are separate steps with the Owner's decisions.
