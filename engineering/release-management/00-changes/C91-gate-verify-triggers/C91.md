# release/C91 gate verify Triggers

Makes the ratchet of release/C90 preventive: the `promoting` ruleset requires `verify Triggers`, so a promotion into `main` that adds a workflow using `pull_request_target`, or raises their number, cannot merge. It answers the Owner's decision of 18 Nov 2026 ("we want a preventive control here", and "it should go now in"), after release/C90 made the ratchet a detective check.

It adds the preventive control release/CTL20 (`gate verify Triggers`, with the Intent release/I50 of release/C90), its Requirement release/F68 and Architecture release/A63, the declaration of the check in `stages.json`, the check in the `promoting.json` source, and a change to the workflow source so that the check also runs in the merge queue (`merge_group`): a required check that never reports there would hold the queue.

Scope, and why not yet `integrating`: the judge exists only on branches that hold `main`'s release/C90. A pull request into an older collector, or a `wire/**` or `hotfix/**` branch, has a base without it, and the check fails closed there, so requiring it in `integrating` would block those branches until each is synchronized. `promoting` is enough to keep a sixth use off `main`: its base always holds the judge, and a `wire/**` or `hotfix/**` branch reaches `main` only through a PROMOTION. `integrating` follows once the collectors hold the judge, as its own step.

It is Engineering source. Nothing is installed, wired or loaded here: the amended workflow reaches `.github` by a second WIRE, and `promoting.json` is loaded by the Owner, by hand, after that WIRE is on `main` and the check has run green on a real pull request.
