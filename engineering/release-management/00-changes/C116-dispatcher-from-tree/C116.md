# release/C116 the Dispatcher raises the PROMOTION of a collector from its tree

Today the Dispatcher decides that the final step of a collector merged from the body of the merged pull request (the chapters of its body) and writes the title of the PROMOTION from the first line of that body. Both are read from prose that an agent writes, and the second one has already produced a wrong title that had to be rewritten by hand. This Change makes the Dispatcher read the merged tree instead, with the judges of the ACT that now exist, and compute the title and the body of the PROMOTION from the collector's name and its Changes. It dispatches and nothing else: the pick (which collector is worked next) stays with people, and "Why now" is a judgement that Promoting writes.
