# release/C66 a merge into an integration branch needs a fresh approval

Declares the preventive control `approve Integration` (release/CTL10) and drafts its source: a `pull_request` rule in the `integrating` ruleset (`05-src/wiring/rulesets/integrating.json`, targets `kaal/**`, `hotfix/**` and `wire/**`) that requires one approving review and dismisses an approval when a commit is pushed after it. It answers the Owner's ruling of 17 Nov 2026: "approval is a necessary rule and going stale on PR update too, reason: too easy otherwise to forge success for development -> integration branches". Today the `integrating` source requires checks only, and the approval of an integration branch exists only in the old integration ruleset (24952357), alongside 13 other requirements.

No bypass actor. The old integration ruleset stays untouched until a new ruleset is proven equivalent, and then one rule at a time (see Follow-up). It is a source in Engineering: nothing is loaded, installed or switched here, and no ruleset or setting changes. Loading is the Owner's, by hand, and timed (see `change.md`).
