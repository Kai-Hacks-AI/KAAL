# release/C27 wire delivery operation

WIRE is a delivery operation beside PROMOTION and HOTFIX. It installs an approved Engineering artifact that is already on `main` into its operational location (`.githooks/` or `.github/`). It develops nothing and is not a PDCA cycle.
