# C133 baseline

The baseline is `main` after #2443 is promoted or not: nothing here depends on it. Five workflows use `pull_request_target` on `main`; the ratchet of `verify Triggers` (release/CTL20, baseline `04-controls/CTL20-gate-verify-triggers/baseline.json`) holds them. `merge-group-admission` (D13) and `promotion-source` (D14, a relay and a handler, release/C121) are fixed in source on `kaal/release/no-pull-request-target` and wait for their WIRE. `sync-review`, `change-isolation` and `lineage-synchronization` are the three left, and paused as D15 to D17 until the route was chosen. The rule that only declared roots travel on `wire/*` is the wire controls plan (CPO, 23 November): its step 1 is the declared-set rule, step 3 the ruleset. This Change depends on step 3.
