# release/C93 the final step of work raises its PROMOTION as a draft

First slice of the Dispatcher concept of the Owner (18 and 19 Nov 2026): the open PROMOTION pull requests are the queue. When the final step of work merges into an integration branch (an ACT into a collector, a fix into a hotfix branch, a copy into a wire branch), a workflow raises that branch's PROMOTION to `main` as a draft, once. This Change is that raise and nothing else: no pick, no SYNC, no ready, no merge. A draft carries no authority and costs no review; what reviews and merges a PROMOTION is unchanged.

It replaces the scan of release/C72 and release/C73 as the way a PROMOTION is raised. The decision is read from the event of the merge, as data. The App, its token and the idempotency of release/C73 are reused.
