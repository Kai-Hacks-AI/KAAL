# release/C96 delivery travels apart from solution, and the consumers read the declaration

The second slice of release/C95. The declaration of delivery locations (release/A67) is now read by the two WIRE consumers. This Change extends it to every delivery location of the repository and gives it two more consumers, each owned elsewhere: Change Isolation, which gains the rule that delivery never travels with solution (detective, gating nothing), and Pull Promotion, whose DO evidence counts delivery from the declaration instead of from a list of its own.

The Owner (19 Nov 2026): delivery never travels with solution, and several delivery locations may share one WIRE. Delivery is every declared location, and the root manifest and lockfile are delivery too. The design is in the step-aggregator design, sections 6 and 6a. Core's own rule that it travels alone is not touched.
