# C53 act

A 4L retrospective of the Change, from the viewpoint of the agent that carried it.

## Liked

The SYNC template now argues from the branch it enters: its Successor row says that the branch requires a merge commit of main and green checks, and that the merge starts nothing. The Reason chapter keeps its two purposes, so the SYNC still says why it exists, but the table no longer tells the reader what happens next elsewhere. T700 went RED in the PLAN and green in the DO; T650 to T653 stayed green after T652 was reworded.

## Learned

The purpose of a SYNC belongs in its Reason chapter, where the person who opens it states it. The table is for what the target branch needs to accept it. Keeping the two apart removed the last place where the SYNC template spoke about another process.

## Lacked

A check that reads the SYNC template for the names of other processes. T700 pins one row; nothing stops a later edit from naming a neighbour in another row.

## Longed For

One reading of the ACT, SYNC and PROMOTION templates side by side, now that each argues from its own branch, before the Owner decides what to install.
