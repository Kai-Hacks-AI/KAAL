# release/C70 verify Stages accepts the extra rules a stage declares

A stage in `stages.json` may declare `alsoRules`: a list of ruleset rules, each `{ type, parameters }`, that its branch ruleset holds after `required_status_checks`. The judge of `verify Stages` accepts exactly those, in that order and with exactly those parameters, and still fails a missing, an undeclared, a reordered or a differently parametrized rule. No stage declares `alsoRules` in this Change, so the judge's verdict on the real sources does not change.

It is the precursor of release/C66, as release/C59 was of release/C56: the judge of `Engineering / verify Stages` is the default branch's own, so a ruleset source that holds a second rule (the approval of release/CTL10) can only pass once main's judge accepts a declared second rule. Nothing under `.github` changes in this Change.
