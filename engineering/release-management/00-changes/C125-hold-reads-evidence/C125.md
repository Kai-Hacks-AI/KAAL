# release/C125 hold Act reads Testing's evidence and does not pass on a catalog it knows is incomplete

`hold Act` asks whether the Cases that apply to a collector are really green at its head. Today the runner cannot answer it: it returns UNKNOWN "Testing's observation of the Cases at this revision is not available" on every run, because nothing hands it an observation. Testing has built the observation (`observe`, `testing/C74`): a map of Case id to GREEN, RED or UNKNOWN, with the evidence that says how far to trust it, in particular whether its catalog of Cases is complete. This Change makes the judge read that evidence file, and refuse a PASS when the evidence says the catalog is not complete, whatever the map says. It also reads both quote forms of a Case id, and lands the three Cases that Testing's mutation pilot justified. Testing reports findings; release owns the verdict. It is the precondition of wiring Testing's producing job, and it installs nothing itself.
