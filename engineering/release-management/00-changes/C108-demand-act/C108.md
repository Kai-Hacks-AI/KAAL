# release/C108 a Change is closed by its ACT before it is promoted

The second of the three small controls of the ACT (Owner, 20 Nov 2026): `hold Act` (release/C103) keeps an `act.md` out of a collector while the work is not finished; `demand Act` (this Change) asks for the `act.md` once the work is finished; `verify Act` will check its format. A collector's PLANs land with CI green while their Cases are really red, DOs turn them really green, and only then can the ACT land: the ACT is the last step, and only then is the collector done. `demand Act` is the other half of the pair: with everything really green and no `act.md`, the Change is unclosed and the promotion must not carry it.

It sits on the promotion, at the Promoting station, and never on the collector, where it would hold back the DO that closes the last red Case and so deadlock the work. This Change is the first slice: the Intent release/I58, the Requirement release/F84, the Architecture element release/A74 (the judge), the preventive control release/CTL29 `demand Act`, and the judge `05-src/phase/judge-demand-act.mjs` with its command line. The judge is pure over the carried Changes and asks the judge of release/C103 whether work is really green, so the two controls cannot disagree about it.
