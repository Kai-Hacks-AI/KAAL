# release/C108 a collector is closed by its retro before it is promoted

The second of the three small controls of the ACT (Owner, 20 Nov 2026): `hold Act` (release/C103) keeps an ACT out of a collector while the work is not finished; `demand Act` (this Change) asks for the ACT once the work is finished; `verify Act` will check its format. The ACT is the retro of the collector, `retro -reflects-> Changes` (Owner, 20 Nov 2026), in `02-learnings/retros/`, kaal-retro's object; a collector has exactly one ACT, one retro that reflects the complete set of the Changes it carries, and a Change is closed when that retro reflects it (Owner, 20 Nov 2026: one collector, one ACT, the full set). A collector's PLANs land with CI green while their Cases are really red, DOs turn them really green, and only then can ACT land: the ACT is the last step, and only then is the collector done. `demand Act` is the other half of the pair: with everything really green and a carried Change that the ACT does not reflect, or no ACT, the collector is unclosed and must not be promoted; and a collector with more than one ACT is refused at any time, however its Changes are divided between them.

It sits on the promotion, at the Promoting station, and never on the collector, where it would hold back the DO that closes the last red Case and so deadlock the work. This Change is the first slice: the Intent release/I58, the Requirement release/F84, the Architecture element release/A74 (the judge), the control release/CTL29 `demand Act` (detective first, preventive when required), and the judge `05-src/phase/judge-demand-act.mjs` with its command line. The judge is pure over the carried Changes, the retros (read only by `reflects`, so it is id-agnostic) and the observations, and asks the judge of release/C103 whether work is really green, so the two controls cannot disagree about it.
