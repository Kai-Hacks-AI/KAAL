# C19 act

A 4L retrospective of the Change, from the viewpoint of the agent that carried it.

## Liked

The amendment being small because the earlier Change had a single place for the rule. One sentence in F18, one assertion in T174, and one new requirement carried the whole ruling. Naming the Case that changes, and why, in the PLAN made the review of the DO a check against a list, not a hunt.

## Learned

A ruling that reverses a delivered statement needs its own requirement and not an edit in place, because the old statement and its Case then have to change in the same step, and the reviewer has to see that the change follows the ruling and does not serve a green run. I also learned that ids are claimed in other threads before they appear in the repository: the repository showed C16 as the highest, yet C17 and C18 were already taken, and I only found that out from a message. Searching the branches was not enough.

## Lacked

One list of claimed ids that is current, including the ones whose Change has no file yet. I took C17 and had to move to C19 after a review round. Also a way to see, before opening a PLAN, that a neighbouring Change (the check of the pull request envelope) must change with mine; I learned it from the ruling and not from the files.

## Longed For

A claim step for an id that costs one line and shows in the same place as the merged ones. A check that flags a delivered Case whose assertion is changed by a later Change, so the reviewer sees the list of such changes without reading the diff.
