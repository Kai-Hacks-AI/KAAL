# C52 act

A 4L retrospective of the Change, from the viewpoint of the agent that carried it.

## Liked

The ACT template now argues from the branch it enters: its Successor row says that the collector requires the 4L retrospective recorded and green checks, and that the merge starts nothing. It names no other process. T690 went RED in the PLAN and green in the DO, and T640 to T642 stayed green after T642 was reworded.

## Learned

A template that names its neighbours drifts when they change. Stating what the target branch requires keeps the row true even when the next step is decided elsewhere. The Owner's ruling made this visible only because the same wording appeared in four templates.

## Lacked

A check that reads a template for the names of other processes. T690 pins this one row; nothing stops a later edit from naming a neighbour again.

## Longed For

The same rule applied to the SYNC and PROMOTION templates (C53, C54), then one reading of all templates side by side before they are wired.
