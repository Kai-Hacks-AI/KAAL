# release/C59 verify Stages accepts the extra checks a stage declares

A stage in `stages.json` may declare `also`: a list of full check names its ruleset requires beside its own `verify Stages (<Name>)`. The judge of `verify Stages` accepts exactly those, in that order, and still fails any other, missing or undeclared check. No stage declares `also` in this Change, so the judge's verdict on the real sources does not change.

It is the precursor of release/C56: the judge of `Engineering / verify Stages` is the default branch's own, so a ruleset source that requires a second check can only pass once main's judge accepts a declared second check. Nothing under `.github` changes in this Change.
