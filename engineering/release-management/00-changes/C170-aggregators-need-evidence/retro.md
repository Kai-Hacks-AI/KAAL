# release/C170 ACT: retro

The PLAN (#2981), the DO (#3053) and this ACT are the whole Change. Nothing is wired: no WIRE, flag entry, ruleset change or swap. The installed workflows do not change by this Change; the aggregators of the plain workflows carry the new rule in their sources.

## What was delivered
The three aggregators (`change-isolation-plain.yml`, `branching-plain.yml`, `promotion-source-plain.yml`) name the entries they expect in their own text (`EXPECTED`, equal to the job's `needs`) and take the results of `needs` as data (`toJSON(needs)`). They pass only when the reported set equals the expected set exactly and every expected entry is `success`. An empty result, an absent, failed, skipped, cancelled or unknown result, and an unexpected entry each fail and name the entry. Names, `if: always()`, permissions, triggers and the `merge_group` reporters are unchanged. Cases T1660 to T1664 are green and T1643 of C168 is untouched.

## What we learned
- **A loop over nothing is a pass.** The C168 aggregators passed when the list of results was empty. The CPO's review of the C168 DO found it; the correction is not a longer loop but a named expectation set apart from what is reported.
- **Equality, not a count.** A count of results is not evidence: one extra entry can hide a missing one. T1664 pins both directions.
- **A SYNC, not a hand merge.** Merging main into the DO branch made Branching refuse it as an unsynchronised synchronization. The collector needed its own SYNC (#3054) after C168 landed; the DO then merged the collector. The rule held: sync a collector only after the promotion ahead of it landed.
