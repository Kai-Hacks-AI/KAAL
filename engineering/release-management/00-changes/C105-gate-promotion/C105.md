# release/C105 gate Promotion

The Owner's decisions of 20 Nov 2026: the `non_fast_forward` flag moves from the old promotion ruleset to the new `promoting` ruleset, the detective push judge release/CTL22 `verify History` has no future, and the real prevention is the CI of the station, one preventive gate per station that requires the detections to pass and holds the rules the station declares. A push that does not move `main` forward is refused before it lands, not revealed afterwards.

This is the first slice of the gate of the Promoting station. It adds the preventive control release/CTL25 `gate Promotion` (the rule `non_fast_forward` in `05-src/wiring/rulesets/promoting.json`, declared in `stages.json` and traced to it, so `verify Stages` judges it), its detective partner release/CTL26 `verify Promotion gate` (does what the station declares hold in the rules in force for the default branch: PASS, FAIL naming what is missing, UNKNOWN; a pure judge and a command line, `05-src/history/judge-held.mjs`) and the Requirement release/F81 and Architecture release/A72 of the partner. The Intent release/I54, the Requirement release/F77 and the Architecture release/A68 stay and move to the gate. release/CTL22, its judge `verify-history.mjs`, its workflow source and its Cases leave in the DO.

The station owns its required list: `stages.json` declares the rules and checks of the Promoting stage, each traced to a control, and the detections it requires do not name the station. Later slices, each its own Change, move the single-check gates release/CTL5 (`gate analyze Vulnerabilities`) and release/CTL20 (`gate verify Triggers`), and likely release/CTL10, into the gate's required set and retire them; the Intents they carry then join the gate. This Change only names that.

Not here, each its own step: loading the ruleset (the Owner's, by hand, with his explicit go), retiring the rule of the old ruleset (the Owner's, after the new rule is loaded and observed in force), reading the rules in force from GitHub through Hosting and running the partner on a schedule or a push, the move of CTL5, CTL20 and CTL10 into the gate, and the protection against a deletion of `main` (the `deletion` rule: its own Intent and control).

## Challenge to the legacy controls

release/CTL22 is retired here. The old ruleset's `non_fast_forward` rule is replaced by the same rule in `promoting`; retiring the old one is the Owner's step after it. The `deletion` rule of the old ruleset stays until its own control exists. CTL5, CTL20 and CTL10 are named as dissolving into the gate in later slices.
