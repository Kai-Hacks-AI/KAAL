# release/C105 hold History

The Owner's decision of 20 Nov 2026: the `non_fast_forward` flag moves from the old promotion ruleset to the new `promoting` ruleset, held by a preventive control, and the detective push judge release/CTL22 `verify History` has no future. A push that does not move `main` forward is refused before it lands, not revealed afterwards.

It adds the preventive control release/CTL25 `hold History` (the rule `non_fast_forward` in `05-src/wiring/rulesets/promoting.json`, declared in `stages.json` and traced to it, so `verify Stages` judges it), its detective partner release/CTL26 `verify History held` (does the list of rules in force for the default branch hold `non_fast_forward`: PASS, FAIL, UNKNOWN; a pure judge and a command line, `05-src/history/judge-held.mjs`) and the Requirement release/F81 and Architecture release/A72 of the partner. The Intent release/I54, the Requirement release/F77 and the Architecture release/A68 stay and move to the preventive control. release/CTL22, its judge `verify-history.mjs`, its workflow source and its Cases leave in the DO.

Not here, each its own step: loading the ruleset (the Owner's, by hand, with his explicit go), retiring the rule of the old ruleset (the Owner's, after the new rule is loaded and observed in force), reading the rules in force from GitHub through Hosting and running the partner on a schedule or a push, and the protection against a deletion of `main` (the `deletion` rule: its own Intent and control).

## Challenge to the legacy controls

release/CTL22 is retired here. The old ruleset's `non_fast_forward` rule is replaced by the same rule in `promoting`; retiring the old one is the Owner's step after it. The `deletion` rule of the old ruleset stays until its own control exists.
