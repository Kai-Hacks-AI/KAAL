# release/C105 hold History

The Owner's decisions of 20 Nov 2026: the `non_fast_forward` flag moves from the old promotion ruleset to the new `promoting` ruleset, the detective push judge release/CTL22 `verify History` has no future, and the real prevention is CI: a station requires detections and holds the rules it declares. A control is named and explained by what it controls, never by how it is realized. Some valid controls are realized only as a rule of a ruleset (`hold History`, and later the protection against a deletion of `main`, and review); some are a detection that CI turns into a preventive control; the table of the controls shows what is controlled, and the station's declaration holds the realization. A control points to its Intent and stays thin: the details live in the Intent and the Requirements and Architecture under it, so one Architecture can serve several controls.

This Change adds the preventive control release/CTL25 `hold History`: the history of `main` is never rewritten, a push that does not move `main` forward is refused. The Promoting station declares it in `stages.json`, beside the detections it requires, traced to the control, and the ruleset source `promoting.json` carries the rule that does it, so `verify Stages` judges source against declaration. Its detective partner is release/CTL26 `verify History held`: it compares what the station declares for the control with the rules in force (PASS, FAIL naming what is not, UNKNOWN; a pure judge and a command line, `05-src/history/judge-held.mjs`) with the Requirement release/F81. The Intent release/I54, the Requirement release/F77 and the Architecture release/A68 stay and move to the preventive control; one Architecture, A68, the ruleset of the Promoting station, addresses both Requirements (F77 holds the history, F81 observes it) and is the one element that the Requirements of the other entries of the station (no deletion, review, the required detections) are added to as they migrate, so every entry is held and tested the same way: its declared entry is in force. release/CTL22, its judge `verify-history.mjs`, its workflow source and its Cases leave in the DO.

The station owns its list; a detection does not name the station. The preventive controls that are realized by requiring a detection at a station (release/CTL5 `gate analyze Vulnerabilities`, release/CTL20 `gate verify Triggers`, and likely release/CTL10) stay controls: later slices, each its own Change, rename and re-explain them by what they control (for example, no known vulnerability reaches `main`). This Change only names that.

Not here, each its own step: loading the ruleset (the Owner's, by hand, with his explicit go), retiring the rule of the old ruleset (the Owner's, after the new rule is loaded and observed in force), reading the rules in force from GitHub through Hosting and running the partner on a schedule or a push, the renaming of CTL5, CTL20 and CTL10, and the protection against a deletion of `main` (the `deletion` rule: its own Intent and control).

## Challenge to the legacy controls

release/CTL22 is retired here. The old ruleset's `non_fast_forward` rule is replaced by the same protection in `promoting`; retiring the old rule is the Owner's step after it. The `deletion` rule of the old ruleset stays until its own control exists.
