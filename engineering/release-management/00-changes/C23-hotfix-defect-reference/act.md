# C23 act

A 4L retrospective of the Change, from the viewpoint of the agent that carried it.

## Liked

The Owner's decision was one sentence, and it became one line in F16, one hint in the draft and three Cases that fail when the reference leaks into Urgency or Approach. The RED run on `main` and the throwaway amendment made the DO a matter of two edits. KAAL Defects already had an identity, `<capability>/D<n>`, so nothing had to be invented.

## Learned

I first could not find the wording of the decision on any PR, and had to ask for it; it was already in the Owner's comment. The first thing to do with a decision from the Owner is to read the latest owner comments on the PR, not the older ones. The CPO also caught a sentence in `change.md` that named the rename of only one Case. A Change that amends a requirement also moves what an earlier Change installs: the draft that release/C17 installs now differs from the installed template, so C17 needs a re-wiring DO after this collector reaches `main`.

## Lacked

A check that the installed template equals the draft on `main`, which would have shown the drift at once. As it is, T181 sits in the engineering collector of release/C17 and compares them only there. A statement in an amending PLAN of which other Changes install the amended text.

## Longed For

A line in F16 or the draft that points to the Cases which hold it, so the amendment names its own evidence. An order for the promotion seat between this collector and the HOTFIX wiring that does not rest on my reading of two threads.
