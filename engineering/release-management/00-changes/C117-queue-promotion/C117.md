# release/C117 the merge queue of main is held by the promoting ruleset source

The merge queue of `main` lives in the old promotion ruleset only. The `promoting` ruleset source, which is where the rules of the default branch move to one at a time, has no queue rule. This Change moves the queue into that source, with the same parameters the old ruleset has (squash, one entry at a time, all green), under a control of its own, release/CTL32 `queue Promotion`, and the Intent it answers, release/I63. Once the rule is live in the `promoting` ruleset, Branching's promotion-freshness verdict, which does by hand what the queue does by construction, can retire (a later Change).
