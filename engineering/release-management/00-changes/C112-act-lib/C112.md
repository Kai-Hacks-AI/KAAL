# release/C112 the judges of the ACT stand on one small library of facts

The three judges of the ACT that are on `main` or on their way (`hold Act`, `demand Act`, `limit Act`) each grew their own reading of the tree. `demand Act` imports `hold Act`'s judge to reach the facts it needs, so one judge depends on another; the way of finding the Cases that apply, the Changes a collector carries and the retros it holds lives inside a judge, where a fourth judge (`verify Act`) and the Dispatcher cannot use it without importing that judge too. The Owner (20 Nov 2026) asked for a layered library before the next judge is built, worked SOLID: one reason to change per module, the judges thin, and a Case that keeps the layers one-way.

This Change is a refactor. It moves code and renames one function for what it does; it changes no verdict, no command line, no declared control and nothing in a workflow. The existing Cases are the safety net and stay as they are, but for the assertions that pinned a source file to its PLAN draft (the Owner, 20 Nov 2026: "brittle tests: bad test design"), which this Change removes, and one rename (see the accept list). It adds the Intent release/I61, the Requirement release/F87, the Architecture element release/A77 and the source `05-src/phase/lib/`. It adds no control: the layering is kept by a Case.

The `applies` flag the judges are to return beside their verdict (architecture's follow-up) and the switch of the Dispatcher to the judges are the next two Changes; the library is the shape they are built on.
