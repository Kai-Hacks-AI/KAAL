# release/C136 the Dispatcher reads from files whether a collector's Changes reached their final step

The event raise of the Dispatcher (release/C93) and its scheduled raise (release/C73) decide that a collector is ready for its PROMOTION from the title and the body of a pull request: `## Change` then `## Records`, a title that starts with `ACT:`. That is a convention of text, not a file of KAAL, and a body that does not follow it makes the run skip and end green: no PROMOTION and nobody told. It happened on engineering C23 (#2094), on four controls ACTs (#2536, #2537, #2538, #2540) and on release's own ACT #2547. This PLAN records the Defect release/D19 with a reproducing Case and decides the remedy: the final step is read from the files of the collector at the merge revision, and a merge that files cannot read is reported, never skipped silently. It changes no source, workflow or WIRE.
