# release/C111 the ACT of a collector is checked for the form of a retro

The third of the three small controls of the ACT (Owner, 20 Nov 2026): `hold Act` (release/C103) keeps an ACT out of a collector while the work is not finished, `demand Act` (release/C108) asks for the ACT once the work is finished, and `verify Act` (this Change) checks that the ACT is a retro of the form kaal-retro defines. The ACT is the retro of the collector, `retro -reflects-> Changes` (Owner, 20 Nov 2026), in `02-learnings/retros/`, kaal-retro's object. The Owner released this control when kaal-retro reached `main` and prioritised all three.

Release does not define the form: kaal-retro owns it, with a finder (handed `{ path, text }` files, it lists the retros with the Changes each reflects and refuses what does not fit) and a judgement of form (`well-formed`, `malformed` with the rules broken, `unjudged` for a format it does not know), both on `main` under `packages/kaal-retro/skills/kaal-retro/scripts/`. This Change is the Exchange answered: the control asks those two scripts, given by path, and says what their answers mean for the collector, so a change of the form needs no change here. It is small: the Intent release/I60, the Requirement release/F86, the Architecture element release/A76 (the judge), the control release/CTL31 `verify Act` (detective first, preventive when required), and the judge `05-src/phase/judge-verify-act.mjs` with its command line.
