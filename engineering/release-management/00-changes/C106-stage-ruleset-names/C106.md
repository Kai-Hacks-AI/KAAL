# release/C106 the ruleset sources are named by their stage number

The six ruleset sources under `05-src/wiring/rulesets/` are named by the lowercase stage (`promoting`), and the judge of `verify Stages` compares that name in code. The rulesets live in the repository are named by the stage number (`30 - promoting`), which the Owner chose on 20 Nov 2026 ("00 left for all, 10 left for development"): 20 integrating, 21 hotfixing, 22 wiring, 23 collecting, 30 promoting, 40 publishing. The source and the ruleset in force can therefore never be the same ruleset: loading a source would create a second one beside the numbered one.

The Owner's go (20 Nov 2026): "yes, align the names". This Change makes the stage declaration the one place that says what each ruleset is called (`ruleset`), makes the judge compare the source with that declaration and hold no name in code, and renames the six sources. The scheme can nest (210, 211); nothing here builds for it.
