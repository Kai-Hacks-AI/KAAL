# C31 baseline

The baseline is `main` at 888ce0f. `main` holds the envelope judge with the HOTFIX head rule, the chapter rules and the verdict contract (release/C21, C22), and the Defect reference in F16 (release/C23).

- The Defect chapter is checked for content and for the shape of the reference, not followed.
- Nothing checks that the Defect is recorded, that it names a reproducing Case, that the Case exists, or what its result is.
- A HOTFIX naming two Defects, or a diff across several capability roots, is not marked.
