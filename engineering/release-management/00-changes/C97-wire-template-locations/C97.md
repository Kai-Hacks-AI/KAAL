# release/C97 the WIRE template points to the declared delivery locations

The Target chapter of the WIRE pull request template (`05-src/wiring/templates/wire.md`) says the destination is "`.githooks/` or `.github/`". The delivery locations are declared once (release/I53, release/A67), and `.kaal`, `.agents` and `.claude` are wirable too. The template carries a list of its own, the duplication release/C95 removed from the consumers. The CPO asked for the source to be corrected before the WIRE of the template (#2046) is approved: the template refers to the declared wirable delivery locations and enumerates no root.
