# release/C113 a judge of the ACT says whether it had anything to judge

The judges of the ACT answer PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN. PASS means two different things today: "judged, and fine" and "nothing to judge here" (no retro reflects a carried Change, so there is no ACT to hold; the pull request is not an ACT, so there is nothing to limit). A place that asks several judges, such as the Dispatcher deciding whether the final step of a collector is done, cannot tell them apart, and so it has had to read the body of a pull request to tell. Architecture (20 Nov 2026) asked for a structured flag beside the verdict: `applies`.

This Change gives every judge of the ACT an answer `{ verdict, applies, message, ... }` through one small module of the shared library, `lib/result.mjs`: `applies` is a boolean; it is false only on a PASS that had nothing to judge; FAIL and UNKNOWN always apply. The final step of a collector is then: hold, demand, verify and limit Act each PASS with `applies` true at the merged tree, and any FAIL or UNKNOWN or a PASS that does not apply means no promotion is raised. What each judge means by "applies" is part of the Requirement. It changes no verdict, no message and no command line, adds no control and reads nothing new. The Dispatcher that asks the judges, with the title and the body of the PROMOTION computed from the tree, is the next Change.
