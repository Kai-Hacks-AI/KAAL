# release/C68 declare verify Envelope

Declares the control `verify Envelope` (release/CTL11): one check that reveals whether the envelope of any pull request, its route, title, chapters, `HOW TO WORK` table and `Driven-by` identity, is well formed. It is the first slice (S1) of the design `verify-envelope-design.md` that the Owner approved on 17 Nov 2026 ("Go for it, sounds clean"). It declares; it builds nothing.

The judge already exists (`05-src/plan-body/envelope.mjs`, release/C12, release/A33) and runs by hand. Today no control names it. This Change gives it the declaration the other controls have, in the agreed scope: the envelope only, never the truth of what is written. The interpreter that reads the rules from the templates (S2), the sidecars (S3), the new rules (S4), the adapter and the CLI (S5), the CI and pre-push instances (S6) and any gate (S9) are later Changes with their own PLANs.
