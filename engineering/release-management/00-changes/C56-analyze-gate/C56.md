# release/C56 gate analyze Vulnerabilities at promotion

Makes the promotion of a known-vulnerable dependency stop. The detective report `analyze Vulnerabilities` (release/CTL2) gets explicit settings in its workflow source, and the promoting ruleset source requires its Promoting check. That requirement is a second control, `release/CTL5`, preventive, with the new Intent `release/I30`. Everything here is source: no ruleset is loaded and no workflow is installed. Loading follows the ruleset update procedure, by the Owner, after an explicit go.
