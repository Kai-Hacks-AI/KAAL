# release/C76 a stage's ruleset stands: active, on its own branches, with no bypass

Declares the preventive control `stand Ruleset` (release/CTL13) and its answer chain for settings the new rulesets hold and no control states yet (release/C70, `change.md`, Left open). It covers:

- `enforcement`: `active` (the ruleset is on, not evaluate-only and not disabled)
- `conditions.ref_name.include`: exactly the branches of the stage (`kaal/**` for Collecting, `hotfix/**` for Hotfixing, `wire/**` for Wiring, all three for Integrating, the default branch for Promoting, `refs/tags/trial-*@*` for Publishing)
- `bypass_actors`: empty (no actor, bot or person skips a rule)

The chain is the one the Owner asked for on 17 and 18 Nov 2026: control release/CTL13 answers Intent release/I41; Requirement release/F58 answers the Intent; Architecture release/A53 answers the Requirement; Test Cases cover each setting; the ruleset source and the stage declaration are the code that turns them from red to green. One Change covers several settings: what matters is the mapping from the control to each setting.

It is a source in Engineering: nothing is loaded, installed or switched here, no ruleset or setting changes, and nothing under `.github` is touched. It follows release/C70 (`verify Stages` judges a ruleset against the rules its stage declares), whose collector tip is its baseline.
