# release/C95 delivery locations are declared once and every consumer reads them

Solution architecture defines what is delivered; delivery architecture defines how it is delivered. Both are code in Git, both are governed and versioned, and they serve different outcomes: a functioning solution, and a reliable delivery of it. The distinction is by responsibility and outcome, not by technology, repository, directory or packaging format; in KAAL a package is one kind of solution unit, and the model holds unchanged where there are no packages. The Owner (19 Nov 2026): the locations of delivery are named places, and they are specific to a consumer. Today four places each carry their own list of locations, and they disagree: `.kaal` is delivered, and Change Isolation knows it, but the WIRE install judge and the merge-event classifier accept only `.github` and `.githooks`.

Delivery locations are one concern of delivery architecture; installation, synchronization, activation and verification may belong to it as well. This Change is narrow on purpose and anchors in that wider architecture.

This Change is the first slice: one declaration of KAAL's delivery locations, and the two WIRE consumers (the merge-event classifier of release/C93 and the install judge of release/F22) read it instead of carrying a list. The other two lists (the evidence rule of Pull Promotion and Change Isolation) are the next slice, in their own Changes, because they belong to other owners.
