# release/C95 delivery locations are declared once and every consumer reads them

The Owner (19 Nov 2026): where KAAL delivers what it installs is delivery architecture, named locations and things, and specific to a consumer. The solution architecture of a package only declares what it installs (the asset targets of its contract); it does not say where a consumer may deliver. Today four places each carry their own list of locations, and they disagree: `.kaal` is delivered, and Change Isolation knows it, but the WIRE install judge and the merge-event classifier accept only `.github` and `.githooks`.

This Change is the first slice: one declaration of KAAL's delivery locations, and the two WIRE consumers (the merge-event classifier of release/C93 and the install judge of release/F22) read it instead of carrying a list. The other two lists (the evidence rule of Pull Promotion and Change Isolation) are the next slice, in their own Changes, because they belong to other owners.
