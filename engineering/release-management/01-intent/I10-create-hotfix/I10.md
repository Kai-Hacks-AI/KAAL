# release/I10 create Hotfix

An urgent correction is understood at a glance as the correction of one defect in a named state: what is wrong, where and with what impact, what is changed and what is left alone, how the correction reaches production and the work in flight, and how it is undone. Its body states that and nothing that the repository, the Cases and the controls already show. Owner: release management.
