# release/I63 a promotion lands on the tip of main, one at a time

Contract: a promotion is merged into `main` through a queue that builds it on the current tip of `main`, one entry at a time, and lands it by squash. The check that a promotion is fresh against `main` is the queue's, not a per-run verdict of a workflow. Reason: a promotion that was tested against an older `main` can break `main` when it lands, and a rule that depends on the order of events leaves a stale verdict (release/D12). Owner: release management. The old promotion ruleset holds this today with its `merge_queue` rule; the `promoting` ruleset source holds the same rule, and Branching's promotion-freshness verdict can retire once that rule is live there.
