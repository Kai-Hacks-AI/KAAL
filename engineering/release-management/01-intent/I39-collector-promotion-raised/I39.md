# release/I39 a collector whose ACT has merged has its PROMOTION raised without a person raising it

Contract: when the ACT of a collector has merged, the collector is ahead of main and no PROMOTION of it is open, a PROMOTION of that collector into main is raised, with the Change id of the ACT in its body. What raises it only raises: it never approves, merges, syncs or edits a branch. It decides from data about the collector, never from anything the collector contains.

Owner: release management.
